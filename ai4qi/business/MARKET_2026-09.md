## Only Ai4Qi
1. Cross-specialty library of published closed-loop audits with before/after numbers.
2. AI protocol grounded in exact standard wording plus published evidence.
3. Personal, portable, free tracker that follows the doctor (rotations, IMGs abroad).
4. The full output chain ending in a presentation-ready deck.
5. Privacy by architecture (records never leave the device).

## Fastest copiers [I]
1. iatroX (same audience, NICE retrieval, SEO). 2. General LLMs / NHS Copilot ("good enough", free).
3. Life QI / AMaT adding AI (trust contracts, sticky). 4. AI portfolio startups. 5. Royal colleges/HQIP (slow).

## Positioning
1. Lead with verifiability: exact standard wording + audits that did it before you (the library is the moat).
2. Own "closed loop by your ARCP date"; export summaries shaped for Horus / risr / ISCP.
3. Be the IMG on-ramp; partner with PLAB/MSRA educators.
4. "No patient data leaves your device" as a governance selling point for clinical tutors and audit leads.
5. Name collision: "AI4QI / ML4QI" is a University of Toronto lab (Khosravani Lab); use a qualifier in brand/SEO.
