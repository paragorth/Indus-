# Accessibility

Last updated: [DATE]

We want everyone who plans or runs a clinical audit to be able to use Ai4Qi, including people who
use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, magnification or voice control.

## Our aim

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA**. Ai4Qi does not yet
fully meet this standard. The known gaps are listed below, with what we are doing about them.

## What we have done

- **Keyboard use.** Ai4Qi uses standard links, buttons and form controls, so it is designed to be
  used with a keyboard. A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing on every page, and
  keyboard focus is shown by an outline.
- **Focus management.** When you move to a new screen, focus moves to its main heading, so screen
  readers announce the new page.
- **Labels and structure.** Form fields are labelled. Pages use headings, lists and landmarks, and
  the page language is set to English (UK). Icon buttons, such as the thumbs up and down, have text
  labels for screen readers and announce whether they are selected.
- **Status messages.** Messages such as "Check your email", build progress and save errors are
  announced by screen readers without moving focus.
- **Zoom and small screens.** You can zoom the page, and the layout adapts to phones and tablets.
- **Dark mode and reduced motion.** Ai4Qi follows your device's dark mode setting, and stops its
  loading animation if you have asked your device to reduce motion.
- **Colour.** Results are shown as numbers and percentages as well as coloured bars, so colour is
  never the only way information is given.
- **Text contrast.** Body text is dark navy on white or near-white in the light theme, and light on
  dark navy in the dark theme, well above the minimum contrast. Secondary grey text and the gold
  labels and section numbers meet the 4.5:1 minimum for small text (the light-theme gold is about
  5:1).
- **Focus and form fields.** The keyboard focus outline is at least 3:1 against its background in
  both themes, and the borders of text boxes and drop-down lists are about 3.7:1 against white.

## Known limitations

- **Timeline and results charts** are drawn with coloured blocks. The values are also shown as
  text, but the charts themselves have not been tested with screen readers.
- **Downloaded files.** The slides, spreadsheets and data files you download have not yet been
  checked for accessibility. You may need to add slide titles or alternative text before you
  present or share them.
- **Linked content.** Published papers, standards and other websites we link to are outside our
  control and may not be accessible.
- **Testing.** We have reviewed the code and measured the colours ourselves. We have not yet
  completed a full manual test with a keyboard and screen readers, had an audit by an
  accessibility specialist, or tested with disabled users. We plan to do all three.

## Reporting a problem

If you find something hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please tell us.

- Email: [CONTACT EMAIL]
- Tell us the page or screen, what you were trying to do, and the device, browser and any
  assistive technology you use.

We will reply within 5 working days, and we will try to find another way to give you what you need
while we fix the problem.

## Enforcement

Ai4Qi is run by Paraggarg Limited (company number 16367622), trading as Ai4Qi, a private company, not by a public sector body. The Public Sector
Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 therefore do not
apply to Ai4Qi. We publish this statement and aim for WCAG 2.2 AA voluntarily.

Service providers in the UK must still make reasonable adjustments for disabled people under the
Equality Act 2010 (or, in Northern Ireland, the Disability Discrimination Act 1995). If you are not
happy with how we respond to your report, please contact us again at [CONTACT EMAIL] so we can look
at it afresh. You can also get free advice from the Equality Advisory and Support Service (in Great
Britain) or the Equality Commission for Northern Ireland. In Ireland, contact the Irish Human Rights
and Equality Commission.

## How we tested

This statement is based on our own review of Ai4Qi's code against WCAG 2.2 AA, and on contrast
measurements of the colours in both themes. We will review it at least once a
year, and whenever we make a large change to Ai4Qi.
