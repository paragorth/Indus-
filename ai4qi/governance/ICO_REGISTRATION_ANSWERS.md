## The short answer

- **The fee is due.** Ai4Qi is run by Paraggarg Limited, a private limited company, which decides how
  personal data is used (controller), uses it on computers, and does so for purposes beyond the
  exempt list. The company's existing registration (ZB979579) covers it.
- **Tier 1 (micro organisations): £52 a year.** £47 if you pay by direct debit (£5 discount).
- **Register here:** https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/register/
  (takes about 15 minutes; must be done in one session).
- Optional check first, the ICO self-assessment (about 10 minutes):
  https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/data-protection-fee-self-assessment/

## Current fees (from 17 February 2025)

| Tier | Who | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — micro | Turnover up to £632,000 **or** no more than 10 members of staff | **£52** (was £40) |
| 2 — small and medium | Turnover up to £36 million **or** no more than 250 members of staff | £78 (was £60) |
| 3 — large | Everyone else | £3,763 (was £2,900) |

Charities and small occupational pension schemes pay tier 1 whatever their size. The ICO treats
every controller as tier 3 until told otherwise, so complete the form.

**Staff** means employees, workers, office holders and partners, averaged over the financial year;
a company director is an office holder and counts. With the director only, the company is in tier 1.

## Who is exempt, and why Ai4Qi is not

You need not pay if you process personal data **only** for: staff administration; advertising,
marketing and public relations for yourself; accounts and records; not-for-profit purposes;
personal, family or household affairs; maintaining a public register; judicial functions; or
without a computer. Some elected representatives are also exempt.

Ai4Qi runs user accounts, profiles, reminders and an AI build service. That is none of the above.

**Not-for-profit exemption:** Ai4Qi is free, but this exemption is narrower. It needs an
organisation set up for not-for-profit purposes that processes only what is needed to establish or
maintain membership or support, or to run activities for its members or regular contacts. Ai4Qi's
processing goes beyond that, and sponsor messages are sent. **Answer "No".**

**What the ICO publishes:** controller name and address, registration number, tier, date paid and
expiry date, trading names, and DPO details if you have one. It does not publish the contact people.

After paying: a confirmation usually arrives the next working day, and the entry appears on the
register of fee payers within seven working days. Put the registration number into the privacy
notice, the DPIA and `RECORD_OF_PROCESSING.md` (done: ZB979579).

**Existing registration (what was done):** the fee is paid by the controller, not per activity.
Paraggarg Limited already paid (ZB979579, tier 1), so "Ai4Qi" was added as a trading name instead of
paying a second time. Keep the tier under review if staff or turnover grow (ICO helpline 0303 123 1113).

**Nature of the processing** (keep this ready if the ICO asks, and for the exemption-form free-text
box if ever needed): "Ai4Qi is a free website that helps UK and Irish clinicians plan and run
clinical audits. We process users' email addresses, optional profile details (grade, specialty,
region), audit progress, reminder details, feedback, and topics typed into an AI audit builder. We
do not process patient data; audit records stay on the user's own device."
