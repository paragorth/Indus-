| LA-75.1c | Massive random fingerprint search. 6,004 random binary number features (v mod m in random residue sets, mod-30 lookup tables, last digit, leading digit, tens digit, divisibility, digit-sum classes, fraction-sign sets); 2,315 distinct informative ones on LA. Statistic per feature: within-document residual correlation (residual = feature minus its stratum mean), i.e. does a document have its own number habit beyond its commodity and magnitude mix. Null: numbers permuted across documents within stratum (1,000 perms); Westfall-Young maxT over features; count of features at P <= 0.01 vs null; 2-fold document split (top 10 chosen on one half, tested on the other, Bonferroni 10). | LA read: 12 features at P <= 0.01 vs 22.9 expected (null 95 % 59; P_glob 0.72); 0 at FWER <= 0.05 (best: fraction-set features, z 3.4, FWER 0.80); 2-fold replication 0/20. LA read+damaged: 35 vs 23.3 (P_glob 0.20), FWER 0, replication 0/20. | NO document-level number habit in Linear A. |
