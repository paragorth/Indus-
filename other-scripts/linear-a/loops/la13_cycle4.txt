Script: tools/la13_c4.py. Output: data/la13/c4_report.txt.
Rationale: cycles 1-3 had no power because sites share few words; scribes of one archive should share names and terms.
Groups = attributed scribes (lineara.xyz scribe field) with >= 15 word tokens: 14 scribes overall, 9 at HT.
