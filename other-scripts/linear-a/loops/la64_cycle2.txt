la64 cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026): the per-head instrument (E1) on Linear B, Linear A and planted archives.
Script tools/la64_c2.py; checkpoints data/la64_ckpt/c2_*.json (git-ignored).
E1: a person count on the same, previous or next line as a commodity quantity, or the person total of the
document (KU-RO of a VIR block, else the sum of the VIR counts). Per head, q x u / persons must fall in a
ration window (day, month or year) of the commodity class. Fodder windows apply for grain only. Each count key
is labelled person, small stock, large stock or none, whichever is best. Unit profile: 241-point grid,
0.01-1,000 l. Nulls: numbers (quantities and person counts) shuffled between documents (20-30 times), random
units on held-out halves (300), and 30-60 nonsense rulers (every window moved by an independent factor of
10^U(-1.5, 1.5)).
Linear A pairs after cleaning (persons with whole counts only): 66 in all (GRA 17, NI 18, CYP 15, DA-SI-*118 4,
OLE 4, VIN 3, *304 3), of which 15 are next to a VIR line and 51 are against a document total.
