| LA-69.1a | Corpora (value-free tokens, numbers, lines). Calibration contrasts of known purpose, NOTE vs FINAL: LB_shape = Linear B KN+PY leaf-shaped (unnumbered/lettered lines, 1,157) vs page-shaped (numbered lines, 554), PY E-series excluded; LB_draft = PY Eb+Eo drafts (67) vs their fair copies Ep+En (10); UR3 = Ur III admin undated AND unsealed (2,500) vs dated AND sealed (2,500), date lines and seal surfaces removed before scoring; OA = Old Assyrian merchants' memoranda (50) vs legal debt notes/contracts (226). Reference archives: OB admin, proto-cuneiform, khipus (400 each). LA: 320 documents with >= 2 words and >= 1 number. PLANT = each FINAL set (and LA itself) with context deleted (header lines, totals, 30% of labels, 50% of words replaced by doc-private abbreviations, 40% of line breaks merged). | Built; 13 base features per document scored against random pools of 300 other documents of the same corpus that never contain it: VOC/VOC3 (words attested elsewhere / in >= 3 docs), BIG (word pairs attested), LSH (line shapes attested), FIRST (opening word used to open other docs), LAB (numbers with a word before them), HDR (number-free first line), TOT (a number = sum of >= 2 earlier ones), ENT (words per number), PRIV (1-sign words unattested elsewhere), REP (within-doc repetition), BARE (number-only lines), XGRP (words attested at another site). Vocabulary features minus a type-shuffled pool expectation; all features also residualised on log words and log lines (fit on calibration documents). | setup |
| LA-69.1b | Per-feature calibration: AUC(FINAL more reader-independent than NOTE), label-permutation P (200); planted notes vs their source. | Planted notes are detected by VOC 0.71-0.99, BIG 0.71-1.00, PRIV 0.76-1.00, XGRP 0.67-1.00 in all four corpora (positive control passes). Real known notes are NOT: no feature orders NOTE < FINAL in all four contrasts (raw or residualised). Leaves are MORE predictable from the rest of LB than pages (VOC 0.36, BIG 0.37 residual, P 0.005); OA memoranda as predictable as legal texts (VOC 0.44, P 0.16); only Ur III internal tablets are less predictable (VOC 0.68, BIG 0.71). Explicitness flips too: LAB 0.73 LB, 0.80 LB_draft, 0.64 OA but 0.26 Ur III; TOT 0.78 LB_draft but 0.26 LB_shape, 0.32 OA. ENT is the most consistent (0.78-0.83 in UR3/OA, 0.39 and 0.03 in LB). | Context-deletion notes have a fingerprint; real working notes of known purpose have no shared fingerprint across Linear B, Ur III and Old Assyrian. The premise 'notes omit what the writer knows' is true only of the artificial plant (A, calibrated negative at feature level). |
