LB-derived values of 52 LA syllabic signs scored by language-independent phonotactics (consonant OCP, place OCP, onset principle, sonority, inventory-frequency fit, phoneme-stream compressibility, blind la21 rows) against 10^5 relabelings per tier: R1 rows/columns relabeled as wholes, R2a vowels permuted inside rows, R2b consonants permuted inside columns, R3 all values permuted. Shuffles: OCP survives in LA and LB alike (word-level OCP), onset and order-based vowel signal vanish in both. Rows: pure vowels, M, R, T, K beat chance (P 0.0005-0.033); D, S marginal; J, N, P, Q, Z, W and single signs not (true LB rows also fail half the time at this size); held out in both halves: pure vowels, M, T.
