la63 cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026): WHAT WOULD THE MINOAN CLERK MISS? The pe51 deletion game ported to Linear A.
Tools: tools/la63_lib.py (corpora, plants, models, views), la63_run.py (single-type game), la63_sum.py
(pseudo-word calibrated classes). Checkpoints: data/la63_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Method. Every corpus becomes documents of ENTRIES (words/logograms closed by one number; Ur III: one
line). Random small models (transformer 1-3 layers or bi-GRU, d 32-64, random lr/dropout/epochs/
token-dropout) are trained on 4/5 of the documents (folds by tablet) to fill in: every numeral at once
(log2 magnitude MAG, fraction class FRAC, arithmetic total TOT), every logogram at once (its commodity
base, LOGO), the words of alternate entries (ENT) and alternate non-numeral tokens (HEAD). On the
held-out fifth each type with >= 3 tokens is erased everywhere (its own target slots never scored);
loss change per key (task x same entry L / elsewhere R) minus the change for 8 draws of as many random
other tokens erased in the same documents = excess. Calibration: each null draw played as a
pseudo-word against the other draws (same documents, same count); z by frequency bin; p = share of
pseudo group scores >= the type's; the threshold is set so that 2% of pseudo-words are classed. Classes:
NUM-L (own entry's magnitude/fraction), NUM-R, LOGO, TOT, ENT, HEAD, else SILENT. No Linear B value
enters any Linear A input; fraction letters are opaque classes. Prior work: pe51 (Proto-Elamite) and
la49 (model dissection); web search found no deletion-profile study of Linear A.
