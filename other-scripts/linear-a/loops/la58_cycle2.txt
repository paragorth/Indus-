LA-58 cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026). Held-out sites and institutional words (tools/la58_c2.py; results data/la58_ckpt/c2_results.json).
Rejection ABC on the same banks (500 nearest of 100k / 60k worlds, statistics scaled by MAD). Held-out site h: accept on every statistic except h's own 13 and its pair overlaps, then predict h's profile; error = mean |z| between the accepted worlds' median and the real profile. Compared with 500 random prior worlds and with the 500 nearest no-institution worlds. Institutional words: 24 accepted worlds re-run to documents, a forest learns 'title' (office word) from 12 word-level features (frequency, number of sites, site entropy, share at the largest site, document-type mix, first position, with numbers, alone on a document, tokens per document) and is applied to real words with >= 3 tokens.
