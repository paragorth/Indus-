# LA-37 cycle 1: FIND THE DOUBLETS (calibration of a blind interchangeability detector)
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-37.1a | tools/la37_common.py, la37_c1.py, la37_report1.py. Every pair of signs with >= 8 tokens (10 in full LB) in 2+ sign words scored blind for contextual interchangeability: T = rank average of (i) cosine of PPMI context vectors (left, right, 2-back, 2-ahead neighbours, word edge '#'), (ii) the Voynich v35 split-half interchangeability index (contexts from two document halves, cross-half cosine over self-cosine), (iii) word-type pairs differing only by a <-> b in one slot, normalised by each sign's swap degree. Statistic chosen once on Linear B (rank average beat each component) and then frozen. Nulls: frequency-matched pairs (percentile within sign-frequency bins) and a within-word sign shuffle (R 100-150, per-pair z, BH q, max-z FWER). Positive control: Linear B KN+PY (DAMOS), blind; scored post hoc with LB values (16 doublet pairs: a/a2, a/a3, ra/ra2, ra/ra3, ro/ro2, pu/pu2, ta/ta2, o/wo, labialised and palatalised pairs; same consonant; same vowel). | LB full (75 signs): AUC doublet 0.78, same C 0.78, same V 0.63; frequency-matched doublet AUC 0.82; top 20 pairs 70 % related vs 23.5 % base (a~a3, ra~ra3 at fm 1.00; ra~ra2 0.99, ro~ro2 0.98, pu~pu2 0.98, ta~ta2 0.94; o~wo 0.60, de~dwe 0.04 missed). LB at LA size (10 document draws, ~3,900 sign tokens, ~4.6 doublet pairs in the alphabet): doublet AUC 0.69, same C 0.67, same V 0.60; top 20 57.5 % related vs 24.9 %. | Grade A (method): a blind distributional detector puts Linear B doublets and same-consonant pairs at the top (2.3-3x base rate in the top 20), also at Linear A size, though weaker. |
| LA-37.1b | Kill controls: the same LB corpora with signs shuffled inside each word (keeps word bags, removes order). | LB full shuffled: doublet AUC 0.69, top 20 55 % related; LA-size shuffled 0.58, top 20 42 %. Order adds signal (0.78 vs 0.69; 57.5 % vs 42 % at LA size) but about half of the related-pair signal is carried by word bags alone (inflected forms of one word share their other signs). | Order-specific signal exists, but the shuffle null is not a clean null for doublets: it keeps much of the true signal. |
| LA-37.1c | Planted doublets in the real Linear A corpus: a new sign X* replaces host X (KA, SI, TA, NA, DA, RE) at rate 0.4 under 4 regimes: token free variation, by word type (lexical), by site group, word-initial only (complementary). | Token: planted pair is the top-scoring pair of X* 6/6, overall percentile 1.00, frequency-matched 1.00. Site group: 5/6 top, percentile 0.999. Lexical (by word type): 0/6 top, percentile 0.73. Word-initial only: 0/6, percentile 0.60. But the within-word-shuffle z flags 0/24 (q and FWER never reach 0.1). | The ranking recovers free-variation and writer-split doublets (12/12 near the top); it is blind to doublets fixed per word and to doublets in complementary distribution (upper bounds: < 3/6 each). The shuffle-z test has no power even for perfect planted doublets (it shuffles the evidence into its null), so it is dropped as the flag; cycle 2b uses half-split replication instead. |
