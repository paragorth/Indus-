LA-71 cycle 1: RESTORATION AUDIT, part 1. How does our Linear A corpus encode damaged, uncertain, restored and erased signs and numbers, what did our parsers keep, and a restoration-aware parser checked by hand against SigLA drawings (6 Oct 2026)
Why: the Proto-Elamite pe73 audit found that its parser counted editorial restorations as read, and one key result changed. Same question for Linear A.
Scripts: tools/la71_parse.py (parser + loader), la71_sigla.py (SigLA re-parse), la71_wear.py (stipple per sign box), la71_c1_check.py (hand check), la71_show.py (marked listing).
Outputs: data/corpus_ra.json (committed, the new default corpus), data/la71_ckpt/ (git-ignored: sigla71.json, wear.json, hand_wear.json, c1_check.json, parse_report.json).

| LA-71.1a | Inventory of what the sources encode. lineara.xyz (LinearAInscriptions.js, GORILA-based): every transliterated token and every character in the 1,721 records was scanned for brackets, half-brackets, '?', vacat, illegible-sign marks and private-use marks. SigLA (779 document pages, already cached by la22): popup classes and reading fields. Control: the explorer.js / utils.js source of lineara.xyz itself, to see how the site treats each mark. | lineara.xyz has NO per-sign GORILA apparatus (no underdots, no half-brackets ⸢ ⸣, no [restored] signs, no vacat). It has: the private mark U+1076B (2,171 uses), which the site strips as a break mark; it sits at broken edges, on its own for lost lines, and 33 times inside a word that the edition joins across a break; [[ ]] erasures (11 tokens: HT 34 [[7]], HT 36 [[20]] [[6]], HT 46b [[4]], HT 127b [[KI+MU]] [[14]], KH 8 [[NI]] [[1/2]], PKZg 22 [[VIN]] ...); ']X' / 'X[' partial brackets (6); '+[?]' and '+[ ]' unread or lost ligature parts (~80); '—' lost sign (56), '≈' (8), '?' unread fraction (4), 'None' (3). SigLA has per sign: sure / unsure, '[?]' unidentified sign (385), 'erasure' role (104 occurrences), sign boxes; stipple (worn surface) is drawn on the copies but not coded; SigLA has no integer numerals. | The only restoration-type information in our main source is the break mark and the 11 erasures. Damaged-but-read signs are invisible in lineara.xyz; SigLA's copies are the only per-sign damage source. |
| LA-71.1b | What our parsers kept. Read build_corpus.py (corpus.json, used by 72 tools), la22_data.py (docs.json, 23 tools), la60_common.load_la, la45/46/47/51/56/58 loaders, la22_sigla.py. Control: re-parse of the cached SigLA pages with a pattern that keeps every 'occ-N' popup. | corpus.json DROPS every break mark (build_corpus.py calls it a 'line-continuation mark'), so words cut at a break and numbers at a broken edge count as complete; it KEEPS the 11 erasures as live text (erased numbers 7, 20, 6, 4, 14, 1/2 enter sums and lists); the 33 bridged words count as whole words; ligatures with '[?]' stay as logograms. la22_data keeps the break mark (gap tokens). la22_sigla.py lost 639 of 5,099 SigLA occurrences: its reading pattern [^<]* fails on every subscripted reading (ra2, pa3, ta2 ...), so la22's SigLA comparison (98.8 % agreement, 13 'SigLA extra signs') ran on 87 % of SigLA and miscounted RA2 / PA3 / TA2 positions as gaps. SigLA codes with variant letters (AB131a, AB21f) were also unread. | Same failure as pe73, in a milder form: nothing marked restored is counted as read, because lineara.xyz marks no restorations, but break-edge tokens and erasures are counted as ordinary text. The la22 SigLA side result rests on a parse that drops subscripted signs (to be re-graded in cycle 3). |
| LA-71.1c | Restoration-aware parser (tools/la71_parse.py -> data/corpus_ra.json). Token schema identical to corpus.json plus 'st' (read / damaged / restored / erased / illegible) and 'fl' flags: edgeL, edgeR (touches a break), cont (the edition's repeat of a break mark at the start of the next line), bridge (word joined across a break = restored), part (bracketed or unread ligature part), erased, and from SigLA (Needleman-Wunsch alignment on sign codes, A/AB prefixes unified): unsure, unid (SigLA '[?]'), variant (SigLA reads another sign), nodraw (no SigLA counterpart), worn (stipple covers >= 0.20 of the sign box; for a number, of the strip right of the preceding sign). Stipple measured on all 779 SigLA drawings (fetched, measured, deleted; dots = small round ink components). Versions: all (= corpus.json), rd (read + damaged; restored and erased become '#R' placeholders that keep their slot), read (read only). Control: version 'all' must equal corpus.json token for token. | 'all' = corpus.json for 1,721 / 1,721 records (0 mismatches). 699 records align to SigLA (4,097 aligned signs). Tokens (words, logograms, numbers, unk) 5,312: read 3,596 (0.68), damaged 1,603, restored 31 (bridged words: libation formulas IOZa 2/9/14/15/16, PKZa 8/12, SYZa 3/8, TLZa 1, KNZc 6 ...), erased 15, illegible 67. Flags: edgeR 830, edgeL 340, cont 475 (not counted as damage), worn 695, part 95, variant 93, nodraw 61, unid 30, unsure 29. Numbers 1,566: read 1,162, damaged 398, erased 6. | Parser built; regression control passes. |
| LA-71.1d | Hand check against SigLA drawings. (i) Wear: 96 sign boxes, 32 from each stipple stratum (cover < 0.1, 0.1-0.3, >= 0.3), shown as unlabelled crops on 4 contact sheets and labelled blind (W = stipple touches the strokes); population estimates weighted by stratum size. (ii) Numbers: every number on 8 KU-RO / list tablets (HT 2, 11b, 85a, 88, 89, 104, 118, 122b; 59 numbers) judged from the whole SigLA drawing as clear or damaged (in stipple / at a break). (iii) Break-mark convention: HT 2 (GORILA facsimile and SigLA), HT 3, HT 85a, HT 122b. (iv) nodraw: HT 11b, HT 24a, HT 27b. Control for (ii): flags placed at random at the parser's rate (0.25). | (i) 43 % of all sign boxes have some stipple touching the strokes. The 0.20 cover rule flags only stippled signs (precision 1.00) and catches 52 % of them (the heavily covered half); 0.05 would catch 95 % at precision 1.00. (ii) numbers: precision 0.71 (5/7), recall 0.63 (5/8); random flags at the same rate: precision 0.14. 49 of 52 numbers the parser calls read are clear (0.94). Misses: HT 104 20+J and 29 (tens written as dots inside stipple; the strip rule misses them), HT 122b 97; false alarms: HT 85a KU-RO 66 (a crack band between word and number, the number is whole), HT 122b 7. (iii) The edition repeats the break mark at the start of the next line after a line that ends in a break (HT 2: left edge intact) -> recorded as 'cont', not damage; marks inside a line are real breaks or cracks. (iv) nodraw signs are drawn by SigLA but read differently (HT 27b VIN, HT 24a ligatures); first 259 'nodraw' were the la22 subscript bug; after the fix 61 remain, treated as damaged (disputed), not restored. | Parser grade B (method): 'read' tokens are ~94 % clean on the checked numbers; 'damaged' is conservative at the edges and misses part of the surface wear. 'Restored' can only be shown for 31 bridged words: lineara.xyz does not mark GORILA restorations, so restorations inside a break may still hide among 'damaged' break-edge tokens (upper bound: the 1,170 edge tokens). |

| LA-71.1 verdict | Cycle 1 | corpus.json treated break-edge tokens, 31 bridged (restored) words and 15 erased tokens (6 of them numbers) as clean text; there is no hidden GORILA restoration layer in lineara.xyz to strip. The restoration-aware parser marks 30 % of tokens as damaged, restored or erased with 0.71 precision on numbers and 0.94 clean 'read' numbers in a hand check. Side bug: la22's SigLA parse drops subscripted signs (13 % of SigLA). | Grade A (data): the counts above. Grade B (method): the parser and its versions. Cycle 2 re-runs the B results in all / rd / read. |
