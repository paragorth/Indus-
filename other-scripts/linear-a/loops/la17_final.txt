| LA-17.final | Words (and, separately, sign types) as pathogens, 13 sites with >= 10 word tokens as hosts. Per-word SI/SIR outbreak in C on a gravity x distance contact network with an unknown source, unknown adoption times, founder copying, loss, Zipf popularity and effort-calibrated detection; cycle 2 adds an unknown deposit (sampling) time per site so turnover can seriate. Likelihood-free inference: 390,000 simulated outbreaks (LA words 160k + 80k, LB 40k + 30k, signs 70k), rejection ABC (0.2 %) and ABC random forests on 121 summaries; plus a simulator-free turnover seriation (pair z vs 300 document-label permutations, geography regressed out, Fiedler ordering). Dates never input; deposit phase per site (median find context: PH MM II ... HT/KH/ZA LM IB) used only to score. Controls: 1,000 held-out prior outbreaks; a planted PH-source outbreak ordered by distance (20 reps); 80 outbreaks planted by a different generator (lexicon copying); doc-label shuffled incidence (30); Linear B KN (LM II-IIIA1) vs PY TH MY TI KH (IIIB). | Source posterior is flat in every version: LA words TH 0.15 / TY 0.12 (flat 0.077), all rejection 95 % rank intervals [1-13]; LA signs HT 0.24, but shuffles also give HT 30/30. Planted PH source recovered 1/20 (cycle 1) and 0/20 (cycle 2, order rho -0.36); alternative-generator outbreaks: source 0.05 (chance 0.077), order rho -0.01. LB: KN first in two of four runs, but shuffled LB makes KN (words) or PY (signs) the source in 29-30/30 with the same order, i.e. document mass, not history; the deposit-time run puts KN 3rd.
