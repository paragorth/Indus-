Held out: the 66 documents whose lineara.xyz source is a paper later than GORILA vol. 5 (1985): 43 word tokens, 155 sign tokens. Training: everything else (GORILA 1-5 and unlabelled). Stage 1 saw only training plus the held-out documents' sizes and sites.
Models: EXCH (exchangeable Chao community bootstrap, 2,000 refits), ABC (6,000 random Zipf communities, 150 kept), HAB (site-aware novelty rates, 2,000 beta-binomial draws). Known words: frequency ranking (EXCH) vs site mix (HAB, lambda 0.5 fixed in advance).
