# LA-9 cycle 2: approximate Bayesian computation with millions of simulated scribes
# Tools: tools/la9_abc.py (simulator + rejection ABC), tools/la9_c2.py (runs). Outputs: data/la9/c2.out (json lines),
#        data/la9/abc_<run>.npz (accepted draws), data/la9/c2_partial_old.out (first, interrupted launch; LA_shuf1-2 at 1M draws kept)
# Simulator: each section's entries are added by a scribe who, per section, uses one mechanism drawn from weights p
# (exact, skip line, double line, dropped written carry, counting-board slip +-place, tally +-j, rounding/ignoring
# fractions, misread fraction sign, lacuna = +-1..5 lost lines drawn from the corpus pool). The total is then WRITTEN:
# integer part + up to 4 fraction signs, greedy largest-first with the drawn values. Prior: values uniform on the
# 49-value grid, p ~ Dirichlet(1), lacuna sign ~ U(0,1). 10 value-free summaries of written totals vs entries
# (Dint = 0, |Dint| = 1, |Dint| = 10^k, Dint = +-an entry, Dint < 0, small other, big gap, fraction sections whose
# total has no sign, total signs within the entries' signs, integer sections whose total has a sign). Keep the
# closest 0.05% (scaled Euclidean distance).
