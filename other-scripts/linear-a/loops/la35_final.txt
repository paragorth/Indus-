| LA-35.final | THE NUMBER BENDS THE WORD (la35; tools/la35_common.py, la35_c1-c4.py). Does a word's ending change with the number after it (1 / 2 / >= 3; round vs not), as singular, dual, plural or counting forms do? (1) Within-stem-family G and cross-stem CMH tests over 117 stem-definition x number-class cells (39 stem definitions, 30 of them random cut points), family-wise max-z, against numbers shuffled within (tablet, commodity) and (site, commodity). (2) Massive held-out rule search: 1,500 random ending -> number-class rules per split x 20 tablet splits. (3) Bare W vs extended W+X pairs and their kill test with length-matched fake pairs. Controls: Linear B count entries (no LB sound values used as input; LB is only a calibration corpus) at LA size and full; planted agreement rules; corpus-wide shuffled numbers. | LA, totals words removed: no ending tracks number (G P_fw 0.44-0.61; CMH 0.16-0.17). With totals, the only signal is final -RO of KU-RO / KI-RO / PO-TO-KU-RO before large numbers (P_fw 0.023). The held-out search finds nothing (M3 AUC 0.42 vs null 0.55). LA's W+X forms carry more '1' entries than W (P 0.003-0.065), but length-matched fake pairs do the same (P 0.31): word length, killed. Controls: planted rule on 15% of entries recovered 3/3 (P_fw 0.007); LB at LA size passes (1) against the site null in 5/6 subsamples and the within-tablet null in 2/6; LB passes (3) in full (P 0.004) and in 3/6 LA-sized subsamples; the held-out search (2) fails LB at LA size (no power). | No number-agreement morpheme in Linear A (B, calibrated negative for rules covering >= 15% of entries; LB-sized effects excluded only against the site null). The one ending that tracks number, -RO of the total words, is accounting (A, known). Grade C, would support / would kill: an LB-sized plural suffix (share of '1' 0.10 lower on W+X) cannot be excluded with 37 LA stem pairs (90% interval -0.11 to +0.18); new W / W+X pairs on non-HT tablets with the longer form before >= 2 would support it, a pooled excess >= 0 over 80+ pairs would kill it. The DA-KU-SE-NE (6, 1) / DA-KU-SE-NE-TI (45) pair cited in the literature is one of 37 pairs and moves nothing (leave-one-out range +0.17 to +0.22). |
