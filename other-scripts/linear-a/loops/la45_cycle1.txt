# LA-45 cycle 1: TWO MACHINES ARGUE UNTIL THE MEANINGS SETTLE - calibration and first Linear A game (random document split)
# tools/la45_common.py, la45_c1.py, la45_report.py, la45_compare.py; checkpoints data/la45_ckpt/c1_*.json, c1.log, c1_report.txt
# Game: a Proposer gives every word/sign type (opaque id; no sound values, no glosses) one of <= 12 abstract meanings. A meaning is only what it
# entails: which meanings may follow it (class bigram), which types it emits, and occurrence frames (next = end/number<10/number>=10/fraction/word;
# adjacent number equals the running section sum or not; document length). Proposers fit by collapsed Gibbs (incremental, ~0.5 us per candidate)
# with an MDL cost per meaning (lambda nats + assignment code). Critics hold a vault (20% of documents) and evolve the half of it that hurts the
# Proposers most; they score held-out bits gained over a one-meaning model minus 1 bit per CONTRADICTION (a held-out frame or transition with
# predictive p < 0.01 in a row seen >= 30 times), on committed words only (train count >= 5). Population-based training: 12 Proposers x 6 Critics,
# 120 rounds; bottom quarter copies the top and perturbs lambda/K; weakest Critic copies the strongest and swaps 10% of documents. Final test = 20%
# of documents never seen by either side. 6 independent populations per corpus (own split, own seed); ~10 M candidate evaluations per population.
# Vocabulary names are attached to meanings by fixed profile rules (TOTAL: adjacent number = section sum >= 25%; COMMODITY: number-adjacent >= 50%
# and token-weighted type frequency >= 8; ENTRY: number-adjacent >= 35%; HEADER: document-initial >= 40%; TERM: frequent; RARE: rest).
