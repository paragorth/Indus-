# LA-21 final: "let universals build the grid"
Scripts: tools/la21_core.c (annealer), la21_common.py, la21_run.py, la21_report.py, la21_c2.py, la21_c3.py, la21_queue_c2.sh, la21_queue_c3.sh. Outputs: data/la21_ckpt/ (git-ignored; npz of every restart, reports report_c1.txt, report_c2.txt, report_c3.txt). Logs: loops/la21_cycle1-3.txt.
Prior check: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md and a web search (Kober/Ventris inflection grids; Corazza et al. 2022 contextual sign clustering for Cypro-Greek) found no blind C x V grid built from phonotactic universals alone.
Method: the 65 commonest signs are assigned blindly to a 1 pure-vowel row + R consonant rows x K vowel columns grid (<= 2 signs per cell). Score = tier-factorised bigram likelihood (consonant tier and vowel tier independent; harmony or its absence fitted) + consonant-row OCP + pure vowels word-initial. Simulated annealing, about 7,200 restarts in all (~7e8 moves); co-assignment over restarts gives stability. No sound values as input; LB values used only to score controls and, afterwards, as an outside check on LA.
