LA-49 final: DISSECT A MACHINE THAT LEARNED LINEAR A (5 Oct 2026)

Idea: do not decipher the script; decipher models of it. 115 tiny masked-token models (transformers with inspectable heads and bidirectional GRUs; random width 16-64, 1-3 layers, 1-4 heads, dropout, learning rate, epochs, train split) were trained to fill masked words, logograms and number sizes on whole documents, then dissected with attention read-off, single-head ablation, causal interventions on numbers (raise earlier numbers; duplicate an entry), word transplants (function vectors), ICA feature matching across seeds, cross-population representational similarity and direction ablation. Opaque identities only; no sound values.
Controls: three word-shuffled Linear A corpora; Linear B (DAMOS) and Ur III at Linear A size and at 4x; Linear A with planted total words and planted word-number bindings. The plan asked for hundreds of models; the machine was shared with two other loops (load 16-32 on 4 cores), so 75 + 40 models were trained and the populations per corpus are small (3-14).

Grades
- A (method): circuit-hunting in tiny models fails its controls for totals, number-word binding and word function at Linear A size; nothing about Linear A follows from those negatives.
- B: a sealing-vocabulary direction (*301, KU, KA, SI, RO, TE, ZE, I, TA, A, O, JA, DA, *164, *411-VS) is found in every model and is causally specific; agrees with la45.
- B (method): residual word geometry is reproducible and recovers known classes in Linear B (commodity, person, place) and Ur III (commodity, place, office, verb).
- C: Linear A commodity group with NI, *304, *306, *308, E, SU; libation-formula group with PA, NA, TU, NE, QE, DI.
- Demoted: nothing in Linear A read here as a total, person or site circuit. KU-RO tops the 'expects a big number' transplant score, but the same score misses to-so, szunigin and the planted totals.
