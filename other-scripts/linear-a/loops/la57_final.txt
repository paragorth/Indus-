Seven unrelated or distant record-keeping systems were reduced to one value-free form: opaque tokens, numbers, line or cord-group breaks; 42 position, number-adjacency, arithmetic and type-level features. The systems are proto-cuneiform, Ur III, Old Babylonian, Ebla and Old Assyrian (CDLI); Linear B (DAMOS); and Inca khipus (Open Khipu Repository; cord colour = token, knot value = number, TOT = top cords). Egyptian hieratic: no open structured transcription found. Three loops: (1) 140,000 random untrained scorers; (2) 2,100 random trained taggers (LR / tree / NB); (3) a 'parliament' where each system trains its own taggers and votes. Each is run leave-one-system-out and leave-one-civilisation-out, against label-permuted nulls inside each training corpus (30 / 5 / 5 replicates), with a planted administration (test only), on Linear A (whole and admin-only), and against 20 type-shuffled + 20 order-shuffled Linear A copies. | Transfers between civilisations: COMMODITY (B). 8/300 trained survivors on all 6 written systems vs 0 in every null; parliament on LB 0.857 > null max 0.841; Linear A logograms recovered as commodities: AUC 0.774 whole / 0.633 admin, vs shuffle q95 0.61 / 0.55. TRANSACTION and PLACE transfer above the null but the null is strong (C+). PERSON is not robust. UNIT and HEADER transfer within cuneiform only (no outside system carries them). TOTAL is anti-universal: 0/300 survivors (nulls up to 8); cuneiform-trained totals score LB to-so 0.17-0.32 and khipu top cords 0.28-0.48, below the label-permuted null; khipu voters do recognise cuneiform totals (0.77-0.79, one direction). Linear A: the world agrees above shuffles on a commodity layer and a 'word welded to a quantity' layer, but not on person / place / transaction. Those votes collapse onto the same signs (*516, *318, *326, *560, U, ZA, DA-QE-RA; A-DU and SA-RA2 in the pooled models). KU-RO is not voted a total (2/5). | NOT CRACKED. Cuneiform, Linear B and the khipus mark totals in mutually opposite ways, so outside bureaucracies cannot find Linear A totals; KU-RO stays known only from arithmetic. B: commodity is the one role every written bureaucracy shares, and it recovers Linear A's logograms. B (negative): outside administrations cannot separate Linear A persons, places and transactions; LA has one 'named/heading entity' axis.
