# LA-32 cycle 1: "the slips tell how they wrote" - real confusions vs shape and sound
Script: tools/la32_common.py, tools/la32_c1.py. Outputs: data/la32_ckpt/c1_report.txt, c1_res.json, mats.pkl (git-ignored).
Prior check: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md (la9 used arithmetic slips, la13/la16 one-sign variants for dialect/private spelling, never scored against shape vs sound); web search found only a qualitative claim that LA tablets were written from oral data because they look messy (no confusion-matrix test).
Shape: 316 LA and 86 LB signs rendered from the Noto Sans Linear A / Linear B fonts, 4 image metrics (blurred correlation, chamfer distance, gradient-orientation histogram, ink profiles), rank-averaged. Sound: la21 blind same-row co-assignment (7 LA runs; 1 LB run); LB values only for the LB control (CON = same consonant, VOW = same vowel) and as a starred outside check on LA. Null: the confusion graph's endpoints are shuffled (each sign keeps its confusion count; partner random), 3,000 reps.
