# LA-8 final: let evolution write the grammar (Linear A vs Linear B vs Proto-Elamite vs shuffled Linear A, with planted controls)
# Tools: tools/la8_common.py (corpora), la8_core.c (scorer, C, Baum-Welch plus one-counter stack), la8_gp.py (GP), la8_report.py, la8_truth.py, la8_stack.py, la8_stacksum.py, la8_attach.py, la8_margins.py, la8_run*.sh. Outputs: data/la8/.
# Compute: about 2.5 million machine evaluations in total, never more than 2 workers. Linear B sound values were not used; transliterations are identifiers only.
| id | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-8.final | GP over typed-slot probabilistic automata (word classes; open = admits novel words keyed by kind and last sign; 1-3 slots per class; Baum-Welch transitions). Fitness = 2-fold held-out bits + description length. Equal compute per corpus (240k evaluations, plus a 48k-evaluation escape restart). Corpora size-matched to Linear A (3,935 tokens). Controls: planted flat form (recovered: K=6/6, ARI 0.999), planted recursive language (recovered: open N/V keyed by their suffixes, ARI 0.97), within-document shuffle (LAS collapses to 1-2 classes and loses 1,131 bits), a second LB and PE draw, a forced K>=8 restart, and a stack arm with a flat-form false-positive control. | Best machines. LA: 4 classes, 4 states, 2 open (entry words; commodities), 0 closed word classes, one loop (word or commodity -> NUM -> (FRAC) -> next). LB: 7 classes, 2 open (words; logograms) plus closed numbered logograms and closed non-numbered formula words (to-so-de, o-na-to, ko-to-na, e-ke...). PE: 4 classes, 1 open sign class, a closed numbered-sign class, a header class. Recursion: no corpus beats the flat-form false positive (LA 63 bits vs PFLAT 243). Number attachment: in LA a number follows a word directly (P 0.52 after multi-sign words vs 0.37 shuffled), as in PE (0.48 vs 0.35-0.37); in LB words avoid numbers (0.07 vs 0.18) and numbers hang on logograms (0.71-0.76). | Linear A is closest to a SMALL FLAT FORM: a list template "entry-word or commodity + number (+ fraction)". It attaches numbers the Proto-Elamite way, and is smaller than Linear B's machine: it has no closed formula-word class and shows no recursion. At this size (about 4,000 tokens) Linear B's administrative tablets are also flat and also show no recursion, so this does NOT show that Linear A is not a language. It shows that the tablets are written as bare entries, without the separate counting logogram and formula-word layer seen in Linear B. Not cracked. |

## Evolved Linear A word classes (functional readings; grade by the evidence margin of LA-8.3c)
- C-ENTRY (open; 185 listed types + every novel multi-sign word): the counted thing or person in a list entry, followed by a number half the time. Members: KU-RO, KI-RO, SA-RA2, A-DU, KA-PA, KU-PA3-NU, DA-RE, MA-DI, SA-RU, KU-NI-SU, DI-NA-U; single signs TE, SI, KA, PA, A, I, TA, RE, TI, NA, TU, RA; damaged ligatures VIR+[?], HIDE+[?], *304+PA. Grade B as a class (92/98 types >= 2 bits, 0 misplaced). Inside it: KU-RO and KI-RO behave like ordinary entries, not like a closed "total" class (grade B for "no separate formula slot").
- C-COMMODITY (open over logograms; 73 types): what is counted. Members: GRA, CYP, VIN, OLIV, OLE and its ligatures (OLE+KI, +MI, +U, +DI, +TA, +NE, +RI, +E), CYP+D, CYP+E, GRA+PA, GRA+KU, MI+JA+RU, *401+RU, QA2+[?]+PU, AROM, CAP, plus SINGLE SIGNS NI (28 bits), *304 (12.5), *308 (11.5), KU, DI, *21M, *131C, *301, *316, ZU, WA. Grade A for the logograms; grade B for NI, *304, *308 as commodity signs (would support: they stand where GRA/CYP stand, before a number, at sites outside Hagia Triada; would kill: they take entry-word affixes or head lists). Grade C for KI, E, JU, *22M, *23M, *131B, *560 (margins < 2 bits).
- C-NUM and C-FRAC: closed singletons. The fraction slot follows NUM (grade A).
