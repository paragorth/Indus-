# LA-12 cycle 1: the tablets are pieces of a jigsaw (exhaustive closure search)
Scripts: tools/la12_common.py, tools/la12_c1.py, tools/la12_run_c1.sh. Output: data/la12/c1_V.txt, c1_C.txt (+ .pkl).
Web check (4 Oct 2026): hand studies of HT archive structure exist (Montecchi; Schoep), and HT 122a/b, HT 123+124
are known two-side totals; no automated cross-tablet subset-sum search with nulls and cut-tablet controls was found.
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-12.1a | Data. 182 inscribed HT tablet sides (la6 entry parser). Blocks = every section sum, whole-side sum (all tags and per commodity tag) and written total; 580 (V) / 579 (C) blocks. 53 targets = KU-RO, PO-TO-KU-RO, KI-RO numbers. Two arithmetic modes: V = integer part and the COUNT of each fraction sign added separately (no values assumed); C = conventional lineara.xyz fraction values (data only), 1/480 units. Exact closures, compatible tags, all subsets of 1-3 blocks from distinct OTHER sides (k1,k2,k3: 1.7e8 triples per run, sparse exact index) and completions own-section + 1-2 blocks (c1,c2). | Saturation map: a typical total of 10-100 has 0-15 k1, 50-900 k2 and 7,000-60,000 k3 exact closures; c2 hundreds. Only totals >= 400 or with rare fraction vectors have < 10 k3 closures. | Any 3-tablet 'ledger' is reachable for almost every HT total by chance. Only k1/c1 can be specific. |
| LA-12.1b | Real vs nulls (100 runs each): N1 entry numbers shuffled across sides within commodity tag, N2 iid draws per tag; written totals and each target's own-section sum kept real (an earlier draft that also shuffled own sums gave a false c-excess from near-miss sections; fixed). Neighbour-value null rejected as biased (decreasing density, round totals: real below it on every statistic). | V: targets with a k1 closure 32 (N1 30.4+-1.8, P=0.24; N2 P=0.11); with c1 6 (6.1, P=0.76); L_c2 P=0.22; L_k2 216.4 vs 209.3+-3.0 (P=0.02); L_k3 400.7 vs 395.5 (P=0.04). C: k1 34 (31.6, P=0.14); c1 8 (7.2, P=0.33); L_k2 P=0.01; L_k3 P=0.01-0.03. Per target (V, 139 non-zero tests): 17 at P<=0.01 (1.4 expected), all of them k2/k3 counts on large totals (HT 1, 11b, 100, 104, 110a, 118, 119, 122b PO-TO-KU-RO); BH(10%) keeps 4-6. | No excess of specific (k1, c1, c2) cross-tablet closures. A diffuse excess of 2-3-block combinations reaching large totals (~+3% log count) that cycle 3 must test against a null that keeps each tablet's magnitude. |
| LA-12.1c | Positive control PL: every HT side whose total closes internally (7 in V, 8 in C) cut into 2-3 fragments (total keeps a random share), fragments mixed back; same search, same nulls (34 runs), 3 seeds x 2 modes. | Planted targets do NOT stand out on their own true statistic: per-target P vs N1 0.03-0.94 (median ~0.2); planted-only H_c1 P 0.27-0.74, L_c2 P 0.12-0.44. Planted-only L_k2 P=0.029 every seed, but uncut HT shows the same L_k2 excess, so that is background, not the plant. | Positive control FAILS at HT size: real cuts of real HT tablets are not recoverable by arithmetic closure alone. |
| LA-12.1d | Positive control PB: 205 random Pylos + Knossos documents; 10 multi-entry documents get a planted exact total, then are cut the same way (3 seeds x 2 modes; LB sub-units in V, true values in C). | Mixed: planted H_k1 P=0.03 in 1 of 3 seeds; H_c1 P=0.03-0.85; L_c2 and L_k3 BELOW null (P>=0.94); per planted target P=0.03 for 3-4 of 10-16 in each seed (the large or fraction-rich ones). | Detection only for rare values. The method can find a join when the total is large or carries a rare fraction pattern, not otherwise. |
| LA-12.1 | Cycle verdict | Cross-tablet closures that beat chance at HT: none specific (k1, c1, c2 all at null). Known same-tablet joins seen in passing: HT 116b KU-RO *304 15 = HT 116a *304 section (k1, P=0.01), HT 122b PO-TO-KU-RO 97 (known 31 on side a + 1 + 65) has k2/k3 counts above null (P=0.01), but so do 7 other large totals that have no known join. Search power is the limit: cut real tablets are not found either. | Jigsaw by arithmetic: NOT SUPPORTED at k>=2 (saturated, no power). Next: (2) the summary-line form with multi-item donors only and a global annealed assembly; (3) attribute test (scribe, findspot, words) that the shuffle cannot fake, and a within-tablet bootstrap null. |
