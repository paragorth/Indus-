# LA-19 cycle 1: THE PALACE SPOKE MANY TONGUES - choose K and calibrate it
Scripts: tools/la19_core.c (collapsed Gibbs), la19_common.py, la19_c1.py, la19_c1_report.py. Output: data/la19/c1_report.json (checkpoints in data/la19_ckpt/, git-ignored).
Model: mixture of K sign-level bigram 'languages' over sign identities (BOS row = initial-sign preferences, EOS column = final-sign preferences and length), Dirichlet(beta x corpus unigram) rows, collapsed Gibbs. Word TYPES (>= 2 signs). K = 1..8; beta grid 3-300 per K; 5-fold held-out likelihood, 3 restarts per fold (K > 1), 200 sweeps; K chosen by the paired 2-SE rule; stability = mean pairwise ARI of 10 full-data restarts (300 sweeps). 65 corpora, about 31,000 Gibbs runs.
