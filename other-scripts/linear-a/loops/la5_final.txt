# LA-5 final: is Linear A administrative vocabulary a code? (cycles 1-4; tools/la5_common.py, la5_c1.py, la5_c2.py, la5_c3.py, la5_c4.py, la5_c4b.py; data/la5_c*.txt)
| id | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-5.final | One battery, the same code for every population: LA admin words (742 types / 1,065 tokens), LB admin words (DAMOS; positive language control, same tablet layout), LB personnel names, Proto-Elamite middles, HTS and ICD-10 designed codes, and planted codes (random IDs and slot codes on the LA and LB sign sets). Half-sample CIs. Nulls: global, position-class and order-2 Markov. Items: adjacency gain, phonotactic gaps, closed edge sets, positional asymmetry, affix extension vs Markov-2, uniqueness, Heaps, bigram-uniqueness ratio, cross-site reuse, frame/content bits, word -> commodity gain, internal vs boundary predictability, vowel echo, consonant avoidance, vowel-initial rate, Kober alternations. Calibration: language band vs code band. | Deciding statistic: same-consonant-series adjacency within words over a position-class shuffle. Language 0.36 to 0.40 (LB, LB_KN, LB_PY, LBpers); codes 0.92 to 1.08 (band 0.75 to 1.39). LA 0.631 [0.503, 0.792], z -4.1, 0/500 random partitions as extreme; non-HT 0.656 (p 0.01; partition p 0.03). It survives removing doublings (0.580) and LB-shared words (0.636). Elsewhere LA is weaker than LB: gaps beyond slot 0.021 vs 0.108; closed finals 0.030 vs 0.215; no vowel echo (0.93 vs 1.28); no inflection grid; more ID-like repetition (bigram ratio 0.92 vs 0.64). Product-code arm: LA words carry 0.07 [-0.11, 0.23] bits about the next commodity, vs 0.32 for LB_KN. The Markov-2 null has no power at this n (it beats every population, LB included). Usage items cannot decide, because relabelling leaves them unchanged. | REJECTED (the arrow). LA administrative words behave like LB on the one calibrated discriminator, at about 60% of LB strength [34-80%]: they are written phonetically (language-encoded names or terms), not as codes. They differ from LB Greek in being uninflected-looking and name-heavy. Caveats: the values are LB-derived (backward projection); 63% of tokens are from HT. Not cracked. |
