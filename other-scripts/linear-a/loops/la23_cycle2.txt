# LA-23 cycle 2: capture-recapture of the person-like words on the 205 Hagia Triada tablets (tools/la23_c2.py; outputs data/la23_ckpt/c2_all_strict.json, c2_strict.log)
Individuals = 'strict' person-like word types (cycle 1 rule; 177 types, 246 tablet-captures on 80 tablets; 136 seen on one tablet, 25 on two, 16 on 3+). Occasions: tablets (Chao1, sample coverage, Bayesian beta-binomial Mh by Metropolis, prior N ~ 1/N), Villa vs Casa (Chapman), 4 findspots and 4 commodity classes (log-linear M0/Mt/Mh/Mth/Mt2, AIC). 2,000 posterior draws with name ambiguity: homonym split rate eta ~ U(0, .3) per recaptured name, one-sign spelling variants (substitution, or insertion/deletion) merged with nu ~ U(0, .5). The tablet bootstrap was dropped after a smoke test: duplicated tablets fake recaptures.
