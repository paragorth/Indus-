LA-48 THE PHYSICS OF GOODS, cycle 3 (5 Oct 2026). Does conservation PREDICT held-out amounts?
Tool: tools/la48_c3.py. Results: data/la48_ckpt/c3_results.jsonl. 5-fold over documents; labels fitted by annealing on the training folds only (3 x 120,000 proposals per fold). For each held-out amount whose node has >= 2 training occurrences: PHYS hit = the amount equals the node's training residual (the amount that would balance it, either direction); COPY hit = it equals any training amount of the node; BEYOND = PHYS hits no copy explains (a true sum or difference). Nulls: 100 random labellings per fold (RANDLAB) and the whole pipeline on quantity-shuffled corpora (QSHUF: 10 runs for LA, 4 for LB and planted, 2 for Ur III).

| LA-48.3c | Linear A, all sites | COPY 62 of 302 vs QSHUF 51-78 (mean 62.9; P about 0.55). PHYS 2 vs 0-1 (RANDLAB 3.9). BEYOND 2 (HT 3 KU-PA3-NU 3; KH 88 NI 10) vs 0-1 in 10 shuffles (P about 0.09) | B (negative; power limited, see 3d): in Linear A a word's amount on one document predicts its amount on another no better than a random amount of the same site and commodity. Linear B (+18 %), Ur III (+4-7 %) and a planted archive with 50 % survival all show this effect; Linear A does not (-1 %). So Linear A words in entries do not behave like standing accounts (a person or store that keeps its quota from record to record), or the surviving records of each account are too few. Killed: the two BEYOND hits (at the shuffled rate). |
