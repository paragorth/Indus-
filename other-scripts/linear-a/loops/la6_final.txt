Functional grades from numbers alone (no sound values involved):
- KU-RO: A, total (x12 the typical amount, P = 0.001; confirms FINDINGS 2a).
- SA-RA2: B, label of the multi-commodity allowance package (CYP/NI/VIN, also GRA/OLE) that follows a counted total. Would kill: SA-RA2 heading a counted list of persons.
- CYP: B, 'precious' good measured in a large unit (70% of amounts <= 1). CYP after a total: C, a levy of about 1/25 of the counted total. Would support: HT 130's lost KU-RO was about 150-270, and HT 30's list totalled about 270-480. Would kill: a clear CYP add-on outside 2-6% of its total.
- TE: C, heading or aggregate word (amounts x2, 20 tablets). A-DU: C, a group heading with large amounts (x4.8, n = 5; heads HT 86a, 88, 95b).
- KU-PA, TU, WA: C, small-amount entry words (x0.2-0.3). Would kill: new tablets where they carry typical amounts.
- Bare-number list words (KU-PA3-NU, DU, PA-DE, SI-PI-KI, TA-NA-TI, ZU, QA-*310-I, *324-DI-RA, DI-DE-RU, MI-NU-TE, DA-ME, KU-NI-SU, SA-RU): C, one class of person-like list entries. The amount is set by the tablet, not by the name (HT 86a = 2 x HT 95b).
- KI-RO: no fixed share of the entry above it; stays 'residual or heading' (FINDINGS).
- No functional synonyms and no converter word ('payment', 'ration') can be graded from the numbers.
