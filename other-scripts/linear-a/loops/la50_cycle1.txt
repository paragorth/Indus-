LA-50 cycle 1: THE FARMING YEAR IS THE ANSWER KEY. Does the written order of goods follow the Aegean farming year?
Date 5 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/la50_common.py, tools/la50_c1.py, tools/la50_c1b.py (LA rows rerun after a class fix, planted p 0.8).
Logs: data/la50_ckpt/c1_main.log, c1b.log, c1b.json (git-ignored).
Novelty check: FINDINGS / STRATEGIES: la20 found a fixed commodity order (no calendar); la25 used crop calendars to date archive FIRES
from commodity MIX, not order. LITERATURE.md and a web search (Linear A commodity order + agricultural calendar) find no test of
written order against a harvest calendar.
Units: 'baskets' = runs of commodity signs inside a tablet not interrupted by a multi-sign word (LA 105, LB KN+PY 174); secondary unit =
first-occurrence list of a whole tablet. NI written alone counted as the fig good (FIC). Pairs weighted 1/(n-1) per basket.
Aegean calendar (harvest / intake midpoints, fixed before scoring): GRA Jun (barley May, wheat Jun), FIC Aug, VIN Sep, OLIV Nov, OLE
Dec, LANA May, LIV Mar, CROC Nov, ME Jun, flax (LB SA) Jun. Sources: FAO crop calendar API (api-cropcalendar.apps.fao.org; TN, MA,
JO: wheat / barley harvest Apr-Jun, fig Aug, olive from Sep-Oct); Hesiod, Works and Days 383-4, 571-81, 609-14; Varro RR 2.2, 2.11,
3.16; Columella 9.15; Pliny NH 19.16-17; Cretan olive (Nov-Jan, explorecrete.com) and grape (late Aug-early Oct, crete.direct) harvests.
Northern-Europe calendar (null): grain Aug, fruit Sep, vintage Oct, oilseed Aug, pressing Sep, shearing Jun, slaughter Nov, honey Aug.
Fit = weighted share of pairs written in calendar order counted from the year start, maximised over 24 half-month starts.
