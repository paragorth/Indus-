LA-20 THE PECKING ORDER, cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026). Every pair of items written in the same list is a contest won by the one written first. Rankers fitted to all lists at once: Bradley-Terry (MAP, pairs down-weighted 1/(m-1) per list), Plackett-Luce (MAP), Elo (20 random passes), mean relative position (POS). Score = 5-fold held-out accuracy on the order of item pairs in unseen tablets; "unmet" = pairs never in the same training list, so only transitivity can predict them. Null = 200 within-list shuffles through the same pipeline; family-wise P = max z over all 26 configurations in each null replicate. Lists: 352 LA tablets/lames/bars (sides joined, a first); W = all words (totals KU-RO, KI-RO, PO-TO-KU-RO removed), E = words directly followed by a number or logogram, L = logograms (base sign), F = first sign of each word. Tools: tools/la20_common.py, la20_c1.py, la20_report1.py, la20_c1_pylos.py. Checkpoints: data/la20_ckpt/c1_*.json.
