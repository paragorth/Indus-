LA-72 cycle 2: HELD-OUT TESTS OF THE CLEAN READING V2c (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/la72_c2.py (decoding, ablations, LB calibration; 2 workers), tools/la72_sum2.py (summary). Output: data/la72_ckpt/c2_LA.json, c2_LB.json, c2_summary.json.
Design: the primary la60-main split uses the frozen files (V2c sha256 7d6df161..., la70 V2 182fd0d6...); 10 more la60 splits are re-gated on their own training halves with the clean (train-only) procedure (V2c) and the la70 procedure (la70 V2). Versions: rd (primary, 191 test documents per split), read (check, 152), rnd (la71 control: the same number of tokens removed at random as read removes, 171). Every reading is scored through the same la70 decoder against 100 role shuffles, 50 random readings and EMPTY. STRICT = the whole document is explained and at least one total closes, a commodity pair agrees with the order, or a rule is checked.
