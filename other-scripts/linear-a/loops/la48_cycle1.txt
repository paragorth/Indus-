LA-48 THE PHYSICS OF GOODS, cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026). Do quantities conserve across documents?
Tools: tools/la48_common.py, tools/la48_c1.py, tools/la48_report.py. Results: data/la48_ckpt/c1_results.jsonl (git-ignored).
Model: words are opaque nodes (site, word, commodity). Header words (no number, before the first entry) hold the account; entry heads are counterparties. Each document gets a label IN / OUT / STOCK; each occurrence adds d*slot*q to its node. Quantities are exact vectors: integer part plus the COUNT of each fraction sign (no fraction values assumed), hashed with random 64-bit weights so that net = 0 is an exact test. Arithmetic totals are dropped blindly. Kirchhoff: a node balances exactly with flows in both directions. PAIR = the balance is equal amounts in and out (repetition); AGG = a genuine sum (a + b = c). Search: random starts + annealing, 4 x 150,000 proposals per run (about 600,000 labelled hypotheses per run, about 1.1e8 over the cycle), plus 20,000 random labellings per dataset as the label-shuffle baseline.
Nulls: QSHUF (amounts permuted among entries of the same site x commodity; 20 runs for LA, LB and planted, 3-5 for Ur III), POIS (Poisson amounts, 3 runs), RAND labels.
Controls: Linear B KN (2,206 documents with entries) and PY (758) from DAMOS; Ur III Puzrish-Dagan (CDLI) at LA size (393 documents, 5 draws) and 2,000 documents; planted conserved economies (officials collect from households and forward the exact sums to a hub; the hub issues rations and records stock), with 100 %, 50 %, 20 % of documents surviving, and an aggregating version (officials forward the sum of 1-3 rounds) at 100 % and 30 %.
