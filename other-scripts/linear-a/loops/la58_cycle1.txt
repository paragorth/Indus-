LA-58 cycle 1 (5-6 Oct 2026). Simulate Minoan government and let the paperwork choose.
Agent-based simulator in C (tools/la58_sim.c): random worlds of six kinds (T single palace + dependents; S capital + satellite offices that share officials and personnel; P independent peer centres; I temple centre receiving offerings; M merchant houses trading laterally; N no institutions), 30 random nuisance parameters (lexicon sizes, Zipf slope, list length, header and total habits, amounts, sealing, travel of nodules to the receiver, roundels, office sharing, personnel mobility, institutional share, redistribution, lateral density, type-specific survival). Each world transacts, writes tablets / roundels / nodules / inscribed objects (opaque word ids, numbers, arithmetic totals), and is thinned to the real surviving document count of each site. Fixed panel of 224 statistics (per site: document-type mix, list length, arithmetic-total rate, median amount, cross-site token share, within-site recurrence, sealing-device word foreignness, header foreignness, words per document, inscribed share, richness; every site pair: shared types normalised; two global). The same C code computes the panel on real corpora. Banks: LA 100,000 worlds (12 sites: HT 1110, KH 226, PH 66, KN 59, ZA 53, PK 25, MA 22, Thera 18, Iouktas 16, Arkhalokhori 15, Petras 12, Syme 12), Linear B 60,000 (DAMOS KN PY TH MY TI KH MI, thinned to 1,634 docs, W-series = sealing devices), Ur III 60,000 (CDLI sample, 7 sites incl. Puzrish-Dagan and Ur, tablets only). ABC-RF: separate forests for world type, site role and parent link; 4,000 held-out simulations for calibration. No sign values used.
