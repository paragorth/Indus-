# LA-14 cycle 2: thousands of random forger/discriminator pairings + univariate dissection + targeted number tests
Scripts: tools/la14_run.py (plan c2), tools/la14_report2.py, tools/la14_c3d.py (univariate, on cached cycle-1 forgeries),
tools/la14_c3t.py (targeted number tests). Output: data/la14/c2.jsonl, c2_report.txt, c3d_report.txt, c3t_report.txt.
Plan c2: 4 corpora (LA, LB, PLA, FW_FLAT) x 10 forgers (MK2, FLAT, NEUR, COPY0, E_num, E_word, E_wordsite, E_splLine, E_numshuf,
E_swapline) x 6 new seeds; each task draws 10 random discriminators = a random subset of 1-6 feature groups x a random learner
(LR C=0.3, LR C=3, random forest, gradient boosting, MLP 32 units). 2,474 discriminators (c1 + c2 together: 15,434 pairings).
Group effects: OLS of AUC on group dummies + learner dummies per corpus/forger, bootstrap over seeds.
New forgers: E_wordsite (replacement word drawn from the same site's tablets), E_splLine (two tablets joined at a LINE boundary).

Cycle verdict: the thousands of random pairings say the same as cycle 1 with less noise: after the world calibration only token-kind
order, number-scale coherence, size order across sections and within-tablet repetition (line templates, repeated words, repeated
numbers) stay. Two cycle-1 leads were artefacts and are demoted (foreign-word detection; token/line splices). Totals are real (FINDINGS 2a)
but invisible at the tablet level: a forger who ignores them is not caught.
