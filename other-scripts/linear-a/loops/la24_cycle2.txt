# LA-24 cycle 2: baseline-free apportionment fingerprints, and a fraction-value test
Tools: tools/la24_c2.py, la24_c2b.py; checkpoints data/la24_ckpt/c2.json, c2b.json.

| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-24.2a | Fingerprints counted per corpus: F1 remainder signature (a list holds q and q+1, q >= 3: the trace of a total split into equal shares and rounded); F1r (that cluster sums to a multiple of 5: a round total split); F2 common unit (>= 3 distinct values > 1, all u x w with w <= 8, u >= 2); F2r (u = 5, 10, 20, 25 or 50). Nulls (2,000 each): N1 amounts shuffled across lists (keeps the preference for round numbers), N1s shuffled within site, N3c frequency-weighted band replacement. Power: 20 planted largest-remainder/ROUND lists in the corpus. Comparators: 300 Ur III ration lists, 113 LB lists. | LA: F1 22 (N1 20.5, P 0.38; N1s P 0.46), F1r 0 (N1 2.0), F2 4 (N1 3.2, P 0.38), F2r 2 (N1 0.5, P 0.09). Planted: F1 26 vs N1 26.7 (P 0.63): F1 has no power. LB: F2 5 vs N1 1.1 (P 0.006), F2r 3 vs 0.1 (P 0.002). Ur III: F2 47 vs N1 103 (rations are everywhere multiples of 10 sila, so the shuffle raises the count), vs N3c 4.3 (P 0.002). | No apportionment fingerprint in Linear A. The common-unit test passes its Linear B comparator (P 0.006), so Linear A's 4/144 vs 3.2 is a calibrated negative for 'lists built on one unit share' (grade B, upper bound: < 3/144 beyond chance would be missed). The remainder signature has no power (planted control fails) and says nothing. |
| LA-24.2b | Fraction absorption (F3): a fraction-bearing list whose entries (all but at most one, >= 3) are u x w (w <= 8) with u NOT an integer, i.e. the fractions carry the remainder of an exact division. Count over the 58 fraction lists under CONV (lineara.xyz values), LA1BIN (attack-1 binary set) and 3,000 random value sets (22-value pool). Specificity controls (3,000 each): CONV's values permuted among the letters; letter combinations shuffled among fraction amounts (CONV kept); random sets from simple values only (1/2, 1/4, 3/4, 1/8, 1/3, 2/3, 1/5, 1/6, 1/16). Power: 200 planted fractional-u lists written in CONV letters. | CONV 15/58 vs random sets 9.1 +- 2.1 (P 0.011); LA1BIN 10 (P 0.38). Planted: found 1.00 with CONV, 0.13 with random sets (power is high). But: letter shuffle 13.8 +- 2.0 (P 0.36), simple-value sets 13.3 +- 2.7 (P 0.31), value permutation 10.6 (P 0.04). Lists hit under CONV: HT 8a, 20, 23a, 24b, 28a, 41a, 50a, 91, 98a, 115b, 126a, KH 6, 9, 58, PH 7b. Letter 'pinning' in the random sets (J 1/2, E 1/2, JE 3/4, L2 1/2) follows the simplicity of the values. | The fits do NOT constrain the fraction values. CONV beats arbitrary value sets only because its values are simple (halves and quarters): any simple set, and CONV with the letters scrambled, does as well. LA1BIN is not favoured either. Fraction readings stay as graded in FINDINGS (J = 1/2 C). |
