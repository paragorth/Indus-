Prior art (web search 6 Oct 2026; LITERATURE.md; FINDINGS/STRATEGIES): no joint physical-window fit of
Linear A unit sizes was found. The nearest work is the inscribed-pithos route (ZA Zb 3, VIN 32: Notti 2025,
Pasiphae 19, 'a unit of about 12.5 l' from a re-measured pithos; Younger's maximum about 1,000 l). That is a
single-object capacity anchor. Here it is used only as an outside check in cycle 3, never as an input. la6 fitted
commodity ratios without physics; pe55 is the Proto-Elamite parent.
Physical windows (litres; sources in la64_lib.py): grain 1.2 l/person/day (FAO/WHO/UNU 2004); wine 0.5 l/d
(Cato Agr. 57); oil 0.05 l/d (Cato 58; Foxhall 2007); olives 0.15 l/d (Cato 58); generic (unidentified
commodity) 0.5 l/d with a wide window; each x1, x30, x360. Household yearly output: grain 2,500 l, wine 1,000,
oil 150, olives 1,500 (Halstead; Foxhall). Vessel content: pithos 250 l (Christakis 2005, 50-1,000 l), jar 20 l.
Fodder for grain per head: small stock 0.8 l/d, large 4.5 l/d (as pe55).
Two instruments. E2 = every entry quantity x unit must be a ration, household output or vessel content (role
unlabelled, robust mixture against unit-free junk). E1 = a person count next to (same, previous or next line)
or heading (document total) a commodity quantity gives a per-head amount that must be a ration (or fodder).
Hypothesis = u per commodity key (0.01-1,000 l per written unit) + a value in (0,1) for every fraction sign
(nuisance; LA: A B D E F H J K L2 L6 W X, JE = J+E, DD = 2D) + per count-key label. 1,000-6,000 random
hypotheses per run, then per-key grid refinement.
