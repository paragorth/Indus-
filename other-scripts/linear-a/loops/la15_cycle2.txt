# LA-15 cycle 2: biogeography of the Minoan word stock (endemic, migratory, keystone; nestedness; capture-recapture; species-area)
Scripts: tools/la15_c2.py. Output: data/la15/c2.json, c2_stdout.txt. Null: fixed-fixed curveball on the document x word matrix (200 nulls; keeps every document's word count and every word's document count).
Controls: LA with word tokens shuffled across documents (word-site links broken); Linear B at LA size (3 random document samples of ~1,332 tokens) and all of LB.

|---|---|---|---|

LB at LA size 0.78-0.84 vs 0.27-0.30 (z 14-16); all LB 0.73 vs 0.26 (z 42). | Words are strongly site-endemic (A).
