What sound structure Linear A must have, beyond the controls (plain statement): written in Linear B values, its administrative words are short (mostly 2-3 signs on 1-1.5-syllable roots), carry both word-initial and word-final added material at similar rates, show no trace of onset-cluster spelling and no vowel harmony, and use a vowel system dominated by a, i, u with e weaker and o nearly absent. That is all the breeding can force; the rest (OCP, consonant stems, coda spelling) is unresolved at this corpus size (583 types).
