# LA-38 cycle 3: which consonant rows, vowel columns and signs carry the signal?
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-38.3a | tools/la38_c3.py, la38_report3.py. Two scores fixed in cycle 1: SC = mean z of ocpC, ocpP, vinit (vs 5,000 R3 relabelings); SV = z of comp (vs 5,000 R2a relabelings). Row test: the k signs of one LB consonant row each exchange their full value with a random sign outside the row (2,000 draws), P = share scoring >= the hypothesis; column test the same with SV. Sign support: C-support = share of exchanges of the sign's consonant with each sign of its own vowel column that lower SC (e.g. RO<->KO); V-support = the same for vowel exchanges inside its own row with SV. (A first version exchanged with any sign, which creates homophones that the plug-in compressibility rewards; it was found on LB, discarded, and rerun before LA was read: data/la38_ckpt/c3_calib_v1_homophone_bug.json.) | Calibration, LB drawn at LA size, true values (4 draws): rows with P <= 0.05: 12-47 % (r and the pure-vowel row in 4/4, w 3/4, d, n, m, p 2-3/4; single-sign rows never). Columns: 0 / 20 pass (column test has no power). Mean sign support 0.72-0.79 (C) and 0.71-0.83 (V); 6-18 % of signs below 0.5. Planted errors (6 value swaps = 12 wrong signs, LB 6 draws and LA 4 plants): AUC right > wrong 0.53-0.85 (median ~0.68); a planted error drops the rows it touches (LA plant: 0-31 % rows pass). | Row tests: calibrated, moderate power (a true row of 4-5 signs passes about half the time at LA size). Column tests: no power; no verdicts on vowel columns from this test. Sign support: weak (AUC ~0.68); usable only to flag, grade C at best. |
