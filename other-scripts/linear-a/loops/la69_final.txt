| LA-69.final | Reader-independence fingerprint calibrated on administrations of known purpose: Linear B leaf vs page tablets, PY Eb/Eo drafts vs Ep/En fair copies, Ur III undated+unsealed vs dated+sealed tablets (date lines and seals removed), Old Assyrian memoranda vs legal texts; planted notes (context deleted) from every final set and from LA; OB, proto-cuneiform, khipus as reference archives. Three methods: 13 document features (attestation of words, pairs, line shapes and openings in other documents, labelled numbers, headers, totals, words per number, private 1-sign words, repetition, cross-site attestation); 10,000 random fingerprints (2 modes) with leave-one-contrast-out, random-sign and label-permutation nulls; a cloze reader (outsider vs insider) per token; fixed-pool cross-site re-test with 6 seeds. | Planted notes are always detected (VOC/PRIV/XGRP AUC 0.7-1.0; cloze P 0.001 in 4/5). Real working notes are not: no feature, no length-free fingerprint (held-out pass 3-8%, most reverse) and no cloze measure orders notes below finals in all of LB, Ur III and OA; LB leaves are MORE readable than pages, only Ur III internal tablets are less. Length-dominated fingerprints transfer but do so equally with random signs (= shortness). LA: raw fingerprints put it beside LB leaves (shortness); length-free and cloze put it level with LB pages and proto-cuneiform (-0.136 vs -0.130/-0.135), far above its own planted notes (-0.233); insider gap 0.007, the lowest of 17 sets. Cross-site loss LA 0.26-0.62 vs LB 0.16-1.01, Ur III 0.29-0.91 at equal pools. | NOT SUPPORTED.
