# LA-32 cycle 2: controls that could kill the method (planted dictation vs planted copying; modern pair)
Scripts: tools/la32_c2.py (planted), tools/la32_c2b.py (English). Outputs: data/la32_ckpt/c2_report.txt, c2b_report.txt (git-ignored).
To avoid circularity the generator and the scorer differ: dictation slips are generated from LB-value consonants and scored with the blind la21 rows; copying slips are generated from the HOG shape metric and scored with the other three shape metrics (blur, chamfer, profiles). 60 replicates per cell, 300-rep degree-kept nulls.
