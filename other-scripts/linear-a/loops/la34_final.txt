Prior work checked: Castellan et al. "Writers Behind Words" (IWCP 2021 abstract) plan ML on SigLA for hands, without published scores; Linear A hand studies (Godart & Olivier GORILA V, Militello 1989, Tomas 2011, Schoep) are expert palaeography. New here: blind, image-based recovery scored against held-out hands with within-site nulls, a box-geometry-only arm, 3,000-hypothesis random search with held-out hands, and use of the blind hands on spelling, commodity, support, vocabulary and site.
| LA-34.final | Method and controls: SigLA drawings (779 documents, 5,105 occurrences cut with SigLA's boxes) -> shape scalars, 16x16 grids and box geometry; arms D (sign-agnostic ductus), M (sign-matched form), R (box geometry); within-site label permutations; positive controls = the two sides of one tablet (46 pairs) and planted affine hands; random-guess search with held-out hands and permuted-label searches; downstream tests residualised on unit size and shared sign codes. No Linear B per-occurrence drawings are openly available (LiBER: whole-tablet photographs), so no LB control. | Hands recovered (B): M nearest-neighbour hand 0.37 vs 0.19 null (92 units, 25 hands), 0.49 vs 0.28 with >= 8 drawings; ARI 0.28-0.31 vs 0.16-0.21; P <= 0.006. Held-out hands in the random search: AUC 0.576 vs 0.492 (P < 0.017). Sides of one tablet: AUC 0.65 (P 0.005). Planted hands are found only at ~+/-10 deg slant + 20 % stretch. What counts: per-sign width and diagonal steepness, line pitch and word gap. Uses: form tracks vocabulary without labels (residual rho 0.083, P 0.007; B), so shape and la16's text route agree; one-sign variant pairs are no closer in hand than unrelated documents (P 0.24; C: variants are not one writer's alternation); no commodity specialisation (P 0.14-0.26, upper bound); support type differs weakly (P 0.02, few pairs; C); cross-site pairs are farther than different writers at one site (site or drawing campaign dominates), so travelling hands are untestable (candidate pairs HT 63-ZA 9, KH 16-KN 32, HT 127-KN 32: C). | Grade B: Linear A hands are partly recoverable blind from drawn sign shapes, and the recovered similarity agrees with shared vocabulary. Grade C: variant spellings lean to different writers; support-type form effect; cross-site candidates. Would support: attributions made from photographs (not SigLA tracings) reproducing the blind clusters. Would kill: the same recovery from a draughtsman-only signal (e.g. equal scores when hands are permuted within find-group), or loss of the vocabulary link on re-drawn material. |
