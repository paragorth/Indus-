# LA-15 cycle 1: count the words like wildlife - how many words and signs did the Minoan administration use?
Scripts: tools/la15_common.py, tools/la15_c1.py. Output: data/la15/c1.json, c1_stdout.txt.
Web check (4 Oct 2026): unseen-species estimators are used on vocabularies (Efron-Thisted on Shakespeare; Kestemont et al. 2022 on lost medieval literature; musicology 2025). No application to Linear A, Linear B or any Aegean script was found, nor in LITERATURE.md / FINDINGS.md / STRATEGIES.md.
Data: lineara.xyz words of 2+ signs, logogram ligatures (A 400+, VS) removed: 1,282 tokens, 925 types, 776 singletons (84%), 85 doubletons, 508 documents. Syllabic signs: 5,440 tokens, 139 types. Linear B (DAMOS, undamaged words): 9,995 tokens, 3,703 types; 89 sign types.
