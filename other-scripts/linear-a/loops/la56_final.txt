Grades
- B (calibrated negative): no word, sign, commodity, role or fraction-sign class is systematically left out of, subtracted from or doubled in Linear A totals. A single word excluded in >= 2 sections would rank first (21/22 planted). Held-out re-test has little power at LA size (25 non-closing totals).
- B (calibrated negative): the LA mismatches look like random slips. At <= 2 changes they are explained as often as noise added to balanced totals; the Ur III control errors are more structured than its noise.
- B (negative): no pair of fraction signs behaves as interchangeable in the totals (best swap +0.4 nats vs null best >= 3).
- C (arithmetic exact; literature check pending): HT 127b is a running total that also counts an erased line. KU-RO 292 = earlier KU-RO 156 + 72 + 24 + 15 + 11 + [[KI+MU]] 14; without the erased line 278. So the scribe totalled before (or without) cancelling the duplicated KI+MU line: a double-count that was corrected on the face but not in the sum. Would support: a photograph showing the erasure is ancient and the second KU-RO is in the same hand. Would kill: a collation reading 292 or 14 differently, or the bracket being an editor's restoration rather than an erasure.
- C (post hoc, n = 4): KU-RO adds across logograms when the logogram follows a name (HT 9b TA-I AROM 2; HT 89 VIR+*313a 13) and keeps separate totals for commodity columns (HT 123+124a OLIV 93 J vs *308 and KI-RO; PH 31a one total per animal sign). Would support: new multi-commodity KU-RO sections that split the same way. Would kill: a commodity-column total that adds another commodity.
- Methods note: the Ur III check shows that an attribute-rule search finds real excluded categories but the global 'best rule' statistic is not specific (totals moved between Ur III lists also produce rules). Judge such searches by planted ranks and per-rule reading, not by best gain.
