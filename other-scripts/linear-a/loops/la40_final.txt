# LA-40 final: type inference as a compiler does it
| LA-40.final | Every word type gets one role (person/group, place, commodity/qualifier, heading/transaction, total, number-word); tablets must type-check under structural rules (closure of totals, headings before lists, logogram contact, one entry role per list). (1) Pre-registered rules + DM clustering, tempered Gibbs, 24 chains x 1e6 updates on LA; word-shuffle null (6 x 12 chains); LB KN+PY at LA size with known classes (6 draws), shuffled LB, full LB; 4 planted roles. (2) 1,200 random learned type systems scored on Knossos, re-tested on Pylos, against a label-permuted search (600). (3) Disjoint-halves stability on LA vs size-matched Pylos, shuffled-word null, ARI. About 1e9 sampler updates in all. | (1) LA: 164/181 types 'forced', 137 to the catch-all L; 27 non-default forced roles unseen in the null (KU-RO, PO-TO-KU-RO T; TE, SA, *307 ... C; A, A-DU, MA, JA, KU-RE, U ... H), but LB control fails: strict accuracy 0.05-0.40 vs shuffled 0.18-0.48, best-map = majority, TO-SO never T, even at full LB size. Plants: T and C recovered (rule-spelled), H not. (2) Learned systems: Pylos macro recall 0.176 vs permuted-label search 0.174 vs chance ~0.17-0.20. (3) Held-out ARI LA 0.10-0.14 vs Pylos 0.22-0.33 (shuffled ~0); KU-RO keeps T in only 3/12 halves. | Grade A (method, calibrated negative): structural type inference cannot name word roles even in Linear B at Linear A size; no Linear A word class is established by it. Grade B: LA word types carry reproducible role identity at ~0.4x LB strength (fits la8's flat grammar). Grade C: KU-RE, U, A, DA, KA-NA as heading-type words written first without a number. Would support: these words opening new tablets without a count; would kill: them as counted entry heads on new tablets. |
