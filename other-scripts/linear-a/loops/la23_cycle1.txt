# LA-23 cycle 1: count the people, step 1: separate person-like words blindly (tools/la23_common.py, la23_c1.py; outputs data/la23_ckpt/c1.json, c1.log, git-ignored)
Prior check: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md (la15 counted word types with capture-recapture, not people; 2d name-like repetition), STRATEGIES.md: no population estimate of individuals. Web: van Soesbergen's 'human personal names in Hagia Triada tablets' identifies names (interpretation, not used); no capture-recapture of persons found. Not done in this form.
Features (identity-free, no sound values): occurrences, tablets, repeats per tablet, share in entry position (followed by a number), followed directly (no logogram), number <= 5, = 1, median number, line-initial, tablet-initial, followed by divider, length in signs, list length, small-number share and logogram count of its tablets. Calibration labels: Linear B series-derived personnel names (DAMOS; 1,627 of 3,368 KN+PY word types).
