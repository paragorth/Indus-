STEP 2. Candidates (simulate the writers / change the medium), each with massive random guessing and a killable control
K1 THE SCRIBE'S HYPHEN. When a Linear A scribe ran out of line inside a sign-group, he cut it. Cuts are a bodily
   act made under time pressure and are the only place where the WRITER, not the modern editor, segments a word.
   If scribes felt sub-units (stem | ending, compound parts) the cut falls at them more than the physical prior
   (position in word) allows. 20,000 random boundary theories (sparse random weights over 10 corpus-internal
   boundary statistics computed ONLY from documents published by 1950: prefix/suffix standalone, prefix/suffix
   family size, successor/predecessor variety, sign transition surprisal, final/initial sign tendency) scored as
   log-likelihood gain over a position-only model on cuts in 1945-published tablets (HT), survivors frozen
   (sha256) and scored on cuts in tablets published after 1950 (Zakros, Khania, Arkhalkhori, Kophinas, Petras ...).
   Controls: cut positions shuffled within word length (whole lottery rerun, 50 runs); planted worlds where cuts
   follow one statistic 60 % of the time; random-hypothesis reference on the test set. LB calibration impossible:
   LB scribes never split words (la47: 0), recorded as such.
   Prior work: web search ('Linear A word division across line break', 'scribes hyphenation syllabic script
   morpheme boundary'): nothing on Linear A cut positions; general note that copyists cut at syllable breaks.
   KEEP (chosen).
K2 GIVEN BEFORE NEW. An abbreviation can only follow its full form in writing time. Within-document pairs where a
   short sign-group equals the first / last / middle sign of a longer one, counted by order. Pre-count (corpus_ra,
   all documents): initial-sign 28 after vs 36 before; final-sign 35 vs 21; mid-sign 29 vs 23; 2-sign rules 1-2
   cases. No given->new asymmetry and n too small for a lottery. DROPPED at pre-count (logged as LA-81.1.0).
K3 PANINI'S LAW FOR MINOAN. Speakers put light before heavy in fixed pairs. 10,000 random sign 'weight' functions
   predict which of two adjacent sign-groups (no number or logogram between them) is written first; frozen on HT,
   scored on later finds. Reserve for cycle 2 (check overlap with la20/la78 order work first).
K4 THE WRITER'S MODEL OF THE READER. Rational omission: a scribe writes a commodity logogram only when the
   reader could not infer it. Random word->commodity maps scored by how well they explain which entries carry an
   explicit logogram vs a carried-over one. Reserve for cycle 3.
K5 PRIMING CHAIN. Seriate each HT hand's tablets as the path that maximises text priming between consecutive
   tablets (random restarts); validate on findspot co-location. Reserve.
K6 SPACE PRESSURE. Short forms of a word at crowded line ends (truncation instead of a cut). Folded into K1 as a
   side count (are words that end exactly at a line end shorter than their type's other spellings?).

Choice: K1 first.
