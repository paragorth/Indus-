# LA-22 cycle 1: restore masked signs (calibration), 4 Oct 2026
Idea (arrow in the dark): predict what the fire destroyed. A random ensemble of thousands of restorers is calibrated on known signs hidden at the corpus's real damage pattern, then used on the real breaks.
Data: lineara.xyz raw file re-parsed with its break mark U+1076B kept (tools/la22_data.py): 1,721 documents, 2,100 break tokens on 512 documents, 792 words touching a break, 129 syllabic sign types, 5,460 sign tokens. The break sits at physical line edges, so the real damage pattern is "a word cut at its left or right end"; masks: int (one sign hidden, word length known), R (right end broken: the sign after a surviving prefix), L (left end broken).
Experts (tools/la22_engine.py), all counts leave the whole document out: FREQ, POS, left/right bigram, trigram (Markov), LEX (lexicon pattern match), LEX1 (one-sign edit), DOCW and DOCS (other words/signs on the same tablet), SITE, SCRIBE, SUPPORT, SLOT (last sign given what follows: number, logogram, word), two neural MLPs (context only; context + tablet sign bag), 2 seeds x 5 document folds. Ensemble: 4,000 random predictors per fold (random expert subsets, weights, product or mixture, uniform floor), the 25 best by log loss on one half of the documents averaged and scored on the other half. Positive control: Linear B (DAMOS), random documents up to LA's 5,460 sign tokens (785 documents, 119 sign types). Null: shuffled-tablet model (words, numbers and logograms permuted across tablets within site; same pipeline).
Scripts: tools/la22_c1.py, la22_freeze.py (LA ensemble and shuffled-tablet ensemble, 4,000 predictors), la22_lb.py. Results: data/la22_ckpt/c1_LA_test50.json (baselines, ablations), c1_LB.json, data/la22/la22_predictions.json (calibration fields).
