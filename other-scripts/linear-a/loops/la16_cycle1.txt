| LA-16.1a | Every pair of word types (>= 2 signs) differing in exactly one position. Weight of a pair = share of its token pairs written by DIFFERENT hands; sign-pair statistic T(X,Y) = sum over its word pairs. Null: hands permuted over documents within stratum, 2,000 permutations -> per-pair z, raw P, max-z FWER. Positive control: Linear B KN+PY, DAMOS hand attributions (1,987 docs, 132 hands, 4,903 word pairs, 1,748 sign pairs), scored post hoc with LB values (doublet = same/near-value special sign or glide, sameV = consonant differs, sameC = vowel differs). | LB (strata site+series): best KE~O z 3.4, FWER 0.24; nothing significant. Top-10/20/40 cross-hand sign pairs: doublet 0/0/0, sameV 1/6/9 vs base rate doublet 1.1 %, sameV 21.6 %. Mean z of (doublet+sameV) minus others -0.09 (P 0.93). LB (strata site): A~E best, FWER 0.25; same picture; only sameC (vowel changes = inflection) is shifted toward WITHIN-hand (P 0.006). Pair level: LB doublet word pairs (A/A3, O/WO, RA/RA3, A/JA, PU/PU2 ...) share a hand in 18.8 % of cases vs 13.9 % for unrelated pairs - known spelling variants are NOT more cross-hand than other one-sign pairs. | POSITIVE CONTROL FAILS. In Linear B, where spelling variants exist and hands are known, the hand-contrast statistic does not pick out variant signs; the one-sign pair space is 98 % different words (minimal pairs) and the few true variants (69 of 4,903 word pairs) are written both within and across hands. The premise "variants are private to hands" is false where it can be checked. |
