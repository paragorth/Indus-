# LA-11 cycle 3: why the Greek control fails (spelling mismatch vs sample size) (tools/la11_run.py c3 MIXED 40 60 --input_scheme DROP --lb_sizes 1500,full; report data/la11/report_c3.txt)
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-11.3a | Spelling-mismatch control. Held-out words of every language are spelled with DROP, while the targets' models are built with MIXED, so input and model follow different writing conventions for the same language. This mimics a script that spells its language differently from our syllabification. Same LA, LB and Markov inputs; 5,500 anneals. | Self-identification survives the mismatch: top-1 in 34/35, top-3 in 35/35. The best relative in own family drops to 6/31 (chance 4.3; median best-relative rank 5). LA: Coptic +0.19, Arabic +0.07, Nahuatl -0.02, Swahili -0.19, Sanskrit -0.24. 27/38 orphan inputs reach a higher maximum (p = 0.72). | A mismatch between our spelling schemes does not hide a language from its own model. The method recognizes **the same corpus**, not the same language family. With the language removed, relatives win little more often than chance (6-9 of 31 across cycles vs 4.3). |
| LA-11.3b | Sample-size control for Linear B. LB is scored at 742 types (3 subsamples), 1,500 types and all 3,678 types, with identities hidden. If the failure in 11.1-11.2 were only a matter of size, Ancient Greek should rise with n. | Ancient Greek rank by z: 22, 23, 33 (742 types), 22 (1,500), 25 (3,678). By raw gap: 9, 5, 8, 6, 6. Modern Greek ranks 31-35 by z. At full size LB's tops are Georgian, Lithuanian, Maltese and Welsh. Larger inputs inflate every z (+1.5 to +3.3), because the reference rows have 742 types. | **The Greek control fails at every size, up to 5x LA's.** The method cannot identify Mycenaean Greek through Linear B spelling against a classical Greek literary corpus, even with all 3,678 LB types. What separates LB from literary Ancient Greek is register, era, the proportion of names and the spelling all at once, and the free map cannot bridge it. Linear A faces the same gaps (unknown era, register and spelling) with less data. So a null result for LA is expected whether or not its language is in the panel. |
| LA-11.3 | Cycle verdict | Three cycles, 3 spelling schemes on the language side (MIXED, DROP, and DROP-on-MIXED), 2 inventories (M = 60, M = 40 bijection), raw and size-equalized models: about 15,800 anneals in total. | The contest has power only to recognize the same corpus (34/35). It has almost no power for related languages (6-9/31 vs 4.3) and none for Greek through Linear B (rank 17-33/35). On LA it shows no language or family above unrelated real words (best z +0.07 to +0.23; p 0.72-0.85 vs orphans). Not cracked. |
