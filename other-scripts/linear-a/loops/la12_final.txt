# LA-12 final: the tablets are pieces of a jigsaw
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-12.final | Three cycles (tools/la12_*.py, loops/la12_cycle1-3.txt). (1) Exhaustive exact search over 182 Hagia Triada sides: every written total vs every 1-3-block sum from other sides and own-section completions (about 1.7e8 triples per run, 2 arithmetic modes: fraction signs counted or valued). (2) Every quantity vs multi-item sums of other sides, then a simulated-annealing ledger assembly (each side donates once). (3) Attributes of joined tablets (scribe, findspot, two sides, GORILA neighbours, shared words), word roles, Khania and Zakros. Nulls: numbers shuffled across tablets within commodity (N1), iid draws (N2), each entry redrawn from its own tablet (N3, keeps magnitude); 40-200 runs per test, identical search, so search size is built in. Positive controls: real HT tablets cut into 2-3 pieces; Linear B (Pylos + Knossos) documents with planted totals then cut; planted 'summary lines'; a planted word. | Cross-tablet closures beating chance: 0 (k1, c1, c2 and the summary-line assembly are at null under N3; an N1 excess of 2-3-block sums vanishes under N3). Joins share nothing beyond chance (same scribe 0.078 vs 0.080; same findspot 0.31 vs 0.30; shared word 0.029 vs 0.032). No word gets a role (min Holm P = 1.0). Controls: cut HT tablets are NOT recovered (per-target P 0.03-1.0); planted LB totals found only when large or fraction-rich; planted summary lines are picked by the annealer (recall 5/10, 10/10, 5/10, 5/10) but lift the aggregate score only about 2 null SD; the planted word is not found. | NOT SUPPORTED, and mostly UNDECIDABLE: at HT size almost every total can be reached by chance in 10^2-10^4 ways once 2-3 tablets are combined, so arithmetic alone cannot reassemble ledgers, even real cut ones. |

Grades:
- A (established, a property of the corpus): exact cross-tablet closure is saturated at Hagia Triada. A typical KU-RO of 10-100 has 50-900 two-tablet and 7,000-60,000 three-tablet exact matches; only k1 matches of large or fraction-rich values are specific. Any claim that a KU-RO 'is' the sum of several other tablets needs non-numerical evidence (join of fragments, hand, findspot).
- A (already known, re-found blind): HT 116b KU-RO *304 15 = the *304 lines of HT 116a (12 + 3), and HT 122b PO-TO-KU-RO 97 draws on side a. These are two sides of one tablet.
- C: HT 116a GRA lines add to 109 (its own KU-RO on side b says GRA 100) = HT 1 KU-PA3-NU 109; HT 116a's GRA list opens with KU-PA-JA. Would support: GORILA/photo shows HT 116a GRA really sums to 109 and HT 1 and HT 116 share a find group or hand. Would kill: a damaged GRA numeral on HT 116a read so that the sum is 100.
- C (probably coincidence): HT 27a 'VIR+[?] 90 / 50' (140) = the sum of every number on HT 27b (140, same scribe 11). Would kill: side b's numbers belong to different commodities (VIN, SA, RE), which they appear to, so the sum is meaningless.
- Demoted: SA-RA2, MA-DI, KU-RO as 'summary-line' words (looked enriched against N1 only; at chance against N3).

Power limits (sampling rule): 182 sides are a small share of what was written; '0 of n' joins is an upper bound, not proof the archive had no split ledgers. A real test needs physical evidence (fragment joins, clay, hands) to cut the search space by 100-1,000x before arithmetic can confirm a ledger.
