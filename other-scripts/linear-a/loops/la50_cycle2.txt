LA-50 cycle 2: THE FARMING YEAR IS THE ANSWER KEY. Which goods share tablets and which avoid each other (seasonal snapshot test)
Date 5 Oct 2026. Script: tools/la50_c2.py (shared code tools/la50_common.py). Log: data/la50_ckpt/c2.log, c2.json (git-ignored).
Idea: if a tablet records one moment of the farming year, goods harvested close together should share tablets more than chance, and
goods half a year apart should avoid each other. S = weighted correlation, over pairs of known-season goods, between the co-occurrence
lift log((obs + .5) / (exp + .5)) and minus the circular month distance. Expected counts from 1,000 curveball swaps of the tablet x good
incidence matrix (row and column totals kept). Calendars as in cycle 1 (Aegean, northern Europe; sources in la50_common.CAL_SRC).

| LA-50.2a | Seasonal co-occurrence, LA (49 tablets with >= 2 of GRA, FIC (NI), VIN, OLE, OLIV). Nulls: 10^5 random calendars; all 120 permutations of the Aegean months; northern calendar. | S(Aegean) -0.006, P_random 0.51, P_perm 0.49; northern 0.076 (P 0.42). Hagia Triada alone: -0.075 (P 0.59); northern 0.28 (P 0.19). Obs/exp: every pair within 0.8-1.3x of chance except FIC-OLIV 4 / 9.2 (figs and olives avoid each other). | No seasonal clustering. |
| LA-50.2b | Linear B control, KN + PY (160 tablets), KN (100), PY (60). Same statistic and nulls. | KN+PY S 0.065 (P_random 0.31); KN 0.079 (P 0.28); PY -0.19 (P 0.85). Northern calendar does better than the Aegean (KN+PY 0.285, P 0.10). The strongest LB structure is series-based: sheep + wool together (74 / 32), crops never with flocks (GRA-LIV 6 / 34; KN 0 / 15). | Control FAILS: Linear B co-occurrence follows the bureau (flock records vs crop records), not the season. |
| LA-50.2c | Planted: LA tablet sizes and good frequencies; each tablet given a random month; goods drawn with weight base x exp(k cos(2 pi (m_good - m_tablet) / 12)); 10 archives per k; 200 swaps for the expectation. | k 0.5: S -0.09, P<0.05 in 1/10; k 1.0: S 0.18, 1/10; k 2.0: S 0.55, P median 0.06, 5/10. | Power only for a very strong season effect (k 2: a good is 7x likelier in its own month than opposite). Moderate seasonality is invisible at LA size. |
| LA-50.2 | Cycle verdict | LA co-occurrence shows no season under the Aegean calendar (P 0.51) and LB fails the same control, because LB tablets group goods by office, not month; the test sees only very strong planted seasons. | KILLED (calibrated negative, weak power). Grade C aside: figs and olives share fewer LA tablets than chance (4 vs 9.2 expected); would support: the same deficit in a larger HT sample; would kill: FIC-OLIV at or above chance once 10+ more tablets with either are added. |
