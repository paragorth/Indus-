Graded readings
- Grade A (calibrated negative, method): the Linear A fraction strings carry no stable fingerprint of one pouring vessel set. The same search recovers planted sets (2 of 3) but gives a different set on every real split, does not transfer from HT to other sites, and does no better than a lookup table of whole strings.
- Grade B: on big amounts the scribes kept only a few fraction signs. On quantities with integer >= 5, J is 0.55 of fraction tokens (0.21 on fraction-only amounts) and JE 0.14 (0.05); K appears once and L2 never in 72 tokens (< 3/72 = 0.04, against 0.09 each on fraction-only amounts); A and F about 0.01. Entropy drop P 0.0003 with integers permuted within site x commodity; read-only tablets P 0.0005. Would support: new tablets with integer >= 5 showing J/JE in >= 50 % of their fractions. Would kill: K or L2 on >= 3 of the next 30 big-integer fractions.
- Grade C (guess): this is a rounding habit, not a pouring cascade. Pouring with tolerance always cuts compounds on big amounts as well, and Linear A does not (compound share unchanged, P 0.80). J and JE behave as the coarse step; K, L2, A, F as fine steps used only when the whole amount is small. E is written early in compounds yet is not kept on big amounts (0.08 vs 0.17), so 'written first' and 'kept on big amounts' are different properties. Would support: the same narrowing in the Linear B capacity subunits on the same commodities (a check with known truth). Would kill: the narrowing vanishing once the HT 'no-logogram' lists are removed (it rests on VIN and unlabelled lists; GRA P 0.18, OLE P 0.28, CYP P 0.55).
- Grade C- (guess): the string-only vessel fits tend to call the big-amount signs 'large' (13/40 survivor maps at P < 0.05 vs 4/40 from control corpora). Would support: a stable set across splits with more data. Would kill: the same rate from random maps on a new split.
- Untestable now: the frozen ratio prediction (unit / largest vessel ~2, adjacent ratio ~2.2). Needs one graded vessel family measured in quantity: Malia Quartier Mu capacity catalogue (Poursat & Knappett 2005), Gillis 1990 conical cups, or the Cambridge Antiquity standardisation paper (doi 10.1017/s0003598x00092942; not open from here).
- Cycle verdict: not cracked. The pouring picture fails its strongest internal prediction and its outside test has no power on open data.
