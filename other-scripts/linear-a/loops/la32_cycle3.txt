# LA-32 cycle 3: massive random guessing over production hypotheses
Script: tools/la32_c3.py. Output: data/la32_ckpt/c3_report.txt, c3_res.json (git-ignored).
Each hypothesis draws a random shape metric (Dirichlet weights over 4 image metrics), a random sound matrix (a random subset of the 7 la21 blind-row runs, raw or thresholded) and a random pair filter (min length 2-4, position, locality: any / same document or hand / rare variant). Pairs are split by a hash of the word pair into discovery and held-out halves. 3,000 draws per corpus (1,451 valid on LA), the top 1 % by |z sound - z shape| re-scored on held-out (1,000-rep null).
