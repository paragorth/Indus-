LA-54 cycle 2: FILL IN THE UNWRITTEN COMMODITY ACROSS THE ARCHIVE
Date 5 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/la54_c2.py (nested random search + imputation), tools/la54_c2_report.py
(calibration, imputations, word links). Checkpoints: data/la54_ckpt/c2_*.json, imputed.tsv (git-ignored).
Two models. FULL: all feature groups. BLIND: no word features (numbers, fractions, rounding, site, support, layout),
so that any word -> commodity link read off its imputations cannot come from the word itself. Each seed: 400 random
configs; nested 5-fold out-of-fold predictions for calibration (selection redone inside each fold), then selection on
all 227 labelled documents and prediction of the 166 unlabelled ones (top 25 pooled). FULL 3 seeds, BLIND 6 seeds
(~9 x 400 x 6 searches x 4 folds = ~86,000 fits).
