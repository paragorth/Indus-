LA-33 QUEUES AT THE STOREROOM, cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/la33_common.py, la33_sims.py, la33_c1.py. Output: data/la33_ckpt/c1.json, c1.log (git-ignored).
Idea: scheduled rations to a fixed staff vs demand-driven (Poisson-like) transactions leave different queue statistics in an archive. 12 summary statistics per group of tablets (list length and its dispersion, recipient recurrence and Jaccard overlap, within-list modal share and CV, amount consistency for recurring recipients, log-amount spread, tail ratio, singletons, share of '1'). 9,000 simulated archives (scheduled staff with ration classes, turnover, rosters; gamma-Poisson demand with Pitman-Yor partners and log-normal amounts; mixtures with weight w; random loss 10-100 %) train a random forest (class) and a regressor (w = scheduled share). Controls: 3,000 held-out planted archives; Ur III (CDLI ATF, 15,639 texts; kind fixed a priori by keyword: sze-ba/siki-ba/i3-ba/sa2-du11 = scheduled, mu-kux(DU)/ba-zi/szu ba-ti = demand), subsampled to 30/60/150 texts per site and kind; within-site label shuffle; Linear B series of a priori kind (14 scheduled: personnel, flocks, assessments, KN Fp; 9 demand: PY Fr/Un/Fn/Vn, KN F/Fh/Ga, TH Fq/Gp), series-label permutation.

Scheduled groups score higher at every site (Umma 0.51 vs 0.43, Girsu 0.52 vs 0.44, Ur 0.43 vs 0.29 at T 60-150). Every LA group is below every Ur III and most LB groups. Entry shuffle lowers w (all 0.12 -> 0.06; GRA 0.32 -> 0.01) | Linear A tablets sit at the demand end of the scale (C: corpora are not on one calibrated scale); real structure beyond shuffle exists (B) |
Verdict cycle 1: the queue classifier passes planted, Ur III (rank) and Linear B (rank) controls but is weak at T ~ 30 and not calibrated across corpora. Linear A looks demand-driven, Khania most of all; grain lists are the most schedule-like group. Next: tablet-level regimes and word regimes (cycle 2), size-matched site comparison and an outside prediction (totals) in cycle 3.
