LA-50 cycle 3: THE FARMING YEAR IS THE ANSWER KEY. Placing unidentified goods in the year; massive random calendar guessing
Date 5 Oct 2026. Script: tools/la50_c3.py. Log: data/la50_ckpt/c3.log, c3.json (git-ignored).
(A) Known goods fixed at their Aegean months, year start fitted on the known goods (LA: January; LB: mid-May). An unidentified good
gets the half-month that maximises the concordance of written-order pairs involving it; 1,000 basket bootstraps give the spread.
(B) 5x10^4 random calendars over every item with >= 3 basket occurrences, scored on a random half of tablets; the top 1 % re-scored on
the other half (10 splits). Then: are the surviving calendars closer to the Aegean year (share of cyclic triples of known goods
oriented as in the Aegean calendar; random 0.50)? (C) Tablet seasons, descriptive.
