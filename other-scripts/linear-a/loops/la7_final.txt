| LA-7.final | 100 signs coded blind from rendered glyphs under random IDs and frozen before any context (31 predictive: plant 12, vessel 7, animal 3, body 9). Pre-set predictions: plant->grain/tree/spice, vessel->liquid, animal->animal, body->VIR. Tested on word + commodity pairs (adjacent n=192, window n=296), first sign vs last sign, single-sign words, word types, and logogram ligature adjuncts (189 tokens, 32 types). Null: picture-label permutation. Control: the same codes on Linear B (DAMOS, 2,457 word pairs, 217 adjuncts).
