# LA-36 cycle 2: does a word mark the direction of change?
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-36.2 | tools/la36_c2.py. Units: every pair of lists sharing >= 1 recipient word in the same commodity (LA 91 units over 49 lists, 78 with a direction; LB 198 DAMOS units; PY Ma 21 target-vs-later-line units). Direction = sign of the median log(y/x) over aligned entries. Marker words = words on one list and not the other. Statistic 1 (units): best binomial P over words seen in >= 3 units. Statistic 2 (lists): each list carrying the word votes its majority direction; binomial over >= 3 lists. Nulls: unit-level direction flips; list-level random effects (each list a random level, direction = sign of the difference; keeps 'this list is just small'). Controls: PY Ma (o-u-di-do-si / o-pe-ro / a-pu-do-si lines are below the target), PY Es dossier, all LB, planted markers (a word forced to the smaller side). | PY Ma: o-da-a2 0/11, ka-ke-we 0/10, o-u-di-do-si 0/10, o-pe-ro 0/5 lists on the larger side; best-word P 0.002 under the list null, 3 words at P <= 0.05 vs 0.05 expected. LA, unit flips: 14 words at P <= 0.05 vs 1.6 (P 0.004; QA-*310-I 1/15, KI-KI-RA-JA, KI-RE-TA2, RE-DI-SE 0/9). Under the list null this falls to 14 vs 14.0 (P 0.53): each such word sits on one list that is simply small. List statistic: LA best P 0.95 (TA-I and TE 3/3 larger, KU-PA3-NU 1/5, KI-RO 1/4, KU-RO mixed over 12 lists). LB all: best P 0.18 (unit level P 0.0005, list level 0.07; do-so-mo 0/11 units). Grade A (method): unit-level direction tests are fooled by list-level amount scale (LA P 0.004 -> 0.53; LB 0.0005 -> 0.07); a list-level null is required. Grade B (calibrated, low power): no Linear A word marks the direction of change; KU-RO (12 lists) does not, and a word that always marked the smaller list on >= 6 lists would have been seen. Words on 3-5 lists (KI-RO 1/4 larger, KU-PA3-NU 1/5) are below the power limit. Grade C: KI-RO and KU-PA3-NU on the smaller side (would support: 3 more pairs with these words on the smaller list; would kill: 2 on the larger). |
