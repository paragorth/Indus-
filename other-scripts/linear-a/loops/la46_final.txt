| LA-46.final | Novelty: la14 ran a document-level forger contest; LA-46 inverts it at item level: which specific relation (sign pair across a word edge, word -> number scale, word -> logogram, logogram -> scale, entry -> next entry, two words on one tablet, site x word, number -> number, word = running total, first/last word) do real held-out tablets show more often than every forger? Forgers: 200-300 random architectures per corpus (back-off n-grams of order 1-4 over randomly abstracted contexts, optional site, line index, copied layouts, tablet clusters up to K 32), 5-fold CV, 2-3 forgeries per held-out document per architecture (~93,000-160,000 forged documents per corpus; ~3 M in all). Rules: strict (beat the strongest of 16 forger groups), ensemble mean, weakest group. Held-out test: residues found on one half scored on the other half against fresh forgers that saw everything else (6 splits). Controls: two forger-written worlds and a within-kind shuffle (negative); planted casts and word->scale constraints, and planted ordered constraints (positive); Linear B KN+PY and Ur III at LA size, words as opaque ids, known structure used only to score. | Strict rule: powerless at LA size (0/12 and 0/6 planted, 0 in LB), only KU-RO = total survives in LA. Liberal rule + world calibration (cycle 1): co-occurring casts in LA 0.50 vs 0.20 (P 7e-5) and LB 0.80/0.41, but this was KILLED in cycle 3 (sides of one tablet counted twice; topic forgers fake casts in LB too). Held-out (cycle 2): residues found on half A fire on unseen half-B tablets at 3.0x the forgers in LA, 2.3-2.4x in LB, 1.8x in Ur III, 4.8x planted, 0.2-1.4x in forger worlds. Final calibrated rule (cycle 3, ensemble mean, p < 1e-3): worlds 0, shuffle 0, planted ordered constraints 3/6 + 3/6 with 0 false passes, LB 1 + 1 (ko-wa -> ko-wo, the girls-then-boys order of personnel censuses). LA: 3 residues: KU-RO + exact running sum; SA-RA2 -> NI; NI -> VIN (one basket: SA-RA2, then GRA or CYP, then NI, then VIN, sometimes OLE, *23M, on 11 HT tablets). Adjacent pairings (commodity-unit, word-number scale, sign pairs, site choices) are never residues anywhere: a forger copies any adjacent statistic. Ur III residues are the dating frame (month + year on one tablet, the year formula last). | NOT CRACKED. A (known, la1/la14): KU-RO totals are the one place every forger fails. B: the only other constraint a surface forger cannot fake is an ordered commodity basket headed by SA-RA2 (GRA/CYP, NI, VIN); it predicts held-out tablets (27 real vs 3.8 forged instances) and matches la20's commodity order (would support: a new HT tablet with SA-RA2 in this order; would kill: SA-RA2 with another order on 3+ tablets). B (method): item-level inversion works only with an ensemble-mean rule and only for ordered long-range constraints; it is blind to adjacent pairings and, once forgers can cluster tablets, to co-occurrence (also in LB). Killed: LA 'casts' (cycle 1). C: SA-RA2 names the basket or its recipient class (untestable here). |
