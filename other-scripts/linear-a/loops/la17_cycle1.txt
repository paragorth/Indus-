# LA-17 cycle 1: "words spread like epidemics" - SI(R) outbreak on the site network, adoption order unknown
Scripts: tools/la17_common.py (data, simulator wrapper, summaries), tools/la17_core.c (per-word outbreak in C), tools/la17_sim.py (checkpointed prior simulations), tools/la17_abc.py (rejection ABC + ABC random forests), tools/la17_c1.py, tools/la17_lb.py. Outputs: data/la17/c1_si_la.txt/.json, lb_si.txt/.json, sims_*.npz.
Fitted data: ONLY the site x word incidence (13 sites with >= 10 word tokens: HT ZA KH KN PH PK IO AR SY TY PE MA TH; 862 types, 917 site-word cells), word tokens per site (effort) and great-circle distances. Dates are NOT inputs; deposit phase per site (median of the find-context field: PH MM II=1, MA 2.25, KN/SY/TH LM IA=3, IO/PK/TY LM I=3.5, HT/KH/ZA/PE/AR LM IB=4) is used only to score.
Model: each word is a lineage born at a literate site (rate ~ effort^alpha), spreading to literate sites at rate beta * contact (exp(-d/L) * size^g), founder copying (share phi of a parent's lexicon when a site starts writing), loss gamma (SIR), Zipf-like popularity and effort-dependent detection calibrated to the observed cell count. 20 parameters (source, 12 adoption times, 7 globals). 160,000 simulated outbreaks for LA (40,000 for LB); 121 summaries (richness, pairwise shared types, occupancy, share-shared).
