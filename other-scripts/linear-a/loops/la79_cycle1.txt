Candidates (each: massive random guessing, a planted or shuffled control that can kill it; would a conventional decipherer do it? no):
 1. COUNTERFACTUAL EXCAVATION HISTORIES. Pretend the island was dug in a different order: every one of the 62 ways to split six site groups (HT, KH, ZA, PH, KN, other) into 'dug first' and 'found later', plus the 3 real publication cuts. Re-score every computable earlier claim (affixes I- SI- A- KI- -ME, -JA -RE -TE; aggregate prefixing/suffixing; consecutive entries share first sign; consonant-repeat avoidance; Linear B shared vocabulary; commodity order; fraction writing order; fraction-by-commodity) on the 'later finds' only, each against its own decoy family (all other signs; 300 random sign hashes x positions; 500 permuted consonant maps; 2,000 random orders; 100 shuffled commodity labels). Shuffled-history control: 40 random-document splits of matched sizes. Planted controls: a universal prefix and an HT-only prefix; a universal and an HT-only commodity order. Output: a grade change list. CHOSEN for cycle 1.
 2. FUTURE-ONLY FITNESS / SURVIVAL LAW. Generate ~200,000 random sign-level predicates scored ONLY on what was found later (never on the past) and ask which properties of a hypothesis (past fit, sign frequency, site spread, complexity, family) predict survival into a second, later future. Controls: shuffled dates, planted drift worlds. Cycle 2.
 3. REVERSE OCCAM. Pick hypotheses by WORST or median past fit and test whether underfitting readings predict later finds better than the best-fitting ones; control: planted stable grammar must still prefer the best fit. (Folded into 2 as a survival-law covariate.)
 4. BACKCASTING. Treat the Hagia Triada archive (the 1945 corpus) as the unknown future: learn from everything found later and predict HT; asymmetry forward vs backward over random hypotheses shows which archive is the 'normal' Linear A; control: same with random site pairings. Cycle 3 (with 5).
 5. FORECAST LOTTERY FOR RILA-S1 AND POST-2020 FINDS. 100,000 random forecasts of observable properties of a new document (sign-frequency band shares, word-length mix, number/word ratio, fraction share); keep those that won at every past cut, freeze their consensus with sha256. Control: survivors under shuffled dates. Cycle 3.
 6. THE DIGGING ORDER AS ORACLE. Does how surprising a find was (surprisal under all earlier finds) depend on which excavation and year, beyond site? Forecast the surprisal of the next finds by site. Control: years shuffled within site. Reserve.

Tools: tools/la79_common.py, la79_claims.py, la79_c1.py; checkpoints data/la79_ckpt/c1_real.json, c1_planted.json.

Success rule per split (scored on the 'found later' side only): z >= 2 and the real key above 90 % of its decoys (affixes, consecutive entries, consonants); order / fraction-commodity: real above 95 % of decoys. 'site' = 62 site-subset histories; LOSO = the 6 histories where one group is found last; 'time' = 1950/1976/1988 cuts; 'rand' = 40 random-document splits.
