Method. The frozen la60 PRIOR reading is the scaffold (commodity class, KU-RO / KI-RO, heading signs). Every recurring word outside it (155 in Linear A) gets a typed hypothesis: one of 7 types (person, place, office, transaction, commodity sub-type, qualifier, measure), defined only by fixed a-priori slot / number-size / first-line / dispersion templates that are the same for every corpus, plus a commodity affinity and a site affinity (with an MDL cost). Each full assignment is scored by held-out decoding: the log-likelihood of the slot, the attached number's size, first line or not, the commodity in scope, the site and the dispersion on documents it was not fitted on. Search: fully random assignments plus annealed hill-climbing from random restarts, about 3.0 million hypotheses scored in all (1.74 M in the typing runs, 1.14 M in the arithmetic search, 0.13 M in the glossary fits). Calibration: Linear B KN+PY and Ur III, reduced to opaque sign identities at Linear A size, with a matching scaffold, scored against known person / place / transaction / office / unit labels, and word-shuffled copies of every corpus.
