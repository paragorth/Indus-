la34 cycle 2: massive random guessing of "what a hand is" (tools/la34_c2.py). 60 per-feature distance layers, all scale-free: 20 sign-agnostic ductus layers, 20 sign-matched scalar layers + 8 sign-matched grid-PCA layers, 12 box-geometry layers; squared distance is linear in the layer weights, so 3,000 random hypotheses (exponential weights, random sparsity 10-90 %) per run cost little. 92 labelled units, 25 hands.
