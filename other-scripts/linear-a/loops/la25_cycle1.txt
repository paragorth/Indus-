# LA-25 cycle 1: THE FIRE SET THE CLOCK. Free-base-rate ABC destruction-month clock.
# Tools: tools/la25_common.py (data, simulator, ABC), tools/la25_c1.py, tools/la25_c2b.py (LB planted lines)
# Logs: data/la25_ckpt/c1.log, c2b.log (git-ignored)
# Data: logogram NAMES only (GRA*, VIN*, OLE*, OLIV*, rest = OTHER; LB: GRA/HORD/FAR, VIN, OLE, OLIV, + FIC, LIV, LANA in the extended set).
# Unit: document x commodity (each commodity counted once per document; entries on one tablet are not independent).
# LA LM IB archives (doc-level GRA VIN OLE OLIV OTHER): HT 51 27 36 12 81 | KH 9 6 8 0 86 | ZA 8 7 1 4 2 | TY 0 0 2 1 1.
# LB: KN 76 17 101 18 1743 | PY 253 16 48 9 343 | TH 29 23 0 10 76 | MY 6 3 2 2 37.
# Generator: per commodity a random agronomic entry month (GRA May-Jul, VIN Aug-Oct, OLIV Oct-Jan, OLE Nov-Feb, FIC Aug-Sep,
#   LIV Mar-May, LANA Apr-Jun), decay time 0.5-8 months (log-uniform), floor 0.02-0.6; seasonal strength gamma U(0,2);
#   terroir noise sigma U(0,1) per archive x commodity; free base rates beta ~ N(0,1.5); one shared month (p 0.5) or
#   independent months. Summary: centred log-ratios per archive; rejection ABC, nearest 500 of 10^6.
# Argued LB seasons used only as control truth: Pylos and Knossos destruction in spring (Feb-May window, prior mass 0.333).
