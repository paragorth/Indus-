LA-48 THE PHYSICS OF GOODS: final (5 Oct 2026)
Idea: ignore meaning. Treat the archive as a physical system in which goods are conserved. Words are opaque nodes (site, word, commodity); each document is a set of flows between its holder (header words) and its counterparties (entry heads), with a label IN / OUT / STOCK. Search the labels (about 2.3e8 annealing proposals in all, plus 20,000 random labellings per dataset) for exact Kirchhoff balances. Amounts are exact vectors: integer plus the count of each fraction sign, with no fraction values assumed. Then read off hubs and directions, and predict held-out amounts. A web search and LITERATURE / FINDINGS / STRATEGIES found no prior conservation-network test on Linear A.
Nulls: amounts shuffled across documents within site x commodity; Poisson amounts; random labels. Controls: planted conserved economies (100 / 50 / 30 / 20 % of documents surviving; exact forwarding and aggregating versions), Linear B KN and PY (DAMOS), Ur III Puzrish-Dagan livestock accounts (CDLI) at Linear A size and at 2,000 documents.
Tools: tools/la48_common.py, la48_c1.py, la48_c2.py, la48_c3.py, la48_c3b.py, la48_report.py. Checkpoints: data/la48_ckpt/ (git-ignored).

Findings
- A (method): exact balance search recovers a planted conserved economy when most of the archive survives: 178 balance points vs 18 +- 6 shuffled, and 76-79 % of balanced nodes are the true pass-through officials. Genuine sums (a + b = c) are found only in a near-complete archive (47 vs 3.5). Detection fades by 30-50 % survival and is gone at 20 %.
- A (method): Kirchhoff balance fixes only RELATIVE direction inside a chain of linked documents; flipping a whole chain keeps every balance. Even in the complete planted economy, IN vs OUT |phi| = 0.00-0.17. Who gives and who receives cannot come from conservation alone; it needs a broken symmetry, such as a known store or a stock sign.
- B (control fails): in Ur III Puzrish-Dagan, a real central ledger of conserved animals, there is no balance beyond chance (P 0.17-1.0) and no direction (|phi| <= 0.12). Its throughput hubs are function words and titles (ugula, i3-dab5, ensi2, mu, iti, kiszib3, zi-ga), not the known central officials. In Linear B the hubs are delivering districts (KN) or function and total words (PY pa-ro, to-sa). The method therefore cannot name stores, palaces or households in any real archive at these sizes.
- B (negative): Linear A shows no conservation beyond chance: 16 vs 10.1 +- 4.4 (P 0.14), genuine sums 2 vs 1.35 (P 0.48). Its 'hubs' are the largest numbers (HT PU-KO, TI-SA, *815-PA-TA-QE) and the total word KU-RO, the same artefact as in the controls. No flow network, hub, leaf or direction is established.
- B (negative; power limited, new): Linear A amounts are not node-specific. A word's amount on one document matches its amount on another no more often than a random amount of the same site and commodity: 62 of 302 vs 62.9 shuffled (-1 %). The full corpora are higher: Linear B +18 % (above every shuffle), Ur III +4-7 %, and planted archives with >= 50 % survival. At Linear A size, Linear B gives +3 to +47 % (6 draws, all above Linear A, but only 3 of 6 at the shuffle floor).
- C: Linear A entry words are not standing accounts (people or stores that keep a quota from record to record). Would support: the same deficit on new Linear A entries. Would kill: an excess >= 20 % with P < 0.05.
- C: PE Wy 5 (tablet) and PE Zg 5 (stone object) both carry *307 OLE 1 + J: one transfer recorded twice. Would support: another Petras document with *307 and the same amount. Would kill: a re-reading of either amount. Other Linear A balances are 1 = 1 pairs or the two sides of one tablet (HT 86a/b, ZA 12a/b).
- Killed: the two out-of-sample 'sum' predictions (HT 3 KU-PA3-NU 3, KH 88 NI 10), which occur at the shuffled rate; the 3+-term balances at HT (*305 across 6 tablets, DI/VIR 2 + 2 = 4), at the shuffled rate.
Not cracked. Next, a stranger idea: break the symmetry on purpose. Treat Linear A totals and fraction-only receipts (la28) as the stock sign that orients the chains, or drop conservation and look for the opposite physics: amounts that are deliberately never repeated, as on single-use vouchers.
