# LA-22 final: predict what the fire destroyed
Scripts: tools/la22_data.py (lineara.xyz re-parse keeping the break mark), la22_lb.py (DAMOS in the same format), la22_engine.py (sign experts), la22_c1.py (sign calibration), la22_c2.py (numbers, logograms), la22_freeze.py (frozen predictions), la22_sigla.py (SigLA parser), la22_sigla_test.py and la22_doubt.py (outside test). Logs: loops/la22_cycle1-3.txt. Outputs: data/la22/ (la22_predictions.json + .sha256, la22_sigla_test.json, la22_doubt.json). Checkpoints: data/la22_ckpt/ (git-ignored; includes the SigLA pages, licence CC BY-NC-SA, not committed).
Literature check: neural restoration of Greek inscriptions (Pythia/Ithaca) and Linear B infilling (ML4AL 2024) exist; no Linear A restorer, and no test of restorations against a later independent re-reading, was found. Tablet-level accounting constraints and the edition-difference test are new here.

| la22:LA-22.final | Arrow in the dark: restore Linear A's lost signs, numbers and commodity signs with random ensembles (4,000 random predictors per fold for signs, 1,000 for numbers and logograms) over 15 sign experts (frequency, position, Markov, lexicon with edit model, tablet words/signs, site, scribe, support, accounting slot, 2 neural MLPs x 2 seeds), 7 number experts (frequency, round, tablet scale, repetition, KU-RO arithmetic, word->number, commodity->magnitude) and 6 logogram experts. Calibrated by masking known items at the real damage pattern (break at a word's left or right end, from the edition's own break mark; 2,100 breaks on 512 documents). Controls: frequency and Markov baselines, Linear B LA-sized positive control, shuffled-tablet null. Grades:
- B: Linear A sign restoration is calibrated and rests on word-internal regularities; tablet context adds nothing (shuffled = real), unlike Linear B where repeated words on a tablet help (-0.04 top-1 when removed).
- B: the la14 accounting skeleton is a working restoration constraint for numbers (tablet scale, repetition, totals) and commodity signs (one commodity per tablet); shuffling tablets removes the gain.
- B (negative): frozen restorations do not anticipate SigLA's sign re-readings; they stay anchored to the corpus's own spellings.
- C: break-edge restorations can anticipate extra signs (HT 3 MA-[DI], p 0.20, found by the lexicon). Would support: >= 5 of the next 10 frozen high-confidence edges (p >= 0.3) confirmed by a new photograph or join. Would kill: 0 of 10.
- C: low restoration probability marks doubtful readings (AUC 0.65), mostly via sign rarity. Would support: model / frequency AUC > 0.6 on a second independent re-reading. Would kill: <= 0.5.
- Frozen for the future (not new methods, just bookkeeping): missing KU-RO entries HT 11a 4, HT 27a 13, HT 39 72, HT 46a 42, HT 100 4, HT 109 121, HT 122a 9; 974 edge predictions with probabilities.
