LA-73 cycle 1: SIMULATE THE WRITERS IN TIME (7 Oct 2026). Corpus data/corpus_ra.json (rd: read + damaged; bridged/erased words out), word tokens of >= 2 signs on tablets.
Prior-work check: the brief's idea (ABC over random Minoan administrations: persons, commodities, tablets written, survival) is largely done: la15 (ABC vocabulary size, 80,000 Zipf worlds), la23 (ABC person counts, 110,000 archives), la58 (agent-based administrations thinned to each site's surviving count, 220,000 worlds). PIVOT, said here: keep the simulated writers but give them TIME. Persons have careers (mean length L = 1), tablets are written over an unknown window W with a retention skew (old tablets discarded), and the question is the archive's clock: is the Hagia Triada archive a few months of paperwork or generations of it? (log R = log10 W/L; R_eff = span holding the middle 80 % of surviving tablets.) Web search (archive time span from name co-occurrence, ABC, Linear A): nothing found. Not done in la1-la72.
Simulator (tools/la73_common.py): persons arrive at rate Np per career, Exp(1) careers, lognormal popularity; a Zipf term vocabulary (share phi of tokens); K offices with locality; within-tablet repeats; retention skew kappa; mentions per career m (gives the implied survival fraction and tablets ever written). Each world writes a corpus with the TARGET'S OWN per-tablet word counts. 15 statistics; 10 train (type/token, repeats, f1, f2, linked tablets, giant component, transitivity, Laplacian lambda3/lambda2, Fiedler-order contiguity of shared words, recurring word pairs), 5 held out (f3+, max df, path length, Gini, degree). Rejection ABC (0.5 %) with local-linear adjustment. Scripts tools/la73_prep.py, la73_abc.py, la73_c1.py; output data/la73_ckpt/c1.json (git-ignored).
