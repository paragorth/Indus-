Cycle verdict: three independent controls (LB full, LB at LA size, Ur III) do not sort function words, goods and
names into the urban-scaling classes, and scaling-based predictions (contents, original size) fail held-out in
the controls. Archive vocabularies do not grow like cities in any administration tested.
