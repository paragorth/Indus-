# LA-22 cycle 2: restore masked numbers and commodity signs (accounting vs lexical constraints), 4 Oct 2026
Numbers: every integer 1-2,000 hidden in turn (1,303 in LA), candidates 1-2,000. Experts (tools/la22_c2.py), LOO document: GLOB (corpus frequency + 1/v tail), ROUND, SCALE (log-normal around the tablet's other numbers), REP (values repeated on the tablet), TOT (KU-RO / PO-TO-KU-RO arithmetic, with slips of 1 and 10), WNUM (values after the same word elsewhere), LNUM (magnitude after the same commodity). Logograms: each hidden (777 in LA); experts GLOB, DOCL (other logograms on the tablet), PREVW (preceding word), FRAC (fraction letters of the following number), MAG (size of the following number), SITE. Ensemble: 1,000 random predictors per document half (product or mixture), best 25 averaged, scored on the other half. Controls: shuffled-tablet LA (numbers, logograms and words permuted within site) as null; Linear B LA-sized sample as positive control.
