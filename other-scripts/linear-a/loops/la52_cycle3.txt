LA-52 cycle 3: held-out halves. Does a word's anchoring in one half of the tablets predict it in the other half?
Date 5 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/la52_run.py (halves LAA/LAB, LBA/LBB, URA/URB), la52_c3.py. Report: data/la52_ckpt/c3_report.txt (git-ignored).
Set-up: each corpus split once into two disjoint random halves of documents (LA 127/128; LB and Ur III samples at LA size, split likewise). Per half: n-gram, template and topic chains, 120 each, plus 120 shuffled-start chains each (gentle regime, 4 generations; 4,320 chains in all). Best-learner retention per feature; per word, frequency-matched z of pooled long-range retention. Tests: (1) feature-level Spearman between halves against a within-family shuffle (does anything beyond the relation family replicate?); (2) word-level Spearman of z between halves against 5,000 label permutations.
