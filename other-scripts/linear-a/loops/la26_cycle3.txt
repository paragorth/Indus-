| LA-26.3a | All 32,766 non-trivial site graphs on HT KN ZA KH GO TH scored on Linear A only (no seal data) on a random half of the documents. Score = mean distance-residual excess similarity on the graph's edges minus off-edges, summed over 4 measures (words, signs, ligature variants, entry structure), each standardized by 40 doc-perms within support. The top 1% vote an edge consensus with as many edges as the ring graph (6). It is re-tested on the held-out half and compared with the real seal graph (Jaccard), against 2,000 random graphs of the same density. 12 splits per arm. Arms: real; planted 4 or 8 new shared words per ring edge; document-label-shuffled corpus. | Held-out replication of the consensus: real +7.27 (12/12 splits positive), shuffled +0.04 (5/12). Consensus vs seal graph: Jaccard real 0.29, plant4 0.35, plant8 0.40, shuffled 0.26; P <= 0.05 in 0/12 splits in every arm. Edge votes (real): KH-GO 0.93, GO-TH 0.95, HT-GO 0.87, HT-ZA 0.84, KN-GO 0.83 ... HT-KH 0.004, ZA-KH 0.14. Real seal graph on held-out halves: T +3.28 (12/12 positive) vs shuffled +0.36 (9/12); planted 3.87-3.97. Rank of the seal graph among all graphs on half A: median top 19% (shuffled 53%). | Linear A has a real, replicable site-similarity structure, but it is not the ring graph. Its strongest feature is the same as in cycle 2: Khania stands apart from HT and Zakros, and pairs involving the tiny sites (GO, Thera) score high because their z-scores sit near zero. The positive control fails: 8 planted words per edge do not bring the blind consensus to the seal graph (0/12). Blind recovery of a ring network from Linear A text is impossible at this size. |
