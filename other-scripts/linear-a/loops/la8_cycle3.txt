| LA-8.3a | v3: each corpus is restarted from its forced K>=8 population, now with no constraint (1,500 generations, same for all). This tests whether the cycle-2 optima were local traps. | LA returns to exactly the cycle-2 optimum (30,971.9, K=4: the same 4 classes as 2 of the 3 v2 seeds). LB improves to 36,362 with K=7: open words; open logograms; a closed class of NUMBERED logograms (OVIS:m, VIR, *154, SA, CAP:f; 27 members, P(next NUM) 0.93); 2 closed classes of non-numbered formula words (to-so-de, o-na-to, ko-to-na, ka-ke-we / pe-mo, ke-ke-me-na, a-ta-ra-si-jo); NUM; FRAC. | LA-8.3b | Recursion arm. Over each corpus's best classes, evolve slots (1-3 states per class) with or without a one-counter stack (push or pop on entering a state, depth <= 4, END needs an empty stack). Equal compute: 2 seeds x 300 generations x 16 offspring per arm. Planted: PREC with its true classes; PREC with its evolved classes; PFLAT as the false-positive control. | Stack gain (fsa minus pda fitness, bits): PREC true classes 737 (REL pushes, a V slot pops: the planted centre-embedding is found); PREC evolved classes 0 (DET and REL were merged into other classes, so the bracket cannot be seen); PFLAT 243 (FALSE POSITIVE: the counter is used as extra memory in a flat form); LA 63 (NUM pushes, a word slot pops: a counter, not an embedding); LB, LB2, PE, LAS 0. | The stack test cannot separate recursion from bounded memory: the flat planted form gains more (243) than Linear A (63). Linear A shows NO evidence of recursion above the flat-form level. Recursion is only detectable here when the closed function classes are already right, which no real corpus supplied. |
