| LA-9.final | Three cycles (tools/la9_*.py). (1) Exact likelihood of each written KU-RO total under 9 error mechanisms (exact, skip line, double line, dropped written carry, counting-board slip +-10^k, tally +-j, rounding or ignoring fractions, misread fraction sign, background for lost lines or a wrong section cut), with a Gibbs sampler over hidden letter values (49-value grid) and the mechanism weights. Working readings (no new value grades; FINDINGS and LA-1 grades stand):
- J = 1/2: stays C. The error fit leans to 1/2 (ABC 2.65x prior, Gibbs mode), but the same lean appears with shuffled totals, so it is a property of the entries, not of the mistakes. Would kill: a photo-confirmed J+J (PH 9b, PH 22a).
- E, JE, A, F, H, L2: no information from errors (posterior at or near the prior).
- Calculating method, grade C: 'unit-stroke slips'. Would support: Ur III or Old Babylonian totals with broken numerals (damage-matched) still miss by 1-9 units far less often than LA/LB. Would kill: damage-matched Ur III shows the same 25-40% small-miss rate.
- Rejected: written column addition with dropped carries (1 clear case, HT 118, -20); counting-board decade slips (1/30, the same rate as Ur III 12/871 within noise).
- Prediction to check: if the small misses are lost strokes, they should cluster in entries or totals that GORILA marks as worn. HT 9a (+2), HT 11a (+4), HT 100 (+4), HT 122a (+9), HT 104 (+1), HT 119 (+1), HT 94a (-1) are the cases to check on photographs.
