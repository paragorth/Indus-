LA-54 final: READ WHAT WAS NOT WRITTEN (5 Oct 2026)
Scripts: tools/la54_common.py, la54_engine.py, la54_c1.py, la54_c1_report.py, la54_c2.py, la54_c2_report.py, la54_c3.py.
Rows: loops/la54_cycle1.txt, la54_cycle2.txt (with the full imputation table for 166 documents), la54_cycle3.txt.
Checkpoints: data/la54_ckpt/ (git-ignored). About 20,000 random classifiers (4 families x random feature groups x random
hyper-parameters) were scored, ~250,000 fits in all.

Idea: 166 Linear A documents write numbers but no commodity sign. Train on the 227 documents that show their commodity,
with every commodity sign deleted, and infer the unwritten commodity from what remains (number size, fraction signs,
rounding, words, site, support, layout). Then fill in the archive and look for new word-commodity links.

Grades
- A: none.
- B (negative): reading the unwritten commodity mostly returns the site. Khania -> CYP, Zakros and Arkhalkhori -> GRA,
  Phaistos and Malia -> 'other'. Linear B controls show 4x more within-document information (0.6 vs 0.16 bits/doc).
- B (method): at LA size the search finds a hidden commodity carried by a word, not one carried by number scale
  (even x5). Number sizes vary too much within each commodity.
- B (negative): no new word-commodity links (12/259 at p < 0.05 vs 13 expected; min q 0.86).
- C: Linear A documents carry ~0.16 bits/doc about their commodity beyond the site (P 1/9 vs within-site shuffles).
  Would support: P < 0.01 with 100 within-site shuffles. Would kill: P > 0.2.
- C: HT95b (KU-NI-SU, DA-ME, A-DU) is a grain document (word-blind model: GRA 0.55; the two words occur only with GRA
  on labelled tablets). Would support: a GRA sign on HT95b in a join or re-reading. Would kill: a non-grain sign there.
  HT10a (KU-NI-SU) is called 'other', so this holds for 1 of 2 documents.
- Wine, oil, people and olives cannot be recovered once their sign is deleted (recall 0.00-0.10). For these classes
  the imputation says nothing.

Limits: P values are bounded by the number of null runs (6-10), because the CPU was shared (load ~30 on 4 cores).
'OTH' merges every commodity class other than GRA, CYP, OLE, OLIV, VIN and VIR. Single-sign words such as NI were
kept as words.
