Institutional words: none. The detector's LA list (*301, ZE, RO, KA, *411-VS, KU, *409-VS, *408-VS, *164, *322) is the sealing-device sign set and is reproduced on shuffled sites.
Grades:
- A (method): paperwork at Linear A size separates 'shared institutions vs none' (99 % for empty worlds; capital + offices 85 %) but cannot place centres or links.
- B (calibrated negative): no centre, link or title can be read, because the controls fail at the same task.
- B: Linear A sites behave as separate administrations, more strongly than site shuffles and at least as separate as Linear B kingdoms. Held-out sites are unpredictable.
- B (descriptive): the Hagia Triada rooms form one vocabulary block (2.5x shuffle, above a planted capital with offices). Cross-site sharing is below random.
- C: Neopalatial administration was organised site by site, not as one island-wide palace system. Would support: a new LM IB archive overlapping its own site's deposits at least 2x more than other sites. Would kill: a new site sharing words with HT at the HT-room level (>= 0.08).
Limits: Ur III and the deposit panel lie outside the simulated cloud. Locality of names alone also produces site islands, so the C reading rests on the absence of any shared office words, not on their presence elsewhere. Not cracked.
