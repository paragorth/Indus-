| LA-20.final | Every pair of items in one list = a contest won by the item written first. BT, PL, Elo, mean position, Bayesian BT (MCMC), mixtures of K=1-4 orders, fitted to all LA lists (352 documents); held-out pair-order accuracy on unseen tablets. Controls: within-list shuffles (200-500; family-wise max over 26 configurations and over K); Linear B Pylos town order and LB lists subsampled to LA size (positive); planted hierarchies and planted 2-order mixtures in LA-sized data; amount-driven null. | Words 0.509, entry words 0.521, first signs 0.524 (FWER >= 0.49), while LA-sized Pylos words give 0.614 (10/10 draws). Logograms 0.715 (FWER 0.005); this beats the amount-driven null (0.548 +- 0.048, P 0.002), is transitive (cyclic triads 0.038 vs 0.098, P 0.035) and is robust (sides split 0.726, HT only 0.689, commodity signs only 0.697; all P 0.002).
