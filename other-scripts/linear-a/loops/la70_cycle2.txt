LA-70 cycle 2: decode held-out documents with the frozen v2 reading; controls, la60 v1, ablations and Linear B calibration (6 Oct 2026)
Scripts: tools/la70_c2.py (LA and LB), la70_c2b.py (ablations). Checkpoints: data/la70_ckpt/c2_LA.json, c2_LB.json, c2b_ablation.json.
Decoder: la60's checks (cover, role steps seen in training, every KU-RO section with >= 2 entries closes, no commodity pair against the order, substantive) plus v2 rules (*308 amount has a fraction; a KI amount is not above the entry before it; KI amounts kept out of the running sum; D = 1/5 and B = 1/3 valued in closures). FULL = all checks; STRICT = FULL and at least one closing total, order-agreeing pair or passed rule.
