LA-33 QUEUES AT THE STOREROOM, cycle 2 (4 Oct 2026). Script: tools/la33_c2.py. Output: data/la33_ckpt/c2.json, c2.log (git-ignored).
Method: a per-tablet regime classifier. 9 features of a tablet inside its archive (length, modal share, CV, share '1', recurrence of its words, best Jaccard with another tablet, amount consistency of its words elsewhere, amount and length relative to the archive). Trained on 1,500 mixed simulated archives (tablet regimes known). Controls: planted mixed archives; Ur III site pools of 40 ration + 80 delivery texts (10 draws per site; entry-shuffled pools); Linear A entry shuffle (20 x); word cohesion = mean within-word variance of tablet scores vs site-stratified permutation of scores (2,000 x). Ur III place/god test: share of {ki} and {d} recipients by known kind.
