la66 final: EVERY LINEAR A WORD HAS A WEIGHT (6 Oct 2026). Port of proto-elamite pe52 (random log-linear models with document effects) and pe58 (factors, ratio lattice, ladders) to Linear A. Cycles loops/la66_cycle1-3.txt; scripts tools/la66_lib.py, la66_c1.py, la66_c2.py, la66_c2b.py, la66_c3.py, la66_c3b.py, la66_sum1.py, la66_sum2.py, la66_sum2b.py, la66_freeze.py; checkpoints data/la66_ckpt/ (git-ignored, 6.5 MB); frozen table data/la66_frozen_factors.json (sha256 3127699d217b345c8c1f4d15a6c124caab4a8a32bf4c5f641fcc1069b475ff36).
Idea: if Linear A entry words are descriptions (quality, grade, age, sex, unit size, ration class), each word and each sign shifts the log quantity of its entry by a fixed amount, and the shifts sit on simple ratios. About 1.0 million random ridge models (word / sign / ligature-part features, multiplicative or additive, document effect as offset or covariate) were fitted on half the documents, screened on a quarter and re-scored on a reserved quarter against a word-identity baseline; then a direct within-document factor estimator, a lattice test and ladder controls. Nulls: quantities shuffled within commodity and within document; word bundles re-dealt within documents; signs shuffled across the words of an entry. Controls: planted weights on the LA skeleton; Linear B (DAMOS) and Ur III herds and rations (opaque) at LA size. No Linear B value is an input.
