Decoder: each held-out document -> roles, gloss and checks. cover = every word gets a lexicon role or
is an unknown word in an entry slot; roles = every role step seen in training; totals = every TOT with
>= 2 entries closes (fraction slack); order = no commodity pair against the frozen order (margin 0.3);
substantive = at least one total, commodity entry or ordered pair. FULL = all five. STRICT = FULL and
at least one closing total or one order-agreeing pair. Controls through the identical pipeline (acceptor
relearned each time): roles shuffled among the reading's types + order scores shuffled (100 per split),
random readings of the same class sizes over training vocabulary (50), the EMPTY reading. 10 more
site-stratified splits besides the primary one. Calibration: Linear B KN+PY at Linear A size (matched
document-length mix) with INDUCED (same rules) and LBPRIOR (true partial LB reading, same kinds).

Cycle 2 verdict: frozen on training documents, the reading decodes 22-26 % of held-out administrative
documents in full (EMPTY 21 %), and beats shuffled readings by ~5 STRICT documents per 191 (2.7 %),
carried by KU-RO closures (5 per half) and the commodity order. A true partial Linear B reading of the
same kind gains the same 2.9 % at the same size. Grade B for the role layer; nothing here reads a word.
