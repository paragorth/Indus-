# LA-11 cycle 1: blind compression contest, scheme MIXED, K=40 LA signs -> M=60 syllables (tools/la11_run.py c1 MIXED 40 60; report data/la11/report_c1.txt)
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-11.1a | Data. 39 languages from 18 families (UD treebanks, eBible.org, CDLI Sumerian ATF; data/la11/sources.json records URLs and licences). Raw texts were romanized, collapsed to 5 vowels and cut into open syllables. MIXED scheme: s or a sonorant before a consonant is dropped, other cluster consonants are written with the next vowel, final consonants are dropped. Only these are kept: syllable unigram and bigram counts over the word types of a training half, plus 1,500 held-out types recoded to opaque integers. Raw downloads were deleted. 35 languages have at least 1,000 training types and serve as targets; Hittite (143), Telugu, Amharic and Yoruba serve as inputs only. | Training types range from 1,207 (Akkadian) to 16,974 (Korean). Syllable inventories range from 72 to 174. | Corpus built. Hittite UD is too small (1,274 tokens) to be a target. |
| LA-11.1b | Contest. Every input has 742 types, matched in length to the LA admin types. For each input and target, simulated annealing (C core, 6 restarts x 50k moves, then a greedy polish) finds the best injective map from the top 40 input symbols to the target's top 60 syllables, under the target's discounted syllable bigram model with word boundaries. Gap = bits/symbol of shuffled input minus bits/symbol of real input; shuffling permutes symbol tokens across types (8 shuffles for LA, 4 for LB, 2 for language inputs). z = gap standardized against the gaps of held-out real words from unrelated families scored under the same target. Inputs: LA; LB with identities hidden (3 length-matched subsamples); 3 random Markov strings; held-out words of all 38 languages. 5,145 anneals. | Power on a language's own words: self-identification top-1 in 34/35, top-3 in 35/35. With the language itself excluded, the best target falls in the input's own family 7/31 times (chance 4.3). **Linear B control fails:** Ancient Greek ranks 23, 21 and 32 of 35 by z (by raw gap, without standardization: 9, 5 and 8); Modern Greek ranks 34, 32 and 35 by z. LB's best targets are Sanskrit, Italian and Tamil (max z +0.6 to +1.0). LA: every z is negative. Top: Arabic -0.10, Coptic -0.12, Nahuatl -0.24, Sanskrit -0.28. Bottom: Finnish, Hungarian, Mandarin (-1.2 to -1.4). 32 of 38 real inputs, scored against unrelated families only, reach a higher maximum z (p = 0.85). Raw gap: LA 0.206 bits/symbol, LB 0.29-0.32, Markov 0.05-0.09. Convergence: restart spread 0.024 bits/symbol; the gap's s.d. over shuffles is 0.006. | **No language absorbs Linear A better than unrelated real words do.** The family-mean permutation (Afro-Asiatic p = 0.005) is driven by having 6 targets of mean -0.49, all below zero, so it carries no positive meaning. The method has power only when the input shares the model's orthography and corpus. It cannot recover Greek through Linear B spelling at LA size. A blind map-free contest therefore cannot name LA's language. LA's sequential structure sits between Markov noise and real words (gap 70% of LB's), which matches LA-5. |
