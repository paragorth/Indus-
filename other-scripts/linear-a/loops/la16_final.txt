| la16:LA-16.final | Private spellings as a Rosetta stone: if one hand writes X-B-C where another writes Y-B-C, X and Y are interchangeable. (1) Every one-sign-different word pair weighted by the share of its token pairs written by different hands; sign-pair statistic vs 2,000 hand permutations (max-z FWER); annealed sign-equivalence partitions (correlation clustering) vs anneals on hand-shuffled z; document bootstrap (LA 300); context-sharing filter. (2) Hand inference from spelling conflicts vs shared words, leave-one-out and 20 % held out; unattributed docs assigned. (3) 10,000 random 5-equation hypotheses per split, top 1 % re-tested on held-out documents (new word pairs only), 60 LA / 20 LB splits. Controls: Linear B KN+PY with DAMOS hands (known variant doublets A/A3, O/WO, RA/RA3 ...), LB cut to LA size, planted private spellings in HT Scribe 9 / PY hand 1 (rate 1.0, 0.5), hands shuffled. | LB positive control FAILS at every stage: known variant pairs share a hand as often as unrelated pairs (18.8 % vs 13.9 %); top cross-hand sign pairs contain 0 doublets and sameV at base rate (P 0.93); annealed classes at null (P 0.50-0.90); spelling conflicts do not predict different hands (0.123 vs null 0.124); held-out gain -0.02 (P 0.82). Planted private spellings: 0/16 recovered (sign search), 0/3 detected (hand inference). LA: no sign pair beats FWER with hands (best JA~KU 0.93); site groups KU~WA FWER 0.017 on two 2-sign pairs only; anneals BELOW null (P 0.97-1.00); conflicts carry no hand information (P 0.61); held-out re-test of random equation survivors -0.04 vs blind selection (P 0.89).
