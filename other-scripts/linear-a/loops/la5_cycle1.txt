# LA-5 cycle 1: code-vs-language form battery on deduplicated administrative word types (tools/la5_c1.py -> data/la5_c1.txt, data/la5_c1.json)
# Arrow: assume LA admin sign-groups are codes. Populations (types of >= 2 signs): LA admin (742; HT 429, non-HT 336), LB admin DAMOS (3,678; KN, PY), LB personnel names (1,643), Proto-Elamite middles (3,130), HTS tariff codes, ICD-10 codes, and planted codes on the LA and LB vocabularies: randID (each type -> iid string, same length, same sign unigram) and slot (each sign drawn from its position-class distribution: a designed slot code).
# Every population drawn to m = 371 types (half of LA) without replacement, 40 draws: the half-sample spread equals the full-sample bootstrap spread, so LA bands are 95% CIs. Nulls: G = global sign shuffle, P = shuffle within position class (first/second/middle/penult/last), M2 = order-2 Markov regeneration at the same lengths.
