la34 "The hand in the strokes", cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026). Data: SigLA (Salgarella & Castellan, CC BY-NC-SA) document drawings, 779 documents, fetched once each (throttled) into the scratchpad; 5,105 occurrences cut out with SigLA's own boxes (identical to SigLA's per-occurrence PNGs up to anti-aliasing). Shape features at <= 96 px (22 scalars: aspect, skeleton length/ends/junctions, centroid, principal axis, eccentricity, skew, shear, 8-bin stroke-direction histogram; 2 pen-width/density features marked scale-dependent; 16x16 ink grid). Held-out labels: lineara.xyz 'scribe' field (published palaeographic attributions), never used in clustering. Tablet sides merged into one unit. All scoring within site; null = hand labels permuted among same-site units. Literature: Castellan, Cohen-Addad, Giffard-Roisin & Salgarella, "Writers Behind Words" (IWCP 2021 abstract) announce supervised/unsupervised ML on SigLA for hands, without published scores; la34 differs by a sign-agnostic ductus arm, a box-geometry arm, a random-guess search and the downstream use tests (cycle 3).
| LA-34.1a | Arm R (box geometry only, no shapes): 12 scale-free features from SigLA's boxes (sign-specific size and aspect residuals, gaps inside and between words, line pitch, baseline slope and jitter, logogram/fraction size ratio, signs per line, occupancy). Units with >= 8 syllabograms; 1,000 within-site label permutations. | 54 units / 16 hands: same-hand AUC 0.627 (null 0.497, P 0.001), ARI 0.201 (null 0.117, P 0.01), nearest-neighbour hand 0.26 (null 0.25, P 0.45). At >= 3 syllabograms (109 units / 29 hands): AUC 0.459 vs 0.404 (P 0.016), ARI 0.17 vs 0.14 (P 0.02). Best single features: word gap (AUC 0.60), line pitch (0.58), baseline jitter (0.62). | Weak but real layout signal; carries tablet-format habits as well as hand. |
| LA-34.1b | Arm D (sign-agnostic ductus): each feature z-scored within its sign code (codes with >= 6 drawings), averaged over a unit; Euclidean. Full and scale-free (pen-width features dropped). | Scale-free, >= 8 drawings (49 units / 14 hands): AUC 0.638 (null 0.534, P 0.004), NN 0.43 (0.28, P 0.012), ARI 0.305 (0.166, P 0.002). >= 3 drawings (92 / 25): NN 0.34 (0.20, P 0.002), ARI 0.17 (0.16, P 0.18). | Hand signal without comparing like with like: a writer's slant/proportion habits survive averaging over different signs. |
| LA-34.1c | Arm M (sign-matched form): for every sign code two units share, distance between the units' mean [z-scalars + within-code PCA of the 16x16 grid]; mean over shared codes. | Scale-free >= 3 (92 / 25): AUC 0.626 (null 0.483, P 0.002), NN 0.37 (0.19, P 0.002), ARI 0.278 (0.212, P 0.006). >= 8 (49 / 14): NN 0.49 (0.28), ARI 0.31 (0.16), both P 0.002. Scale-dependent features add nothing (full: ARI 0.27 / 0.36). | Best arm. Blind recovery of published hands well above chance, comparable to la16's text-based leave-one-out (0.37 vs 0.16). |
| LA-34.1d | Real positive control: the two sides of one tablet (same writer by construction) kept as separate documents, 231 documents, 46 side pairs; is the other side nearer than other same-site documents? | D: AUC 0.640 (null 0.478, P 0.005); M: AUC 0.652 (null 0.455, P 0.005); ARI 0.10 (0.05, P 0.035). | Passes: the pipeline sees "same writer" where it is known for certain. Caveat: both sides were also drawn on one sheet. |
| LA-34.1e | Planted control: fake hands (real partition permuted within site, so real hands are destroyed), each fake hand's drawings sheared and x-stretched by a random amount up to s before extraction; 3 reps per s. | s 0.05 (~3 deg, 5 %): AUC 0.44-0.49 (null 0.45), not recovered. s 0.10: AUC 0.49-0.54 (D P 0.005-0.10). s 0.20 (~11 deg, 22 %): D AUC 0.60-0.71, NN 0.36-0.44, ARI 0.21-0.35 (P 0.005); M AUC 0.58-0.64, NN 0.28-0.34. | Detection threshold ~ +/-10 deg slant plus 20 % stretch. Real hands score like the strongest planted hands in M, so real hand differences are at least that large (more than affine: allograph choices). |
| LA-34.1 | Cycle verdict | Blind shape features recover published Linear A hands within site (M arm: NN 0.37-0.49 vs 0.19-0.28; ARI 0.28-0.31 vs 0.16-0.21; P <= 0.006), the known-same-writer side control passes, and planted hands are recovered only when large. | Grade B: hands are partly recoverable blind from SigLA drawings. Limits: attributions are themselves palaeographic (partial circularity); SigLA drawings are modern tracings, so a draughtsman or drawing-campaign effect that follows find-groups cannot be excluded; no Linear B per-occurrence drawings were found openly (LiBER gives whole-tablet photographs only), so no LB positive control. |
