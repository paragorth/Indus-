# LA-40 cycle 3: do role assignments hold on held-out tablets? (tools/la40_c3.py; data/la40_ckpt/c3.json, c3half.json, c3_parts/)
# Tablets split at random into two disjoint halves (6 splits). The pre-registered system (cycle 1) and the 3 best learned
# systems (cycle 2, by Pylos score) are run on each half (4 chains x 3e5 updates). Agreement = share of word types seen in both
# halves with the same modal role, minus the agreement expected from the halves' role marginals ('excess'); also the
# label-free adjusted Rand index (ARI) between the two halves' role partitions. Null: words shuffled inside each half.
# Control: Pylos documents, halves of Linear A size (PY 2x) and size-matched to the Linear A halves (PY 1x). Rerun after a
# container restart; each split is checkpointed.

| LA-40.3 | Cycle verdict | Role partitions inferred from structure are reproducible across disjoint halves in Linear B (ARI ~0.3 at matched size) and much less in Linear A (ARI ~0.12, about 0.4x; excess over marginals about 1/3 of Pylos), while shuffled words give ~0. So LA word types do carry some position/role identity, but much weaker than LB's, as la8's flat two-open-class grammar implies; none of it can be named, because the naming step fails its LB control (cycles 1-2). | Grade B (calibrated, relative): weak role consistency. |
