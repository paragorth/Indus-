la66 cycle 1: EVERY LINEAR A WORD HAS A WEIGHT, calibration (6 Oct 2026). Scripts tools/la66_lib.py, la66_c1.py, la66_sum1.py; checkpoints data/la66_ckpt/c1_*.json (git-ignored).
Corpus: 1,499 Linear A administrative entries (386 documents; la60 loader, libation supports dropped, KU-RO/KI-RO/PO-TO-KU-RO lines dropped). Entry features: every word (W:), every sign of a multi-sign word (S:), every ligature part of the commodity logogram (G:). Target: log integer part (fraction-only = 0.5), centred by stratum = commodity x fraction use; 'tab' mode subtracts the leave-one-out document mean (document effect), 'sys' mode adds it as a covariate. Engine (pe52 port): per split, 2,000 random ridge models (random feature kinds W/S/G, min frequency, 30-100 % feature keep, ridge 0.3-100, hard sparsity, multiplicative (log) or additive (relative raw) scale, document covariate on/off) fitted on half the documents, top 10 % by gain over the WORD-IDENTITY baseline on a quarter, refitted and scored on the reserved quarter; 10 splits = 20,000 models per run, 32 runs (640,000 models). DESCRIPTOR = effect present in >= 75 % of splits, same sign in >= 90 %, |median| >= 0.15 log, and removing it hurts novel held-out strings in >= 75 % of >= 3 ablations (pe52 rule, frozen before LA was scored). No Linear B value is an input.
