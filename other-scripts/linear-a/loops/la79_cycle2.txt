la79 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): the survival law. 200,000 random sign-group role hypotheses (7 families: final, initial, second sign, first x last, last x length, any-sign membership, whole word; K 2-5) predicting what follows a sign-group (la78 'prior' scoring). Properties knowable in 1950 (documents published by 1950): in-sample fit (ins), leave-one-site-out (loso), leave-one-findspot-out (lofo), site entanglement (mutual information of the classes with site; label-free), class balance, K; plus the first future W1 (fit <= 1950, score 1951-76). Outcome: survival into the later futures (gain on W2 <= 1976 -> after AND W3 <= 1988 -> after). Controls: 5 shuffled-date runs (20,000 each); 16 planted worlds (8 stable, 8 drifting after 1960; truth = random final/initial K3 partition, beta 1.0, real per-site label mix; 5,000 decoys each). Tools: tools/la79_c2.py, la79_c2_report.py; data/la79_ckpt/c2_*.npy, c2_report.json.
Web/literature check: no 'which hypothesis properties predict survival on later finds' study for any undeciphered script; la78 only scored rank correlations.

| LA-79.2.1 | Survival rate: real vs shuffled dates vs planted decoys. | Real 19.2 % of 200,000 survive both later futures (two seeds 19.2 / 19.2 %); shuffled dates 50-69 %; planted decoys: stable worlds 21-42 % (mean 27 %), drifting worlds 15-28 % (mean 20 %). | The real survival rate sits with the drifting planted worlds, below every stable one but one: weak sign that Linear A sign-group roles change between the 1945 archive and later finds (C; would support: next 50+ new clean groups give survival <= 20 % again; would kill: >= 30 %). |
| LA-79.2.2 | The law in real data: Spearman of each 1950-knowable property with the later-future gain, pooled and within family; survival by decile. | Pooled: ins -0.15, K -0.26, ent -0.05, loso +0.05, W1 -0.06. Within family: ins -0.20 to -0.45 (all 7), ent -0.20 to -0.33 (final, initial, second, word), loso +0.05 to +0.12 (sign families), W1 +0.06 to +0.12. Top-ins decile survives 5-13 % vs bottom decile 24-44 % (sign families). Top 0.1 % by past fit sit at the 3rd-26th percentile of W1 and 15th-35th of later gain. Same in both seeds to 3 decimals. | Reverse Occam holds in real data: the worse a random reading fits the 1950 corpus, the more likely it survives on later finds; readings whose classes encode site survive less (B as a description). |
| LA-79.2.3 | Shuffled-date control (5 x 20,000). | Signs flip: ins +0.04 to +0.43, ent +0.02 to +0.38, W1 -0.06 to +0.23, loso -0.20 to +0.01; ins~W1 +0.20 to +0.41. | Under random-document splits the opposite law holds (fit wins), so inside-corpus testing would teach the wrong rule here (A, method; agrees with la78). |
| LA-79.2.4 | Planted worlds: does reverse Occam need a grammar that changes? Rank of the truth and the decoy-pool law in stable vs drifting worlds. | Stable worlds reproduce the law among decoys: ins~W1 -0.18 to -0.68 (real -0.66), ins~later -0.17 (mean), ent~later -0.21, loso~W1 +0.12 to +0.34 (real +0.23). Truth: top on in-sample fit in 16/16, W1 percentile 1.0 in 8/8 stable vs 0.00-0.01 in drift; survives 8/8 stable, 1/8 drift. The top past-fitting DECOYS fall low on W1 in stable worlds too (0.00-0.97). | KEY: the la78 reversal (best past fit predicts the future worst) is produced by the real site mix in a world whose grammar is perfectly stable, as long as the truth is not in the pool. It shows that in-sample fit picks site-mix artefacts, not that Linear A changed (B). LA-78.2.3 ('real behaves like stronger drift than planted', B) -> C: with the site mix planted, real lies inside the stable range on rho(ins, W1) and rho(loso, W1); only the survival rate (2.1) leans to drift. | LA-79.2.5 | Practical rule for grading (what to require of any future Linear A reading). | From 2.2-2.4: require (i) leave-one-site-out gain > 0, (ii) low site entanglement (below the pool median), (iii) W1 time-travel percentile >= 0.95; do NOT reward in-sample or random-CV fit. In stable planted worlds the truth passes all three in 6/8 (2 fail loso > 0) and its entanglement is at the 24th-57th percentile; in drift worlds 0/8 pass (iii). | Adopted as the la79 grading filter (B, method). |
