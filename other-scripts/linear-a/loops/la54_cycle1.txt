LA-54 cycle 1: READ WHAT WAS NOT WRITTEN (calibration of masked-commodity inference)
Date 5 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/la54_common.py (corpora, features, nulls, plants), la54_engine.py (random classifiers),
la54_c1.py (jobs), la54_c1_report.py. Checkpoints: data/la54_ckpt/c1_*.json (git-ignored).
Data: corpus.json; 393 documents with numbers, 227 show a commodity logogram (label = most written commodity class:
OTH 68, GRA 46, CYP 38, OLE 31, VIN 21, VIR 18, OLIV 5), 166 have numbers but no commodity sign.
All logograms are deleted before features are built. Features (7 groups): number size (12), fraction signs (16),
rounding (5), words (84; opaque ids, top 80 types), site (9), support (6), layout (4). Each random config draws a
random subset of groups, keeps 40-100 % of their columns, and one of 4 classifier families (ridge-softmax, kNN,
Gaussian NB, nearest centroid) with random hyper-parameters. Per run: 300 random configs, each scored by 4-fold CV
on the training 70 %; the top 25 are pooled and judged on the 30 % held out (3 stratified holdouts per run).
37 runs x 300 configs = ~11,000 random classifiers in cycle 1, each fitted 4 x 3 times (~135,000 fits).
