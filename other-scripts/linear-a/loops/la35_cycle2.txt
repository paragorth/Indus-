| LA-35.2a | Massive held-out rule search (tools/la35_c2.py). Train score = sum of within-stem-family (CMH) z for '1'; top 50 rules kept; held-out scores: M1 Spearman(train z, test z) over endings, M2 mean test score of the top 50 rules, M3 family-centred AUC of '1' from the train ending effect. Stems = first 2 signs (final-sign endings), first sign, and first 2 signs with the whole remainder as ending.
