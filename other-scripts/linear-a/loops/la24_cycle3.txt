# LA-24 cycle 3: SHARE-VECTOR REUSE (same recipients, same shares, different totals)
Idea: if a clerk apportioned by fixed shares, the same recipient words get proportional amounts on different tablets: a2(w)/a1(w) equal for every shared word and != 1. Equal amounts (ratio 1) are 'fixed allotments' (la6.4d) and are counted apart.
Tools: tools/la24_c3.py, la24_c3b.py; checkpoints data/la24_ckpt/c3.json, c3b.json.

| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-24.3a | Document sides; entries = recipient word (2+ signs, once per side) with one amount of one commodity. Every pair of sides sharing >= 2 words (same commodity): k = largest group of shared words whose ratios agree within 2 % (r != 1). Statistics: pairs with k >= 2, k >= 3, summed k; eq2 = pairs with >= 2 equal amounts. Nulls (2,000): P1 amounts permuted within each side (keeps the side's values, breaks word-amount links); P2 band replacement keeping repeats. Positive controls: Linear B (438 sides, 1,836 pairs), PY Es (Es 644-729, known proportional contributions), 15 planted LA pairs (second side = round(k x first)). | Controls: PY Es k2 27 vs P1 9.1 (P 0.0005), all LB k2 1,633 vs 1,543 (P 0.004); planted LA k2 11 vs P1 3.2 and P2 8.3 (P 0.0005, 0.0045), k3 7 vs 0.16. P2 kills even PY Es (28.7, P 1.0), because sides whose amounts are all equal are proportional under any value swap; P1 is the usable null. Linear A: 15 pairs only; k2 4 vs P1 2.5 (P 0.13), k3 2 vs 0.65 (P 0.11), summed k 10 vs 5.6 (P 0.044); P2 P 0.87. eq2 3 vs P1 1.9 (P 0.15). | Weak and not robust: P 0.04-0.13 against within-side permutation, nothing against band replacement. The method works (Linear B and planted pass), but Linear A has only 15 testable pairs. Grade C overall. |
