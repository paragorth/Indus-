LA-56 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026). Minimal explanations and blame: which entries does every non-closing total need changed?
Tool: tools/la56_c2.py (enrichment recomputed in tools/la56_report.py with the permutation restricted to items that can be blamed). Checkpoints: data/la56_ckpt/c2_*.json, c2_report.txt.
Method: for each total that does not close (25 LA, 96 Ur III), enumerate every reading with 1, then 2, then 3 changes: a main entry dropped, subtracted or doubled; an earlier total, after-total entry or other-side total added. Fraction values: 400 random draws; weight = share of draws under which the reading closes. Posterior blame per entry = its share of the minimal readings. Feature blame = sum over sections, against 1,000-2,000 within-section permutations of feature sets (main with main, extras with extras), family-wise max over features per operation.
