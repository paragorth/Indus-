| LA-57.1a | EVERY BUREAUCRACY ON EARTH VOTES, corpora (tools/la57_common.py, la57_build.py). Seven known record-keeping systems were reduced to one value-free form: opaque token ids, numbers (value, fraction flag), line or cord-group breaks. The systems are PC proto-cuneiform (Uruk IV-III, 3,827 docs), UR3 Ur III admin (5,000), OB Old Babylonian admin (4,081), EB Ebla admin (1,283), OA Old Assyrian admin (290; all four from the CDLI dump), LB Knossos+Pylos (DAMOS, 3,838) and KH Inca khipus (Open Khipu Repository: 605 khipus, cords = colour token + knot value, cord groups = lines). Each known system gets 4 draws at Linear A size (~5,200 tokens). 42 features per word occurrence: 24 local (position, line edge, adjacency to a number, magnitude, fraction, number = sum of others / max / rank, document and line size) and 18 type-level aggregates, rank-normalised within each draw. Role truth (controls only) comes from conventional meanings: COM, TOT, PER, PLA, TRA, HDR (office/header/date), UNI. For KH, TOT = top cords (a physical attachment class, not arithmetic; 98 cords on 12 khipus). Egyptian hieratic: no open structured transcription of accounts was found, so it was not used. Neo-Babylonian: only 18 transliterated admin texts in the dump, so it was dropped. | Eligible roles (>= 30 pos/neg per system): COM 6 systems (PC UR3 OB EB OA LB), TOT 5 (UR3 OB OA LB KH), PER 5, PLA 5 (UR3 OB EB OA LB), TRA 5 (PC UR3 OB EB LB), HDR 5 and UNI 5, both cuneiform-only (no outside system carries them in this reduction). Planted administration (la45 generator) is test-only. | Setup. UNI and HDR cannot test cross-civilisation transfer (all their systems are cuneiform). |
| LA-57.1b | Massive random guessing (tools/la57_c1.py): 20,000 random sparse linear scorers per role (1-5 features, Gaussian weights, no training) = 140,000 hypotheses, scored by occurrence AUC on every system. Leave-one-system-out (LOSO): the 200 scorers with the best minimum AUC on the other systems form an ensemble, which is scored on the held-out system. Leave-one-civilisation-out (LOCO): cuneiform / Aegean / Andes. NULL: role labels permuted within each training corpus (type level; khipu occurrence level), 30 replicates of the whole selection, scored on the TRUE held-out labels (P floor 0.032). | LOSO ensemble AUC (null median, P): TOT UR3 0.98 (0.34, .03), OB 0.91 (.03), OA 0.96 (.03), LB 0.22 (0.42, P .81), KH 0.20 (0.54, P 1.0).
