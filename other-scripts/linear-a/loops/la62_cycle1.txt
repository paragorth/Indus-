(tools/la62_c1.py; data/la62_ckpt/inventory.json, git-ignored. Licensed PDFs and photos stay in the scratchpad.)

Snapshot = data/LinearAInscriptions.js (lineara.xyz) -> corpus.json, 1,721 entries (faces counted separately), 66 tagged
post-1985 by image source. On 6 Oct 2026 the live lineara.xyz file and the GitHub raw file are byte-identical to the
snapshot, so lineara.xyz has no newer data.

INVENTORY (open sources only; 'in' = already in the snapshot)
  N1 KN Zg 58, ivory HANDLE, Knossos Anetaki cult centre (Room 1 Ivory Repository), dug 2011-17, published 2024
     (Ariadne Suppl. 5: 27-43, open PDF; news 2025). ACCOUNTING text: face a two VAS+syllabogram, a 'ten', a new sign,
     *180, *181; face b a 4-sign group + HIDE; face c boar + pig, no numerals; face d two VAS+syllabogram with units and
     SIX different fraction signs in sequence. Open media: one photo of the whole object (Fig. 7; signs not legible at
     the published resolution) and the prose. No drawing, no transcription. Snapshot: record exists, EMPTY.
  N2 KN Zg 57 faces B-D (+ most of A), ivory RING, same deposit, ~119 signs, no numerals. A: 12 animals, ~10 vases
     (6 amphorae, rhyton, tripod; VAS+PA, VAS+RU), 16-18 signs; B: six sign-groups, then GRA, FAR?, OLIV; C: >= 9 groups,
     5 TELA (one TELA+KA); D: ~9 HIDE (one HIDE+KA/QE/KO). The groups are not transcribed in the open article; it says
     few of them have parallels in known Linear A. Snapshot: KNZg57a 7 tokens (*401+RU x2, *418+L2, *652, *653, NI,
     VAS), KNZg57b empty.
  N3 RILA-S1 (Del Freo & Zurbach 2024, Etudes Cretoises 21.6): 107 documents, 427 signs, everything published since
     1985 up to 2023. Not open. Our snapshot holds at least 66 post-1985 items; how many of the 107 are missing is
     unknown. Needs a human (library), see la62_final.
  N4 Akrotiri loomweight, 3 signs (Notti 2023, IULM, restricted): probably = THEZg15 (in).
  N5 KH 104, 105, KH Wc 2124 (Kadmos 2018), KH 97-99, KH Wc 2123, KH Zb 1 (PDIA V 2007, open): in.
  N6 INA Zb 1 (INSTAP 2022), KY Za 2 DA-MA-TE, LACH Za 1, ZO 1, TEL Zb 1, MIL Zb 1-4: in.

Cycle 1 verdict: the open outside corpus is almost empty. One new accounting text (KN Zg 58) and the
non-numeric faces of KN Zg 57, both from a cult deposit at Knossos and known from prose only. The real outside test
set is RILA-S1 plus the forthcoming Anetaki II volume (Kanta, ed.), both need a human.
