# LA-19 final: the palace spoke many tongues
Scripts: tools/la19_core.c, la19_common.py, la19_c1.py, la19_c1_report.py, la19_c2.py, la19_c3.py, la19_c4.py. Outputs: data/la19/. Logs: loops/la19_cycle1-4.txt. Checkpoints: data/la19_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
| la19:LA-19.final | Arrow in the dark: Linear A words come from several languages, as in a trading port. Mixtures of K = 1-8 sign-level bigram phonotactic models (initial / final sign preferences, length via EOS; sign identities only) fitted to word types by collapsed Gibbs, about 31,000 runs over 65 corpora; K by 5-fold held-out likelihood (2-SE rule) and restart stability. Then (2) components vs site, support, commodity, number size, position, hand; (3) HT -> non-HT transfer and tablet homogeneity; (4) a minority tongue as outliers (per-sign surprisal under K=1). Controls: K=1-resampled LA (K=1 in 3/3), same generator twice (K=1 in 3/3), 10 planted 2-language mixtures in a shared syllable space (K >= 2 in 8/10; stable ARI 0.45-1.0; outlier test z 10-19 in 5/5 at 80/20), LB personnel names (K 5; attributes fire in about half the fits; outlier test KN z 2.6, non-Greek label z 6.8), Ur III and OB names, 36 single real languages. | LA best K = 4, gain 0.079 nats/word, stability ARI 0.17-0.18. But 33/36 single languages also choose K >= 2 (median gain 0.114); LA ranks 21/36 on gain and 25/36 on stability: LA is no more mixed than one language. Components track nothing outside the model (0 of 24 tests after Holm; none inside HT), do not transfer from HT to other sites (held-out gain negative), and cluster on tablets only by site (HT-only z 0.8-1.4). Outliers: Phaistos-only words (z 3.3) and roundel words (z 4.2) look foreign, but both effects vanish when words with rare or unnamed signs are removed (z 1.4, -0.5). | MANY TONGUES NOT SUPPORTED (B, calibrated): Linear A word types look phonotactically like ONE lexicon, no more heterogeneous than a single real language at the same size. Limit: a minority tongue < ~20% with phonotactics close to the majority (like sux+akk or jpn+tur at 80/20) would not be detected by the component test; the outlier test would see it (planted z >= 9.8), and sees none. Method lesson (A): 'K >= 2 by held-out likelihood' is the default for any real word list; mixture-K is not evidence of multilingualism without a single-language calibration. |
