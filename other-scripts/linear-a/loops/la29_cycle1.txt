# la29 cycle 1: THE LAND WROTE THE LEDGER (CONTEXT idea #6, terroir). Tools: tools/la29_land.py, la29_common.py, la29_c1.py. Derived per-site land values: data/la29_land.json. Rasters (AWS Terrain Tiles z11, WorldClim 2.1 2.5', CLC 2018 via EEA REST, SoilGrids 2.0) stay in the scratchpad. Checkpoints: data/la29_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
| LA-29.1a | Land data: 27 sites (20 Cretan LA, Thera/Kea/Melos, LB PY/TH/MY/TI). Per site at 2/5/10 km: land fraction, distance to coast, mean/sd elevation, slope, arable (slope<5, <600 m), upland (slope>15 or >800 m), olive (<500 m, slope<25), vine (50-700 m, slope 2-20), >1000 m share; WorldClim rain, summer-dry share, mean and coldest-month temperature; CLC 2018 shares (olive groves, vineyards, arable, pasture, shrub, mixed agriculture, orchard, bare) on a 1-km lattice within 5 km (81 points); SoilGrids clay/sand/SOC/pH at the site (masked points moved 1-3 km). Spatial structure: e-folding length of 5-km catchment means over central Crete 12-14 km. | 53 variables; 51 vary over the 10 Cretan commodity sites; M_eff (eigenvalue participation) 4. Examples: HT olive_5 1.00, cl_olive 0.49; Syme upland_5 0.77, rain 796 mm; Psykhro upland 1.00, rain 921 mm; Zakros cl_pasture 0.55; Khania cl_orchard 0.18. | Data step (A as data; values are modern proxies of ancient land, not ancient land use). |
| LA-29.1b | Commodity mix: 10 Cretan sites with >= 4 commodity documents (HT 231, KH 125, ZA 23, PH 18, KN 12, MA 12, AR 7, PE 6, PK 4, TY 4 document x category counts); categories GRA VIN OLE OLIV CYP LIV(CAP/HIDE) VIR *301 *304 *401 OTHER. LOSO multinomial (one land variable, ridge, equal site weight), skill = mean held-out per-entry log-likelihood gain over the pooled mix. Null: 10^4 Gaussian random fields at the site coordinates (correlation lengths 10-40 km); best-variable statistic vs max of M_eff fields; size alone (log documents); 200 shuffled site labels. | Best vine_5 +0.031 (P single field 0.049, P max-of-M_eff 0.18), vine_10 +0.030, elev_2 +0.021; 1/51 variables beat the 95 % single-field null (expected 2.6). Size alone -0.019 (P 0.41). Shuffled site labels give a HIGHER best-variable skill (median +0.059, 95 % +0.107) than the real arrangement (rank P 0.86). | NOT SUPPORTED: no land variable predicts a held-out site's commodity mix beyond random smooth fields, size, or shuffled labels. |
| LA-29.1c | Planted controls at the real document counts: OLE up and GRA down by beta per SD of a land variable (20 reps each). Modern-ledger control: CLC land-cover shares at the 10 sites used as 'commodities', drawn at LA counts and at 10x, predicted from DEM/climate/soil only (10 reps, per-rep 1,000-field null). Linear B weak control: KN, PY, TH, MY (4 archives, document-level GRA VIN OLE OLIV FIC LIV LANA OTHER). | Planted beta 0.5: detected 0-3/20; beta 1.0: 9-19/20 (right variable 12-19/20); beta 2.0: 20/20. Modern ledger: 5/10 at LA counts (skill +0.110), 10/10 at 10x. LB: best cl_orchard_5 +0.003; 2/51 variables beat the field null (chance). | Power: a land effect of about x2.7 per SD on two major commodities would have been seen; a real land-driven mix (modern land cover) is found half the time at LA size. LB control fails (4 archives cannot carry it). Caveat: shuffled labels beat the Gaussian field null, so the Gaussian null is lenient for skewed land variables; this only strengthens the negative. |
