# LA-14 cycle 1: THE FORGERY CONTEST (forge Linear A instead of modelling it)
Scripts: tools/la14_common.py (corpora, forgers, features, discriminators), tools/la14_run.py (plan c1), tools/la14_report.py.
Output: data/la14/c1.jsonl (one row per pairing), c1_report.txt, c1_auc.json, forg/ (cached forgeries), world_FW_*.json.
Web check (4 Oct 2026): no real-vs-forged discriminator study of Linear A (or any undeciphered admin script) was found;
LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md and STRATEGIES.md have nothing like it (la8 evolved a grammar, la5/la11 scored models, none forged).
Data: LA = 292 tablets, lames and bars with >= 4 content tokens (words, logograms, integers, fraction letters, line breaks, dividers).
LB = 292 size-matched DAMOS documents. PLA = real LA with a planted long-range agreement (first sign of the first word fixes the
first sign of the LAST word through a hidden random key over the 40 commonest signs). FW_X = 292 documents written by forger X
trained on all of LA (the negative control: a corpus that IS the forger).
Protocol: each seed splits a corpus in half; forgers train on half A, discriminators see half B (real) vs forgeries, 5-fold
stratified GROUP CV (identical forgeries and copies of one source stay in one fold), AUC. Forgers: UNI (sign unigram), MK1-MK4
(sign-stream Markov, interpolated backoff), WMK1-2 (word-level Markov), FLAT (la8 flat form re-implemented: order-2 kind chain,
per-kind emissions, numbers conditioned on previous kind, novel words by sign bigrams), NEUR (feed-forward neural LM, 8-symbol
window, 96 tanh units), and copy-edit forgers that copy a half-A tablet and make ONE edit: COPY0 (none), E_num (a number replaced
by a corpus number), E_numnear (number scaled x e^+-0.7), E_word / E_first / E_last (a word replaced by a corpus word), E_logo,
E_swapadj (two adjacent tokens), E_swapline (two lines), E_splice (two tablets joined at a token cut), E_numshuf / E_wordshuf
(numbers / words permuted inside the tablet). Real half B is restricted to tablets the edit could apply to.
Feature groups (11): len, uni (token unigrams, numbers bucketed), sign, sbig (sign bigrams, initial/final signs), tbig (token and
kind bigrams/trigrams), pos (first/last tokens, line-initial words by zone), arith (totals: last = sum of the rest, any number =
sum of a run, max share, distinct numbers, roundness, descending order), numw (word -> number-size couplings), long (head sign x
later signs/logograms, head>tail pairs, logogram agreement), rep (within-tablet repetition, line-template regularity), ngram
(learned: sign-stream 1-3-gram counts). 1,080 tasks x 12 discriminators (11 single-group LR + all-groups LR) = 12,960 pairings.

Cycle verdict: the contest works (null 0.5, planted feature found, LB caught where expected), but its raw scores are dominated by a
finite-sample artefact that only the negative control reveals. After calibration Linear A keeps four kinds of structure that no
forger (Markov 1-4, word Markov, flat grammar, neural LM) reproduces: tablet-level repetition/line regularity, arithmetic, head-body
agreement and position. Cycle 2: thousands of random pairings (feature subsets x 5 learner types) plus two new copy forgers
(E_splLine: splice at a LINE boundary, so no local seam; E_wordsite: replacement word drawn from the same site) to separate
'tablet coherence' from 'site vocabulary'.
