Report: the farming year is not the answer key. The order of goods is fixed, but it is a staple ranking (grain, olive, fig, wine), shared with
Linear B, not a harvest sequence. The calendar test has a hard ceiling (5 identified goods give at most 24 cyclic orders), the Linear B control
fails because Linear B's own order is administrative, and the planted farming year is recovered blind, so the negative is calibrated.
