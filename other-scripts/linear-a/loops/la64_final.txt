la64 final: MINOAN NUMBERS MUST OBEY PHYSIOLOGY (6 Oct 2026)
The Proto-Elamite pe55 approach ported to Linear A. Cycles: loops/la64_cycle1.txt (calibration),
la64_cycle2.txt (per-head instrument and nulls), la64_cycle3.txt (outside object, subunit check).
Scripts: tools/la64_lib.py, la64_c1.py, la64_c2.py, la64_c3.py, la64_c3b.py. Checkpoints: data/la64_ckpt/
(git-ignored).
Idea: every written quantity times an unknown unit must be a physically possible amount: a ration (FAO/WHO
energy needs; Cato's wine, oil and olive issues; Foxhall's oil consumption), a household's yearly output, or a
storage vessel's content (Christakis' Neopalatial pithoi; transport jars). Where men are counted next to a
commodity, the per-head amount must be a ration. Thousands of random hypotheses were scored: a unit size for
each of 15 commodity keys (GRA, VIN, OLE, OLIV, CYP, NI, AROM, *304, *306, *308 and the la57 words DA-SI-*118,
TE-TU, *28B-NU-MA-RE, U-*325-ZA, DI-DE-RU), nuisance values for 12 fraction signs, and person/stock labels for
the count keys. Each was fitted on half the documents and scored on the other half. Nulls: numbers shuffled
between documents, random units, and 30-60 nonsense rulers. Controls: Linear B (DAMOS; units and subunits
hidden; truth 96 l dry, 28.8 l liquid), Ur III (sila hidden), planted Linear A-shaped archives. No Linear B
sign value is an input. The commodity classes of GRA, VIN, OLE and OLIV follow their pictures (assumption);
every other key is 'generic'.
