STEP 2. Candidates outside those families (each: would a conventional decipherer do it? no):
 K1 THE READER'S PULL (editors as an instrument to be calibrated). SigLA's stipple ('worn', measured from the drawing, la71)
    is physical surface damage and does not depend on the content. If wear is content-blind, a worn sign-group was, before
    anyone read it, drawn from the same population as an unworn one of the same site, support and length. What the
    editors made of it is then an experiment on the editors: random misreading makes worn groups LESS familiar
    (fewer repeats elsewhere), reading toward known words (gravity) makes them MORE familiar. Massive random guessing:
    tens of thousands of random reader models (error rate, gravity, target pool, frequency exponent, share of signs worn)
    are run on unworn groups and scored by ABC against the worn groups. Controls: pseudo-worn unworn groups with no reader
    (must give error ~0), planted readers (random-error and gravity) to recover, wear labels shuffled within strata.
    Outside test: SigLA's independent re-drawing ('variant' = another sign) is a second reader, held out of the fit:
    gravity predicts that groups SigLA reads otherwise are MORE familiar than their strata; random error predicts LESS.
    Web check (7 Oct 2026, 'editorial bias damaged signs Linear A statistical test'): nothing found.
 K2 INVERSE TAPHONOMY. Explicit destruction physics (tablet length, fracture into pieces, size-dependent recovery by
    excavation era, joins) fitted by ABC on the edge-break flags and surviving lengths of documents published by 1950;
    survivors must predict the damage profile of later finds (time travel), against shuffled publication years and a
    planted physics world. Then ask what the lost part contained. Web check: no Linear A fragmentation/recovery model.
 K3 PHYSICS PREDICTS ARITHMETIC. The K2 physics, fitted without any number, predicts how much of each KU-RO section was
    lost on broken tablets; scored on the written totals (a channel the fit never saw), against shuffled totals.
 K4 THE SQUEEZE. Scribes short of room at a line end abbreviate: sign-groups at the end of crowded physical lines
    (la81 line layer) as shorter variants of groups elsewhere; thousands of random drop rules, line positions shuffled.
 K5 THE UNRECORDABLE. Structural zeros (feature combinations expected >= 3 times, seen 0) mined at HT and tested
    leave-one-site-out against zeros mined in site-shuffled corpora. (Closest to la18/la65; kept last.)
 K6 FIRE AS A SORTING MACHINE. Little's law on each burnt snapshot: stock of nodules and roundels per tablet entry as a
    dwell-time ratio per site, compared with planted office simulations. (Small n per site; kept last.)
Choice: K1 now (it calibrates every reading the earlier loops used), then K2, then K3 (or K4 if K2 dies).
