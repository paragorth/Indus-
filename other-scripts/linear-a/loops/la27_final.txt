# LA-27 final: THE BALANCE PANS KNOW THE NUMBERS (4 Oct 2026)
# Idea: excavated Minoan balance weights give the physical unit ladder (unit c. 61 g, mina 8 units, talent c. 29 kg ~ an
# oxhide ingot). Linear A amounts of weighed goods should heap on that ladder better than on 10^4 random ladders, and
# the fraction signs should fall on its sub-units. Controls: Linear B weight notation recovered from numbers alone,
# planted ladders in LA-sized numbers, jittered numbers, split-half re-test. Tools: tools/la27_weights_data.py,
# la27_common.py, la27_c1.py ... la27_c4.py. Data: data/la27_weights.json (86 published masses); checkpoints

(2) Held-out heaping of LA integers on the physical ladder 1:8:60 (and the data hint 1:4:120) vs 10^4 random ladders, per commodity, planted (5-15 %), jittered and split-half controls. (3) LB control: ceiling search over 10^4 random ratio vectors from sub-unit counts; fraction values vs physical sub-units with planted power. (4) The LB ceiling estimator applied to repeated LA fraction signs, with planted fifths/thirds/binary and Fisher tests | (1) No quantum in the masses (61 g: P 0.45; best 17.1 g, P 0.59), although planted lattices with <= 6 % error are found 0.69-0.85; no 8-unit mina step. Ladder used = literature 1:8:60 (ingot 475 units = 59 minas). (2) LA integers: P1 P 0.37 (all), 0.56-0.996 per commodity; nothing replicates (split-half P 0.75); planted 5 % heaping found 8/8. The balance pans do not show in the written integers: no LA commodity heaps on the 1:8:60 ladder or any ladder beyond decimal counting (B, calibrated negative), and the published masses alone do not fix a quantum (B). Weighed-goods logograms cannot be identified by heaping. Positive, value-free: by the LB-calibrated ceiling rule, D is a counted part (>= 3, probably 5, per next unit) and B >= 3, while K, L2, JE, F, H act as single denominations (B). D = 1/5 and B = 1/3 fit; a binary D or B (attack-1 1/8) does not (C, would kill: a re-read of HT 115a DDDD plus a fifth D anywhere).
