# LA-6 cycle 4: words put back. Numeric fingerprints, converters, fixed allotments
# Tools: tools/la6_fingerprint.py, la6_fingerprint2.py, la6_allot.py -> data/la6_fingerprint.out, la6_fingerprint2.out, la6_allot.out
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-6.4a | Fingerprint of each entry word on >= 3 tablets (63 words; 1,008 labelled entries): residual log quantity (minus its commodity's mean), commodity profile, fraction share, multi-commodity share, after-total share. Word identity in the numbers: F = between-word / within-word variance of the residual. Null: quantities shuffled within commodity class (bare numbers a class), 2,000 runs. Per-word two-sided P. | With totals: F = 2.48 vs 1.01, P = 0.0005. Without KU-RO / KI-RO / PO-TO-KU-RO: F = 1.27 vs 1.00, P = 0.094. Per word (P < 0.05, 7 of 63 where 3.2 are expected): KU-RO x12 the typical amount (P = 0.001); A-DU x4.8 (0.023); E x2.7 (0.026); TE x2.0 (0.029, 20 tablets); TU x0.3 (0.033); WA x0.25 (0.046); KU-PA x0.2 (0.015). SA-RA2 x1.1 (P = 0.69), but the most multi-commodity word (NI 10, OLE 10, GRA 8, VIN 7). | Outside the totals, words carry little numeric identity beyond their commodity. The numbers grade only a few words (see the final grades). |
| LA-6.4b | Functional synonyms: Euclidean distance on standardised fingerprints; for each pair of words P = share of 300 label-shuffle runs in which the pair is as close. | 88 of 1,953 pairs at P < 0.01 against about 20 by chance. The tight pairs are all 'bare-number list words' with similar magnitudes (DU ~ SI-PI-KI ~ PA-DE, TA-NA-TI ~ KU-PA3-NU, ZU, QA-*310-I, *324-DI-RA); most share 0-1 tablets. | One real functional class, 'entries in a bare-number list' (person-like lines counted without a logogram). The numbers cannot split it into offices or synonyms. No commodity-word synonym pair. |
| LA-6.4c | Converters: a word whose quantity is a fixed multiple of the entry before it on the same tablet. HIT = distinct tablets within +-10% of one ratio; null: both quantities drawn within commodity, 2,000 runs. Check: exact repeats of the previous quantity against the corpus-wide repeat rate (146 of 656 = 0.223), binomial with Bonferroni over 42 words. | Raw HIT hits: MI-NU-TE, DI-DE-RU, KU-PA3-NU, KA, SA-RU (P 0.001-0.012), all at ratio 1.0 (they repeat the amount above). Against the corpus repeat rate: DI-DE-RU 3/3 (P = 0.011, Bonferroni 0.46), KU-PA3-NU 4/7 (0.048, Bonferroni 1). KI-RO has no fixed ratio to the entry above (P = 0.69; ratios 0.0-8). | **No converter word.** The 'hits' are the general habit of writing equal amounts in a row. KI-RO is not a fixed share of the entry above it. |
| LA-6.4d | Fixed allotments: does the same word (2+ signs) get the same amount on different tablets? 73 words on >= 2 tablets; a match = equal first quantity (same class) on two tablets. N1: quantities shuffled within commodity; N2: shuffled within tablet (keeps each tablet's standard amount), 2,000 runs. LB control: first word of a document and its first amount. | 17 words with a repeat (26 tablet pairs) vs N1 13.1 (P = 0.11) and N2 16.6 (P = 0.52). The visible case is the HT 86a / 95a / 95b dossier: DA-ME, MI-NU-TE, KU-NI-SU, DI-DE-RU, SA-RU with GRA 10 each on HT 95a/b and 20 each on HT 86a (HT 86a = 2 x HT 95b for all 5 shared names). The within-tablet null explains it fully: the tablet sets the amount, not the name. LB control is also weak (12 vs 8.3, P = 0.067), so this test has low power. | No word-specific ration across the corpus. Amounts are set per tablet (a standard of 10 with doubling between tablets in one dossier), the same 'tablet-local rate' found in LA-6.1c. |
