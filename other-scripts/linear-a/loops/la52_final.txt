LA-52 final: COPY IT A THOUSAND TIMES AND SEE WHAT REFUSES TO DIE (iterated learning)
Date 5 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/la52_common.py (learners, features, plants, objective), la52_run.py (chain runner), la52_report.py (cycle 1), la52_c2.py (per-learner), la52_c3.py (halves).
Logs: loops/la52_cycle1-3.txt. Checkpoints and reports: data/la52_ckpt/ (git-ignored; c1_report.txt, c2_report.txt, c3_report.txt).

| LA-52.final | Novelty: iterated learning (a language-evolution method) used as a meaning detector on an undeciphered corpus. A chain of simulated learners each learns the corpus from the previous generation's output and writes a new corpus of the same size. Structure the writing system can carry by itself should survive or grow; structure that dies under every learner must have been held by something outside the writing (the world being recorded). About 7,000 chains (~30,000 learned-and-rewritten corpora): random mixtures of back-off n-gram, slot-template, topic-clustered and GRU learners with random bottleneck, copy noise and memory limits (cycle 1, harsh); single-type chains (cycle 2, gentle); disjoint document halves (cycle 3). Measure: excess of every relation instance over a kind-preserving shuffle, and its retention relative to chains started from a shuffled corpus (the learners' own attractor). Nulls: shuffled-start chains; an objective that preserves 40 arbitrary features. Controls: planted world-anchored rules (latent official, exact sum) vs planted learnable-meaningless rules (word before GRA, word -> fixed scale); Linear B KN+PY and Ur III at LA size, words as opaque ids; held-out halves. | Planted: world rules retain 0.00-0.08 (percentile 0.00-0.14), meaningless rules 0.21-1.05 (percentile 0.74-0.94). The preservation objective lifts its targets (0.29 vs 0.08). In all three corpora retention is ordered by relation DISTANCE: running totals, entry succession, co-occurrence, logogram order and site decay in about one copy; word->scale, logogram->scale and first/last word survive. Linear B: ko-wa -> ko-wo (census order), the *150 -> *142 -> CORN order and contribution lists die under every learner; the land-tenure formula (te-o-jo, do-e-ro, ko-to-no-o-ko, e-ke, pe-mo) refuses to die; commodity->unit and to-so -> GRA are among the MOST persistent (the control fails for adjacent meaning). Ur III: transaction verbs, dates and counted animals decay, measures persist; word anchoring replicates across halves (rho 0.28, P 0.009). LB: replicates (rho 0.68, P 0.005). Linear A: lowest retention = KU-RO running total (0.04) and the SA-RA2 basket order (SA-RA2 -> NI 0.06, NI -> VIN 0.07), but both are typical of their families; word anchoring does NOT replicate across halves (rho 0.03, P 0.46). | NOT CRACKED. A (method, calibrated): iterated-learning decay ranks structures by how far apart their parts are, not by whether they mean something. Meaning held between neighbours (commodity-unit, total->commodity, person->ration) is copied as easily as meaningless adjacent rules, so it can never look world-anchored. B (calibrated negative): no Linear A word or sign carries replicable world-anchored structure by this measure (it passes in LB and Ur III at the same size). B (convergent, known): KU-RO totals and the SA-RA2 -> NI -> VIN basket are the Linear A structures no learner keeps, the same items la1 and la46 found by other routes. They add no new meaning. C: OLE+RI (z -3.4, full corpus only) is the one LA sign whose long-range links die faster than its frequency predicts. Would support: z < -2 in both halves once more tablets with OLE+RI exist. Would kill: z > -1 in a re-run with a new seed. B: the commodity signs GRA+PA, OLE+DI, GRA and the word JA-SA-SA-RA-ME are 'learnable' (they keep their long-range links under topic learners), i.e. they behave like the LB formula words. They sit in repeated document templates and are not world-anchored. |

Grades
- A (method): retention under iterated learning = locality/learnability. Calibrated by planted rules (separate), the preservation objective (detected) and LB adjacent meaning (invisible).
- B (negative): no replicable word-level world anchoring in Linear A (halves P 0.46; LB P 0.005; Ur III P 0.009).
- B (known, convergent): KU-RO totals and the SA-RA2 basket order are the least learnable LA structures.
- C: OLE+RI anchored (would support / would kill above).
- Demoted: none of the cycle-2 low-z names (SI-PI-KI, *28B-NU-MA-RE, KA-PA, KA-DI, MI-NU-TE) survived as candidates. They are chain-replicable but too rare to test in halves.
Sampling note: 255 LA documents. Absences here are upper bounds: a word with fewer than ~3 long-range co-occurrences cannot be scored at all.
