# LA-13 cycle 1: Linear A contains its own dialects (exhaustive cross-site one-sign substitution search)
Scripts: tools/la13_common.py, tools/la13_c1.py. Output: data/la13/c1_report.txt, c1_stdout.txt, c1_*.json (checkpoints).
Web check (4 Oct 2026): hand studies note regional differences in free-vowel use (Linear A vs B, by region) and site/scribe palaeography;
no automated cross-site substitution-rule search with label-shuffle, Markov and planted controls was found.
Data: all lineara.xyz inscriptions with words of >= 2 signs, 6 site groups as "languages": HT 439 types, OTH (all other sites pooled) 249,
ZA 112, KH 87, KN 60, PH 54. Only 43 types occur in >= 2 groups (the cognate base is tiny).
A pair is CLEAN when w1 (site A) is absent at B and w2 (site B) is absent at A. Rule = (A, B, X at A, Y at B[, position I/M/F]); X or Y may be
"_" (one inserted sign). Support k = independent pairs = min(#distinct w1, #distinct w2). Search size corrected by max-statistic nulls.

| LA-13.1c | Positive control (power). Three random substitutions planted in Khania at rate 1.0 and 0.5 (3 draws each, signs of KH frequency 4-15); Linear B subsampled to LA type counts (KN 439, PY 87, TH 112, others 249) with 3 rules planted in PY (3 draws x 2 rates); 100 label permutations each. | LA planted: 0 of 18 planted rules recovered; best planted support k = 2 (rank 19-1,100), vs null 95% threshold 5. LB planted: 0 of 18 recovered (k <= 1). | The search has NO power at Linear A size: even a total, exceptionless sign substitution at one site is invisible, because the sites share almost no vocabulary (43 types). Absence of rules is therefore NOT evidence against dialects (< 3/18 power). |
| LA-13.1d | Negative controls.
