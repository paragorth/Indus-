# LA-45 final: TWO MACHINES ARGUE UNTIL THE MEANINGS SETTLE
| LA-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| LA-45.final | Four cycles (tools/la45_common.py, la45_c1.py, la45_c2.py, la45_c3.py, la45_c4.py, la45_report.py, la45_compare.py, la45_c1_tablets.py, la45_c2_support.py, la45_c3_pairs.py, la45_c3_oracle.py; checkpoints data/la45_ckpt/, git-ignored). Adversarial self-play: Proposers give every value-free word/sign type one of <= 12 anonymous meanings (class bigram + emitted types + occurrence frames incl. section-sum arithmetic), fitted by incremental collapsed Gibbs with an MDL cost per meaning; Critics evolve the subset of a vault of unseen documents that maximises the Proposers' loss (held-out bits minus contradictions = held-out events the meanings call impossible); population-based training 12 x 6, 120 rounds; 6 independent populations per corpus; abstract names (TOTAL, COMMODITY, ENTRY, HEADER, TERM, RARE) by fixed profile rules. ~1.4 billion candidate evaluations over 28 corpora x 6 populations. Controls: planted meaning system, Ur III and Linear B reduced to opaque ids (truth used only to score), type-shuffled (S1) and within-document-shuffled (S2) twins, site and time splits. | Planted: recovered every time (NMI 0.69-0.80 vs null 0.05-0.14; TOTAL 1/1; COMMODITY 9/9; persons ENTRY; order-borne 58-82%). Ur III / Linear B: roles above chance (NMI z 3-7), Knossos -> Pylos and Umma -> Puzrish-Dagan transfer (+0.1..+2.4 bits), word order worth 1.0-1.3 bits on tablets (6/6); total words missed in both. Linear A, random split: 54 types settle (agreement >= 0.8), gain 1.7-2.7 bits, but 39/54 settle identically with order destroyed; on tablets the meanings give +0.01 bits and order adds nothing (drop -0.06, 3/6; controls 1.0-2.4, 6/6); a hand partition logogram/word/KU-RO finds only +0.19 bits. Hagia Triada meanings mispredict other sites (-0.9 bits, 6/6) and later finds (-2.6 bits, 6/6) where real controls stay >= 0. Shuffled LA gives no stable meanings beyond frequency (gain ~0.1). | NOT CRACKED. GRADE A (method): calibrated self-play recovers planted, Ur III and Linear B roles from value-free tokens. GRADE B: the main logograms (VIN, OLE, OLIV, VIR 1.0; GRA, NI 0.83) plus single signs KI, DI, RE, TI, MA form one counted-commodity meaning (stable across populations, survives held-out criticism, but document-level, not order-borne). GRADE B: single-sign nodule/roundel words (*301, KA, KU, SI, RO, ZE) form a stable heading meaning (document-level). GRADE B (calibrated negative): Linear A tablets carry little order-borne role structure that generalizes (<= 0.2 bits vs >= 1 bit in controls), and HT usage does not carry to other sites or newer finds. GRADE C: ligatured OLE+U/+MI/+DI behave as counted entries, not commodities, by order (would support: same split in a new HT find; would kill: vanishes without HT 9/86/95 copies).
