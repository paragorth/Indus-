LA-53 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026). CLAY IS ATTENTION, AND ATTENTION IS STATUS.
Scripts: tools/la53_common.py (parsers that keep physical lines for LA, LB/DAMOS and Ur III/CDLI; features; ranker), tools/la53_c1.py.
Novelty check: web search (layout / spacing / status of recipients in Linear A) found only qualitative work on page layout (Tomas, Finlayson); LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md and STRATEGIES.md have no layout-surplus or status ranking. SigLA sign sizes are not in our SigLA parse (la22), so sign size is not used.
Attention per word occurrence (opaque ids): alone on its line, lines spanned by its entry, entry heads the document, extra words in the entry, earliness, entry bulk vs document median, signs in the word. Expected attention = regression on log quantity, missing quantity, commodity, document size, site (and word length when it is not itself a feature). 2,000 random specs (feature subset, Dirichlet weights, covariate set, shrinkage); surplus per type averaged over specs. N1: attention rows permuted within commodity x document-size strata (100-400 runs); N2: quantities shuffled across entries.
