pe29 cycle 1 -- REBUILD THE WRITING SESSIONS: do the independent weak evidences agree pairwise? (tools/pe29_cycle1.py; log data/pe29_ckpt/cycle1.log)
Evidence (tablet x tablet): pool = IDF cosine of signs on 2-40 tablets; hand = agreement of the variant chosen for each base written on both tablets minus chance;
clay = batch-centred colour distance (pe26, same photo batch only); mus = exp(-museum-number gap/25), same prefix; size = -|log h,w,t| distance.
1,300 Susa tablets (>= 2 signs). Ur III control: 1,028 dated Drehem tablets of Amar-Suen 5 (343 days; date lines removed). Null: one matrix relabelled inside
strata (museum prefix x numeral system x line-count bin; clay inside photo batch), 200 draws.
