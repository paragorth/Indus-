pe24 cycle 1 -- "the ~a signs are red ink": marker-level, exhaustive
Tools: tools/pe24_common.py, pe24_ur3.py, pe24_cycle1.py. Numbers: data/pe24_cycle1_{plant,ur3,pe}.json.
Cases: 49 clean PE tablets with a written total (32 count, 17 capacity; cleaning as attack_arith; per-tablet decimal/sexagesimal and fraction value sets). 23/49 close with every entry +1.
Search: every {+1, -1, 0} assignment to the 10 commonest variant markers (~a ~b ~c ~d ~f ~g ~i ~e ~1 ~l; 59,049 assignments); entry coefficient = product over its markers. Fit on a random half (max closures, then fewest non-+1), score the other half; 50 splits.
