pe81 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): THE BLANK IS THE FORM (silhouettes as outside data).

Why this: cycle 1 showed site-level outside data has five points and no power. Photographs are outside data with ~1,000 points:
nobody interpreting Proto-Elamite produced the outline of a clay blank. pe75 used only catalogue height/width/thickness and
pe26 only colour/texture. Here only the SHAPE of the outline counts, with size and aspect removed: corner fill, rectangularity,
convexity, taper, side bulge, and the pillow profile of the top edge (lens vs slab). Idea (counter-intuitive): the blank was
shaped for a kind of document before any sign was written, so the outline is a pre-printed form, and text classes should be
predictable from outline shape on tablets the model never saw. Conventional decipherers read signs; this ignores them.
Web check (7 Oct 2026): Dahl's 'writing phases' describe tablets as oblong / flat / rectangular (qualitative, by eye); no
quantitative outline study and no shape-vs-content test found. (Dahl's interpretation is not used as evidence.)

Design (pre-registered before any shape feature is computed):
 - Shapes: CDLI thumbnails (pe26 cache, ~300 px fat-cross) segmented on the black background; obverse = largest blob in the
   upper half, top edge = blob above it. 10 scale-free features. Size/aspect covariates (log area, aspect, thickness/width from
   the catalogue, plus damage share 'frac_x') are regressed out of every shape feature and every text feature.
 - Text: the 182 pe81 tablet features (no meaning used).
 - Massive random guessing: 20,000 random (1-2 text features, 1-2 shape features, signs) pairs; partial Spearman on a
   training half (tablets split by museum-number blocks of 10, so drawer neighbours stay together); survivors = |rho| above
   the 99.9th percentile of the same statistic with text rows shuffled within size x damage strata; re-tested on the held-out half.
 - Kill: held-out replication rate of survivors (same sign, p < 0.05) not above the shuffled-row null (95th pct over 20 nulls).
 - Planted control: a fake text feature correlated with one shape feature at partial rho 0.15 / 0.25 must be recovered on the
   held-out half.
 - Outside freeze: if anything survives, its predictions for the 88 untransliterated Tehran tablets (P520211-P520300, from their
   photos) are frozen with sha256 for checking when they are transliterated.
