pe41 final -- LET MACHINES REINVENT WRITING, THEN COMPARE (5 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/pe41_lib.py (economy, population learning, features, graph alignment), pe41_run.py, pe41_analyze.py,
pe41_analyze2.py, pe41_diff.py, pe41_calib.py. Numbers: data/pe41_c1.json, pe41_c1_diff.json, pe41_c2.json, pe41_c3_calib.json,
pe41_c4.json, pe41_c4_calib.json. Runs (git-ignored): data/pe41_ckpt/.
Not done before (web search 4 Oct 2026: emergent-communication work invents pictographs, e.g. arXiv 2502.01568, but nobody aligns
machine-invented accounting scripts to PE; pe6 hand-coded an economy and r3 evolved scripts for corpus statistics, neither votes
sign meanings).

What was run: 2,238 machine societies (964 + 974 + 300), each with a random toy economy, inventing a sign system through a
Lewis signalling game, a name-length trade-off, cultural hill-climbing of what to write where, and order conventions.
Each invented system was aligned to PE, PE halves, shuffled PE, proto-cuneiform (PC), shuffled PC and 8-12 held-out invented
systems by structure only (16 behaviour features + co-occurrence-graph Hungarian matching). Votes were pooled.

Verdict: NOT CRACKED. Machines reinvented writing and could read each other, but not proto-cuneiform, so their meaning votes
on PE are C at best. Two B results survive: PE's corpus shape needs a large inventory with frequent new variants, and archaic
scribes wrote redundantly in a way that reconstruction games do not reward.
