c3: from half A only, rungs = every A-surviving rate, markers = count-line signs on >= 3 A-tablets of a rung (p <= 0.01, 100 swap nulls).
On half B every count line with a marker predicts a capacity line within 5 lines = count x rung.  Nulls: 200 swap (same notation
shape), 200 free (any shape), and the same lines scored with another rung's rate.  c3b splits count 1 (the shape nearly fixes the
value) from count >= 2 (a real rate test).
