pe48 SIGNS AS GOODS IN A TRADE NETWORK -- cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): calibration on Ur III provinces and Linear B sites
Model (tools/pe48_lib.py): each sign/word is TRAVELLING (same per-token rate at every site) or HOMED at a site with a
gravity fall-off g = eps_e + (1-eps_e) exp(-D/L) (M_j/M_h)^b, D = great-circle or route-graph distance, four contrast
floors per sign. 400 random theta (L 30-5,000 km, eps, b, metric, penalty) per run, 5-fold site-stratified CV,
score = held-out presence log-likelihood gain over the all-travelling model on OUTPOST tablets. Survivors = top 10%.
Shapes: PE-shaped draw (hub 1,502 docs; outposts 27/22/12/11/1/1) x3 per hub, and BALANCED (outposts 200 each) x1.
Gold (controls only): Ur III T = 64 commodity and title words, N = personal names (word after kiszib3/giri3 or before
szu ba-ti), M = month names; Linear B T = logograms + ~55 title/term words, N = line-initial words before a logogram.
Nulls: site labels shuffled among documents (same search). Plants: PLANT in 30% of outpost-1 docs and 0.3% of hub
docs; ROUTE in 20% of outposts 1-2. Script tools/pe48_cycle1.py; per-run results data/pe48_ckpt/c1_*.json.
