pe84 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): STYLUS CENSUS. Written before results were seen.

K1 died (cycle 1). Next candidate from the cycle-1 list: K2. Idea: do not read the signs; read the stylus. The stroke-width /
relief spectrum of the obverse (12 features: relief at 0.4/0.8/1.6 mm, granulometry shape at 1/2/3.5/5 mm, impression
width, contrast, density, coherence, diagonal energy) is treated as a fingerprint of the tool. If one stylus = one writer
or one sitting, tablets with near-identical fingerprints should share vocabulary beyond everything that also makes them
alike (find lot by Sb number gap, same volume, same photo batch, size, line count, brightness, photo scale, vocabulary
sizes). Conventional palaeography attributes hands by sign shapes; this ignores shapes and asks the tool.
 2a meter check: is obverse-vs-reverse of ONE tablet closer than obverse vs the reverses of other tablets of the same photo
    batch and volume? (AUC > 0.5 means the fingerprint is tablet-specific; it cannot tell tool from clay or lighting.)
 2b 3,000 random stylus metrics (random feature subsets, random weights, raw or rank space); per metric and publication
    half, k-NN vocabulary lift = mean covariate-residualised Jaccard similarity between each tablet and its 5
    stylus-nearest tablets (z vs random neighbours). Select top 2% on one half, re-test on the other (z > 1.645), both
    directions. Null: vocabularies permuted within volume x size x line strata, 20 runs, the same 3,000 metrics.
    Planted: 25 'scribes' = groups of 4 stylus-nearest tablets each given 2 private shared signs.
 Support: replications above the null maximum AND median lift above the null 95th percentile in both halves.
 Kill: replications within the null range, or the planted scribes not found (method dead).
 Caveat written in advance: numeral-vs-sign content moves the meter (cycle 1, PE-84.1.3), so a text-composition link is
 expected through content; a vocabulary (sign identity) link is not.
