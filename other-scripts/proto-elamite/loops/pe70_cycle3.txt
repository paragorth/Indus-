pe70 cycle 3: invert the problem -- guess who signed from the text alone, held out by seal
(tools/pe70_c3.py; data/pe70_ckpt/c3.json, c3_ctrl.json)
20,000 random 'party marker' hypotheses (1-3 tokens drawn from tokens on seal-id tablets, rarer favoured), read as 'tablets
carrying ANY of these belong to one party'. Score on TRAIN seals: share of marker-carrying tablet pairs that share a seal,
minus the base share (lift). Survivors = top 1% by train lift; statistic = their mean lift on HELD-OUT seals (seals split
in half; 10 splits). Nulls: seal ids shuffled among seal-id tablets, freely and within volume (same hypotheses, same splits).
