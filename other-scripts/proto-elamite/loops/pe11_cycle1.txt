pe11 cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): CALIBRATION -- can a blind "film" search rebuild the year at all?
Scripts: tools/pe11_common.py, pe11_cycle1.py, pe11_cycle1b.py, pe11_cycle1c.py. Numbers: data/pe11_cycle1.json, pe11_cycle1b.json, pe11_cycle1c.json.
Prior art (web search, LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md): Dahl's studies of Susa animal husbandry read single tablets; no attempt found to order undated PE (or any) tablets into a seasonal sequence by fitting a herd/stock cycle.
Method: each tablet = one frame (24 features: shares of the 16 commonest final signs, capacity share, fraction share, log counted total, log capacity total, mean log entry, share of 1s, log entry count, share of long strings). Latent month z in 0..11 per tablet, searched by annealed Gibbs + hard EM (6 restarts x 25 temperatures) under a closed-loop model (harmonic, = a year) and an open-drift model with the same parameter count (quadratic). Label-free statistics: ring fit R2c and loop excess D = R2c - R2line. Nulls: column shuffle, Gaussian-copula surrogate (same marginals and rank correlations, no ring), random-walk surrogate (secular drift). Recovery (needs true months): share of tablets placed within +-1 month after the best of 24 rotations/reflections, vs 100 month permutations.

| PE-11.1 | Cycle verdict | The film search has power to ORDER tablets when a season exists, but without known dates nothing can certify the order: the label-free statistics need a season ~40x stronger (share of variance) than the real Drehem livestock season (1%, 9% even in month bundles) | CALIBRATION: any PE result from this method can only kill a very strong season (>= ~40% of feature variance); a PE null is NOT evidence against a yearly herd cycle.
