pe47 final (5 Oct 2026): THE TABLETS ARE PRINTOUTS OF ONE HIDDEN SPREADSHEET.
Idea: Susa kept a master table (units x commodities x periods) and each tablet is a slice or an aggregate of it. Three cycles: (1) random search over table shapes (each sign an axis index or not) scored by held-out exact values; (2) arithmetic links between tablets (summary = sum of a detail tablet; the same rare numbers printed twice); (3) a rank-1 multiplicative table (tablet scale, row-indexing headers, pairs joined by one rare number). Controls: a planted master table sliced at 6% survival to PE size; Ur III Drehem and Umma, PE-sized samples and full corpora. Nulls: values shuffled across tablets within commodity sign and system; entries re-dealt between tablets; a random-tensor world; headers shuffled.

Results
- A (method limit): exact cell reconstruction and computed-sum links recover the plant but fail on Ur III, at PE size and (for sums) at full size. The axis labels (unit vs commodity vs period) are not identifiable from exact values, even in the plant.
- B- (negative, weak power): no PE tablet pair carries the same rare numbers twice (0 pairs, < 3/1,213). Ur III at the same size gives 2-4 such pairs (drafts, duplicates, account-extract copies; full Umma 33 vs 2.3 null, same year 0.58 vs 0.03).
- A (data, generic): PE tablets have one multiplicative scale (held-out gain 0.41 vs nulls -0.10), as Ur III and the plant do.
- B (negative): no PE header sign indexes a row with its own scale (held-out R2 at the shuffled-header level), while Drehem recovers its known axes this way (officials, 'dead' ba-usz2, provinces; R2 0.05-0.14 vs -0.02).
- C: PE pairs joined by a number seen exactly twice share more signs (Jaccard 0.073 vs 0.051-0.055, p 0.02); Ur III at PE size does not show this, so it is uncalibrated. Top pairs P008088-P008754 (M288 180), P008919-P009204, P009380-P009547, P008019-P008639. Would support: a new or collated tablet repeating one of these numbers with the same signs. Would kill: the excess goes under a genre-matched null.
