Verdict cycle 2: NULL. No slot of the Proto-Elamite entry is computed from the numeral by any residue, digit-sum,
running-sum or random modular rule among ~130,000 tested (5 slots x 26,233), and none carries a size class beyond the
number system. The method is calibrated: it finds a planted mod-3 mark on 10% of entries at z ~10 and the Ur III unit
word at z 43. Grade B (negative): "PE entries carry no check digit or size-class sign in the first, last, or post-header slot".
