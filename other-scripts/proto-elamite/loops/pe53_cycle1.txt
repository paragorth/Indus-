pe53 cycle 1: THE LAMBS TELL THE MONTH -- calibration and first PE run. Scripts tools/pe53_common.py, pe53_cycle1.py; numbers data/pe53_cycle1.json (+ data/pe53_ckpt/, git-ignored).
Prior art (web search 5 Oct 2026): no study scores PE (or Ur III) age classes by a birth-pulse shape; Dahl 2005 reads herd signs philologically; pe11 searched for a yearly ring in general tablet features (null), pe20/pe37 used herd totals and bones, not the season.
Outside curve: births spread over 3-4 months (Balasse et al. 2017, Animal 11, sheep tooth-enamel d18O); western Iran extensive flocks mate in July and lamb in December (Kurdi sheep, Ilam; J. Agric. Sci. Technol. 2011, 13(5): 701-708); Arabi sheep of Khuzestan lamb in autumn-winter, weaned ~100 days. Curve family: pulse width 2-4 months, 0.7-1.3 young per female, monthly loss 2-10%, young class kept 4-12 months, scribal logit offset -1.5..+3; 576 curves. Score per assignment: S1 = log marginal likelihood (record young share = mixture over time of year) minus a timeless beta-binomial; S2 = same minus a generic two-component mixture (does the BIOLOGICAL shape beat any bimodality?).
Data: 45 PE herd records (pe20 extraction, chain copy P008389 dropped; unwritten lines = 0) on 23 tablets, 8 candidate signs (M362, M367, M346, M006 and their ~a / @g forms), 6,050 assignments to Y / F / other (all, exhaustive). Ur III Drehem: 9,882 dated livestock receipts (CDLI), 8 opaque terms (udu, sila4, masz2, masz2-gal, u8, ud5, kir11, munus-asz2-gar3); truth Y = sila4, masz2, kir11, asz2-gar3; F = u8, ud5.
