| PE-11.F1 | Calibration. Annealed Gibbs + hard-EM search of a month (0-11) for every tablet under a closed-loop (year) vs open-drift model with equal parameters; label-free loop excess D and ring fit R2c vs copula / shuffle / random-walk surrogates. Real control: 8,255 Drehem livestock tablets with known months. Planted: PE-shaped simulated herd office (lambing, culling, harvest stock, herd size). Pure synthetic rings | Drehem: month explains 1.0% of feature variance (gazelle fawns 5.6%); the search orders Drehem tablets only marginally (0.29-0.32 within +-1 month vs 0.28-0.30 chance) and D is BELOW the copula null. Simulated office: month R2 <= 0.07, D never significant. Label-free detection needs the season to carry ~40% of the feature variance | the method can order frames, but without dates it can certify only a very strong season. Real herd seasonality is ~40x too weak to be seen this way |
| PE-11.F2 | PE film: 638 tablets, ALL / HERD / GRAIN feature sets; 1,500 random feature subsets with a family-wise null from 8 copula replicates of the whole search; top 1% re-tested on held-out tablets; timeless commodity-loop control (TYPELOOP) | ALL z +1.4, HERD z +2.4 over copula (p 0.03-0.05); GRAIN none (p 0.52). Random search FWER p 0.56; 0 of 15 held-out survivors (best Bonferroni p 0.97). TYPELOOP (no time) z +2.0; Drehem (real seasons) z -2.0. Inferred "year" has lumpy occupancy (0-137 tablets per month) and runs through tablet types (capacity -> large counted -> M218/M371 small-count lists) | NO TIME ORDER beats the null. The small loop is a tour of commodity types, not a calendar |
| PE-11.F3 | Sign switches at inferred season boundaries (127 non-feature signs; arc share of 3 adjacent months; nulls: global and within-commodity-cluster permutation; FWER) | planted season header recovered at z 18 (FWER p 0.001) when the order is right, invisible when it is not; real PE best M338 (FWER 0.064, strict null), header M388 (0.085); null-A leaders (M036, M051, M340, M266) sit at the capacity end of the commodity loop | NULL: no month, season or seasonal-operation sign predicted |

Plain answer: no time order beats the null. A blind search over tablet orders can rebuild a year only when the season dominates the numbers (about 40% of the variance). In real Ur III herd accounts with known months, the season carries about 1%, and the same search barely orders them and gives no label-free signal. The PE tablets close a slightly better loop than Gaussian surrogates, but a timeless mixture of three commodity types closes the same loop, real seasonal herd data do not, no random feature subset replicates on held-out tablets, and the inferred "months" are a tour of tablet types. The switch test is calibrated (it finds a planted season word at z 18 when given the right order) and finds nothing in PE.
Grades: A none. B: "no strong yearly cycle (>= ~40% of feature variance) in PE tablet composition" and "the PE commodity space has a weak loop structure that a timeless type mixture reproduces". C: M338 and header M388 as seasonal-word candidates (would support: they cluster in one season in any dated or stratified PE context, e.g. a Malyan or Yahya level; would kill: an even spread there). Calibration fact, reusable for Linear A and Voynich: frame-ordering without dates needs a season far stronger than real herd accounts have; any future "time" attack needs dated anchors, not more search.
Next stranger idea suggested by the data: the tablets do sit on a loop of commodity types. Treat that loop as a "route" through the institution (capacity store -> large herds -> small lists) and ask whether header signs mark stations on the route.
