pe75 cycle 3: BLANKS FROM ONE BATCH (7 Oct 2026)
Idea: if clerks took pre-formed blanks from a stock made in one session, tablets from one batch share all three dimensions, which the text never sees. Do dimension twins share content (sign sets, header sign, first numeral system) beyond what size alone explains? pe36 used size as one weak evidence among many for sittings; exact three-dimension twins as a blank batch, with size-matched decoys, were not tested.
Script: tools/pe75_cycle3.py; checkpoint data/pe75_ckpt/cycle3.json. 781 whole PE tablets.
