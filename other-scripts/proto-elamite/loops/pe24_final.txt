pe24 FINAL -- "the ~a signs are red ink"
Tools: tools/pe24_common.py, pe24_ur3.py, pe24_cycle1.py, pe24_cycle2.py, pe24_cycle3.py. Numbers: data/pe24_cycle1_{plant,ur3,pe}.json, data/pe24_cycle2_{ratio,sign}.json, data/pe24_cycle3.json. Checkpoints and logs: data/pe24_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Rows: loops/pe24_cycle1..3.txt.
Idea: variant markers (~a, ~b, @g ...) or particular signs mark entries in another accounting state (owed, dead, not delivered) that count against the total (-1), not at all (0), or at a fixed ratio. On 49 clean PE tablets with a written total (23 close with every entry +1), every assignment was searched (exhaustive 3^10 and 5^7 over markers; annealing over 61 signs), fitted on half and re-tested on the other half. Nulls: markers re-dealt among signs; totals replaced by random numbers of the same size.

| ID | Method and control | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| PE-24.F1 | Controls | Ur III balanced accounts (21, CDLI): the blind search returns credit -1 and subtotal 0 as the unique best, held-out +2.5 tablets vs nulls <= 0.4 (p 0.03). Planted -1 markers (~a, ~b, ~c, ~d) recovered as unique best, held-out +2.9 to +4.0. Planted plain sign recovered (7/10 splits); a planted sign on only 3 tablets is not | PASS for markers and common signs; power is low for signs on < 5 tablets |
| PE-24.F2 | Markers as negatives, zeros or ratios | 0 extra closures for any of 59,049 (+1/-1/0) and 78,125 (with x2, x1/2) assignments; held-out 0.0 / -0.12. Random totals gain 1.9 / 3.8 on average from the same freedom (29/30 and 20/20 above the real 0) | A (negative, calibrated): no variant marker is red ink. The variants that do not close are not closed by any sign rule |
| PE-24.F3 | Every sign as a negative or zero | no full-data gain; held-out -1.65 (as shuffled markers); split fits disagree | B (negative; power-limited for rare signs) |
| PE-24.F4 | Rescue sets | negating 1-3 entries rescues 1/26 non-closing tablets (random 6%); omitting 1-3 rescues 6/26 (random 13%, p 0.01), but the omitted entries are unmarked and of no common kind (bare-number lines, M220, M036, M105, M388 lines); omitting all bare lines breaks 6 closing tablets | C: the misses are small one-entry slips or omissions, not a second ledger state |

Held-out total-closure rate: 23/49 (47%) at baseline; the best signed fit gives the same 47% on held-out halves (gain 0.00, null 95th percentile 0.0 for shuffled markers, 0.47 for random totals).
Plain answer: the red-ink idea is dead for the clean totals. The same search that blindly finds the credit side of Ur III balanced accounts and any planted negative marker finds nothing in Proto-Elamite: no variant letter and no sign makes a single extra total add up, while random totals of the same size gain 2-6 tablets from the same freedom. So where ~a matters (M362 vs M362~a in the herd accounts, pe20), it marks a parallel counted group that is added like any other, not a debit, death or shortfall. Totals that fail are off by about one entry. Limits: only 49 clean tablets; the herd tablets that carry the M362/M362~a split have no clean written total, so the test cannot reach them directly. Nothing is read; not cracked.
