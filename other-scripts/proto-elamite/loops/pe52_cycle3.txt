pe52 cycle 3: are the one-off strings predictable from sign weights? (nested, 5-6 Oct 2026; tools/pe52_cycle3.py)
Tablets halved; on one half alone the random-model search (4 splits x 80 models per half; PE tab 4 halves, others 2,
because the machine was shared) picks rule-B20 weighted signs; a weight-only ridge model fitted on that half predicts
the other half. R = 1 - MSE(model) / MSE(baseline), baseline = tablet mean (tab) or tablet-context-only model (ctx).
'hapax' = multi-sign strings seen once in the whole corpus (pe50's string universe); 'recur' = seen >= 2 times.
