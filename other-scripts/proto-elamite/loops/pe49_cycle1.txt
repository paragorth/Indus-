pe49 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): find the clerks by their tics (tools/pe49_common.py, pe49_ur3.py, pe49_cycle1.py)
Habits only: variant preference within 16-50 interchangeable families (PE ~variants; Ur III same-stem homophones; one-sign insertion/deletion word pairs in both), comma omission, numeral unit-order violations, unexchanged counts, numeral position, blank-space and ruling lines, edge and column use, reverse share, wrap lines, first/last line numeric, '!' and sic marks, line filling. Each habit residualised on length and dominant numeral unit. Consensus over 600 random habit subsets (50%) x 80% tablets x k 6-16 x seeds (masked distance, MDS, k-means); cut by spectral clustering at k 4-16.
Nulls: habits shuffled column-wise across tablets (3 x 150 runs); specialty = mean MI(content sign; hand) vs hand labels permuted within length x dominant-unit strata (200x).
Controls: Ur III Umma, 12 seal owners titled dub-sar x 60 tablets (720), signs made opaque; planted PE skeleton (724 Susa tablets, 8 clerks with variant, comma and blank-line tics, clerk follows a content topic 60% of the time).
