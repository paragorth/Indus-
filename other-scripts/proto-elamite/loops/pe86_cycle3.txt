pe86 cycle 3 (7 Oct 2026): lost first lines, predicted from the surviving clay; leads re-tested on drawings.
Plan and kill lines written before running (tools/pe86_cycle3.py; data/pe86_ckpt/c3.json).

 3a Logistic header predictor on the 589 intact tablets: size covariates alone vs size + clay (top, bottom, reverse
    header-end fill, hulls, top minus bottom). Grouped CV by publication batch; 100 shuffled-label nulls of the clay
    gain. Kill: clay gain <= null q95.
 3b Pseudo-test on 186 tablets whose line 1 is damaged but present (header status readable; their header end may be
    damaged too). Support: clay+size AUC above size-only with a bootstrap CI above 0. Kill: CI includes 0 and gain < 0.02.
 3c Header KIND: does the clay tell an M157 header from the others (grouped CV)? Kill if AUC gain over size < 0.03.
 3d FREEZE: header probabilities for the 294 tablets whose first line is lost (primed line numbers), with sha256.
 3e Re-test on the hand-drawn line-art record (all drawings with intact line 1; and the subset with no photo outline)
    of the cycle-1/2 leads: one-sign+number unheaded tablets square (+); |M327+X|, |M175+M136|, M388, |M157+M131|
    headers round (-); headed tablets with numeral-free line 2 round at the bottom (-); illegible header-shaped line 1
    round at the top (-) and square at the bottom (+). One-sided p from 2,000 stratified shuffles. A lead survives only
    with p < 0.05 on the drawings with the predicted sign; otherwise it is dropped.

Results (data/pe86_ckpt/c3.json, c3.log). Frozen file: data/pe86_frozen_lost_headers.json (sha256 48c41b0b...7110; see
pe86_final.txt on the re-freeze).
