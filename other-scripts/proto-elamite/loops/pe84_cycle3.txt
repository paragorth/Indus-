 3a Same meter, other script. 280 Uruk III/IV proto-cuneiform tablets (full CDLI photos, random draw of those with a
    photo, a width and an inscribed reverse; text from data/pe2_pc_corpus.json). Within-tablet test as PE-84.1.3
    (numeral share difference vs physical difference). Expectation if the meter is general: same signs as PE (depth and
    E04 down, big-granule share up on the numeral-richer face). If PC differs, the PE meter result is script- or
    photo-specific.
 3b Clay clock on PC with the numeral control (as K1b). Proto-cuneiform reverses very often carry totals; if the
    calibrated clock shows 'later, shallower totals' in PC but not in PE, PE totals were written in one sitting with the
    entries; if neither shows it, the method cannot see drying stages at CDLI photo resolution.
 3c Frozen outside prediction (Tehran): for the 87 untransliterated Tehran Susa tablets (P520211-P520300; photos in
    the scratchpad from pe81), the meter-only classifier (logistic on the reverse-minus-obverse physical differences,
    trained on the 815 Louvre/Tehran transliterated tablets) predicts which have a 'total-only reverse' (<= 2 numeric
    lines) vs anything else (blank or longer reverse). Training AUC is reported with 5-fold CV and the held-out publication half; the
    per-tablet probabilities are frozen with sha256 before anyone transliterates them. Would support (B): AUC >= 0.70 on
    the Tehran tablets once read. Would kill: AUC < 0.60.
