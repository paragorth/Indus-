pe34 'Compound signs are sums', cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026). Tools: tools/pe34_build.py, pe34_common.py, pe34_cycle1.py; results data/pe34_ckpt/c1.json (git-ignored).
Prior work: Born, Kelley, Monroe & Sarkar 2021 (ACL Findings) test compositionality of PE complex graphemes with image/text EMBEDDINGS. pe34 differs: explicit administrative behaviour (36 features: numeral system, size of count, position in entry, header line, reverse, tablet header, neighbours), a held-out transfer test (a modifier's shift learned on OTHER bases predicts the compound on an unseen base), modifier-shuffle nulls, proto-cuneiform and Linear B controls, and planted systems.
Units: PE 126 compounds (|A+B| -> base A, modifier B; |A+B+A| -> B@frame; |A+A| -> @dup; numeral insets kept as modifiers; 'X' excluded), >= 2 tokens, base attested alone >= 3 times. ARCH (Uruk III/IV admin, CDLI): 250 compounds |AxB|, |A+B|, |A.B|. LINB (DAMOS KN+PY): 30 (OVIS:m/f etc., ligatures TELA+TE etc.).
| PE-34.1a | Additive transfer: for each compound whose modifier occurs on >= 1 other base, predict behaviour = base vector + mean (compound - base) shift of the same modifier on other bases. Statistic dot = mean over targets of <observed residual, predicted residual> (residual after base + generic compound shift); null: modifier labels shuffled among compounds (2,000x) | PE 71 targets: dot 0.032 vs null -0.004 +- 0.012, p 0.006 (cos 0.11). Raw squared error: base+modifier shift is WORSE than base alone (0.61 vs 0.45): single compounds are too noisy for unshrunk shifts, but less bad than shuffled modifiers (G p 0.02) | Signal: in PE a modifier pushes behaviour in the same direction on different bases (weak, cos 0.11). Not yet checked for tablet confounds |
