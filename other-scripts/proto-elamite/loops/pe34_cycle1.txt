Prior work: Born, Kelley, Monroe & Sarkar 2021 (ACL Findings) test compositionality of PE complex graphemes with image/text EMBEDDINGS. pe34 differs: explicit administrative behaviour (36 features: numeral system, size of count, position in entry, header line, reverse, tablet header, neighbours), a held-out transfer test (a modifier's shift learned on OTHER bases predicts the compound on an unseen base), modifier-shuffle nulls, proto-cuneiform and Linear B controls, and planted systems.
Units: PE 126 compounds (|A+B| -> base A, modifier B; |A+B+A| -> B@frame; |A+A| -> @dup; numeral insets kept as modifiers; 'X' excluded), >= 2 tokens, base attested alone >= 3 times. ARCH (Uruk III/IV admin, CDLI): 250 compounds |AxB|, |A+B|, |A.B|. LINB (DAMOS KN+PY): 30 (OVIS:m/f etc., ligatures TELA+TE etc.).
