pe26 cycle 1 -- does the clay's colour say where it came from? (4 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/pe26_download.py (CDLI tn_photo thumbnails, ~300 px, EXIF kept; images stay in the scratchpad), pe26_controls.py
(CDLI search API for control tablets), pe26_features.py (segmentation against the black background; CIELAB percentiles, hue,
chroma, rg-chromaticity; high-pass relief, gradients, LBP, mottling), pe26_common.py, pe26_cycle1.py.
Prior work: image classifiers for provenance and period of cuneiform tablets exist (CuneiML, J. Open Humanities Data 2023;
"Shaping History", arXiv 2406.04039, shape-based dating). None isolates clay colour from photo batch or scores against hXRF.
Data: 1,267 of 1,585 PE tablets have CDLI photographs; 1,298 PE + 252 control photos are in colour (51 PE and most Louvre
Uruk III controls are greyscale). Photo batch = EXIF software + processing date (the EXIF date is a Photoshop batch-export
time, 3 files per second in museum-number order, not the shooting time).
Coverage: NMI Susa 446, Louvre Susa 792, NMI Yahya 4 (all in one export run with 93 Susa tablets), Ozbaki 1 (own batch),
Penn Malyan 2. NO colour photo of any Sialk, Sofalin or NMI Malyan tablet; of the hXRF imports only ST-11 (P009157) and
YT-01 (P009536) have photos.

Verdict (cycle 1): ordinary cross-validation separates every site pair (0.80-0.98), but that is photo batch: CDLI photographs
were taken and exported by collection drawer, so batch and find site coincide almost everywhere. The one contrast with
sites inside one batch (Yahya vs Susa, NMI) is large (AUC 0.995), but the positive check with known different clay
(Susa vs Girsu, Louvre) does not survive batch control. Clay appearance beyond batch: NOT ESTABLISHED (C).
