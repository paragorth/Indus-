Cycle verdict: the size-fit statistic works (planted recovery, Ur III kinds separate), but the Ur III control shows the opposite of the naive expectation: single receipts fit their clay best, and short multi-day tablets fit worst. PE sits below every Ur III kind (beta 0.4). Next: split PE by genre/system/seal/site/office, check the floor (minimum tablet size) and fix the mixture slope at 1.
