pe51 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): WHAT WOULD THE CLERK MISS? Deletion game on small predictive models
(tools/pe51_lib.py, pe51_run.py, pe51_sum.py; checkpoints data/pe51_ckpt/)
Idea: meaning is what is lost when a sign is erased. Random small models (transformer 1-3 layers or
bi-GRU, d 32-64, random dropout/lr/epochs/token-dropout) are trained on 4/5 of a corpus to fill in
masked parts of a tablet: all numerals at once (number SYSTEM, log2 MAGNITUDE, which line is the
TOTAL), half of the non-numeric (header) tokens (HEAD), and whole entry lines (ENT). On the held-out
fifth, every frequent sign is erased everywhere (replaced by a DEL token the models saw in training)
and the loss change of every other target is recorded, split into targets in the sign's own line (L)
and in other lines (R); the sign's own slots are never scored. Null: as many random OTHER sign tokens
erased in the same tablets (5 draws); excess = sign minus null. t over models; class = largest of
NUM-L (SYS/MAG of own line), NUM-R, TOT, HEAD, ENT with t > 3 and excess >= 0.02 nats, else SILENT.
Stable = same class in both tablet halves and in both seed halves. Pseudo-sign (one null draw
scored as a sign) gives the false-class rate. Prior work checked: Born et al. 2022 (sequence models
for PE headers) and LITERATURE.md; no deletion-profile study found.
