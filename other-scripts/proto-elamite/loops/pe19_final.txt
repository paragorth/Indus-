Results:
- C: frozen orders agree with the hidden chronology above random (best: CA axis 1, Susa rho +0.47 p 0.026, early sites AUC 0.76, Malyan AUC 0.92). The size baseline gets most of this: entry length alone comb 1.25, CA 1.84; trait-shuffled orders max 0.54. After sign count, CA keeps partial rho 0.43 (Susa) and 0.49 (Malyan). Would support: new stratified tablets follow the CA order at the same size. Would kill: a size-matched set where it fails.
- C: "later tablets are bigger, with longer entries and compounds; 17-16 tablets are one-line numerical or single-sign notations." This matches the published four-stage picture, but our data cannot separate it from the fact that larger tablets carry more traits.
- Relative chronology of signs and variants: NOT supported. The 'early' end of the CA order is format (1-line tablets, single-sign entries, no compounds; M362, M269~b, M106~a, N51). The 'late' end is one content cluster (M097~h, M386~a, M242~b, M338~b, M218~b, M029~b, M332~d). These describe the CA axis, not dates (planted control failed).
- B (method): in PE the content/office axis swamps any plausible drift. Massive random subsets (3,000) reproduce internal structure (half-split rho 0.87), but the internally best subsets are no better chronologically than the worst.
