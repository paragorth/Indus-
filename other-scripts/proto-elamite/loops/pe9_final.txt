pe9 FINAL -- "the names were drawn from a bag": were Proto-Elamite name strings built from a finite set of tokens,
stamps or counters on the scribe's desk?
Tools: tools/pe9_build.py, pe9_common.py (one sequential predictive used both to simulate and to score:
LM, POLYA tablet topic, BAG_WR, BAG_WOR with K types x c copies and refill, STAMP; use-profile model; numba
kernels), pe9_cycle1.py, pe9_cycle1b.py, pe9_cycle2.py (reference tables), pe9_cycle2_abc.py, pe9_cycle3.py.
Data: data/pe9_corpora.json; data/pe9_cycle{1,1b,2,3}.json and logs; data/pe9_ckpt/ (1,000,000 PE + 100,000 Ur III
+ 100,000 Linear B simulated corpora). Web search (4 Oct 2026): no prior urn/bag or ABC model of PE names found.
Controls: middles shuffled across tablets; planted LM, POLYA and depleting-bag corpora; Ur III admin names and
Linear B personnel names per tablet; 300 leave-one-out pseudo-observed corpora; 52 reserved tablets (20%) for
pre-registered predictions.

Answers to the questions asked:
- Which model wins by held-out likelihood: the tablet-topic (Polya cache) model, ahead of stamps and an
  inexhaustible bag; the depleting counter bag ties with the plain language model (gains nothing). Grade A for
  "a tablet effect exists" (restates pe7 at 0.4 bits/sign, now out of sample); grade B for "inexhaustible, not depleting".
- Bag size and refill rule, if one insists on a bag: refilled every tablet (not per session of 2-8 tablets),
  about 10-50 sign types, supplying about 40% of a tablet's name signs, each type with >= 3 copies (1-2 copies
  excluded: posterior 0.00 / 0.01, and reserved re-use rate outside the 2-copy band). Grade C (the ABC cannot
  separate BAG_WR from BAG_WOR well at high eps: LOO 18/63).
- Depleted vs inexhaustible signs: no depleted sign (0 of 90; upper bound < 3/90). Inexhaustible (tablet-sticky)
  grade B for M370, M376, M124, M388, M001, M054 (also found by pe7); grade C for M145, M203, M136, M210,
  |M036+1(N30D)|, M002, M109, M217 (would support: z >= 2 again in a new tablet sample; would kill: excess at
  the shuffle level in the reserved or non-Susa tablets; P3 did not replicate them at 52 tablets).
- Testable prediction for new tablets (passed on the 52 reserved): a sign already used in one name on a tablet
  is 2-3x as likely as baseline to appear in another name on the same tablet, and its third use is still
  boosted (no drop below baseline). Re-use rate per sign in new multi-name tablets about 0.12-0.22; under
  a 1-2-copy token bag it would be <= 0.14. For further tablets (e.g. newly published Susa or Malyan texts):
  POLYA should beat LM by about 0.3-0.5 bits/sign.
- What does NOT fit any model: PE has more one-off signs than any frequency-weighted generator produces
  (hapax share 0.35 vs 0.22-0.29 in the accepted simulations; Ur III shows the same misfit in smaller form).

Caveats: 95% Susa; names = pe4 middles (some may be descriptors); string lengths are conditioned on, not
modelled; reserved set only 52 tablets; the Linear B control failed (real names on themed tablets also share
signs), so the result says HOW names share signs on a tablet, not WHETHER they are spoken names.
Plain answer: the bag idea in its strong form (finite counters or stamps, drawn and used up) is killed: signs
are never used up on a tablet; they become more likely with each use. What survives is a per-tablet naming pool
that is reused freely: a topic, a scribe's habit, or a group's shared name element.
