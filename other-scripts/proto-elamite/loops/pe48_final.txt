pe48 SIGNS AS GOODS IN A TRADE NETWORK -- final (5 Oct 2026)
Idea: every sign is a commodity on the Iranian-plateau trade network: TRAVELLING (same rate at every site) or HOMED at a
site with a gravity / diffusion fall-off (distance, route graph, site mass). Thousands of random parameter sets, 5-fold
site-stratified cross-validation scored on held-out outpost tablets, leave-one-site-out diffusion forecasts.
Nulls: site labels shuffled; signs resampled by frequency; outpost labels permuted among plateau tablets.
Controls: Ur III provinces (Umma, Girsu, Drehem, Ur, Nippur, Garsana, Irisagrig) and Linear B (KN, PY, TH, MY, TI, KH),
drawn at PE shape (hub 1,502 tablets, outposts 27/22/12/11/1/1); planted local and route signs.
Prior work checked: pe17 (plateau fingerprint by sign choice), pe22 (no site-endemic sign), web (Damerow & Englund's Yahya
sign list comparison, no trade/gravity model). Not duplicated: pe39, pe45, pe47.
Tools: tools/pe48_lib.py, pe48_cycle1.py, pe48_cycle2.py, pe48_cycle3.py, pe48_cycle3b.py. Checkpoints data/pe48_ckpt/.
Frozen: data/pe48_frozen_forecasts.json (sha256 prefix 1946e391ffe27be8).

Verdict: the trade-network frame gives one robust fact (the plateau sign mix is not Susa's, A) and one useful inversion
from the controls (outpost-specific signs are what an office handled, not local names). Nothing is read; nothing is cracked.
