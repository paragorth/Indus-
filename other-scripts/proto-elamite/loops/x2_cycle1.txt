Cycle-1 verdict: the decisive control fails for the requested learned alignments (Procrustes-seeded, adversarial-style random restarts, Gromov-Wasserstein). Only direct numeric-profile matching recovers real Linear B <-> Ur III equivalences above chance, and only at the level 'flour/grain-like measured good' and 'herd animal'; total words align. Cycle 2 adds structure-aware alignment (similarity flooding over tablet co-occurrence; joint SVD of profiles) with bootstrap stability, calibrates it on LB <-> Ur III at LA x PE size, and only then reads LA <-> PE.
