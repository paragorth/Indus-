pe41 cycle 1 -- LET MACHINES REINVENT WRITING, THEN COMPARE (tools/pe41_lib.py, pe41_run.py, pe41_analyze.py, pe41_diff.py;
numbers data/pe41_c1.json, data/pe41_c1_diff.json; runs in data/pe41_ckpt/c1.jsonl.gz, git-ignored).
Simulator: random toy economy (1-25 offices, 0-12 places, grain/animal/product/worker goods with capacity or counted units,
qualifiers, 10-1500 persons, 5 tablet types ration/delivery/labor/inventory/debt, purity, totals) + a population that must
let a reader rebuild each transaction: lexicon from a Lewis signalling game (Roth-Erev, sign budget K 20-600), name length from a
cost/collision trade-off, what-to-write-where from cultural hill-climbing on reader reward minus sign cost, slot order convention,
variant innovation. Each invented sign has a known class (OFFICE PLACE ACTION COMMODITY QUALIFIER NAME TOTAL).
Alignment: 16 per-sign behaviour features (frequency, header/total/entry-initial/final/solo share, capacity/fraction numeral share,
dispersion, line position, partner diversity, line length, 4 co-occurrence-neighbour averages), rank-normalised per corpus;
Hungarian assignment refined twice by co-occurrence-graph consistency. 964 populations (of 1000 seeds), each aligned to PE,
3 token-shuffled PE, PE tablet halves A/B, proto-cuneiform (PC, 5,257 tablets) and 12 held-out invented systems.
