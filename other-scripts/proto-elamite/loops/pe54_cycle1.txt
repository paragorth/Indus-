pe54 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): FOLLOW ONE FLOCK THROUGH THE ARCHIVE -- calibration and first PE run.
Scripts tools/pe54_lib.py, pe54_ur3build.py, pe54_cycle1.py; checkpoints data/pe54_ckpt/ (git-ignored: c1_plant_zm.json, c1_ur3_zm.json, c1_pe_zc.json, c1_pe_zm.json).
Prior art (web search 5 Oct 2026): nothing links PE (or Ur III) herd entries into flock histories by composition; pe5 linked herds by NAME (|M362+X|) across MDP 17,096+/17,085/17,097; pe20/pe37/pe53 used counts within one record. Not a duplicate of pe51 (deletion game) or pe52 (sign weights).
Engine: sighting = counts in the 8 herd classes (pe20 extraction; PE 45 records on 23 tablets, copy P008389 dropped unless stated). Linking hypothesis = random role per class (X, or F/M/Y of species 0/1: 7^8 = 5.8 M possible) + random annual rates from open husbandry ranges: young per female 0.5-1.2, first-year survival 0.5-0.8 (lamb/kid mortality 44-48% in traditional flocks, CGSpace 66812), adult survival 0.75-0.92 (adult mortality 21-23%, same), female offtake 0-0.15, other-adult offtake 0.1-0.6 (offtake 20-24%, NW Cameroon pastoral systems), recruitment to females 0.4-0.6; gap 0-3 years marginalised. Pair score = log P(later sighting | earlier one moved forward) - log P(it | a random other entry). Links = max-weight matching (one successor/predecessor, never same tablet). zc = unwritten class counts as 0; zm = unwritten/0 cells are unobserved.
Ur III control: 119 Girsu/Umma flock blocks (first class block of tablets with u8/ud5 and >= 3 classes; owner = nig2-ka9-ak PN / PN sipa / standalone name line; CDLI ATF + catalogue years), 103 owner strings, 16 owner pairs (32 sightings), 9 pairs in different years.
