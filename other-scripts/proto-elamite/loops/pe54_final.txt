Plain answer: the flocks cannot be followed. Even in the Ur III books, where we know the herdsman, his flock's mix of ewes, rams and lambs changes too much between accounts to recognise it, and the direction of time cannot be read from births and deaths. In PE, the herd entries are no more linkable than shuffled ones. The only trace is that two or three named herds have similar numbers on two tablets, as if copied from the same count. Not cracked; nothing read.
