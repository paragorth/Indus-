Verdict: NOT CRACKED, and further from it than the word 'reading' suggests. What exists is a working accounting skeleton (grade B): unit ladder, sums, tablet-level system, a standard per-head allotment, measured vs counted classes. It predicts held-out numerals and totals and survives shuffled-role and random-unit controls, but its semantic layer (what M297, M288, M388 ARE) is not measurable: the proto-cuneiform calibration shows that knowing the true commodities gains ~0.001 bits per entry and 0 decoded tablets with this method. A crack needs an outside anchor: the frozen predictions (vessel litres, new-tablet rates, Tehran tablets) are the only route left that this pipeline can offer. Nothing is read; no name, commodity or word is identified.
