pe13 cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): the shape of the scribe's memory. Tools: tools/pe13_build.py, pe13_common.py, pe13_cycle1.py, pe13_sum.py. Checkpoints: data/pe13_ckpt/c1/ (git-ignored).
Model: each sign in line j predicted by (1-lam) x unigram + lam x a cache of earlier lines weighted by a kernel K(d) (d = distance in lines), with floor w, refractory factor rho at d=1, column factor g1 and other-surface factor g2. 1,201 random kernels (flat, exponential, power-law, step; with or without floor, dip, resets; two clocks) per corpus, lam fitted on 4/5 of tablets and scored on the held-out 1/5 (5 folds); plus maximum-likelihood fits of the interpretable kernel and of a free step kernel. Gains in millibits per sign over the flat (topic) kernel.
