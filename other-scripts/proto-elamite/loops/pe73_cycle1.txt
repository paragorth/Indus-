pe73 cycle 1: DO THE B RESULTS REST ON TRANSLITERATION ERRORS? Photo and line-art check of the key tablets (6 Oct 2026).
Scripts: tools/pe73_select.py (claims + blind sets), pe73_sheet.py (planted-error sheets), pe73_crop.py (viewing crops), pe73_score.py. Checkpoints and all verdicts with notes: data/pe73_ckpt/ (claims.json, vnum_*, knum_*, ksign_answers.txt; git-ignored). CDLI photos (cdli.earth/dl/photo/<P>.jpg) and line art (cdli.earth/dl/lineart/<P>_l.jpg) for 107 tablets stay in the scratchpad.
Design: every numeral reading to be checked is shown either as transliterated or with ONE planted change (count +-1, unit swapped, unit added), plant key hidden until scoring; the reader marks AGREE / DISAGREE / UNREADABLE separately for the photo and for the line art (Scheil's copies for MDP; the copy is a second witness, not the clay). The pe60 photo reader was not used for line values (validated at 12% exact); it is reliable only for system/header/reverse-total presence, which no claim here needed beyond what was read by eye.
