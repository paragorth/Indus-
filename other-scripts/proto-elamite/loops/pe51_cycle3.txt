signs, every pair sharing held-out tablets is erased singly and together. Interaction
I = D_ab - D_a - D_b per task (own slots of a and b never scored). Null: a erased with as many random
other tokens as b has (4 draws); excess = I - mean(I_null). JOINT pair = excess >= 0.01 nats,
z > 3, sign agreeing in all but one model, >= 4 models. Pseudo-pairs (a + random tokens scored as a
pair) give the false-call count under the same rule.
