pe55 cycle 1: THE NUMBERS MUST OBEY AGRONOMY AND PHYSIOLOGY, calibration (5 Oct 2026)
Scripts tools/pe55_lib.py, tools/pe55_cycle1.py; checkpoints data/pe55_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Prior art: web search (5 Oct 2026) found no joint physical-window test of PE count/capacity pairs over
several activities. pe16 used human ration needs only (one ruler, latent period); pe27/pe28 scanned exact
rates without physics. This loop adds fodder (small and large stock), sowing and yield per area, random
unit sizes and per-sign activity labels, scored by held-out tablets.
Physical windows (litres of hulled barley per unit per issue; 0.6-0.7 kg/l, ~3.5 kcal/g):
  person-day 1.2 l (0.5-2.5; FAO/WHO/UNU 2004 energy 2,000-3,200 kcal/d); small stock-day 0.8 l (0.3-2.1;
  barley supplements 150-750 g/head/day, CSIRO Anim. Prod. Sci. EA9930403, EA9890029; grain fattening to
  ~1.3 kg/d); large stock-day 4.5 l (1.7-12; working oxen 10 g/kg body weight concentrate, Edinburgh
  draught-ox theses, DFID R5198 reports); each x30 (month) and x360 (year); sowing 120 l/ha (45-280;
  30-180 kg/ha traditional, 120-200 kg/ha drilled rainfed trials in Iran, FAO AGRIS 1977-78 season);
  yield 1,500 l/ha (450-4,500; Iran rainfed mean 2.1 t/ha). Beer is a grain-equivalent of person-day
  rations here; wool has no capacity pairing (PE has no weight system).
Data: a pair = a count line and a capacity line on neighbouring lines. PE 390 pairs on 221 tablets
  (a-priori capacity values, N39C = 1). Ur III 2,266 pairs (pe27 extraction), nouns made opaque (U###),
  sila hidden as 'base unit' (truth about 1 l). Ur III field texts: 188 tablets, 257 area/capacity pairs.
Hypothesis: u (l per base capacity unit, prior 0.01-100), A (ha per area count unit, 0.01-10), one
  activity label per key sign. Score: each pair is either the activity or junk (robust mixture, junk =
  log-normal on the key's own median, so junk is unit-free). 3,000-6,000 random (u, A) draws; labels both
  random (pure guessing) and best-per-key. Held out: fit on half the tablets, score the other half.
  Nulls: capacity values shuffled across lines (whole pipeline re-run), random units with frozen labels,
  labels permuted among signs.
