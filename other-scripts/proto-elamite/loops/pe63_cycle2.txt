Scripts: tools/pe63_c2.py (alignment, slot roles, planted roles), tools/pe63_report2.py (Ur III truth check, PE slot
table), tools/pe63_c2b.py (coupled slots: one quantity moving with another at a fixed rate). Checkpoints
data/pe63_ckpt/c2_res.json, c2_truth.json, c2b_res.json.
Slot roles (per reference line, over all member pairs): CONST (nothing changes), QTY (signs fixed, number changes),
ID (sign swaps with number and system unchanged in >= 60% of swapping pairs), COM (sign swaps with a system change or
a >= x3 scale change in >= 50%), STEP (sign variant fixes the quantity; >= 3 variants on a constant ratio or
difference), MIX (rest); TOTAL (value = sum of the other same-system slots).
