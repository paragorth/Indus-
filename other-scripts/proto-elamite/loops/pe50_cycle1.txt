pe50 cycle 1: count the invisible (capture-recapture with tablets as capture events). Scripts tools/pe50_ur3.py, pe50_pe.py, pe50_lib.py, pe50_cycle1.py, pe50_sum1.py; output data/pe50_ckpt/cycle1.json, sum1.txt (git-ignored).
Entities ("marked animals"): PE entry middles >= 2 signs (MID2; also MID3, FULL2, VAR2 with variants, DIRTY2 with damaged lines, NSIGN = middles with a pe6 name-spelling sign), header strings (HDR), distinctive number+string entries (DENT). Ur III Drehem (15,648 admin tablets) and Umma (28,409): officials by fixed frames (OFF: ki X-ta, giri3 X, X i3-dab5, kiszib3 X ...) and entry strings (ENT2). Estimators: Chao2, iChao2, jackknife 1/2, Chapman (random halves), three-list log-linear (AIC over independence / 2-way / Darroch Mh; random thirds or excavation-volume thirds), binomial-thinning mixture for the survival rate q (tablets written = n / q).
