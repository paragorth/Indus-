Plain answer: the totals do not leave anything out by rule. The search that finds the Ur III credit-section exclusion and every planted rule finds nothing in Proto-Elamite. The best rule closes one extra tablet and breaks another, and it does worse on held-out tablets than random totals, random labels or shuffled entries. When a total fails, the missing amount is about one entry, and which entry it is looks random. These are slips (a missed line, a misread numeral, a lost line), not an overseer's share, a previous balance or a second category. Nothing is read; not cracked.
