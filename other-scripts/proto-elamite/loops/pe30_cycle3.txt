pe30 cycle 3 -- which entries do the failing totals leave out? (roles, carry-over, lost entries)
Tools: tools/pe30_cycle3.py, pe30_cycle3b.py. Numbers: data/pe30_cycle3.json, data/pe30_cycle3b.json.
58 of the 91 tier 1+2 tablets do not close with all entries. For each, the single-feature rules EX(f) that close it name the candidate left-out entries. Null for "which entry": features permuted among the entries WITHIN each tablet (values fixed, so the size of the miss is kept and only the identity of the left-out entry is random).
