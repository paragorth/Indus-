Arrow in the dark: the multi-sign strings that open PE entries are administrative codes (office + unit + serial, like
account numbers or postcodes), not personal names. Assumes the opposite of pe45. Prior art: pe4 tested factorial
item descriptions by MDL and in-order slot exclusivity on the same strings, without external variables, tablet
context, serial signatures or prediction; no published slot-code search on PE was found.

Search: ~150,000 random slot schemas in all (positions cut by fixed / from-end / sign-set / run rules, random slot
alphabets) plus 32,000 random unordered field partitions, always fitted on half the tablets and re-scored on the
held-out half (two splits). External variables: tablet id, P-number batch, header sign, publication volume, class
sign, number system, quantity, line order, previous entry. Nulls: signs shuffled within strings, strings shuffled
across tablets, Markov resynthesis, signs shuffled across strings. Controls at PE size: planted 3-slot code on the
real PE tablet frame (recovered: slot NMI 1.00 / 0.66-0.75 / 1.00, office z +45, serial z -6); HTS tariff numbers in
opaque symbols (first group recovered, NMI 0.88-1.00); Ur III Drehem livestock attributes (above null); Ur III
names and Linear B names (slightly above null, no office/serial pair).
