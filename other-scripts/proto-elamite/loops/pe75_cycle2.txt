pe75 cycle 2: DOES THE CLAY KNOW THE ACCOUNT BEFORE IT IS WRITTEN? (7 Oct 2026)
The brief's question, asked through physical variables pe21 did not use: thickness, aspect (h/w), thickness for its area, and slack (area left over for the amount of text = planned-but-unused room). Baseline = finished text amount (log lines, log glyphs, obverse and reverse lines: the line-count baseline, trivially known after writing) + start-of-text features (header present, top-8 header signs, first numeral system). A physical feature counts only if it adds held-out information beyond that baseline.
Scripts: tools/pe75_cycle2.py (random guessing, numpy ridge-logistic), pe75_cycle2b.py (strict null), pe75_cycle2c.py (frozen held-out test), pe75_cycle2d.py (capacity-document control). 781 whole tablets for fitting; held out: 172 tablets with CDLI preservation '75%' (never used for fitting). Frozen: data/pe75_frozen_physical.json, sha256 32fcb52105776d443e9d3b900c6eee25c539fe4e38f0e5daab097bf01fbe461b (written and hashed before held-out scoring).
