pe60 cycle 2: READ THE UNSEEN PHOTOS OURSELVES, AFTER A BLIND CALIBRATION (6 Oct 2026).
Scripts: tools/pe60_crop.py (crop/contrast helper), tools/pe60_score_read.py (line-level scoring of a blind photo transliteration vs CDLI ATF), tools/pe60_score_tab.py (tablet-level features vs ATF, majority and permutation controls). Face-splitting and contact sheets were scratchpad helpers (photos are CDLI images and stay in the scratchpad).
Blind readings frozen before scoring: data/pe60_ckpt/blind1_mine.atf (sha256 dd0c4fc7...2d3f), data/pe60_ckpt/blind2_mine.tsv (sha256 99b51876...a5d); new-tablet readings data/pe60_ckpt/photo_reads_new.tsv (sha256 42bd0aaf...1694), all git-ignored.
Procedure: CDLI fat-cross photo split into faces, contrast-stretched, read by eye. Calibration tablets drawn at random (fixed seeds) from Susa tablets that have ATF and a photo; the ATF was not opened before the reading was frozen. Only a few line drawings of other tablets (sign-reference sheets for M288, M297, M157, M376, M056, M044, M219, M136 and the pe52 signs) were looked at with their ATF before calibration; none of those tablets was in either blind set.
