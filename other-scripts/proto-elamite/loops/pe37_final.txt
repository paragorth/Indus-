Plain answer: the bones give a clear ruler (a goat-led Banesh economy, heavy culling of 1-3-year-olds, living herd about 29% young), but the ruler cannot name the PE herd signs. Matching summed counts to three proportions leaves tens of thousands of equally good assignments, planted truth is not recovered, random invented herds fit PE as well as the real bones, and on held-out tablets the bone profile does worse than random profiles. In Ur III, with contemporary Malyan bones, the method finds the goat categories but not the sheep categories, because scribes counted rams and wethers and weaned lambs, not the biological herd. So a bone mismatch cannot kill a PE reading either. Assignment that "fits": none supported (best posterior 0.05). Not cracked; nothing read.
