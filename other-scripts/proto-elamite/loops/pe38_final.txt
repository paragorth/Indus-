pe38 'Type inference for Proto-Elamite signs' - final (5 Oct 2026). Tools: tools/pe38_core.c, pe38_common.py, pe38_c1.py,
pe38_c2.py, pe38_c3.py, pe38_report.py. Cycles: loops/pe38_cycle1-3.txt. Checkpoints: data/pe38_ckpt/ (git-ignored; the Ur III
corpus is built from the CDLI dump in the scratchpad).
Idea: give every sign type one role (name, profession/class, commodity, animal, container/measure, header/office, transaction
verb, total marker, qualifier) and require every tablet to type-check under accounting rules (entries = countable head + number;
totals carry a total sign or the head role of the entry they close; headers name an office, person or transaction). Search all
role assignments at once with tempered Gibbs chains; find roles forced in nearly all good solutions; test against nulls, planted
roles, proto-cuneiform and Ur III with known roles at PE size, and held-out tablets.
Prior art checked: pe6 (PE-4) assigned class/name roles by an ABC economy (no opener/verb/total roles); Born et al. 2019/2022
cluster signs and segment document structure. Neither searches role assignments under accounting type-checking with controls.
