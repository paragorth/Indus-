# Scripts: tools/pe27_common.py, pe27_cycle1-7.py. Checkpoints: data/pe27_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Rows: loops/pe27_cycle1-7.txt.
# Idea: a tablet with two number systems may write one quantity twice (a count and its grain in capacity units).
# Such 'bilingual numbers' give exchange or ration rates. Exact-ratio scans over every count/capacity pair,
# tablet, sign pair and line window; nulls re-deal values among tablets or among lines of the same sign;
# search-corrected; Ur III and planted controls; held-out test.
| PE-27.F1 | Controls | Ur III: the workers -> grain rate 60 sila (1 barig) is recovered by the tablet scan (p 0.005), split halves (83%), sign-tied scan (gurusz -> sze-bi, p 0.005), computed-line scan ('sze-bi' = 1x and 3/2x the counts) and sign-pair scan (p 0.01). Planted PE rates on >= 5% of tablets are recovered (p 0.01-0.03); a planted rate on 2.7% of tablets is not found by the windowed computed-line scan | PASS; power limited to rates that recur on several tablets with varying counts |

Bottom line: the bilingual numbers are local, not corpus-wide. Two sign families convert counts into capacity at fixed per-head rates: M288 = 1/2 N01 (or 1 N01) per counted person-unit, held out p 1e-5; M106 = 1 per head of the herd, or 1 N30C. Together they fix the capacity ratios N30C:N24:N39B:N01 = 1:3:6:30. Not cracked (no sound value, no prediction yet checked outside the corpus).
