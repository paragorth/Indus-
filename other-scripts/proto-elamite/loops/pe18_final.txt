pe18 FINAL (4 Oct 2026) -- "the number system is an accent"
Idea: if the number system follows the tablet (pe6), maybe the choice of system marks where the scribe or counterparty came from (lowland Susa vs plateau/pastoral partners).
Tools: tools/pe18_common.py, pe18_cycle1..5.py. Rows: loops/pe18_cycle1..5.txt. Numbers: data/pe18_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Corpus: 1,502 Susa and 74 plateau tablets (Yahya 27, Malyan 22, Sialk 12, Sofalin 11, Ozbaki, Shahr-i Sokhta); plateau text = only 348 sign tokens. Systems classified from numeral codes only (C, C@, B, S@, N23, FRAC, DEC = >= 6 N14 or N45, SEX = N34/N48, AMB). Seal flags from the ATF (178 tablets).

Grades
- B (negative): decimal (and sexagesimal) is not a highland number system; Susa decimal tablets show no plateau register (an effect of >= 20% plateau-token substitution is excluded); nothing about the writer (variant forms, headers, vocabulary, seals) predicts the base beyond goods and layout.
- B (negative): sealed tablets are not dispatch documents marked by number system; sealing follows genre (not capacity) and header, and leans AWAY from plateau-likeness.
- C: the capacity-variant system (C@) is the only system with a plateau lean (1.9x rate on the plateau, Yahya 4/27; Susa C@ tablets shaped like plateau tablets). Would support: C@ excess in newly published Yahya/Malyan tablets or plateau clay (hXRF) among Susa C@ tablets; would kill: C@ at the Susa rate in new plateau finds.
- C-minus (demoted): B / N23 tablets written in a plateau register. Goods-matched it is mostly M346/M367, i.e. the same counted thing.
- C: the goods-to-system mapping differs on the plateau (Susa-trained models predict plateau systems worse than the prior).

Plain answer: the number system is not an accent. No system is commoner on the plateau, Susa decimal tablets do not look like plateau writing, and sealed tablets are not a dispatch class tied to a system. What predicts the system beyond the goods is the tablet's layout: decimal lists have longer entries and fewer totals. The only weak highland lean is the capacity-variant system (C@). So the tablets were written in Susa's own registers for Susa's own book-keeping, not in the counterparty's numbers. Limits: 348 plateau tokens make the register test detect only shifts of about 20% or more; corpus is a sample (absences are upper bounds).
