pe70 cycle 2: does one seal mark one party's dossier, with its own vocabulary?
(tools/pe70_c2.py, pe70_c2b.py; data/pe70_ckpt/c2.json, c2b.json, c2_ctrl.json)
Groups: tablets impressed with the same seal id. PE: 12 recurring seals on 42 tablets after merging id pairs that name the
same tablet set (PES0222 = PES0930). Statistics on content tokens only: COH = mean idf-weighted Jaccard of same-seal pairs;
OWN = (seal, sign) pairs with the sign on >= 2 of the seal's tablets and hypergeometric p < 1e-3.
Nulls: seal ids shuffled among the tablets that carry a seal id (group sizes kept), freely and within volume.
