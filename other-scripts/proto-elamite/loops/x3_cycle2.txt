# X-3 cycle 2: low-data regime (500-token Y slices) with an explicit null for the identity measure
Same tools and model as cycle 1 (tools/x3_run.py with X3_REPS=3, X3_CONDS=real,relab,relab2,band). Pretraining seeds 0-2 x 3 fine-tune replicates (different 500-token slices and inits) = 9 paired runs per cell; 177 jobs, ~2,400 fine-tunes, ~2.2 h on 2 CPU workers. Targets: LA, PE, VOY from all 10 corpora, plus controls LBa>LBb, PLa>PLb, PLa>PLc, LB<>GRC, UR3<>AKK, LAT>AKK, LA>LB, LB>LA, LA>GRC, PE<>PC. New: relab2 = a second, independent relabelling of X; IDnull = relab2 - relab, the distribution of the ID measure when no identity can differ. Raw: $X3_SCR/res_c2.jsonl.
