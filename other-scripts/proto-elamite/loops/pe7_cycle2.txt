PE7 cycle 2: do tree clades map onto tablets, headers, offices or the herd office?
Tools: tools/pe7_cycle2.py, pe7_sum2.py. Data: data/pe7_cycle2.json, .log, pe7_cycle2_herd.log, checkpoints data/pe7_ckpt/c2_*.
Per replicate (8): 200 distinct names, edit-model distances, best Fitch tree (NJ + 5 random restarts, SPR), MCMC pair co-membership (clades <= 8 leaves).
PS = Fitch score of the label on the best tree vs 499 leaf permutations (ratio = null/obs, > 1 = clustered). dist = mean edit distance (bits) of label-sharing pairs minus other pairs vs 999 permutations (z > 0 = closer). co = same with posterior co-membership.
Header and volume are tablet properties: in dist/co they are permuted at tablet level with same-tablet pairs excluded; their PS is permuted at name level and so inherits the tablet effect.

Cycle verdict: clades do not follow offices, headers or excavation volumes; they DO follow tablets. Whether this is a household/lineage signal or tablet-level formula sharing is the question for cycle 3 (which signs carry it, and is it positional like a surname).
