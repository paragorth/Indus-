# PE-39 final: machine translation with no dictionary, numbers as the only bridge (Proto-Elamite <-> proto-cuneiform)
| PE-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| PE-39.final | Three cycles (tools/pe39_common.py, pe39_run.py, pe39_profile.py, pe39_sum.py, pe39_calib.py, pe39_calib2.py, pe39_hubnull.py; 44 trained translation systems; checkpoints in data/pe39_ckpt/, git-ignored). PE lines (6,531) and proto-cuneiform admin lines (16,079) as two languages; only numeral tokens shared (code x count, 'N14:2'). Shared-encoder seq2seq with denoising autoencoding and online iterative back-translation; architectures: plain GRU (cycle 1), GRU whose embeddings are built from each sign's numeral profile (cycles 2-3), small transformer. Lexicon = target sign most raised by the source sign in translation; held-out gain = round-trip NLL rise when the target is banned, vs frequency-matched random bans. Controls: proto-cuneiform half renamed (truth = identity), Ur III <-> proto-cuneiform (37-word gold frozen first), numbers shuffled on both control and PE (per seed and two FIXED shuffles x 6 seeds), no-learning numeral-profile match, hub-preserving null | Known-answer pair: plain GRU 2% of frequent signs right, numeral-embedded 3-10%, transformer 1%, against 10-16% for simply matching numeral profiles; translations are sharp (P(t|s) ~0.7-0.99) and mostly wrong. Held-out back-translation gain is as large for wrong pairs as for right ones (+0.60 vs +0.18 nats). Seed agreement does filter: >= 4 of 6 seeds agree on 3 signs, all 3 correct (>= 3: 5/9). Ur III pair: stable pairs are all hubs, 0 of 2 gold-scorable correct. Real PE: 4 pairs at >= 4/6 (M388 -> EN, M136 -> SZE, M111 -> SZE, M147 -> U4) vs 2 and 0 under the two fixed numeral shuffles; 18 vs 8 / 7 at >= 3/6; M388 -> EN also appears in a null | **Not cracked; nothing read.** Unsupervised translation with numbers as the only shared vocabulary does worse than counting numerals, even when both sides are the same script. Real numbers produce about twice as many seed-stable PE -> PC translations as shuffled numbers, so a weak number-carried signal exists, but at most a handful of pairs and about half are expected by chance |

Grades:
- A (method limit): back-translation invents a confident private code. On the known-answer pair it recovers 1-10% of frequent signs, below a no-learning numeral-profile match (10-16%). Held-out round-trip gain does not separate right from wrong pairs. The Ur III <-> proto-cuneiform pair fails outright with a system + magnitude bridge.
- B: seed agreement is a precision filter when the bridge is strong (control: >= 4/6 seeds 3/3 correct, >= 3/6 5/9), but it reaches only 2-5% of signs.
- B (negative): real PE gives about 2x the stable translations of fixed numeral shuffles (>= 3/6: 18 vs 8 / 7; >= 4/6: 4 vs 2 / 0). Roughly half of any stable PE pair list is null-level; no individual pair passes.
- C (class level, restatement): M354 -> GAR (3/6 seeds, held-out gain +0.67). Both are written partly in the bisexagesimal N51 notation, so this repeats the known B-system ration class. Would kill: the pair vanishes when N51 lines are removed.
- C-: M136 -> SZE and M111 -> SZE (4/6 seeds each; average PE numeral profiles; neither in the two nulls). Would support: they stay stable in 10+ more seeds and in a PE half held out by tablet, and their lines co-occur with the capacity-class signs more than chance. Would kill: SZE remains a hub target that collects other signs in new nulls (it took 3-4 signs in the nulls), or the pairs drop below 3/6 in new seeds.
- C-: M147 -> U4 (4/6, only 20 occurrences). Would kill: below 3/6 in new seeds.
- Rejected: M388 -> EN (frequency hub; also 3/6 in a numeral-shuffled null); M124 / M295 / M318 -> AN, M059 / M075 -> BA, M112 / M379 -> HI@g (hub targets); M036 -> HI@g (cycle 2, 2/2 seeds, not stable in 6).

Rules kept: no git commit; at most 2 CPU workers (the host was shared, load ~25 on 4 cores, so 600-2,000-step runs took 8-40 min); checkpoints JSON only in data/pe39_ckpt/ (git-ignored, no .npz). Web check: no published PE <-> proto-cuneiform unsupervised translation (Born et al. used sequence models within PE only); X-2 (embedding alignment LA <-> PE) is the closest earlier loop and also failed its control.
