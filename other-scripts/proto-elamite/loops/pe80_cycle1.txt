STEP 2. Candidates outside every family (quick web search on each, 7 Oct 2026)
C1 NUMBERS THAT ARE NOT AMOUNTS. Assume some numerals are labels (day N, team N, plot N, serial N), not quantities. Labels betray themselves by ORDER: inside a tablet they climb by one (1,2,3...), or follow a fixed step or doubling, regardless of size. Thousands of random (scope filter x recurrence) hypotheses, within-scope order shuffle and rank-preserving cross-tablet resample nulls, train/held-out halves; calibration on Ur III (opaque words; truth = 'u4 N-kam' day labels) and a planted label run. Outside test if anything survives: a day label must stop at 30 (lunar month), a month label at 12-13. Web: numeral work on PE treats every numeral as a quantity (Born et al. 2023 disambiguation, arXiv 2502.00090); no label search found. Not F2: F2 tests sums and rates of amounts; this asks whether a number is an amount at all. PICKED FIRST: cheapest to kill, and a hit would be a new kind of meaning (time or serial).
C2 TAPHONOMY AS A LIBRARY CARD. Damage (lost lines, x, '#', broken edges) is written by the tablet's afterlife, not the scribe. If kept documents (stored in jars, consulted) break differently from discarded receipts, damage profile per tablet is an outside label of the document's life. Random text-feature classifiers predict damage beyond size/thickness/volume; held-out; null = damage re-dealt among same-size tablets of the same publication volume; calibration proto-cuneiform (Uruk rubbish fill) vs Ur III (market). Web: no study using breakage to type documents found.
C3 SHELF LIFE. Each commodity has a physical half-life (dairy days, beer days-weeks, fish, grain years, textiles, metal). Perishables are recorded as small, frequent, never-stocked flows; durables accrue stocks and carry-overs. Random assignments of class signs to half-life bins scored by a record-life simulator; calibration on Ur III opaque commodities with known perishability. Outside ruler: published spoilage half-lives. Web: nothing found.
C4 HOLES IN SIGN SPACE. Persistent homology of the sign co-occurrence complex: sets of signs that pairwise co-occur on tablets but never inside one entry are paradigms (mutually exclusive slot fillers). Random filtrations; line-shuffle null; Ur III / PC paradigms as calibration. Web: no TDA on any undeciphered script found. DROPPED: too close to F3 (slot grammar) and pe31 interchangeability.
C5 WHICH WAY DID THE ARITHMETIC RUN? Top-down allocation (round total split, remainder dumped on one entry) vs bottom-up accumulation (entries first, total = sum). Signature: position and roundness of the 'remainder' entry. DROPPED: overlaps pe23/pe44 (roundness by position; planned vs counted AUC 0.50 on Ur III).
