pe6 cycle 2 -- posterior role assignment of real signs, with shuffled, planted and half-corpus controls
Tool: tools/pe6_cycle2.py (+ pe6_pipe.py). Numbers: data/pe6_cycle2.json, log data/pe6_cycle2.log.
Pipeline per arm: ABC fit on the arm's corpus (20,000-run bank, best 1%, regression) -> 200 posterior corpora ->
per-sign features (frequency, header / header-first share, entry-initial / final / middle / single share, total-line
share, number-system profile, dispersion over tablets, neighbour variety, tablet purity) -> 5 random forests
trained on disjoint groups of simulations -> mean role probability for every real sign with >= 15 tokens (164 signs).
Roles: OPEN (opener), PRE (prefix/qualifier), CLS_S / CLS_C / CLS_F / CLS_O (class sign of a counted / capacity /
fraction / other-system good), SYL (name-spelling sign).
