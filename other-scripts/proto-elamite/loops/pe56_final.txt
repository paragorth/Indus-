Cycle 2: residues from half A tested on unseen half B against fresh forgers. Cycle 3 (new): each residue dissected by oracle forgers given one kind of knowledge (own-entry permutation, numeral permutation, header-keyed pools, topic pools). Nulls: forger worlds W1, W2; shuffled PE SH1, SH2.
