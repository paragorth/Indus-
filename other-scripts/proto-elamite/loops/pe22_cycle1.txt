# PE-22 cycle 1: census of the unseen (4 Oct 2026; tools/pe22_cycle1.py, data/pe22_ckpt/cycle1.json)

Signs are species, tablets are sampling units, sites are habitats. 1,581 tablets (4 Larsa seals dropped), 15,245 sign tokens. Estimators reused from la15 (Chao1, ACE, Chao2, jack2, coverage, Zipf-Mandelbrot ABC). Chao1 95% intervals are the Chao log-normal intervals (the tablet bootstrap of Chao1 is biased low: it is not used as an interval).
