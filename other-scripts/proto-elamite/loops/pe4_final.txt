PE4 final: are the varied Proto-Elamite entry middles item codes (factorial descriptions of the counted goods) rather than personal names?
Tools: tools/pe4_build_controls.py, pe4_common.py, pe4_cycle1.py ... pe4_cycle4.py. Data: data/pe4_controls.json (Ur III Drehem herd lines, Ur III textile lines, Drehem 'ki PN-ta' names, from the CDLI ATF dump), data/pe4_cycle{1,2_k3_N300,2_k4_N300,3,4}.json + .log. Logs: loops/pe4_cycle{1,2,3,4}.txt.
Calibration: positive = planted 5-attribute herd code and real Ur III livestock / textile descriptions (word and sign tokens); negative = Drehem personal names, Ur III seal names, Linear B personnel names. Nothing about PE readings is used.

| PE-1.F | (1) MDL: factorial slot x value code vs free-string name code, 110-string matched samples x 8 | strict factorial gain: item ledgers +0.81..+3.17 bits/string, names -0.02..+0.25, PE classes -0.03..+0.21; chunk factorial: PE -1.98..-2.37, names -1.45..-2.28, ledgers -1.91..+1.65 (sign-level herd missed) | PE middles compress like names, not like item codes, in every commodity class |
| PE-2.F | (2) Held-out slot exclusivity/order vs slot-label permutation; forbidden pairs; combinatorial filling | exclusivity lift: ledgers 2.0-6.2 (incl. sign-level), names 1.6-1.9, PE counted / class-sign middles 1.2-1.4, PE capacity middles 2.0; forbidden-pair excess lowest of all corpora in PE (-0.02..+0.09); filling ~1 for names and PE alike (not diagnostic) | factorial prediction fails for counted and class-sign middles, undecided for capacity middles; PE middle signs combine more freely than either codes or names |
| PE-3.F | (3) Outside check: do middle signs predict lot size within tablets? (within-tablet permutation) | Ur III attributes S/null 7-12 (54-59 tokens p < 0.01); names 2.25 (p 0.06); at PE size attribute codes are detected 70-95% of the time; PE multi-sign middles 1.06 (p 0.35, 1/62 tokens), counted 0.94 (p 0.60); PE signal exists only for one-sign entries and capacity-class signs (M354, M288, M243, M036: the known determinative layer) | middles carry no information about the quantity; the commodity signs do |

Plain statement by commodity class:
- Counted goods (SDB entries without class sign, and middles before a class sign such as M288, M263, M346): NOT item codes. They fail all three tests and look like names (or something even freer than names).
- Capacity goods (C entries): undecided. Their middles are the most slot-like PE strings (MDL +0.21, exclusivity lift 2.0) but no more so than Ur III seal names, and there are too few multi-sign capacity entries (73) for the quantity check.
- The attribute-like information in PE (scale, measured vs counted) sits in single signs and in the final class signs, not in the varied middles.
Hypothesis 'the names are item descriptions': REJECTED for counted classes, NOT SHOWN for capacity. Gloss changes: none (A 0, B 0, C 0).
Caveats: 95% Susa; Ur III ledgers are 1,000 years later and written in a glottographic script, so the positive controls are real codes but not proto-writing; the strict MDL is blind to multi-sign attribute values (cycle 2 exclusivity is not); 'no scale information' is an upper bound for 62 tokens.
Next: the PE-2.2 side result -- PE middle signs co-occur almost like shuffled tokens (fewest forbidden pairs of any corpus) -- deserves its own test against syllabic name lists at matched inventory size.
