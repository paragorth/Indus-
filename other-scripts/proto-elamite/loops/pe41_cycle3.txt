pe41 cycle 3 -- let a script we can read choose which machine societies to trust (tools/pe41_calib.py; numbers
data/pe41_c3_calib.json; uses the 974 cycle-2 populations). Each population is scored by balanced accuracy of its own
alignment labels on the pre-registered known proto-cuneiform signs (47 commodity, 6 title, 9 place, 5 action).
Cross-validation: 50 stratified half-splits of the known signs; choose the best q of populations on half 1, pool their votes,
score on half 2; nulls: the unselected pool and 20 random pools of the same size per split.
