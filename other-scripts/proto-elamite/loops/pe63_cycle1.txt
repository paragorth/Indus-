| PE-63.1a | Template search. A random template = 1-4 features drawn (rarity-weighted) from one random tablet: an exact line (sign set + number system), a sign, the header sign, the closing line, the line-type skeleton (header/system sequence), the line count, or a block of 6 consecutive CDLI numbers (two offsets). The group = every tablet with all the features (3-40 tablets). Score = HELD-OUT cohesion: mean pairwise tablet-calibrated z of IDF-weighted content similarity (line keys + signs) after removing everything the template itself defines. 1,000,000 templates per run. NULL: the same search on 3 line-shuffled corpora (every line moved to a random tablet slot of the same kind and position class; lengths and line frequencies kept). A group counts only if it beats the maximum of all 3 nulls (3 M templates) in its size class (3 / 4-5 / 6-9 / 10+). Dossiers = greedy merge of significant groups (a later group joins when >= 2 and >= 1/3 of its members are in one dossier; a new member must have mean pairwise z >= 3 to it; no chaining) | PE: 6,862 distinct groups; family-wise thresholds 9.1 / 6.2 / 5.6 / 4.0; 50 significant groups vs 1-5 in each null run scored against the other two nulls (1, 5, 1). | The real corpus has 10-50x more near-copy groups than line-shuffled corpora. |
| PE-63.1b | CONTROL planted dossiers: 5 seeds; a real tablet (5-12 lines) copied 6 times with one sign swapped and number fixed (slot A), one sign swapped with a new number system and scale (slot B), one sign swapped with a x2 quantity step (slot C); planted copies get no museum adjacency. 300,000 templates each, PE null thresholds | recovered 30 of 30 planted tablets (6/6 in every seed); extra members 2-5 per seed, always including the source tablet itself (a true near-copy) | PASS (grade A, method finds a planted dossier at PE size). |
