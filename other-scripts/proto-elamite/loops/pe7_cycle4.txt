Cycle verdict: the co-tablet resemblance is broad (many rare signs), not name variants, not adjacent blocks, and structurally like small partial groups. It is most economically a tablet-local sign stock (a scribe's, a unit's or a place's naming pool); kinship is not excluded but is not needed and was not seen in the one place it would show most (adjacent listing).
