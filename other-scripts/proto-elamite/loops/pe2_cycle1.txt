PE2 cycle 1: distributional bridge PE <-> proto-cuneiform (tools/pe2_build_pc.py, pe2_common.py, pe2_cycle1.py; data/pe2_pc_corpus.json, data/pe2_cycle1.json)
Data: PE = data/pe_corpus.json (1,585 tablets, 95% Susa). PC = CDLI ATF lang qpc, catalogue period Uruk IV/III, genre Administrative: 5,257 tablets, 33,625 lines (Uruk 3,819; Jemdet Nasr/uncertain 889; Umma 385). Built from the same CDLI dump (cdliatf_unblocked.atf, cdli_cat.csv).
Profile per base sign (>= 20 entry tokens; PE 138 signs, PC 256): position in entry (sole/initial/final/middle), numeral system of own entry (capacity C, modified capacity C*, bisexagesimal B, fractions F, plain SDB, other O; classed by notation codes only), tablet context (system mix of the other entries on the tablet), header rate, reverse rate, mean log quantity. Z-scored within each script, cosine similarity, Hungarian assignment of top-60 PE into top-200 PC.
Caveat on the classifier: PC capacity (SE system) entries written only with N01/N14/N34/N45 look 'plain'; PC sign SZE is only 16% C by notation. Systems are therefore not fully commensurable between the scripts.
