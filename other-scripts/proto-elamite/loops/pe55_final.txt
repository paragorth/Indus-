pe55 final: THE NUMBERS MUST OBEY AGRONOMY AND PHYSIOLOGY (5 Oct 2026)
Cycles: loops/pe55_cycle1.txt (calibration), pe55_cycle2.txt (PE fit, random rulers), pe55_cycle3.txt
(replication between halves). Scripts tools/pe55_lib.py, pe55_cycle1-3.py; checkpoints data/pe55_ckpt/.
Idea: a count written next to a capacity amount gives litres per head (or per hectare). If the pair is a
real activity, that must sit in the physical range of rations, fodder, sowing or yield (FAO/WHO energy
needs, sheep and ox feeding trials, Iranian barley seed-rate and yield trials; cited in pe55_lib.py).
Thousands of random (unit size, area unit, sign->activity) hypotheses, scored by how many pairs land in
range at once, tested on held-out tablets; nulls: numbers shuffled across lines, random units, labels
permuted, and 150 nonsense rulers.
