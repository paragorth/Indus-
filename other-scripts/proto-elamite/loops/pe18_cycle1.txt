pe18 cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): THE NUMBER SYSTEM IS AN ACCENT - do Susa tablets in a given number system look like plateau tablets?
Tools: tools/pe18_common.py, tools/pe18_cycle1.py. Numbers: data/pe18_ckpt/c1.json, c1.log (git-ignored).
Systems per numeric line (no readings): C capacity, C@ capacity variant (@b/@c/@g), B bisexagesimal (N51/N54/N46), S@ counting variant, N23, FRAC, DEC (>= 6 N14 or N45), SEX (N34/N48, not DEC), AMB (small counts, base undecidable). Plateau = Yahya 27, Malyan 22, Sialk 12, Sofalin 11, Ozbaki 1, Shahr-i Sokhta 1 (74 tablets, only 348 sign tokens). Susa = 1,502.
Plateau-likeness of a tablet (numerals excluded): mean per-token log-likelihood ratio plateau vs Susa (Susa model leave-one-out) for all signs, non-class signs, variant forms given base sign, header sign; Gaussian LLR on 9 format features; combined = mean of family z-scores.
