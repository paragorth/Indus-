# pe46 cycle 3: does a code system predict unseen codes? Ordered slots and unordered sign-set fields (5 Oct 2026)
Method a (tools/pe46_cycle3.py): 1,500 random schemas per corpus on fit tablets; for the 5 best, the slot marginals give an independence (code) model; its 200 most probable UNSEEN combinations are the predicted codes. Score: share of held-out tablets' new strings that are predicted codes (hit rate), two splits. A sign-bigram chain with the same budget (200 unseen strings) is a name-like rival.
Method b (tools/pe46_cycle3b.py): codes whose fields are sign ALPHABETS in any order. 4,000 random partitions of the 40 commonest signs into 2-6 fields (+300-step hill-climbing of the best 5) fitted on half the tablets; exclusivity = share of strings with at most one sign per field; top 20 re-scored on held-out tablets; null = same pipeline after signs are shuffled ACROSS strings (lengths and frequencies kept); lift = real / null.
