pe29 FINAL -- REBUILD THE WRITING SESSIONS (4 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/pe29_common.py, pe29_cycle1.py, pe29_cycle2.py, pe29_cycle3.py. Cycle files: loops/pe29_cycle1-4.txt. Checkpoints: data/pe29_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Idea: tablets written in one sitting should share a name-sign pool, a writer's variant choices, clay, museum-number neighbourhood and size.
Combine the weak evidence (random weights and evidence subsets, mutual 2-NN links, 100 restarts), hold one evidence out and ask whether
sessions built from the others predict it; then test whether the sessions behave like daily archives (shared rates, cross-tablet sums,
recurring names with fixed amounts). Controls: Ur III Drehem, Amar-Suen 5 (1,028 tablets, 343 known days, date lines removed); planted sessions.
Nulls: evidence relabelled within format strata, within museum-number blocks of 10, random and 'drawer' sessions of equal size.

Bottom line: tablets near each other in the museum sequence were written alike (shared rare signs, variant habits, size), but nothing shows
they are sittings rather than lots or genres, and the rebuilt groups contain no cross-tablet accounting. Even the Ur III control recovers
true days only 1 time in 25, so PE sessions, if they existed, are not recoverable from this evidence. Not cracked. Nothing is read.
