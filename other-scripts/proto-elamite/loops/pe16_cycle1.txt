pe16 cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): HUNGER IS THE RULER -- positive control on Ur III. Tools: tools/pe16_common.py, tools/pe16_cycle1.py. Checkpoints: data/pe16_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Prior-art check: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md and STRATEGIES.md contain no attempt to size PE units from human or animal food needs. A web search ("Proto-Elamite capacity unit litres ration physiological"; bevelled-rim-bowl volumes) found only the usual metrological readings and vessel measurements, not an inference from physiology. Not done before.
Model: a per-recipient amount a (in base units) = need of class c (litres/day) x period D (days) / unit size u (litres), with log-normal scatter. The physiology is fixed outside the texts: energy need by class (man 3,000 +- 400 kcal, woman 2,300 +- 300, child 1,500 +- 350), grain share of energy 0.6-0.95, an allotment factor (median 1.3, log-sd 0.35), barley 3,200-3,500 kcal/kg and 0.58-0.70 kg/l; grain fed to sheep 0.4 kg/day, equids 2 kg/day, cattle 3 kg/day (log-sd 0.45-0.5). Latent: the class of each (tablet, label) group, the period of each tablet from {1, 30, 360} days, the class mix, and the scatter. A common factor on all needs is exactly degenerate with u, so the data are asked only for u' = unit size in adult-man-days; litres = u' x litres/day of an adult man (prior median 1.4 l). The grid step is log 30 / 26 so that 30, 360 and 12 are whole steps; an earlier run with an arbitrary grid let grid rounding break the u <-> D tie (that run is discarded, kept as cycle1_v1_gridartefact.json).
Data: 2,750 Ur III distributive lines ('n NOUN amount-ta', 836 tablets; man 1,077, sheep and lambs 608, child 368, woman 346, cattle and calves 316, equids 30), amounts parsed to sila. Truth: a sila is about 1 litre; men 60, women 30, children 10-20 sila a month; hired men 6 sila a day; sheep 1-1.5 and cattle 5-12 sila a day.
