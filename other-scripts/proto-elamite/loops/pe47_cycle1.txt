pe47 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): THE TABLETS ARE PRINTOUTS OF ONE HIDDEN SPREADSHEET - random search over table shapes.
Script tools/pe47_search.py (summary tools/pe47_c1_sum.py); results data/pe47_ckpt/c1.json (git-ignored).
Data: PE 1,213 tablets / 5,116 entries with fully readable numerals (count values sexagesimal, capacity in the pe24 chain); Ur III Drehem and Umma, random PE-sized samples (1,213 tablets) from 14,168 / 24,366 parsed CDLI tablets; PLANT = master table X[30 units x 20 commodities x 6 periods] (rank-1 in log + noise), printed as unit-period slices, commodity-period slices and period-summed summaries, 6% survival, PE entry-length distribution, 60 noise tokens.
Model: a hypothesis gives each of the 120 commonest tokens a role U (row), C (column), P (slice/period) or X; an entry's cell = its U, C, P tokens (entry + header); a missing axis = sum over that axis. Hidden table = sparse cell value counts, Witten-Bell back-off cell -> (U,C) -> C -> baseline (last token, system). Score = mean log-likelihood gain of exact entry values on held-out tablets (tablet-disjoint halves) over the commodity-only baseline. 400 random role vectors (+ all-X) and hill-climbing of the best 4 (120 flips each), fitted by inner 2-fold CV on half A, tested once on half B. Nulls with the same search: N1 values shuffled across tablets within (last token, system); N2 whole entries re-dealt between tablets; N3 (PE only) random-tensor world of the plant's shape with iid PE values.
