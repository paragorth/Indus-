# X-2 final: let Linear A and Proto-Elamite translate each other (unsupervised alignment on numbers and commodity contexts)
| X-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-2.final | Three cycles (tools/x2_build.py, x2_common.py, x2_fast.py, x2_cycle1-3*.py; about 40,000 alignments). Every entry in four commodity-account corpora (LA 352 tablets, PE 1,510, LB 2,478 DAMOS documents, Ur III 61,728 CDLI documents sampled) represented by script-free contexts (quantity bin, sub-unit, round ten, header / entry / trailer, total line, list position and length, commodities per tablet, designation length, quantity vs tablet median) plus internal co-occurrence. Alignments: Procrustes seeded on the shared contexts with CSLS self-learning, 30 random-rotation restarts, Gromov-Wasserstein (10 couplings), shared-profile cosine, joint SVD, similarity flooding over tablet co-occurrence; bootstrap stability. Controls: LB <-> Ur III with a gold dictionary frozen before the runs (19 commodities, 5 words), at full, PE and LA x PE sizes; LA <-> LB (same logograms by descent); shuffled corpora on both sides; per-label and family-wise shuffled nulls; split halves; triangle LA -> PE -> Ur III vs LA -> LB, with a class-preserving null | Decisive control: the requested unsupervised-MT alignments (Procrustes, random restarts, GW) do not recover LB <-> Ur III commodities at any size (MRR at or below the permutation null). The best no-learning profile match and flooding recover them only at PE size (flooding P 0.049 vs shuffled worlds) and not at LA x PE size (P 0.15-0.46). Hits are class-level (FAR -> flour, OVIS -> sheep); stable errors swap oil and barley, bronze and beer, men and lambs. LA <-> LB identity: 1 of 7 (NI). Stability does not predict correctness (control precision of family-wise survivors 8-19% vs 4% chance). LA <-> PE: family-wise survivors HIDE -> M288 and GRA -> M288; only GRA -> M288 replicates in split halves (43-75%); CYP pairs with the capacity class signs M297 / M036 / M379; triangle consistency (P 0.008-0.02) dies under a class-preserving null (P 0.18-0.41). Total word aligns across scripts (built in); LA heading words (A-DU, *516, TE) meet PE openers (M157, M327+M342) only at slot level | **No LA <-> PE sign-level alignment survives the controls.** The two scripts do not translate each other from numbers and context: the method fails on the pair where the answer is known (LB <-> Ur III) at the size that matters, and on two scripts of one tradition (LA <-> LB). What crosses scripts is one bit per commodity, measured-in-sub-units vs counted, and the slot frame (heading, entry, total). Not cracked |

Functional readings (grades; none rises above C):
- GRA (LA) ~ M288 (PE), grade C: 'the general bulk head of each ledger'. Survives the family-wise shuffle (joint) and replicates in 43-75% of split halves, but M288 is PE's hub class sign and hub pairs are stably wrong in the control (expected precision ~10-15%). Would support: the pair persists after hubness correction (CSLS on the mean matrix, M288 split by number system C vs SDB) and in a held-out non-Susa PE subset. Would kill: it moves to another hub when M288 is removed or down-weighted.
- CYP (LA) ~ {M297, M036, M379} (PE capacity-tied class signs), grade C, class level only: 'goods measured in a large unit with sub-units'. Restates FINDINGS (LA CYP 72% fractional; PE test b capacity class) rather than adding a reading. Would kill: the pairing survives when the sub-unit features are removed (then it is not about units).
- Rejected: HIDE -> M288 (14 LA tokens; does not replicate in halves); KU-RO -> M348 (changes with method; PE writes no total word); OLE, VIN, VIR, NI, OLIV -> any PE sign (stability at shuffled level, 0-7% split concordance).
- Slot level, not readings: LA heading words A-DU / *516 / TE ~ PE openers M157 / |M327+M342|; total word ~ total line.

What would change this: a control that passes at LA x PE size. On present evidence, that needs larger Aegean data (more LA tablets) or features beyond numbers (e.g. physical tablet format, seal use), not a better aligner.
