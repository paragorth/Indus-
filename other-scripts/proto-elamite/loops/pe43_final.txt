Verdict: NOT CRACKED. The form tree is real usage structure (variants behave like their root, A; compound parts are kin, B, calibrated on proto-cuneiform) but it is not a dictionary: its families in the control script are numeral-system families that match the lexical lists only weakly (p 0.02), it gives no time order (birth order = frequency; proto-cuneiform sheds variants), and coining is driven by a sign's own variant richness, not by its meaning family.
