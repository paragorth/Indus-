pe51 FINAL (5 Oct 2026): WHAT WOULD THE CLERK MISS? (cycles: pe51_cycle1.txt, pe51_cycle2.txt, pe51_cycle3.txt)
Tools: tools/pe51_lib.py (corpora, models, deletion views), pe51_run.py (single-sign game),
pe51_sum.py (profiles, classes, stability), pe51_cycle2.py (nulls), pe51_pairs.py and
pe51_pairs_sum.py (pair game). Checkpoints: data/pe51_ckpt/ (git-ignored).

Method. 64 small random models (transformer or bi-GRU) fill in masked tablets: every numeral (system,
magnitude, total line), half of the header tokens, whole entry lines. On held-out tablets each
frequent sign (and, in cycle 3, each pair of the 30 most frequent) is erased everywhere and the
damage to every other prediction is measured, against erasing as many random tokens in the same
tablets. Corpora: PE (1,175 tablets), a planted corpus with known roles, Ur III (1,500 Drehem/Umma
tablets, words as opaque tokens), proto-cuneiform (2,948), and two PE nulls (signs shuffled within
lines; signs re-dealt across lines).

Calibration (what the game can and cannot see)
- A (method): it finds number carriers. Planted commodities 11/11 and units 4/4 classed NUM-L;
  Ur III goods+units ranked with AUC 0.82 (gurusz, ma-na, gur, sila3, gin2, sar, sze at the top);
  proto-cuneiform SZE first (t 5.0), then GAN2, UDU, GAR. Names are always silent: planted 60/60,
  Ur III 7/7, proto-cuneiform titles 5/5.
- A (method limit): it is blind to total markers and document-type markers. Planted ones rank first
  but never pass; Ur III szu-nigin2 and ba-zi / mu-kux / i3-dab5 / kiszib3 are not found (AUC
  0.48-0.52); the planted doc-marker redundancy is not found by the pair game either (0/60). A
  sign whose information the rest of the tablet repeats is never missed. So 'silent' = 'name OR
  redundant marker', and the planned TOT and HEAD loss classes cannot be filled.

Proto-Elamite loss classes
- Grade B: almost every frequent PE sign is SILENT (137 of 141). Erasing it changes nothing but its
  own slot beyond what erasing random tokens does. The null corpora give as many classed signs as
  real PE (3-4 vs 3 at equal models) and the summed number damage falls only 10-15% when lines are
  shuffled, so most of what a PE sign is worth to these models is which tablet it is on, not which
  line it is in (agrees with pe46 'bag of signs').
- Grade B: NUM-L class (number carriers) = M297 and M376 (classed in every model set, t 3.9-7.3),
  with M367 and M288 at the edge. M297 is tablet-level (survives both nulls: it marks a capacity
  tablet; 191/292 of its lines are capacity). M376 is line-level (its effect halves when lines are
  re-dealt; never capacity, 44/147 lines with fractions).
- Grade B-: the capacity triangle M387, M036, M297 (with M288|M305 on magnitude) shares one piece of
  number-system information inside lines: erasing two of them hurts more than the two losses added
  (M387|M036 z 15.6 at 10 models; replicated 5 -> 10 models; gone when lines are re-dealt; 13 joint
  cells vs 5 false calls). The same game on Ur III returns unit+goods groups (gur|sze, sila3|kasz,
  udu|sila4). This is the third independent method that isolates this core of the earlier C-grade
  capacity twig. Would kill: new tablets where M387 or M036 lines carry counting numerals as often
  as capacity ones.
- Grade C: count-side joint pairs M346|M367 and M054|M003 / M009|M003 (never-capacity, BARE-COUNT
  sets); M054|M003 is tablet-level. HEAD candidates M317, |M136+M365|, M220 do not replicate (C-,
  and the controls say this class is unreadable anyway).
- Earlier C-grade groups: the pe45 name-frame elements (M056, M010, M001, M157, M210) are all
  SILENT and form no replicating joint pair: they behave as names would (consistent, not a test of
  pe45, because names and redundant markers look the same here). The header twig shows no header
  information that the body lacks (HEAD AUC 0.19).

Verdict: not cracked, nothing read. The deletion game is a calibrated detector of number-bearing
signs and nothing more; on PE it confirms the capacity / never-capacity split and the capacity core
M387-M036-M297, and says the rest of the frequent signs are interchangeable for every prediction
these models can make.
