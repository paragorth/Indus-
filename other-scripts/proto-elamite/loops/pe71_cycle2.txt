pe71 cycle 2: name or office? same value, same seal? where does it sit?
(tools/pe71_c2.py, pe71_c2b.py, pe71_c2c.py; data/pe71_ckpt/c2.json, c2b.json)
The sealing lead died in cycle 1, so this cycle asks what the M153 compounds are without assuming they sign anything.
A slot family = occurrences (tablet, value); PE value = the partner sign of S in |S+...| compounds. Find-lot: same volume,
publication numbers within 10 (PE); same reign year (Ur III). Ur III control: Umma at PE size (1,581 tablets, 10 draws), words
opaque in the test; seal-legend words split into personal names and titles only to label the control.
