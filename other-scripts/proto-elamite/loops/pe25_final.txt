pe25 TWO HANDS ON ONE TABLET - final (4 Oct 2026)

Idea: the reverse total, edge tag or header might be added by a checker. Fingerprint
= free graphic-variant choices (121 base signs with a real variant choice) plus
comma omission. Hand index H = variant agreement within a tablet minus agreement
with same-stratum tablets (header base x publication series), scaled; pi = share of
reverses in another hand. Context-differing pairs remove "same item written twice".
Controls: synthetic hands (Dirichlet around corpus frequencies, concentration
matched to the real within-obverse consistency); one hand per tablet, reverse by
1 of 5 checkers, 30% mix, meaning-fixed variants; checker pools per stratum;
second hands planted on one line class. No Ur III corpus with transcribed
second-hand additions is available (CDLI ATF does not mark hands), so the
calibration is planted only.

Results
- B: reverses are written in the obverse's own variant habits: pi -0.03 (90% CI
  -0.27 to 0.18; context-matched 0.14, -0.17 to 0.41). Plants: one hand -0.15 to
  -0.02, checker reverse 0.94-1.02. A systematic second hand on reverses is killed;
  up to ~40% of reverses by another hand is not excluded.
- B: single-line reverses (the totals) are as consistent with the body as body
  halves are (pi -0.54, CI to 0.15). The last obverse line is in the body hand.
- C: multi-line reverses slightly less consistent (pi 0.35, CI 0.01-0.64; one-hand
  plant up to 0.24), probably new content, not a new hand.
- B: no single official writes the reverses of a header-and-series group (D' 0.018,
  CI -0.016 to 0.075; one-checker plants 0.13-0.26). A 3+ checker pool, or
  checkers copying the writer's variants, is not excluded. No office links.
- Random-rule scan (2,500 predicates, 144 testable): 0 of 20 survive on held-out
  tablets; planted classes are found; the single-hand plant passes 2/20, so the
  scan is weak (B, weak).
- Untestable: edge tags (1 of 120 edge lines has a variant-bearing sign), headers
  (29 tablets), comma habit (11 omissions).
Nothing is read; nothing is cracked.
