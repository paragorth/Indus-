After cycle 1 the remaining use-models make predictions about GROUPS and FACES rather than single attributes:
 2a FILING BATCH / JOINT: neighbours in publication order inside a volume (the excavators' and editors' lot order) share
    top-end squareness beyond the header (U1 batch filing, U3 binding). Null: order shuffled within volume (2,000).
    Planted: a 0.4 sd block effect in runs of 4 consecutive tablets. Bottom end as a check.
 2b DOSSIERS / SEAL UNITS: pe63 dossier members (D1-D6) and same-seal tablets (pe70/pe71 units) have more similar residual
    top fill than random same-size groups drawn from the same volume (5,000). Planted: dossier members given a shared
    offset (0.7 of variance). U3/U4 predict YES at both ends; U7 predicts NO.
 2c OFFICE STYLE: among headed tablets, the first header sign (families with >= 8 tablets) predicts residual top fill
    beyond random relabelling (Kruskal H, 2,000 permutations). U4 predicts YES.
 2d TAG FACE (U1 spine, the one survivor): the top-edge view (pe83 c1d: topedge_rect, topedge_end_fill) is flatter /
    fuller on tablets that carry a top-edge tag, among headed tablets; and tagged headed tablets have squarer residual top.
    Bottom-edge view as the control face. Permutation 5,000. Support: tag effect > 0 at p < 0.05 on the top face and
    not on the bottom face. Kill: tag effect <= 0 on the top face.
 2e OUTSIDE (frozen data/pe85_ckpt/frozen_outside.json, sha256 72fbd7eeb78131600432760d661aea029dd866a5c1937bd0f1e8edcbdf1729f1):
    excavated findspot squares and levels from the CDLI catalogue (Susa Acropole S.ACR; Malyan ABC/TUV), line-art outlines.
    O1 header end squarer on headed intact tablets; O2 same-square tablets more alike in top squareness; O3 levels differ.
    Caveat frozen with it: the per-tablet values were listed once before freezing (no statistic), so the test is not blind.
