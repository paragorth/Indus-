pe34 'Compound signs are sums' - final (4 Oct 2026). Tools: tools/pe34_build.py, pe34_common.py, pe34_cycle1-3.py. Cycles: loops/pe34_cycle1-3.txt.
Question: are PE compound signs (|A+B|, |A+B+A|, |A+A|, numeral insets) behaviourally compositional, i.e. does a modifier shift numeral system, count size, position, header use and neighbours the same way on different bases?
Prior art: Born, Kelley, Monroe & Sarkar 2021 (ACL Findings) found partial compositionality with image/text embeddings; Kelley (CDLJ) describes complex graphemes philologically. pe34 is different: explicit administrative behaviour, held-out transfer of a modifier to unseen bases, modifier-shuffle nulls, Linear B and proto-cuneiform controls, planted modifiers.
| PE-34.F1 | Controls | Planted modifiers at real compound sizes: 20% strength found 5/5, 0% 0/5. Shuffled-label calibration: 1/20, 0/20, 3/20 false positives (PE, ARCH, LINB). Linear B (:m/:f on 4-5 animals, ligature syllables): transfer p 0.012, tablet-disjoint p 0.001, factorisation p 0.02-0.05 (PASS). Proto-cuneiform (122 held-out |AxB| etc.): p 0.28-0.81 (FAILS: insets name contents but do not change line behaviour) | Method works where modifiers act on behaviour; it cannot see semantic composition that leaves behaviour unchanged |
| PE-34.F2 | Is PE compositional in behaviour? | All features: transfer p 0.08 (tablet-disjoint 0.19); additive / scaling / rank-2 factorisation p 0.16 / 0.67 / 0.24. A first run with variant forms counted as different bases gave p 0.006: an artefact, corrected | B (negative): PE modifiers are not behavioural operators (no shift >= the 20% planted effect), like proto-cuneiform insets and unlike Linear B sex markers |
| PE-34.F3 | Which modifier does transfer? | Quantity family p 0.006 (tablet-disjoint), carried entirely by M342 (drop it: p 0.28). |X+M342| is less often numbered than X on 5 of 7 bases incl. unused singletons (others: 34%); |M327+M342| is on the first line 87% | C: M342 turns a sign into an unnumbered, header-type form. Would support: new |X+M342| on new tablets unnumbered / first line more often than X; would kill: numbered as often as X |
| PE-34.F4 | Meaning classes from behaviour | M342 'header / not counted' (C); M388 'entry-final' (C, may be its own base behaviour); 1(N14) inset with 'mod*' numerals (C, 2 of 3 bases are 2-token forms). No modifier detectably marks female/young, herd subtype, ration grade or container. Linear B: :m and :f both read as 'counted animal'; the method cannot separate the sexes | Behaviour gives at most a coarse class; nothing is read |
| PE-34.F5 | Verdict | Not cracked. Compound signs are not sums of behaviour: bases carry behaviour (p 0.0005 in all three scripts), modifiers mostly do not | B negative; one C lead (M342) |
