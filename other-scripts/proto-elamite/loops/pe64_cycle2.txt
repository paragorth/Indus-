Rungs = rates validated in cycle 1 by a named rule ('grid' or 'top' selector; strict p_swap <= 0.005, lenient <= 0.05).
Ladder statistic S = share of rung pairs in a simple ratio (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 3/2, 4/3, 5/2, 5/3, 5/4); null = 5,000 random
rung sets of the same size drawn from every rate the same rules produce on 50 swap-null corpora (tablet-weighted).
Signs per rung: count-line signs on >= 2 tablets of a rung's events vs 200 swap nulls (Ur III full: 60), same rules, same rate.
