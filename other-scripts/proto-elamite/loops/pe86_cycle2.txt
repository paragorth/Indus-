pe86 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): massive random guessing of header definitions, judged by the clay. Plan and kill lines
written before running (tools/pe86_cycle2.py; data/pe86_ckpt/c2.json).

 A random definition is a rule on line 1 (or line 1 or 2): numerals allowed or not, token count range, legible-only or
 not, a random sign set S (1-15 signs from the 120 commonest line-1/2 tokens) used as 'first token in S required',
 'first token in S excluded' or ignored, and a combination with the standard definition (replace / OR / AND).
 5,000 definitions per split. Score = partial r (rank top fill vs definition, size/shape/lines/damage/reverse removed).
 Train = a random half of publication batches (volume x 50 publication numbers); the top 50 are re-scored on the
 held-out batches and on line-art tablets with intact line 1 that have no photo outline (independent drawing record).
 5 splits.
 Kill controls:
  K1 shuffled squareness labels on the train half (5 nulls per split): if the real top-50's held-out gain over the
     standard definition is not above the null's, the search found nothing.
  K2 planted header sign: PLANT added to line 1 of 20 unheaded tablets (weighted by squareness rank^2, train and test):
     the top definitions must contain PLANT in an OR rule (pass >= 4/5 splits).
  K3 length-only family: the same search with no sign set. If the best full definitions do not beat the best
     length-only definitions held-out, the survivors only re-discover line length.
 Pre-registered support: a definition family beats the standard definition held-out (photo and line art) in >= 4/5
 splits AND beats K1 and K3. Kill: the standard definition is never beaten held-out by more than the null gain.

Results (data/pe86_ckpt/c2.json, c2.log). 589 photo tablets (intact line 1), 103 line-art-only tablets; 20,000 random
definitions per run; 5 batch splits; 25 shuffled-label nulls; 5 length-only runs; 5 planted runs. Standard definition
held-out r: 0.26-0.38 (photo halves), 0.36 (line art).
