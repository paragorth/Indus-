Plain answer: there is no detectable corpus-wide ledger. Across 1,585 tablets, totals do not reappear elsewhere more than chance, and labelled copies are no commoner than shuffled data. The test has power: Ur III of the same size passes it. There is one local ledger, in the herd office (|M362+X| herds, about 9 tablets). In it, one herd page is copied into a multi-herd summary, two pages of a periodic 14-herd ration list survive, and the list's first column tracks herd size at about 1:5. It reconfirms N14 = 10 and fixes no uncertain sign. Caveats: Susa only, published sample only, and 'absence' is an upper bound.
