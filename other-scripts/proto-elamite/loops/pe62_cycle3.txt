PE-62 cycle 3: WHICH NAME SIGNS CARRY THE CLASS, AND WHAT IS LEFT WHEN THE WHOLE TABLET IS MASKED
Date 6 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/pe62_c3.py (carrier tests), tools/pe62_c1.py modes bnonbr / nonbr / lshufbnonbr.
Checkpoints data/pe62_ckpt/c3_*.json, c1_*nonbr*.json (git-ignored).

Two questions. (1) Hidden carriers: when the class sign is missing, which name-string signs still hold the class? W test: are
labelled entries carrying sign s of class k more often than other labelled entries on the SAME tablets (G statistic; null =
labels permuted within tablets, 400x; BH q)? X test: do tablets carrying s have an unusual class mix (null = random tablets from
the same museum batch, 400x)? Replication: carriers found on the 70% DEV tablets re-tested on the 30% held-out tablets (same top
class; binomial against 1/classes). Outside check: do unmarked entries carrying a capacity-class carrier have more capacity
numerals than other unmarked entries on the same tablets? Planted carrier: a random name sign relabelled to one class.
Controls: PC and Ur III at PE size. (2) Whole-tablet masking: what the 1,254 unmarked entries on tablets with NO class sign
actually face. The random search is re-run with the neighbour group barred (no class visible anywhere on the tablet), with and
without numerals; label-shuffle nulls; PC and Ur III controls.

Verdict cycle 3 (B-/C): the name strings hold little hidden class information. A handful of signs lean to one class within
tablets (M056, M217 -> allotment; M259, M340, M390 -> person lines), and only those replicate on held-out tablets, at the edge of
chance. The one strong local pattern is M056: it is either last in the line or directly before M288. Lines where it stands
alone have allotment-sized numbers (C). When a whole tablet omits its class, PE leaves 0.11 bits in everything except the
numerals, below PC and far below Ur III, where words carry the commodity. The clerk left the class out because the tablet
(and the number system) already said it.
