PE-67 cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026). EACH OUTPOST WRITES ABOUT WHAT IT DIGS UP: tables and calibration.
Prior-work check: web search (no study links PE sign profiles to excavated site assemblages; Wikipedia/Potts summary says 21 of 27 Yahya tablets record grain, an interpretation we do not use); LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md (pe17, pe22, pe48, pe65 test sign mix by site, never against excavated materials). Not a repeat.
Data built: data/pe67_materials.json (7 PE sites x 13 materials, ordinal 0/1/2, cited: Nicholas 1990 TUV, Zeder 1991 Open Context, Potts 2001 Yahya, Sialk/Arisman metallurgy, Sofalin silver furnaces and BRB beeswax, Shahr-i Sokhta lapis/carnelian/shell/textile; Ur III province control table with word truth); data/pe67_sign_profile.tsv (104 signs seen off Susa: tablets per site, number systems, line roles).
Method (tools/pe67_lib.py, pe67_c1.py): per sign and material, a site-level score z = sum_j (M_jm - mean M_m) * presence residual r_sj; 20,000 random hypotheses of 3-8 compatible (sign, material) pairs (capacity-counted signs -> grain-like goods; counted signs -> animals/objects); top 1% survive; links = most-supported pairs; leave-one-outpost-out statistic T = sum over links of (M_om - mean) * r_so on the held-out outpost. Sites with < 3 sign-bearing tablets (Ozbaki, Shahr-i Sokhta) are prediction-only. Sign-bearing tablets: Susa 1435, Yahya 25, Malyan 17, Sofalin 9, Sialk 4.
