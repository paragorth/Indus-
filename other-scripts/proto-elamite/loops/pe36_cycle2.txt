pe36 cycle 2 (4 Oct 2026): held-out replication. Each corpus (controls first matched to 2x the PE-name evidence) split into two halves by tablet (seed 7); blind grids fitted separately on each half (200-300 restarts per half); Spearman rho of the row co-assignment matrices over signs shared by both halves' top 65, and the mean co-assignment in half B of pairs sharing a row >= 0.5 in half A ('carry'). Null: the same halves with within-string shuffles (2-3 seeds x 100 restarts). Tools: tools/pe36_c2.py, pe36_queue_c2.sh; output data/pe36_ckpt/report_c2.txt. Restarted once after a container restart (finished chunks kept).
