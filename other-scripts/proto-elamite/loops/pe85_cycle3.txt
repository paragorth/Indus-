pe85 cycle 3 (7 Oct 2026): CHOOSE vs MAKE, a gravity model, and held-out re-tests. Plan and kill lines written just after launch.
Only the first 3 lines of c3.log had been seen (the observed top r 0.363, known from cycle 1) when this file was written.

 3a CHOOSE vs MAKE (simulate the writers). Two mechanisms give 'headed tablets have a square top':
    CHOOSE: blanks come with two ends of random shape; a writer who will write a header turns the squarer end up
            (probability q). Then the OTHER end of a headed tablet is the rounder one: bottom r must be NEGATIVE.
    MAKE:   the writer squares the end that takes the header (shift q); the other end is untouched: bottom r ~ 0.
    Simulation: headed tablets get end pairs drawn from headless tablets of the same size/line stratum, then CHOOSE or
    MAKE is applied over a grid of q (60 runs each); compare the simulated (top r, bottom r) with the observed
    (0.36, 0.09). Kill MAKE if the observed bottom r falls inside the CHOOSE band at the q that matches the top r and
    outside every MAKE band; kill CHOOSE in the opposite case. Also: top-minus-bottom asymmetry of headless PE tablets
    against proto-cuneiform headless tablets (the pipeline's own top/bottom bias).
 3b GRAVITY (U8, new): tablets stood to dry on the header end flatten it under their own weight; heavier blanks
    flatten more. Prediction: header x log(mass) > 0 on top residual, on both MDP 26 and the other volumes, and on the
    line-art record. Kill: <= 0 on both photo halves.
 3c Re-test of cycle-2 neighbour concordance (filing batch) on held-out halves (MDP 26 vs others) and on the line-art
    record; support: top r > null q95 on both halves and on line art, bottom not. Kill: below null q95 on both halves.
 3d Held-out tablets that never entered cycles 1-2: line-art-only tablets with intact line 1 (no photo outline).
    Core claim re-test (header -> square top, kill if r < 0.15), header x reverse (cycle-1 candidate; support > 0),
    tag on top (U1; support > 0).

Results (data/pe85_ckpt/c3.json, c3b.json; tools/pe85_cycle3.py, pe85_cycle3b.py). 589 intact photo tablets (405 headed).
