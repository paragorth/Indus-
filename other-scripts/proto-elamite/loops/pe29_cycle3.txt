pe29 cycle 3 -- do the rebuilt PE sessions behave like daily archives? (tools/pe29_cycle3.py; logs data/pe29_ckpt/cycle3_sum.log, cycle3_tot.log)
Sessions = connected components of cycle-2 session links (all five evidences; links in >= 30% of 100 restarts), size 2-12: 236 sessions,
1,025 tablet pairs (578 tablets stay single). No evidence reads a numeral, so amounts are an independent check.
Nulls (500 each): N1 sessions relabelled inside strata (same sizes); N2 'drawer' sessions = a random tablet plus its museum-number neighbours.
Planted: 25 sessions where one tablet's total is overwritten by another member's entry sum.
