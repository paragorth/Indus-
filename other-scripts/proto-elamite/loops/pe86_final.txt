Grades
- B: on intact tablets the clay is an independent header detector: clay outline adds about 0.09 AUC beyond size and
  layout, held out by publication batch (p 0.01 vs shuffled labels).
- B (negative, bounded): no random redefinition of 'header' (sign sets required or excluded, line-1 length, legibility,
  line 2) predicts the squared end better than the standard 'line 1 has signs and no numerals' on held-out batches or on
  drawings. Our definition and the clay agree; no header sign is missed or misclassified at the size of a group of about
  15-26 tablets (planted recovery 4/5 at 15 in the direct scan, 3/5 at 26 in the definition search; smaller groups are
  not excluded). Would kill: a sign group on new tablets whose inclusion or exclusion raises held-out r by >= 0.03.
- KILLED: |M327+X| (and M388, |M157+M131|, |M175+M136|) as 'headers that are really something else' (train-only,
  fail on drawings); one-entry tablets as unheaded-but-squared; 'numeral-free line 2' as a two-line header (it is a
  broken line; the round bottom is damage); illegible header-shaped lines as missed headers with a square top.
- C: tablets whose numeral-free line 1 has an unread sign (CDLI comments it as a header; our loader drops it to x) have a
  SQUARE BOTTOM (photo +0.22 passes the family line; drawings +0.16 p 0.02, held-out +0.38 p 0.004) and an
  inconsistent top (round on photos, squarish on drawings). Would support: the same on new tablets with an unread
  opener; would kill: bottom difference <= 0 on the next 20 such tablets. Reading: possibly worn or re-oriented tablets.
- C-: frozen header probabilities for 294 tablets whose first line is lost (data/pe86_frozen_lost_headers.json).
  The clay failed the damaged-line-1 pseudo-test, so a null result is expected. Would support / kill: rule in file
  (top third by clay_minus_size header rate >= 0.15 above bottom third; kill < 0.05, n >= 20 resolved).
- Not possible: header KIND from the clay (M157 vs others, gain 0.000).
