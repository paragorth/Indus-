pe59 cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026): THE CRACK ATTEMPT, part 1. The surviving B-grade (and strongest C-grade) results were assembled into one working reading (pe59-R1; tools/pe59_lib.py READING), frozen before any held-out scoring (data/pe59_reading_frozen.json, sha256 a hash of reading + pe52 weights + split; the file asserts on re-run). Reading: CAP system N39C 1, N30D 2, N30C 4, N24 12, N39B 24, N01 120, N14 720 (pe27/pe42, B-), N39C 0.6-0.8 l (alias 5-7 l, C); CNT 1/10/100/300 or sexagesimal, fractions 1/2 or 1/6 (B); one system per tablet, plain N01/N14 take it (B); header roles M157 / M327 family / M136+M388 (capacity, C) / others; entry frame prefix + name-string + class sign; MEASURED, COUNTED, FRACLINE (M376), ALLOT (M288, 60 or 120 N39C per unit of the preceding count line), PERSON (pe28/pe32 class); pe52 weights; pe44 fixed allotments; no total sign, total = sum in the tablet system (pe38, pe30); Yahya lexicon (pe48, C).
Grammar (tools/pe59_models.py): writes whole tablets. Signs left to right per line: S0 unigram, S1 Witten-Bell bigram, S2 + tablet cache (name pool), S3 + data-only slot extras (line-type first slot, cross-line bigram, site lexicon); G = S3 + reading roles (class signs by tablet-type posterior, allotment after PERSON line, header roles, outposts). Numerals: N0 marginal, N1 | final sign, N2 + tablet cache; G = N2 + tablet-type latent from roles, log-normal tablet scale in the reading's units shifted by pe52 weights, M288 rate rule, fixed-allotment copy. Totals: N2 vs G (+ entry sum in the predicted system). Split: tablets by sha256(id) into 803 train / 782 held-out; all hyperparameters fitted on train with leave-one-tablet-out counts; parameters hashed (data/pe59_ckpt/c1_PE_frozen_params.json, 4f504e3727e4abae) before held-out scoring. Controls: 20 refits with every sign role re-dealt to frequency-matched signs; 10 refits with random unit ladders (random integer ratios, same code order); data-only tablet-type classifier (naive Bayes over all signs); tablet-bootstrap CIs.
