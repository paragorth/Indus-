# Prior-art check: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md, web search. Closest prior: x4 forgery battery (X-4.4: real vs 4 fitted generators, corpus-level AUC only). Not done before: per-feature unforgeability ranking across thousands of random forgers, calibrated on proto-cuneiform and a planted world.
# Design: each forger = random spec (30% from-scratch: sign Markov order 0-2 or whole-entry slot grammar, numbers conditioned on the final sign with random strength, optional true totals; 70% edits of real tablets: 1-3 of entry swap, number swap (system-matched or not), within-tablet number shuffle, sign substitution, string swap, header swap, drop/duplicate entries, number jitter, rates log-uniform 0.05-1, optional recomputed totals). 3-way tablet split per forger: forger fitted on A, detector feature models on B, real held-out C vs forgeries. 23 global + 6 x 40 per-sign features. Strength = full logistic detector AUC (2-fold). U(f) = mean signed single-feature z over the strongest quarter of forgers; W(f) = same over the weakest quarter. Threshold = 99th percentile of |U| in a shuffled-label run, scaled to n.
