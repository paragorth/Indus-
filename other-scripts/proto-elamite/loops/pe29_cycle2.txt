pe29 cycle 2 -- link tablets into sessions, leave one evidence out (tools/pe29_cycle2.py; logs data/pe29_ckpt/cycle2_*.log)
Method: 100 restarts; each draws random evidence weights (Dirichlet) and switches each evidence off with p 0.3; links = mutual 2-nearest
neighbours on the weighted sum of z-scored similarities; 'session link' = linked in >= 30% of restarts. Held-out test: cluster on all but one
evidence; lift = mean standardised held-out similarity over session links. Null: every clustering evidence relabelled independently inside
strata (museum prefix x numeral system x line bin; clay inside photo batch), same pipeline, 30 draws (min p 0.032). Ur III: true day known.
