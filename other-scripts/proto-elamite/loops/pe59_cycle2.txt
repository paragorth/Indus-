pe59 cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026): DECODE the held-out half with the frozen reading (tools/pe59_cycle2.py). The grammar was refitted on the training half and its parameter hash matches cycle 1 (4f504e3727e4abae; params_match true). For each held-out tablet with >= 2 clean entries (564 tablets) the decoder writes a structured gloss: header role, edge tag, document type (capacity or count account, predicted from the signs alone), each entry split into qualifier + name-string + class sign with its quantity in reading units (capacity in N39C and litres at 0.6-0.8 l per N39C; counts in units), the M288 allotment check, and the total check. Full glosses: data/pe59_ckpt/c2_PE.json.
Checks: C1 the predicted system is consistent with every numeral; C2 every role-bearing entry carries a numeral its role allows (measured -> capacity; counted/person/fraction line -> not capacity codes; person counts <= 60; M288 after a count line -> 60k or 120k N39C); C3 the written total closes in the predicted system. 'Full' = C1 and C2 and (C3 where a total exists). 'Strict' = full, with at least one role check and a checkable total that closes. Controls: (i) real roles on tablets whose numerals were transplanted from another held-out tablet with the same number of numeric lines (20 draws), (ii) 8 refits with sign roles shuffled.
