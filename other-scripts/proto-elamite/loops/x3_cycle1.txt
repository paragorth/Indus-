# X-3 cycle 1: which script teaches which (pretrain on X, fine-tune on a slice of Y, score held-out Y)
Tools: tools/x3_build.py (corpora, scratch only), x3_common.py (model, conditions), x3_run.py (driver, checkpointed jsonl), x3_sum.py, x3_matrix.py. Raw results: $X3_SCR/res_c1.jsonl (scratch, not committed).
Corpora (sign-level lines): LA 8.3k tokens, PE 27k, VOY 156k (EVA glyphs), LB 67k (DAMOS syllables + logograms + NUM), PC 134k (proto-cuneiform), UR3 120k (CDLI Ur III admin, sux, readings), AKK 120k (CDLI akk, readings; the Semitic text), ELX 4.7k (CDLI Elamite, readings), LAT 120k (Caesar letters), GRC 262k (Iliad respelled in a Linear-B-like CV syllabary). Hittite (27 CDLI lines) and Hurrian (105 lines) too small: not run. Labels kept as each convention writes them, so LA / LB / GRC share syllabogram labels, UR3 / AKK / ELX share cuneiform readings, PE / PC share numeral codes, LA / LB / UR3 / AKK / ELX share NUM; VOY and LAT share letter shapes by EVA convention only.
Model: neural 4-gram (64-d sign embeddings, MLP 128, tied output + bias), 150 commonest X signs, 150 commonest Y signs (rest UNK in every condition). Pretrain 8,000 tokens of X (ELX: all 4.7k), 1,500 steps. Fine-tune 240 steps on a 2,000-token Y slice, step chosen on a Y validation set (<= 1,000 tokens), loss on a Y test set (<= 1,500 tokens, blocks of 20 lines). 3 seeds; 219 jobs, ~2,900 fine-tunes, ~3 h on 2 CPU workers.
Conditions: scratch; real X; relab (X's signs randomly renamed: same structure, no identities); band (renamed only within frequency-rank bands of 8); shuf (X tokens shuffled, line lengths kept); markov (random Zipfian Markov text); body (real X, sign embeddings and output bias re-initialised: pure structure). Y signs are looked up through REAL X labels ('label' mode, when >= 20% of Y test tokens have a label X also has) or through frequency rank ('rank' mode, always). ID = relab - real (bits/token on held-out Y; > 0 = identity transfer).
