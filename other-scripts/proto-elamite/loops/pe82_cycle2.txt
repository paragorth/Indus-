pe82 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): WHAT THE READER COULD NOT HAVE KNOWN (novelty tolerance). Candidate I2 of loops/pe82_cycle1.txt.
Tools: tools/pe82_c2.py, pe82_c2_an.py; checkpoints data/pe82_ckpt/c2_*.json.
Idea: a sign the reader has never seen can be absorbed only where the context fixes its role, so the places where later
publication batches show signs never seen in earlier batches mark open-class slots (names), and signs that sit there are
name material; closed-class codes (commodities, units) should never neighbour novelty.
Design: discovery batches D, held-out batches H (two batch groups each way; PE: MDP 26 + 26S + TCL 32/MDP 31 vs MDP 17 + 06 +
other; PC: Uruk III / Uruk IV / other, three ways). A token in H is NOVEL if its base sign never occurs in D (PE 166-299 novel
tokens; PC 732-2,145). Tolerance of a context = smoothed rate, in D, of signs found on only one D tablet. 3,000 context
definitions per split (all 92 subsets of 1-3 of 8 features + random sign partitions of the neighbours into 2-20 classes);
score = held-out AUC gain over the entry-position x entry-length reader. Selection on one H batch group, re-test on the
other (and reverse). Null: novelty labels shuffled within (entry length x position), 2 replicates, same selection.
Pre-registered reading (before scoring): if a context survives, the signs that license novelty are compared with the pe45
name-frame elements (M056 M010 M001 final; M157 M210 initial; M054 M009); PC goods signs (PC_WORLD) should be closed.
