pe6 cycle 1 -- "grow a fake Susa": fit the agent-based economy to the real corpus (ABC)
Tools: tools/pe6_common.py (simulator, statistics), pe6_bank.py (reference table), pe6_pipe.py, pe6_cycle1.py.
Numbers: data/pe6_cycle1.json, posterior draws data/pe6_post_real.npy, bank data/pe6_bank/ (20,000 runs, 40 chunks).
Simulator: offices with rosters of named units (workers/herds), goods with fixed number systems, office goods
portfolios, tablets = one office x one transaction; scribal practices = header (opener / opener+office name / bare
name), entry designation ([prefix] + [name] + [class sign] or class sign alone), bare-numeral sub-lines, totals
(signed with the class sign or bare, with error rate), polyvalence (opener/prefix/class shapes reused in names),
damage (break, illegible tokens). 38 parameters, 43 statistics in 7 groups (tablet length, entry structure,
number-system mix, totals, header/entry relations, designation recurrence, damage). Every sign is a role; nothing is read.
Real values use the counting set 1/10/100/300 and the a-priori capacity set (FINDINGS attack 2): a hypothesis.
