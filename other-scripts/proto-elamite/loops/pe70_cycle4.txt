(tools/pe70_c4_photo.py; data/pe70_ckpt/c4_key.json, c4_codes.json, c4.json; photographs: CDLI, kept in the scratchpad)

highest sealed-profile score (cycle 1 logistic model, trained leave-one-volume-out, so no tablet scores itself) show
rolled seal impressions on their photographs more often than the lowest-scoring tablets.
