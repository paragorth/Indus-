pe20 cycle 1 -- "the flock does the arithmetic": blind search of herd-category assignments scored by herd biology
Tools: tools/pe20_common.py (records, biology scorer, random search, Gibbs), tools/pe20_cycle1.py (plant | ur3 | pe).
Numbers: data/pe20_cycle1_plant.json, data/pe20_cycle1_ur3.json, data/pe20_cycle1_pe.json; logs and the 200,000 random scores in data/pe20_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Prior work checked: LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md and STRATEGIES.md (no herd-biology scoring; pe11 used herd features only for seasonality; pe5 found the herd ledger). A quick web search found single-tablet readings of PE herd accounts (sex/age categories), but no blind search that scores assignments by flock demography.
Signs searched (8): M362 (herd header count, incl. |M362+X| and 'name M362'), M367, M346, M006, M362~a, M367~a, M346~a, M006@g. Search set: the 10 herd blocks of MDP 17,096+325+380 (P008294). MDP 17,191 (its copy) and every other herd record are held out for cycle 2.
Model: each sign is X (unconstrained) or one of F, M, YF, YM, Y, T in species s0 or s1. Score = log-likelihood ratio against a fitted generic count law. M|F ~ NegBin(rho*F), rho 0.02-0.30; Y|F ~ NegBin(f*F), f 0.2-1.3 (pastoral lambing/kidding ranges, used as data); YF/YM sex split BetaBinomial(10,10); T = species sum (mixture). The rates and NB dispersion (0.7, 2, 6) are marginalised on grids, per species.
