# X-3 cycle 3: what kind of identity crosses? Syllabograms only, and consonant vs vowel halves
Same model. 1,000-token Y slices, pretraining seeds 0-2 x 3 fine-tune replicates (9 paired runs per cell); 126 jobs (resumed once from the jsonl checkpoint after a container restart), ~2,000 fine-tunes. New: mode 'syl' (only lower-case syllabic labels shared; NUM, logograms, fractions, numeral codes kept apart); conditions cperm (X's syllabograms renamed by a permutation of consonants, vowels kept: 'ka' trained on the row of 'ra') and vperm (vowels permuted, consonants kept: 'ka' on 'ku'). IDc = cperm - real, IDv = vperm - real (> 0 = that half of the value carries transfer). Pairs: LA, LB, GRC, LBa/LBb; Ur III, Akkadian, Elamite readings; AKK / UR3 / ELX -> LA, LB, GRC. Raw: $X3_SCR/res_c3.jsonl.
