Pre-registered for any future vessel test (frozen 6 Oct 2026, before PE-period capacities are seen): with N39C 0.6-0.8 l, PE-period standard vessels should show at least three modes whose pairwise ratios match PE ladder steps (2, 3, 4, 6 within x1.15), and the match must beat 95% of random ladders and random mode sets as in PE-57.3c-d. Fewer than three modes = untestable, not support.
