PE-56 cycle 2: do residues found on half the tablets fire on the unseen half more than forgers that saw everything else?
Date 6 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/pe56_c2.py, tools/pe56_report2.py. Checkpoints data/pe56_ckpt/c2_*.json.
Design (la46 cycle 2 adapted): 3 random half splits per corpus. Half A only: inside-out contest with 40 random
architectures (5-fold, 2 forgeries per tablet), residues = ensemble-mean p < 1e-4. Half B: 30 FRESH random
architectures trained on A + the other B folds (they see every residue instance in A) forge each B tablet twice.
Score: held-out firing ratio = residue instances on real B tablets / expected instances on forged B tablets.
Control set: same families and size, drawn from A's relations with p >= 0.05. Corpora: PE, W1, W2 (forger
worlds), SH1 (shuffle), PL1, PL2 (planted), UR1 (Ur III at PE size), PC1 (proto-cuneiform at PE size).
