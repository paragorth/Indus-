# X-4 cycle 3: the Voynich v31 generator-vs-language classifier, retrained with list-genre languages
# tools/x4_feats.py (32 v31 features, 3,780 samples of 100 words), x4_cycle3.py; data/x4_ckpt/c3.json, c3.log (git-ignored)
| X-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-4.3a | REF0 = v31 as published (1,334 v31 reference samples; LANG = prose only). ~1,000 random classifiers (3-12 random features x LR / kNN / LDA / RF), selected by grouped CV on half A of the corpora (top 10%), re-tested on half B (>= 0.5); survivors vote. Null: corpus labels permuted | 82 survivors; null 0 / 499. Voynich ZL 77% GEN (IT 81%; v31 had 83 / 86%), own X-4 Voynich samples 72% GEN. KNOWN list languages, never seen in training: Linear B words 68% INVENT, Linear B entries 60% MAGIC, Ur III words 57% INVENT, Ur III entries 46% GEN, proto-cuneiform 37% MAGIC; LANG 4-10% for all of them; syllabic Greek prose LANG 7%. LA and PE: spread over INVENT / GIBB / GEN / MAGIC, LANG 7-16% | v31 replicated (A). But its LANG class does not recognise a single known language written in a syllabary or in list form; so the v31 'not language' axis is a prose-in-an-alphabet axis |
| X-4.3b | REF1 = REF0 + list-genre languages (LB, UR3 words and entries, proto-cuneiform entries) + syllabic Greek + X-4 prose re-encodings in LANG. Planted: generators fitted to LA and PE (TRI, SLOT, CPV, WBG) scored as test objects | 94 survivors. LA 97% LANG, LA entries 98%, PE entries 94%, BUT their fitted generators also 88-96% LANG (planted control fails). Voynich 61% GEN, 29% LANG (ZL v31 samples 73% GEN) | Kill: the classifier learns 'syllabic / list texture = language', not language. LA and PE LANG votes are void |
| X-4.3c | REF2 = REF1 + generators fitted to the known list corpora (TRI, SLOT, CPV, WBG of LB, UR3, PC) in GEN; plus REF2 with one family left out and scored (honest votes) | 97-101 survivors; null 0 / 496. Honest held-out votes: Linear B words 89-91% LANG (entries 31-44% LANG), Ur III words 71% GEN, Ur III entries 95% GEN, proto-cuneiform 98% GEN. LA 56-91% GEN (LA entries 91%), PE entries 93-99% GEN, and their fitted generators the same (61-100% GEN). Voynich ZL 49-57% GEN, 15-20% LANG, 28-32% GIBB; X-4 Voynich 41-47% GEN, 21-34% LANG | Grade B: once list languages and list generators are both in the reference, a known language (Ur III Sumerian, words or entries) is called a generator more firmly (71-95%) than the Voynich is (41-57%). Only Linear B words stay LANG. LA and PE fall with Ur III and proto-cuneiform (and with their own generators), so the classifier cannot tell them from a generator or from a known accounting language |
| X-4.3d | Does the Voynich's generator vote survive the list-genre reference? (REF0 -> REF2) | GEN 77% -> 49-57% (ZL), 72% -> 41-47% (X-4 samples); still the plurality class, with LANG 15-34% and GIBB 21-32% taking the rest | Grade B: the v31 generator verdict is partly a reference-set effect (it weakens by a third), but it does not flip to LANG; the Voynich does not look like a list language either (its votes differ from LB, UR3, PC, LA, PE, which are all near-unanimous) |
