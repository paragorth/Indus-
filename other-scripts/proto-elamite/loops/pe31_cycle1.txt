pe31 cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): calibration of the interchangeability statistic before any real PE pair was looked at.
Name-like strings: PE entry lines (header excluded), no x or lacuna, final class sign stripped, >= 2 signs left (1,9xx strings).
Statistics per sign pair (signs with >= 6 tokens): MP minimal-pair frames; CTX v35 index (split by tablet halves);
POS position-profile cosine; TAB tablet co-occurrence (negative = complementary); EV Dirichlet-multinomial evidence
for merging the two signs' left and right neighbour distributions. All z-scored against frequency-matched pairs
(same pair of frequency bins). Truth: Ur III and OB seal names (same value, different index: a/a2, li/li2 ...),
Linear B doublets (a/a2/a3, ra/ra2/ra3, ro/ro2, ta/ta2, pu/pu2), and 10 PE signs split into two planted homophones
(per token, or per tablet as a scribe allograph), 3 repeats each. Scripts tools/pe31_lib.py, pe31_cycle1.py, pe31_cycle1b.py.
