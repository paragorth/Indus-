PE-62 cycle 2: FILL IN THE GAPS AND SEE WHAT CHANGES
Date 6 Oct 2026. Scripts: tools/pe62_c2.py, tools/pe62_export.py. Checkpoints data/pe62_ckpt/c2_*.json (git-ignored).
Output: data/pe62_imputed_classes.tsv (4,745 unmarked PE entries: imputed class and confidence, with numerals and blind).

The top-15 ensembles of cycle 1 were retrained on every labelled entry and used to fill in all unmarked entries. Three gains were
tested, each with the number-blind ensemble, so that the numerals being predicted were never seen: (i) do the filled-in classes
predict the entry's own numerals (system + size bin, P(key | class) fitted on labelled entries)? (ii) do they make totals
close on tablets whose numerals can be read either as capacity or as counts? (iii) do they predict the tablet's number system
(a stand-in for the pe59 grammar's system layer)? Nulls: imputations permuted across all unmarked entries and within each
tablet (500x); for (ii) a coin per tablet (2,000x). Calibration: the same score on masked labelled held-out entries, where the
truth is known, and on proto-cuneiform.

Verdict cycle 2 (B, negative): filling in what the clerk left out changes almost nothing. Imputed classes are the tablet's own
class 92% of the time, add 0 closing totals, add +0.009 bits/entry to the numerals and +2.3 points to the number-system guess.
The calibration shows why: on labelled entries whose class is known, the number-blind imputation carries no entry-level
information about numerals in PE or PC. The clerk left the class out where it was already implied by the tablet. The
tablets themselves are the class marker.
