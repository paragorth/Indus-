PE-65 cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026). ONE STATE OR MANY OFFICES ACROSS IRAN? Port of LA-58 to Proto-Elamite.
Prior work: agent-based models of Mesopotamian settlement exist (MASS / Wilkinson et al.), and pe6 fitted one simulated economy by ABC; no study fits simulated multi-site paperwork to the Proto-Elamite site distribution. Not a repeat of pe17/pe48 (sign mix by site) or pe6 (single economy).
Simulator tools/pe65_sim.c (C): 7 world kinds over 5 sites (Susa hub + Yahya, Malyan, Sialk, Sofalin): T one state (random centre + dependents), S capital + satellite offices (shared officials and personnel), P independent offices sharing a script, I temple-estate centre receiving offerings, M trading houses (lateral), H mobile herding offices (1-4 offices, each moving along a random route of >= 2 sites, writing at whichever site they stand, own header and herders' names), N no institutions; 33 nuisance parameters (lexicons, Zipf slope, list length, headers, totals, amounts, sealing, travel of sealed tablets, short notes, office sharing, personnel mobility, survival by document type, Susa sub-office vocabularies and how strongly they map onto excavation batches). The centre is drawn uniformly over the 5 sites (Susa is not privileged). Paperwork: one opaque 'word' per line (the line's sign string without numerals), numbers, totals; documents = tablet / short note (<= 1 numbered line) / sealed tablet. Thinned to the real survival count of every panel unit: Susa dealt into 3 batches (A = MDP 17, 455; B = MDP 26 + 26S, 636; C = MDP 06, TCL 32, MDP 31, CahDAFI etc., 411), Yahya 27, Malyan 22, Sialk 12, Sofalin 11. Panel of 114 statistics (13 per unit + 21 pair overlaps + 2 global), same C code for real corpora. Bank: 300,000 worlds (data/pe65_ckpt/bank_PE). ABC-RF (forests for type, roles, parents, 'Susa-centred state' = T/S/I with Susa as centre); 4,000 held-out worlds for calibration. Controls drawn at exactly the PE shape (same bank): Ur III (CDLI; hub Umma split into random batches, Puzrish-Dagan 27, Girsu 22, Ur 12, Nippur 11; truth one state) and Linear B (DAMOS; hub KN, PY 27, TH 22, MY 12, TI 11; truth separate palaces), 5 thinning replicates each. No sign values used.
