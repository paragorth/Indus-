pe20 cycle 2 -- re-test outside the search set: held-out herd records, the chained herd lists, random biologies
Tools: tools/pe20_cycle2.py (AB | C). Numbers: data/pe20_cycle2_AB.json, data/pe20_cycle2_C.json; logs in data/pe20_ckpt/.
Held-out set: 35 herd records from 22 tablets (every PE record with >= 2 of the 8 signs, except MDP 17,096+ and its copy MDP 17,191; the herd lists 17,085 and 17,097 are excluded as records). Most held-out records have only 2-3 of the signs, so the test is weaker than the search.
