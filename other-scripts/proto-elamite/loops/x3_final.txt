# X-3 final: which script teaches which (transfer learning across ten corpora, with relabelling controls)
| X-<n>.<cycle> | method and control | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| X-3.final | Three cycles (tools/x3_build.py, x3_common.py, x3_run.py, x3_sum.py, x3_matrix.py; ~7,300 fine-tunes, ~560 pretrainings; summaries and gzipped raw results in data/x3/). Small neural 4-gram model pretrained on 8,000 tokens of X, fine-tuned on 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 tokens of Y, scored on held-out Y. Ten corpora: LA, PE, VOY, LB, proto-cuneiform, Ur III admin, Akkadian, Elamite, Latin letters, Iliad respelled as a CV syllabary. Hittite / Hurrian too small in CDLI (27 / 105 lines). Controls: X shuffled, X relabelled (and a second independent relabelling as the null), X relabelled within frequency bands, random Markov text, pretrained body with fresh embeddings, consonant-only and vowel-only relabelling; positives: LB half -> half, planted hidden-grammar pair, LB -> respelled Greek; negatives: Latin -> Akkadian, Latin -> EVA | (1) Identity transfer is measurable only through a shared transliteration: label mode finds it (LB half 1.2-1.7 bits/token, planted pair 0.2-0.55, LB -> Greek 0.1-0.3, Sumerian <-> Akkadian 0.1-0.3; null 95th pct 0.075-0.077), but rank mode is blind: the same planted grammar with signs renamed gives 0.005 bits (cycle 1), and low-data rank 'identity' is only the Zipf prior (gone under band relabelling). (2) Structure: administrative-line corpora (PC, PE, LA, AKK, LB) teach each other 0.05-0.4 bits, surviving relabelling (PC -> LB +0.43, PC -> LA +0.33, PE -> LA +0.27 at 500 tokens; identity part 0); Latin letters and Voynich teach nothing; Voynich learns from no corpus (all 9 teachers -0.01 to -0.19). (3) LA, PE, VOY among themselves: no identity transfer detectable (no shared labels) and structural transfer only at the generic admin-format level. (4) LA <-> LB syllabic identity +0.12 / +0.13 (both directions, beyond NUM and logograms; consonant and vowel halves both carry it); LA <-> Greek 0.00-0.03; Akkadian / Sumerian -> LA syllabic 0.01-0.04 (the cycle-2 0.10 was the NUM label); PC -> PE numerals +0.18 at 500 tokens | **Who teaches whom is genre, not kinship.** Accounting tablets teach accounting tablets whatever the script; prose teaches nothing; Voynich neither teaches nor learns. The only identity-level links found are the ones built into the transliterations (LA with LB values, cuneiform readings, PE / PC numeral codes). Not cracked; nothing read |

Grades:
- A: identity-level transfer requires shared labels; frequency rank cannot stand in for identity (planted renamed grammar: 0.005 bits). So this method cannot test whether LA, PE and Voynich share signs with each other or with anything else.
- A: Voynich is isolated in transfer space (learns from none of 9 corpora, teaches none). This matches X-1, where it groups with alphabetic prose, and it fails to learn even from Latin letters.
- B: LA and PE are learnable from any administrative corpus, and the gain survives relabelling (structure: short lines, final numerals, Zipf profile). PE -> LA is a top teacher, but its identity part is 0 (IDb -0.01).
- B (replication, not new): LB syllabic values carry information about LA sign identities in both directions (+0.12-0.13 bits/token, null 0.075), with both consonant and vowel halves. LA does not share Greek's syllable statistics (+0.00-0.03), while LB does (+0.17).
- C: Elamite -> LA syllabic identity +0.10 (Elamite corpus 4.7k tokens; total transfer negative; pair null -0.04). Would support: the same with a larger Elamite readings corpus and in the reverse direction. Would kill: it falls below 0.075 with more data or another seed set.
- Rejected: Akkadian -> LA identity (cycle 2, +0.10). It disappears when NUM is not shared (+0.01).
