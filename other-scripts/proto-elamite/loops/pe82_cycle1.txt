I1 INK WHERE THE READER WOULD STUMBLE (uniform-information writer). A writer who optimises for a reader spends
   optional signs exactly where the reader, from the rest of the tablet, would mispredict the kind of entry (its
   number system). Reader models = random context predictors (header sign, previous line, look-ahead, tablet
   majority, position, surface) trained leave-one-batch-out. Hypotheses = thousands of (sign set x reader model) pairs;
   score = within (tablet x system x length) covariance of 'marker present' with the reader's surprisal. Survivors
   selected on discovery batches, re-tested on held-out batches. Controls: sign strings shuffled within (tablet x system
   x length) strata (kill); planted UID marker on the real skeleton (power); proto-cuneiform with pre-registered
   goods/commodity signs (do known commodity signs come out as stumble-markers?). Conventional? No: it reads the
   writer's effort budget, never a sign value. PICKED first.
I2 WHAT THE READER COULD NOT HAVE KNOWN (novelty tolerance). A reader can absorb a sign never seen before only where
   context fixes its role. Fit, on discovery batches, thousands of random context definitions; score each by how well
   'novelty tolerance' of a context predicts where signs unseen in discovery appear in held-out batches. Then classify
   known signs by how often they sit in novelty-tolerant slots (open class = names; closed class = codes). Controls:
   novelty labels shuffled within line-length strata; planted open-class slot; PC calibration (personal-name-like vs
   pre-registered goods signs). Second.
I3 WRITERS PROTECT WHAT IS WORTH STEALING (security economics). Entries covered by an arithmetic check (a closing total
   that equals the sum) are tamper-evident. Random sign sets scored by 'protection rate' beyond tablet length and
   system; held out by batch; outside ruler = pe80 frozen size ranking (sha256 b692200a...), fixed before this loop.
   Controls: totals re-dealt among same-length tablets; PC calibration with goods of known value. Third.
I4 HOMOGRAPH LOAD (where must the reader use context?). Same sign, two behaviours (bimodal magnitude or system) by
   context; random context partitions; held out by batch; PC calibration with conventional polyvalent signs.
   Kept in reserve.
I5 HOW MANY REGISTERS DOES THE READER CARRY? Random finite-state readers that carry 0-3 tokens of state from line to
   line, scored on predicting the next entry's system; topic-setting vs state-needing signs. DROPPED: overlaps pe59
   cache models and pe9 tablet-topic cache, and I1 already uses the reader's context.
I6 INVERSE REINFORCEMENT OF THE WRITER'S ORDERING OBJECTIVE. DROPPED: equals pe13 canonical-order search.
I7 READER-COMPETENCY FACTORISATION (minimal vocabularies of specialist readers). DROPPED: pe8 forms, pe18 genres,
   pe49 clerk habits, topic models in the literature (Born et al. 2019).
Order: I1, I2, I3 (I4 if one dies early).

Pre-registration for I1 (written before any PE score was computed):
 - Target: line system (common.system_of; C, C*, B, N23, S-frac, SDB, mod*). Lacuna lines dropped. Signs = base forms, 'x' dropped.
 - Statistic: for marker set M and reader model R, Z = sum over strata (tablet, system, n-signs) of cov(present, surprisal),
   standardised by its exact within-stratum permutation variance.
 - Discovery: batches MDP 26 + MDP 26S + other; confirmation: MDP 17 + MDP 06 + TCL 32/MDP 31 (and the reverse split).
 - Survivor: discovery z >= 3.5 AND Bonferroni-free replication: confirmation z >= 2 with the same sign.
 - Kill: survivors on the stratum-shuffled corpus >= real survivors (mean of 3 shuffles), or planted recovery < 50%.
 - Also length-level UID: n-signs vs surprisal within (tablet, system).
