pe80 cycle 2 (7 Oct 2026): TAPHONOMY AS A LIBRARY CARD (candidate C2 of pe80_cycle1.txt).
Idea: breakage and surface wear are written by a tablet's afterlife (kept in a jar and consulted, or thrown out and trampled), not by the scribe. If what is written predicts how a tablet broke or wore, beyond its size, thickness, line count and excavation/editor batch, damage is an outside label of document life. Web search and LITERATURE/FINDINGS/STRATEGIES: damage was only ever a filter (pe73, pe74, pe75), never a target.
Tools: tools/pe80_c2.py (PE), tools/pe80_c2_ur3.py (calibration). Checkpoints data/pe80_ckpt/c2_*.json.
Targets: BREAK = share of lines that lost text; WEAR = share of surviving tokens marked '#', minus the expected '#' rate of the tablet's sign mix (leave-tablet-out), so hard-to-read sign shapes do not count. Both residualised on volume fixed effects, log area, thickness, log lines. Features are rates over intact entries only (tablets with >= 3 intact entries: 574), so a broken tablet does not lose them mechanically (first run with presence features gave 'no header', 'number of systems' and 'reverse share' as top hits: pure mechanics; removed). Null: residual permuted within volume x line-count tercile; FWER by max statistic; half A train, half B replicate (one-sided p < 0.01, same sign).
