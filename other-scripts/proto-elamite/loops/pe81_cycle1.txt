K1 THE TEXT SORTS THE SITES, THE WEATHER JUDGES. Ignore what any sign means. Thousands of random text statistics (1-3 features
   from ~180: system mix, magnitudes, layout, sign presence) are scored per site; survivors are statistics whose ordering of the
   five writing sites (Susa, Malyan, Yahya, Sialk, Sofalin) repeats between disjoint tablet halves more often than when tablets
   are re-dealt among sites. The survivors' consensus site axes are frozen (sha256) BEFORE any environmental number is fetched.
   Outside test: NASA POWER long-term climate at each site (precipitation primary; temperature, humidity secondary), elevation,
   distance from Susa, settlement area. Primary test is outposts only (Susa is extreme on everything and already known to differ:
   pe18, pe65). Controls: site-shuffled corpus (kill), planted rulers at real sizes (power), 24/120 ruler permutations (p).
   Web (7 Oct 2026): no study linking PE site text profiles to climate or environment; la55 used hectares for Linear A only.
   Conventional decipherer? No: it never asks what a sign means, and the answer key is the weather. PICKED.

Pre-registration (written before any site statistic or environmental value is computed or fetched):
 - Primary: Spearman between the frozen OUTPOST-only consensus axis (PC1 of survivor site scores, outposts only) and annual
   precipitation; p = rank among the 24 orderings of the 4 outposts (exact). Pass needs p <= 1/24 AND planted power >= 50%.
 - Secondary (descriptive, Bonferroni x5): temperature, relative humidity, elevation, distance from Susa, area.
 - Kill: survivor count not above the site-shuffled null (95th pct), or planted recovery < 50%.
 AMENDMENT (before any statistic was computed): the PC axis has no sign, so an undirected 4-outpost ordering can reach at best
 p = 2/24 = 0.083. The primary test is therefore DIRECTED: text-only 'Susa-likeness' of each outpost (mean standardized distance
 of the outpost's score from Susa's over the stable statistics; frozen). Primary prediction: outposts whose climate is closer to
 Susa's (|log precipitation - log precipitation at Susa|) write more like Susa. One-sided Spearman over 4 outposts, exact p over
 24 orderings (floor 0.042). Secondary (directed, Bonferroni x4): distance from Susa (farther = less Susa-like), |temperature
 difference|, |elevation difference|; descriptive: area. Undirected PC1/PC2 vs every ruler is reported as descriptive only.
