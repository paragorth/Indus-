Units: PE entries (numbered lines, header dropped; 674 tablets with >= 3 entries) and PE lines (numbered and unnumbered). Features: first sign, last (class) sign, numeral system, lead numeral unit, size bin, entry length, size x length, numbered or not, presence of each of the 60 commonest signs. Statistic: lag-p agreement (same category at distance p on one tablet). COMB(f, p) = mean relative excess at multiples of p (<= 12) minus mean at other lags 2-12 (lag 1 is fitted): a weave makes a comb, topic or sections do not.
