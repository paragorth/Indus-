pe47 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026): if tablets are printouts of one sheet, numbers should link tablets ARITHMETICALLY.
Script tools/pe47_links.py (summary tools/pe47_c2_sum.py); results data/pe47_ckpt/c2.json.
(a) SUMMARY -> DETAIL: an entry on tablet S equals the computed (unwritten) sum of tablet D's obverse entries of one system, or of D's entries that share a last token. (b) PRINTED TWICE: two tablets share >= 2 entries with the same last token and the same rare value ('twins'), or >= 2 rare values whatever the tokens ('value twins'). Rare = value >= 5 and seen <= 3 times in its system. Nulls (20 each, 3 for full Ur III): N1 values shuffled across tablets within (last token, system); N2 entries re-dealt between tablets. Links are then validated with information the numbers never saw: token overlap of S and D, whether the summary entry names a sign of D, and (Ur III) same king-year / month of the catalogue date.
