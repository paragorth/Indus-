 C3.1 Second, independent source: CDLI line art (1,440 PE drawings, 150 ppi scans; mostly the excavators' hand drawings in
      the MDP volumes, drawn about a century before the CDLI photographs, by different people, with different 'lighting'
      (none) and no segmentation threshold on clay). Outline = filled outer contour of the drawing nearest the image centre
      in its upper part; ink-proof variant = convex hull of all strokes of that drawing. Credibility: drawing aspect vs
      catalogue aspect rho must exceed 0.6, and drawing corner fill vs photo corner fill rho must be positive.
      Test: partial r (pe81 covariates) of header vs drawing corner fill (all corners; top end; bottom end).
      Kill: r < 0.10 for the top end on the drawings (the photo effect is 0.43). Pass: >= 0.20. In between: damaged.
      Note the drawings share one weakness with the photos: a draughtsman also sees the header; hull and top/bottom split
      are reported, and the reverse-face photo test (cycle 1) is the guard against ink.
