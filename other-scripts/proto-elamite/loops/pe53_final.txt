Best age and sex assignment: none supported. The nominal top (M362, M362~a young; M367, M006 adult female) is C-: would support a new herd tablet where M362~a/M362 ratios co-vary with an independently dated season; would kill: (already) pe20's reading of M362 as the reference count, and the written-only re-run.
Within-year order: not recoverable (the 'order' is presence or absence of an M362 count).
Seasonal signs: none.
Plain answer: the biology is real and the Ur III books show it (lambs peak in about January), but only when hundreds of dated receipts are pooled. A single tablet's young share is set by what kind of record it is, not by when it was written, so the lambs cannot tell the month of an undated tablet. The calibrated Ur III control fails, the planted archive is recovered only roughly, and every PE 'season' collapses into record type. Not cracked; nothing read.
