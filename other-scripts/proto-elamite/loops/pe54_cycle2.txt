pe54 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026): do composition links PREDICT held-out sightings? Script tools/pe54_cycle2.py; numbers data/pe54_ckpt/c2_{pe,plant,ur3}.json (git-ignored).
Design: tablets split in halves (PE 20 splits, plant and Ur III 12). The linking hypothesis is searched on the train half only (PE 3,000 random hyps + climb 3; plant 2,000; Ur III 600). Each held-out class cell is predicted from the train sighting that best predicts the held-out entry's OTHER classes (posterior over partner and gap), in bits per cell against the background (value of a random train entry, same noise). Baselines: same hypothesis with a random partner (random linking, same demographic rules); a random hypothesis; 'flock unchanged' (identity, gap 0). Null: every split repeated on a within-class shuffled corpus. Zero convention zc.
