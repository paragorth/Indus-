pe9 cycle 3 -- depleted vs inexhaustible signs, and the pre-registered test on 52 reserved tablets
(tools/pe9_cycle3.py; data/pe9_cycle3.json, .log). Predictions P1-P4 were fixed in loops/pe9_cycle2.txt and in
the script before the reserved tablets were opened.

Plain result: on tablets never used in fitting, signs already used on a tablet are 2-3x (second use) and more
(third use) as likely to be used again; a counter bag with one or two copies of each sign is excluded, and no
sign behaves like a depleted counter. The per-sign classes and the bag-vs-model separation by the 3+ statistic
are too weak at 52 tablets.
