 K1 ASSEMBLY RULES / CHECKERBOARDS. Treat each tablet as an island and each sign form as a species. Ecology's
    fixed-fixed (curveball) null keeps every tablet's richness and every sign's incidence; pairs that co-occur LESS
    than it allows are 'checkerboards' (competitive exclusion). In a script, exclusion + identical context = the same
    thing written two ways by different hands/offices/periods (hidden allographs, office synonyms). Massive random
    guessing over thousands of random 2-4-form 'exclusion guilds', selected on MDP 26/26S, re-tested on all other
    volumes. Controls: planted allographs (a frequent form renamed on a random half of its tablets), curveball-
    randomised pipelines (survivor-count null), and calibration on PE and proto-cuneiform same-base ~variant pairs
    (natural positives). CHOSEN for cycle 1.
 K2 CHARACTER DISPLACEMENT. Signs that meet on the same tablet in the same slot ('sympatric') should differ more in
    their numbers than the same signs where they occur apart, if the clerk kept near-synonyms apart; yields contrast
    sets (semantic neighbours). Control: sympatry relabelled by curveball; planted displaced pair. Cycle 2 candidate.
 K3 NESTEDNESS / EXTINCTION ORDER. Are small tablets nested subsets of big ones (NODF vs curveball)? The order in which
    forms drop out as tablets shrink is the clerk's priority list (core vs optional information). Calibrate on PC and
    planted priority. Cycle 3 candidate.
 K4 LAW OF ABBREVIATION FOR FUSION. Pairs written both as A B and as |A+B|: does frequency (or tablet type) predict
    fusion? Fused pairs = lexicalised phrases. Partly near pe34; held back.
 K5 NUMERIC CROSSTALK. Does a tablet's numbers anchor its museum neighbour's numbers (clerk carry-over)? Near pe29;
    held back.
 K6 FRAGMENT PHYSICS. Fragment-size distribution as a trace of how the archive was destroyed (dropped vs crushed vs
    dug). Not aimed at meaning; dropped.

K1 details (frozen in data/pe87_ckpt/plan_c1.json with sha256):
 - Unit: tablet x sign FORM incidence (variant letters kept, compounds kept whole; 'x' dropped); forms on >= 6 tablets.
 - Null: curveball trades only between tablets of the same stratum (strata = header family {M157, M327-family, other,
   none} x capacity numerals present {y/n} x volume group), so topic/office blocks are kept; 300 null matrices per set.
 - seg_z(a,b) = (null mean - observed joint tablets) / null sd. ctx(a,b) = cosine of context vectors (left sign, right
   sign, position class, numeral system of the line), computed on the same split.
 - Calibration (natural positives): same-base ~variant pairs vs frequency-matched different-base pairs; AUC of seg_z,
   ctx and seg_z + ctx-z. PC: same on 5,257 proto-cuneiform tablets (Uruk IV/III), strata = period x capacity.
 - Guessing: EVERY different-base pair of forms with >= 6 train tablets (exhaustive, tens of thousands; a random
   draw of 6,000 pairs would miss a planted pair 80% of the time) plus 6,000 random 3-4-form guilds; score =
   mean pairwise seg_z + mean pairwise ctx z on TRAIN; top 50 of each re-tested on TEST (own curveball null); survivor =
   test mean seg_z >= 1.64 and test ctx above the 75th percentile of random different-base pairs.
 - Shuffled control: the whole pipeline on curveball-randomised train and test incidence (20 runs): survivor null.
 - Planted: 6 forms with >= 20 tablets renamed to 'PLANT_k' on a random half of their tablets; must appear in
   >= 4/6 of planted pairs among the top-50 and survive held out.
 Kill lines: real survivors <= 95th percentile of the shuffled pipeline -> K1 killed as a reader of hidden allographs.
 Calibration AUC (same-base variants) < 0.6 on PE AND PC -> the score cannot see allographs; any survivor is C- only.
