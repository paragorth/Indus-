# pe27 NUMBERS IN TWO LANGUAGES, cycle 1 (4 Oct 2026): tablet-level exact-ratio scan between number systems
# tools/pe27_common.py, tools/pe27_cycle1.py; checkpoint data/pe27_ckpt/cycle1.json (git-ignored)
# Idea: a tablet with two number systems may write the same quantity twice (count of people/animals and their
# grain in capacity units). Every pair (count line x, capacity line y) on a tablet gives an exact ratio y/x
# (capacity in N39C units, a-priori value set). H(r) = number of tablets carrying ratio r. Null: the capacity
# line-sets re-dealt among the tablets that carry both systems (200 permutations), Poisson score against the
# null mean, search-corrected by the max score over all r in each permutation.

A few odd fractions (85/21) come from near-duplicate texts | PASS: the scan returns the known ration rates |
