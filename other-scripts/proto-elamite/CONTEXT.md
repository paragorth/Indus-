# Proto-Elamite: history, geography and interactions (context for new strategies)

Compiled 4 Oct 2026. Archaeological data only; nobody's sign readings are used as evidence. Distances: great-circle, computed here from approximate coordinates (±10 km); roads are longer.

## Time frame
- Script in use c. 3100–2900 BCE (Susa Acropole I levels 16–14B). Dahl et al. 2013: four writing stages, from single-sign notations (17AX) to long entries (14B). Overlaps Late Uruk / Uruk III–Jemdet Nasr. ([Yeganeh et al. 2025, ORA](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:a8f083aa-bb85-458b-bb40-915206867912))
- Before it (Susa II / Uruk): tokens, bullae (most from Susa), numerical tablets; the last also at Godin, Hissar, Habuba Kabira, Jebel Aruda, Tell Brak. ([Wikipedia: PE script](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Elamite_script))
- After it: Susa IV turns back to Mesopotamian culture; Linear Elamite only c. 2300–1880 BCE (LITERATURE.md item 8).
- Phases: Susa III, Banesh (Fars), Yahya IVC, Shahr-i Sokhta I, Sialk IV.

## Movement of tablets, sealings and craftsmen
- Handheld XRF of 35 Susa, 20 Malyan and 15 Yahya tablets plus sealings (Yeganeh, Holakooei, Nokandeh, Piran & Dahl 2025, *JAS Reports* 61:104973):
  - Sealings are local everywhere; Malyan ABC and TUV sealings form separate clay groups (per-sector storage control).
  - Tablets moved: Susa ST-11 = Yahya clay; Malyan MT-03 = Yahya, MT-20 = Susa; Yahya YT-02, YT-06 = Malyan, YT-01, YT-07 = Susa.
  - Signatures: Susa high S (gypsum); Malyan Ca ~14 wt% (dolomitic); Yahya Ca ~4.5 wt%, Fe ~4.5 wt%, Cr ~300 ppm.
  - The texts record no transaction in the stones and metals that archaeology shows moving.
- Ring scrapers (INAA) do not match local pottery: potters moved site to site across the Uruk/Proto-Elamite world (Alden & Minc 2016, [doi 10.1016/j.jasrep.2016.03.022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jasrep.2016.03.022)).
- Our corpus: 183 of 1,585 tablets carry a seal note in the ATF; most seal designs are unidentified (PESxxxx).

## Goods and routes
- Lapis lazuli: Badakhshan (Sar-i Sang) origin for over half the tested Shahr-i Sokhta pieces, one Pamir source; Shahr-i Sokhta holds the only excavated lapis workshops (c. 3200–2300 BCE) ([Turin](https://iris.unito.it/handle/2318/2096024)).
- Copper: Arisman smelted arsenical copper and speiss on a large scale (c. 20 t of slag), using ores including Talmessi (Anarak), over 500 km away ([Tübingen](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/handle/10900/51674)). Tin bronze comes centuries later.
- Chlorite: carved vessels of the Halil Rud (Jiroft) are mostly 2500–2200 BCE (Konar Sandal radiocarbon), later than PE; Konar Sandal's "geometric" tablets are a separate system ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Konar_Sandal)).
- Routes: the High Road (Mesopotamia–Godin–Hissar–east); Khuzistan–Fars via the Zagros; Fars–Kerman–Sistan along the southern plateau.

## Seasons and herding
- Khuzistan lowlands: 300–500 mm rain, all in winter (Nov–Dec rain is critical); summer pasture dies and water dries up ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Khuzestan)).
- Bakhtiari transhumance (modern analogue): routes up to 300 km; spring move from c. 21 March lasts 15–45 days, return in autumn 8–30 days; summer camps May–September; high passes shut c. 3 months; only 5–7 usable cols, so routes and campsites are fixed ([Iranica](https://iranicaonline.org/articles/baktiari-tribe)).
- Herd accounts are read by Dahl 2005 (*SMEA* 47) as goats and sheep by sex and age; a hypothesis for us, not evidence.
- Malyan fauna (Zeder 1991; [Open Context](https://opencontext.org/projects/d6b25ec9-2884-4e3c-00e8-0c5a6472fa63)): administrative areas got narrow age ranges and butchered cuts; two supply systems.
- Rations: bevelled-rim bowls, c. 10 cm high, 18 cm wide, mass-produced, three quarters of Uruk-site pottery ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beveled_rim_bowl)).
- Political models (early state, nomadic confederation, chiefdom) are unsettled.

## Out-of-the-box ideas
Each: idea / data / control that could kill it / why it is not a standard move.

1. **Flock arithmetic.** Assign herd-entry signs to random roles (adult F/M, young F/M, other), 100,000 draws, scored by a Leslie-matrix likelihood (young ≤ ~1.5 per female, young sex ratio ~1:1); survivors must fit the chained tablets MDP 17,191 → 17,096+ (pe5). / Our herd-office tablets; FAO fertility ranges; Ur III Drehem lines. / Numerals shuffled within tablet; Drehem roles must be recovered blind. / Reads signs through animal biology.
2. **Predict the clay.** Random-feature classifiers on the 74 plateau tablets vs Susa flag "plateau-looking" Susa tablets; predict which hXRF-tested Susa tablets are foreign (ST-11; SS-16, ST-24/31/32/33). / Yeganeh et al. 2025 Table S2 plus a map from ST/MT/YT numbers to museum numbers. / Site labels shuffled; chemistry-sample tablets withheld from training. / Scored by geochemistry outside the corpus.
3. **A seal means the tablet left.** Sealings stay home, tablets travel: 10,000 random content predictors of the 183 seal flags; are sealed tablets weaker in the tablet pool (pe9), richer in totals, commoner off-Susa? / Seal notes in pe_raw.atf; CDLI photos. / Seal flags shuffled within publication volume. / Uses the object's physical life.
4. **Decimal as the highland register.** Do Susa decimal tablets share more vocabulary with plateau tablets than sexagesimal ones (rarefied overlap, 10,000 resamples)? / Our corpus; Uruk proto-cuneiform outgroup. / System labels permuted within tablet-size bins. / Treats number base as a geographic dialect.
5. **Blind seriation.** Order all tablets by evolving traits (entry length, compounds, headers, systems) with random-start annealing; check against the 23 stratified CahDAFI tablets (17–14B) and early Sofalin/Ozbaki. / CahDAFI levels (Le Brun 1971; Dahl et al. 2013). / Trait-shuffled tablets; random orders. / Treats the corpus as a fossil series.
6. **The itinerant scribe.** As potters moved, fingerprint tablets by variant choice (~a/~b, compound vs sequence) and test whether off-Susa tablets match particular Susa "hands" above chance, decaying with distance (Sialk 353, Malyan 463, Yahya 931 km). / Variant suffixes in the ATF (1,641 forms). / Variants shuffled within base sign; a planted travelling hand. / Models people moving, not meanings.
