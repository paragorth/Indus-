Grades:
- A (method): the doubling fingerprint separates visual copying, meaning-level repetition and chance on 11 of 13 controls with a known mechanism; it does not separate reduplication from tally or list ditto; self-calibration r 0.70-0.98 (V-78.2.1).
- B (measurement): the Voynich has no adjacency rule. A payload token recurs one word later as often as two or three words later (spike 0.98-0.99, both transcriptions, all leaf halves 0.96-1.02), unlike any language's words (0.04-0.23), any doubling mechanism (1.5-2.7) and like the generators (0.86-1.11) (V-78.1.1).
- B: Voynich doubles are not visual copies: the second word is re-spelled (k~t, ch~sh, e~ee, q-) at least as often as other recurrences in the same line (6/6 halves/transcriptions), unlike raw-copy generators and planted slips (V-78.1.3). With the mixture fit, copying slips (v63) and copy-and-vary self-citation are bounded to <= ~0.11 of doubles at 90% (V-78.2.2).
- B (negative): the token is not a word, syllable or letter of Latin, Italian, German, Tagalog or Malay under the Voynich pipeline: letters match the RATE of doubling but avoid neighbours and lack line re-use (V-78.3.1).
- B (exists) / C (size): lag-1 doubles have their own small type preference beyond every generator (z 7-11 vs <= 3.6: chor chor, chcky, chckdy) (V-78.1.2).
- C (guess): the Voynich token behaves like a symbol of a notation or table (a value drawn from the line's own small stock, alternating, with no neighbour ban), not a unit of running language; the only meaningful stream with this shape among those tested is chant notation (V-78.3.2). Would support: another non-linguistic notation (step lists, tables, tablature) with spike ~1, line 1.2-1.3, lag2 > adj at a rate near 0.03. Would kill: a language text at any unit size, or a word-order-free list, giving the same four numbers.
- C (guess): in Currier B text short two-word phrases (ol/qol + chedy/shey) are a repeatable unit, repeated with free re-spelling (ABAB 2.8-3.2x shuffle vs generators <= 1.86; concentrated in f75-f86 and f103-f113) (V-78.4.1-2). Would support: the same pairs and excess on a third transcription; would kill: the excess vanishing without f75v, or a B-fitted line-aware generator reaching abab >= 3.
- Demoted: v75's reading of doubling as 'a doubling mark (the same again, a plural or a count)' -> C-: at most ~0.2 of doubles have a meaning-level signature, and the model cannot say whether that part is plural, ditto or count.

What it implies about the text: the property that made the Voynich look unlike every designation system is mostly the ABSENCE of the neighbour ban that grammar imposes on words of running language, together with line-local re-use of a small stock. If the text carries meaning, its written unit is not a word in a sentence; it is closer to an entry in a row or a symbol in a notation, where the same value may stand next to itself. No value or referent is assigned. Not cracked.
