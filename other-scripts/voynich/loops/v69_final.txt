v69 WHERE THE SCRIBE SLOWED DOWN, final (5 Oct 2026). Cycles: loops/v69_cycle1-6.txt. Tools: tools/v69_lib.py, v69_c1.py ... v69_c6.py. Checkpoints: data/v69_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Images stayed in the scratch cache.

Idea: a writer of meaningful text slows down for unfamiliar words (names, technical terms, rare words, first mentions) and dashes off familiar ones; slow writing should show as larger, more upright, more evenly spaced glyphs with more pen lifts. 8 per-word care features were measured on 11,576 Voynich words (Q20 f103-f116 + f58r/v; Beinecke scans, v18 word boxes) and 9,409 Latin control words (28 CREMMA-Medieval-Lat pages), residualised on glyph content, length, line position, line index and paragraph start. Nulls: care permuted within (glyph count, first, last glyph) strata; place-stratified nulls for first mentions; planted care bumps, planted per-word signatures, and glyph-bigram planted negatives.

Results
- Instrument works on physical behaviour: line-final words are compressed (Voynich -0.23 SD width per glyph) and lighter (Latin -0.47 SD) (V-69.2a).
- Grade B (method, negative): the premise fails its own control. Professional Latin copyists in CREMMA show no extra care for rare words (z +0.6), page keywords (+0.6) or first mentions (+0.1); only corpus hapaxes (z 2.2, 1 of 36 tests); no random word class of 4,774 survives held-out pages beyond the null (1 vs median 5.5). A copied book hand does not slow down measurably for unfamiliar words at 2000-px scan resolution, so the Voynich null below does not count against meaning.
- Grade B (bound): Voynich rare words carry < ~0.15 SD extra care, first mentions < ~0.1 SD (planted-bump power: z 3.6 at 0.2 SD, 2.4 at 0.1 SD for first mentions). Observed: rarity z -1.0, keyword -0.1, hapax +0.05, first mention -0.4; same in Q20 alone and in the f58 hand.
- Grade B (negative): no carefully written Voynich vocabulary. 471 types: 3 above z 2.5 (null draw: 1), 2 below (null: 5); random class search: 2 survivors vs null 0-3. Top types (aiiin, oteol, checthy, sheol, shodaiin) are at the null rate: grade C-, dropped. Top care tokens are 1-2 glyph box artefacts (ain, o, r, y, ar); paragraph-first lines are written with LESS care (18% of top tokens vs 27% of all); f58r/v least careful pages (0.35-0.62 of expected), f105r and f111r most (1.4-1.7).
- Grade B (killed lead): repeats of the same Voynich word looked more alike than one-glyph neighbours (z 4.3, mostly rare words), but the excess falls to z 1.95 with line-distance matching and to z 0.3 with a length-only null, while glyph-bigram plants with no word identity reach z 3.4. It is glyph content plus line proximity, not a word-level writing unit. Latin could not calibrate this test (planted 0.25 SD signature only z 1.2).
- In the Latin control, a single name stood out (priapvs, z 4.1, 11 tokens; virgilivs z 2.1), but another spelling of a name was among the least careful (virgilius -2.1): names are not reliably slowed for.

What drives care in the Voynich: line position (line ends compressed), page (f58 vs Q20), glyph content; not rarity, page keywords, first mention or any word class tested. Not cracked. Would revive: a control written by an author composing (an autograph draft, not a professional copy) that shows care for rare words with this instrument, then a Voynich test at the same power; or higher-resolution scans (Beinecke full-size TIFF) with stroke-level timing cues (blobs at stroke ends, hairline joins).
