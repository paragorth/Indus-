With each corpus's climbed concept alphabet (cycle 2; controls also with the true alphabet), along each line:
  WIN3  distinct concepts in 3 consecutive words (a tabula column keeps a run of words inside one triple -> low)
  SHARE Jaccard of the concept sets of adjacent words
  ROT   adjacent words with equal concept count >= 2: per-slot step rank(next)-rank(prev) mod k in the canonical order; share of the commonest non-zero step (a turning wheel -> high)
