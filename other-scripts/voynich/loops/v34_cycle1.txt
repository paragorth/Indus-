Data: Voynich = 25 text pages (Q20 stars f103r-f116r + f58r/v), Yale IIIF 2000-px images (scratch only), per-word x-extents from the v18 alignment. v18 clipped every line at the text-block edge, so lines running into the margin all shared one end; v34_lineends.py re-follows clipped lines into the margin (ink runs >= 3 px, gaps <= 1 glyph unit, stop at dark page border; first version with 2.2-unit gaps ran into stains and page edges and was replaced after a visual check). Crowding = slack = (page right margin R - line end) / page glyph unit, R = 90th percentile of line ends; tightness = -slack. Paragraph-final lines and lines ending > 25% of the column short are dropped: 848 lines.
Positive control: CREMMA-Medieval-Lat (HTR-United; 9 MSS, 12th-15th c., 70 column-pages, 2,152 lines), line ends from the annotated ALTO baselines, abbreviation marks of the last token counted on the raw transcription (combining marks, MUFI abbreviation letters, private-use signs). Negative control: machine-wrapped printed text (Gutenberg Caesar, Descartes, Kafka, Manzoni, Cervantes; 3,000 lines; slack = wrap width - line length).
Statistic: rank correlation of tightness with each last-word feature; null = tightness shuffled among lines of the SAME page (keeps every line-final effect, kills only crowding), 2,000 permutations, familywise p (max |z| over features). Repeated with last-word length as covariate.
