v69 WHERE THE SCRIBE SLOWED DOWN, cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026). Tools: tools/v69_lib.py (per-word care features from the Beinecke and CREMMA images, v18 word boxes), tools/v69_c1.py. Out: data/v69_ckpt/c1.json (git-ignored).
Words: Voynich 11,576 (25 pages: Q20 f103-f116 + f58r/v); Latin control 9,409 (28 CREMMA-Medieval-Lat pages, 6 manuscripts). Features: width per glyph, ink height, uprightness (shear), components per glyph (pen lifts), stroke width, ink-run CV (spacing), column-height CV (size regularity), ink darkness. Each page-z-scored and ridge-residualised on glyph content (top-25 glyph counts), glyph count, position in line, line index, paragraph start, alignment cost. Pre-registered care index = wpg + hgt + upright + ncomp - gapcv - irr. Nulls: care permuted within (glyph count, first glyph, last glyph) strata, 1000x; first-mention test shuffled within (page, 5-line band, glyph count).
Web check: no published per-word handwriting-care study of the Voynich with controls (only qualitative ductus remarks and the Scribe 1 / Scribe 2 contrast).
