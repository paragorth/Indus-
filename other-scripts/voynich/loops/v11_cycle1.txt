# v11 cycle 1 -- "the text is a walk through a hidden map": fitting hidden-space models (tools/v11_lib.py, tools/v11_cycle1.py;
# checkpoints data/results/v11/c1_<corpus>_<fold>.json, log c1.log)
# Data: ZL3b paragraph lines, uncertain words dropped (34,857 tokens); IT2a as transcription check (same pages: transcription-robust, not replication).
# Every control corpus is poured into the exact ZL3b line layout, so the page-level 2-fold split (alternate folios) is identical.
# Units: word types, top 300 per corpus; within-line bigrams with both words in vocabulary. Scores are HELD-OUT gains in bits per bigram
# over a unigram(+self-repeat) model, mean of 2 folds.
# Models (numpy, full batch, Adam 500 it., L2): MAP dist-d: P(b|a) = (1-rho) softmax(-|x_a-x_b|^2 + beta_b + g[a=b]) + rho u_b
# (a walk with step kernel, popularity, teleport rate rho); NON-GEOMETRIC bilin-d: same with u_a.v_b instead of -|x_a-x_b|^2 (2x the parameters).
# Restarts: dist1/dist2 spectral-init + random, keep best train fit. Key statistic: MAP ADVANTAGE = gain(dist2)/gain(bilin2).
# Planted controls: random walk on a 20x20 grid (Chebyshev radius-2 steps, Voynich word types and frequencies placed at random) with 30% or
# 60% noise tokens; same walk on a random 24-regular graph (same degree, no geometry).

Cycle-1 verdict: no hidden 2-D/3-D map. The instrument recovers a planted grid (R^2 0.59, kNN 12x chance) and rejects a planted
graph; Voynich behaves like natural language and the planted graph (map advantage 0.57 vs 1.92). Next (cycle 2): model-free
geometry (clustering, triangle closure, Isomap, ball growth), walk statistics in the learned space, map reuse across page halves,
and a lag-2 composition test.
