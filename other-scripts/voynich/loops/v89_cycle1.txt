# Tools: tools/v89_texts.py (v58 parsers, now keeping tokens), tools/v89_lib.py (features, alignment, search), tools/v89_c1.py, tools/v89_report.py. Checkpoints data/v89_ckpt/ (texts.json, c1.jsonl, c1.log; git-ignored).
# Novelty check: v58 aligned entry LENGTHS of 25 works (negative); Montemurro-Zanette 2013 (LITERATURE.md l.163) built word networks inside the Voynich only. Nothing in LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS.md, STRATEGIES.md or a web search aligns the Voynich to candidate texts by WHICH units share vocabulary. v89 does that; length is regressed out so it is not v58 again.
# Feature: per unit the set of word types (df>=2, top 0/30/100 types dropped at random); pairwise cosine; double-centred; each lag's mean removed; unit-size terms (log k_i+log k_j, product, squared and absolute difference) regressed out; top 2 eigen-components stripped (book/block structure any two long texts share). Score = Pearson between target and source residual matrices over pairs of ODD units (A, selection); held out = same alignment on EVEN-unit pairs (B).
# Alignment search per (target, source): ordered grid (every offset x 13 spans, entry-by-entry 'unif' map) ~ 600-8,000 maps + 400 random maps of 5 modes (length-proportional cuts, the same snapped to entry boundaries, random cuts, unif with jitter) + 30-step refinement of the top 5. Same search re-run on 12 block-shuffled target orders (blocks of n/16, random reversal; keeps local drift, breaks long order) -> zB(block); and against 27-28 other texts -> zDecoy (B against the other sources' B).
