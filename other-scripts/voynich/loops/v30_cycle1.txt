Method: cheapest ordered rewrite X -> Y. Rule = (lhs 1-3 symbols, rhs 0-3 symbols, optional word-edge context), applied in one simultaneous left-to-right pass (a deterministic transducer). Stochastic greedy search: 200 random guided proposals per step (lhs from X's 150 commonest substrings, rhs from Y's), best one kept, a rule dropped if it costs < 2e-4; up to 30 rules; 2 seeds (different page splits and proposals). Distance D = JSD(char bigrams) + JSD(char trigrams) + JSD(word types), '#' at word edges. Fit on 3,000 words of each side, scored on 1,500 held-out words from other pages. Floor F = D between two disjoint same-size samples of Y. ex0 = D0 - F (raw distance), exK = residual after the rewrite, clos = (D0 - DK)/ex0.
Ladder: ReF v1.0.2 diplomatic German manuscripts 1350-1450 (Bavarian F022/F005/F004 vs F001/F002; hochalemannisch F091/F092/F089/F088; ripuarisch F154/F155); CATMuS abbreviated Latin (13-15th c.) vs Italian (14-15th c. non-Dante); two groups of Commedia manuscripts (Triv.1080+Bodmer 55 vs BnF ita 79+Ott.lat.2373+Marciana it.Z.50) = same work, other scribes; Czech Dalimil (edition spelling) vs the other half respelled with 13 pre-Hus digraph rules (cz, rz, ss, g for j, w for v, ie, no length marks) = spelling reform; German vs Italian, Latin vs German, Czech vs German, Voynich A vs Latin = unrelated; German with 3 letter swaps / a full random substitution = cipher key change.
