v24 cycle 2 (4 Oct 2026): before-form signature with rebuilt and new controls. tools/v24_cycle2.py; log data/v24_ckpt/c2.log
Same statistic and null as cycle 1 (500 sites max per set, ~1,050 rules). New: identity|trigram-LP (null edits matched on bigram legality AND trigram log-probability within 0.25 nats of the real before-form) replaces the leaky identity|bigram test. 'Word ratio' = share of before-forms that are words / random-edit expectation.
