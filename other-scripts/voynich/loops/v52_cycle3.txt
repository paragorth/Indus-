# v52 cycle 3: where does each attribute live, in one slot (field code) or across the whole word (vocabulary)? For every corpus and target variable, the best single slot over 438 schemas (cycle-1 top 30, cycle-2 climbed 8, 400 fresh random) is chosen on half A; its held-out gain on half B (bits/token over the prior) is compared with the held-out gain of the whole word (type counts, prior shrinkage, unseen word -> prior). CONC = slot / word. Scripts tools/v52_cycle3.py (first attempt), v52_cycle3b.py.
