v73 ONE WORD PER LINE IS THE MESSAGE, THE REST IS SETTING: cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v73_lib.py, v73_texts.py, v73_c1.py, v73_c1_report.py, v73_truthD.py.
Idea: each line holds one key item (an ingredient, a name) set among formulaic filler; find the marking rule by massive random guessing and read the one-word-per-line stream.
Prior work: forum suggestion that the line-initial letter marks the only enciphered word (voynich.ninja threads 4515, 5044), never tested with controls; v67 tested consensus-per-line, not a single marked word.
Rule bank: 2,580 rules (120 single-feature rules both signs + 2,500 random 1-3-feature weightings over 60 word features: position, reverse position, length, frequency, page-unique, hapax, gallows/bench/ch/sh content, initial and final glyph, line-mode mismatch (v67 classes), glyph rarity, section specificity, after/before each of the 10 commonest words, odd glyph bigrams). Argmax per line = the item.
Stream statistics on held-out pages (leaf parity), each as E minus R (R = a random word per line from the same lines): PI within-page cache gain, XP recurrence on pages +-1..2 minus 4 far same-section pages, VS Jensen-Shannon separation of the stream from the non-selected words, TT type/token. Composite = z(XP)+z(VS) against 5 nulls fitted to each target (MK2 glyph trigram, SELFCIT and SC10 copy-and-vary, JUNC junction resynthesis, LSHUF within-page line shuffle). Top rule chosen on discovery pages only, tested on held-out pages.
Controls: the Antidotarium Nicolai Latin ingredient lists (5,058 items, van den Berg 1917 OCR) written one item per line into the ZL3b page/line skeleton inside MK2 or SELFCIT filler fitted to the Voynich; items spelled through a one-word codebook either as ordinary Voynich words (VOY, rank-matched) or as new Voynich-shaped words (NOVEL); hidden marking POS2 (3rd word), AFTER (after the word 'chol'), FREE (random slot, no cue). False-positive targets: SELFCIT and MK2 text fitted to the Voynich, run as if real.
