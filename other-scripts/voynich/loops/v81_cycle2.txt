v81 CYCLE 2 (6 Oct 2026): IS THE REST OF THE WORD SURFACE? (re-roll test) and WHERE IN THE WORD DOES THE ORDER LIVE? (slot inversion)
Tools: tools/v81_c2.py, v81_c2_report.py. Checkpoint data/v81_ckpt/c2.pkl. Same 14 corpora as cycle 1.
Re-roll: the initials hypothesis' own generator - inside each (section, onset class) pool the word tails (rest of the variant string after the onset; top-60 tail types + other) are permuted across tokens, 20 times. Statistics (plug-in MI, real minus mean re-roll, z): S1 I(page; tail), S2 I(tail_i; tail_i+1), S3 I(tail_i; onset_i+1), S4 I(onset_i; tail_i+1). Definitions: E1c+line k=1, E1c+line k=2, q+line stripped k=1.
Slot inversion: 200 new random definitions applied to the onset (first k units), middle (units 2..k+1) and coda (last k units) of the same word; held-out cycle-1 features.
