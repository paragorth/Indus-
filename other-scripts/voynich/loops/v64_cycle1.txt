v64 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): THE WORDS ARE CONCEPT COMBINATIONS (Lullian ars). Massive random concept-alphabet search.
Scripts: tools/v64_lib.py (corpora, controls, parser), tools/v64_score.c (C scorer), tools/v64_cycle1.py, tools/v64_report1.py.
Novelty: web search (5 Oct 2026) finds only a forum essay (voynich.ninja thread 5466, "The Lullian Apophatic Machine", Mar 2026) proposing a Lullian generator with no calibrated test; v9 tested ring volvelles (prefix/core/suffix rings, one whole-word disc), not a concept alphabet found by search, nor free-combination wheels/tables over searched units.
Model. A concept alphabet = 6-20 glyph n-grams (n<=4). Words parsed greedily (longest concept first), unmatched glyphs = padding. Two held-out word models, both mixed 50:50 with the train word unigram:
  FREE (the Lullian wheel/table model): P(#concepts) x multinomial draw of the concepts x canonical-order term (fixed Copeland order learned on train; pi for in-order, rest spread over other orders) x padding-gap strings (initial / medial / final categorical with spelled escape).
  M1: same padding model, concept sequence by concept Markov-1.
  Score G = held-out bits/word of an alphabet-free glyph Markov-2 minus bits/word of the concept model (Gfree, Gm1). Split by folio (or 30-line pseudo-folio) mod 4: select on train q0/test q1, re-test top 20 on train q2/test q3.
Corpora (~35k tokens each): Voynich ZL3b and IT2a paragraph text (glyph units, benched gallows and ch/sh as one unit); nulls: glyphs shuffled inside each word, glyph Markov-2 resynthesis, self-citation generator; POSITIVE CONTROLS: (ars) text read off a reconstructed Ars brevis tabula generalis (B C D E F G H I K + T, 20-cell column of each triple, runs of 3-8 cells, 15% free words, section-biased columns), (med) Lullist medical combinatoria (16 concepts: 4 qualities, 4 degrees turned like a wheel along the line, 4 humours, 4 organs; canonical order; section profiles); both in opaque glyph codes of 1-2 glyphs with Voynich-like padding (initial q/o 40%, medial e 25%, final y/n/l 60%); NEGATIVE CONTROL: Isidore Latin, letters -> opaque 1-2-glyph codes, same padding; plus Markov-2 resyntheses of both positive controls.
20,000 random alphabets per corpus (Latin 8,000): 188,000 alphabets in all.
