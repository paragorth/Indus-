# v16 cycle 2: annealed FREE segmentation, no rule at all (tools/v16_cycle2.py, v16_report2.py; results data/results/v16/cycle2/, boundary masks *.npz)
# Spaces deleted; simulated annealing over all boundary sets (single-boundary toggles, Metropolis, T 1.5 -> 0.02 bits over 30 sweeps + 4 greedy sweeps; ~5 million moves per run) minimising MDL2 on the whole corpus. 13 runs. A first schedule (T0 = 6 bits, 16 sweeps) did not converge (Voynich ended above its written MDL); it is kept in cycle2_hot/ and not used.
| V-16.2.1 | Negative control: anneal from the written spacing of true-spaced Latin and Italian | Latin: MDL2 2.713 -> 2.564 (-0.148 b/g), boundary F vs written 0.911, 22,215 units vs 19,667 words (endings split off). Italian: 2.997 -> 2.839 (-0.159), F 0.899 | Free MDL search 'improves' real word breaks by ~0.15 b/g by splitting endings; it keeps ~90% of them. This is the calibrated size of a meaningless improvement |
| V-16.2.2 | Positive control: anneal from a planted spacing | Planted every-5 / VC spacing beaten by 0.33-0.45 b/g (2-3x the true-spaced gain); boundary F vs planted 0.60-0.81; F vs TRUE words rises 0.17 -> 0.38, 0.13 -> 0.24 (Latin), 0.20 -> 0.40, 0.36 -> 0.47 (Italian) | PASS in direction and size: planted spacings are abandoned twice as hard and the search moves toward the real words. Recovery is partial (F <= 0.47): a low-temperature search cannot fully escape a bad start |
| V-16.2.3 | Voynich ZL3b and IT2a, anneal from written | ZL 2.679 -> 2.511 (-0.169), F vs written 0.871; IT 2.648 -> 2.507 (-0.141), F 0.878. Units 34,925 vs 35,069 tokens (same count), types 4,180 vs 7,205, length 4.5 +- 1.9 vs 4.5 +- 1.6, Zipf -0.93 (R2 0.979) vs -1.04 | Voynich gain and boundary agreement sit inside the true-language band (-0.15/-0.16, F 0.90-0.91) and far from the planted band (-0.32/-0.45, F 0.60-0.81). By this test the written Voynich spaces behave like real boundaries, not like a planted mechanical spacing |
| V-16.2.4 | Null: Markov-2 resynthesis (spaces from a local chain) and within-line glyph shuffle | Markov-2: -0.173, F 0.860 (same as Voynich). Glyph shuffle: -0.728, F 0.236 | The test cannot tell a locally generated spacing from real word breaks (Markov-2 lands with the languages). It CAN tell a spacing unrelated to the glyphs (shuffle) and a planted mechanical spacing. So the Voynich spaces are 'glyph-coherent', which a Markov-type generator also gives |
| V-16.2.5 | Search from a random spacing (density 0.22) | Latin ends at +0.539 b/g above its written words (F 0.51); Voynich +0.058 (F 0.46); Markov-2 -0.018 (F 0.47) | Power limit: the annealer does not rediscover words from scratch even in Latin. Side result consistent with V-16.1.0: an arbitrary local optimum is almost as good as the written spacing in the Voynich (+0.06) but far worse in Latin (+0.54) |
| V-16.2.6 | Junction effect after free re-spacing (excess MI last glyph -> next first glyph, within-line unit shuffle) | ZL 0.188 -> 0.129 (-31%); IT 0.175 -> 0.124 (-29%); Markov-2 0.202 -> 0.127 (-37%); Italian 0.079 -> 0.054; Latin 0.038 -> 0.048; planted every-5 0.54 -> 0.27 | The junction effect SURVIVES re-spacing: about 70% remains, still 2.5-3x the languages. It is not an artefact of where the scribe put the spaces. It shrinks by the same fraction as in the Markov-2 null |
| V-16.2.V | Verdict, cycle 2 | - | No: the best free re-spacing of the Voynich improves on the written spaces only by the amount real word breaks are improved (and by the amount a Markov chain's spaces are improved); planted mechanical spacings are improved 2-3x more. The spaces are not lies of the planted kind. Grade B (two transcriptions, both controls behave). Caveat: a Markov-2 null passes too, so this does not show the spaces are LINGUISTIC word breaks, only that they sit where the glyph stream itself has its seams |
