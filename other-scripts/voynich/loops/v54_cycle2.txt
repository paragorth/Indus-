v54 cycle 2: the reduced alphabet in which witnesses become identical, scored on held-out pages (5 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/v54_c2.py, tools/v54_c2_report.py; checkpoints data/v54_ckpt/c2_*.json (first draft with uncalibrated
scores in c2_v0/, superseded). Method: 5 page folds. On the training pages only: find witness pairs, collate them,
and keep the K = 4 or 8 edit operations most OVER-represented in real witness pairs relative to chance pairs
(posshuf null: rate per pair real minus rate per pair null, times pairs). Rewrite the book in the reduced alphabet
(merge substituted units, drop deleted units, collapse doubled units). Held-out scores: accuracy of a naive Bayes
predicting each page variable from 20-token chunks of unseen pages, and the bits/token an absolute-discounting word
bigram gains over the unigram on unseen pages. Comparators: no reduction; 30 random reductions per fold with the
same make-up (same number of merges, deletions, collapses, frequent units); and the reduction made from the most
common chance-pair operations (context-free neighbour confusions). z = (collated - random mean) / random SD over
fold means.
