v73 ONE WORD PER LINE IS THE MESSAGE, THE REST IS SETTING: final (6 Oct 2026). Cycles: loops/v73_cycle1.txt, v73_cycle2.txt, v73_cycle3.txt. Scripts: tools/v73_*.py. Checkpoints: data/v73_ckpt/ (git-ignored).
Idea (arrow in the dark): each line carries one key item set among filler; find the marking rule by massive guessing and read the item stream.
Done: about 8,100 marking hypotheses per target (2,580 feature rules, 2,514 model-feature rules, 3,000 pointer tables, 1 learned conditional-logit marker, 4 ascended pointer tables), chosen on discovery leaves and tested on held-out leaves, on ZL3b and IT2a, 21 planted list texts (Antidotarium Nicolai and Apicius ingredient lists, one item per line in Voynich-fitted filler, items spelled as ordinary Voynich words or as new Voynich-shaped words, 6 hidden marking schemes) and 6 generator false-positive targets, each against 5 nulls fitted to it (MK2, SELFCIT, SC10, JUNC, line shuffle). Item stream scored for neighbour-page recurrence (XP), separation from filler (VS), within-page cache gain (PI), type/token, and recurring ordered pairs (v67 kill test).
