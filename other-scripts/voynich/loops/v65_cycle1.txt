v65 THE TEXT REMEMBERS WHICH PAGE CAME NEXT -- cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026): carry-over strength in the binding, controls and nulls
Tools: tools/v65_lib.py (physical model, corpora, carry-over matrices, exhaustive quire spaces), tools/v65_c1.py. Checkpoints: data/v65_ckpt/c1.json, c1.log (git-ignored).
Prior-art check: in-house v8 V-3.3 found conjugate leaves more alike than chance (z 4.7, symmetric whole-leaf similarity); v8 V-1.2 seriation; v58 searched leaf orders against SOURCE texts (not page-to-page carry-over). Outside: Layfield & Davis 2026 (DHQ; LSA) resequenced bifolia by maximising similarity across page transitions (herbal excluded); a 2026 forum review (voynich.ninja thread 5911) notes no permutation null and no shuffled-known-text calibration, and no comparison with physical evidence is reported. So: run it with the missing controls, as a DIRECTED tail->head seam, held out by word class, and test against physical adjacency evidence.
Physical model: quires/bifolios/leaves from the ZL3b IVTFF headers ($Q,$B,$F); arrangement = nesting order x inside-out refold per bifolio (text upright); lost leaves (f12, f74) and lost centre bifolios (Q8 f59-64, Q20 f109-110) are chain breaks. Exhaustive spaces: 384 per 4-bifolio herbal quire, 3,840 for Q13, 322,560 for Q20; 192 pages with paragraph text.
Seam = idf-weighted overlap of the last 3 lines of page a with the first 3 lines of page b (word types with df 2..20% of pages), double-centred within section x Currier language. Page = same on whole pages (topic/drift). Resid = seam after regressing out page. Arrow = J(a->b) - J(b->a). pct = percentile of the binding arrangement among all arrangements of its quire (13 quires with >= 8 arrangements). Features: EVA glyph-unit words, or payload skeleton (q e d ch sh dropped). Word classes: md5 hash halves.
Controls laid into the exact Voynich page/line/word layout in binding order, letters -> opaque 1-2 symbol codes with Voynich-like padding (q-/o- initial, e medial, -y/-n/-l final): Isidore (continuous), Konrad von Megenberg Buch der Natur plant chapters (continuous, and one chapter per page), Macer floridus (one entry per page). Nulls: word-bigram Markov resynthesis per section x language, each page generated independently (no cross-page memory); self-citation copy-and-modify generator (window 60 words) written through the book in binding order (has memory).
