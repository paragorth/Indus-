v76 VOYNICH PARAGRAPHS AS DOSSIERS: cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v76_c2.py, v76_c2_report.py (V76_C2TAG=c2 raw tokens, c2b tokens compared after deleting d). Checkpoints data/v76_ckpt/c2_*, c2b_* (git-ignored).
Method: for each of the 75 corpora, the 50 best templates on discovery-half pairs give candidate dossier pairs (cross-page, same section; up to 6,000 per corpus; ALL pairs and holdout-half pairs H1). Each pair is aligned token by token (global alignment, match = identical token). FIX = matched share of aligned columns (template); SLOT = an aligned substitution with matches on both sides (a single-token swap inside a fixed frame), SLOTR per 100 aligned columns; POSH = entropy of slot positions (6 paragraph bins x line-initial/medial/final) minus that of all aligned columns. IDENTIFIER test PSD = share of slot fillers that are page-own (found in another paragraph of their own page and absent from the partner's page) minus the same for frequency-decile-matched tokens of the same two paragraphs; OWND = the looser version (page-own without the partner condition). Cycle 2a (raw E1c tokens) showed that in the planted controls most 'slots' were the same word with and without a padding d (the planted surface pads with d and E1c keeps d), so cycle 2b compares tokens after deleting d in every corpus alike; rows below are 2b unless stated.
