v61 cycle 1 - The words are glued together: undo the sandhi. Exhaustive edge-rewrite scan with line edges as base forms
(tools/v61_lib.py, v61_c1.py, v61_show.py, v61_edge.py, v61_xline.py; checkpoints data/v61_ckpt/c1_*.json, c1_log*.txt)
Prior art checked: v29 (V-29.3.2) measured single-glyph conditional MI at junctions and found no sandhi. v61 differs: rewrite rules on whole
endings/beginnings of up to 3 glyphs (so -edy/-eey/-ol type alternations that leave the last glyph unchanged are included), fitted and
then tested on held-out pages, with the neighbour-free line edge as an untouched test of which form is the base. Web search: no fitted
sandhi-undo model of the Voynich with controls found.
Hypothesis space per corpus: every ordered pair of word endings (S,B), 0-3 glyphs, attested on >= 2 shared stems, x (each single glyph +
4,000 random glyph classes) for the next word's first glyph. Regressive (R) = ending conditioned on next word; progressive (P) = run on the
reversed text (beginning conditioned on the previous word's end). 2.4-48 million hypotheses per corpus (about 330 million in all).
Line-end pull |E|: for each rule, where the share of S among line-final (P: line-initial) words lies on the axis from the trigger context C to
elsewhere N, minus where the blind overall share lies (sandhi: the edge word is the base, |E| >= 1; line-blind text: 0). Top 100 rules
with held-out z > 3, n >= 20.
