v71 cycle 3: time's arrow at every scale (stranger version of the shuffle test). Instead of measuring predictability loss, a held-out classifier is asked to tell the written order of two adjacent units from the reversed order: W adjacent words in a line, L adjacent lines in a paragraph, Lm the same without the paragraph's first and last lines, P adjacent paragraphs in a page. Pair features phi(A)-phi(B) (bags of hashed word classes or glyph prefix/suffix classes, lengths) and a hashed junction term; L2 logistic regression without intercept; 10 random feature configurations; at most 600 pairs per scale; folds by page. Arrow excess = accuracy minus the accuracy on the same chunk with the units shuffled inside their parent. Same 107 reference chunks, 54 generator chunks and 24 Voynich chunks as cycle 1. Tools: tools/v71_c3.py, v71_c3_report.py; data/v71_ckpt/c3/*.json, rep_c3.txt.
