# v18 'THE PEN REMEMBERS' - cycle 1: pen dips from the page images, and does the text reset at a dip? (tools/v18_lib.py, v18_fetch.py, v18_extract.py, v18_dips.py, v18_cycle1.py; data/derived/v18_words.json; data/results/v18/c1_*.json)
# Images: Yale IIIF (manifest https://collections.library.yale.edu/manifests/2002046), 25 text-only pages (f58r, f58v, f103r-f108v, f111r-f116r) at 2000 px width, scratch cache only.
# Pipeline: background-normalised ink contrast; text lines = row-profile peaks in 200-px strips linked into tracks, weak gallows-ascender tracks suppressed, tracks matched to ZL3b paragraph lines by a DP with a line-pitch term (all 25 pages: 1,203 of 1,212 lines matched); word boxes = blank-column runs chosen by a DP against glyph-count widths, with optional drop of margin stars/stalks. 11,599 words measured. Visual audit of 16 random lines: ~75-80% of words boxed correctly; errors are mostly a one-word shift after a star or merged words.
# Ink measures per word (ink pixels in a +-0.3-pitch band): median contrast, darkest-30% mean, 90th percentile, -median grey, ink area per glyph, red-blue hue (both signs). Darkness residualised on a per-page quadratic surface (lighting, page curl) and optionally on the word's own glyph content (20 glyph counts, length, width ratio, line-start).
# Prior art (web search, LITERATURE.md, FINDINGS, STRATEGIES): ink-weight variation from re-inking is described qualitatively (Stolfi's notes; voynich.ninja f8r thread on darker/lighter strokes); no measurement of dip points or test of the text against them was found.
# Dip search: 6,048 settings = 7 measures x content-residualised or not x 3-word median smoothing or not x 6 detectors (jump1 x[t]-x[t-1]; jump2; sawtooth; rise over the last-5 minimum; CLEAN gap1 x[t+1]-x[t-2] and gap2 mean(x[t+1],x[t+2])-mean(x[t-3],x[t-2]), which never use the darkness of the two words at the boundary) x 6 thresholds (top 3-20%) x minimum spacing 1/3/5 words x line starts in/out.
# Reset features at the boundary entering the dip word t: junction PMI (last glyph of t-1, first glyph of t), word-bigram PMI, length, length change, log frequency, near-repeat of t-1/t-2, line-initial-likeness of t's first glyph. Each standardised within position strata (line start / 2nd / line end / interior); analytic z against resampling within page x stratum. Reset predicts junc<0, bigr<0, rep<0, init>0; reset score = mean over settings of (-z_junc - z_bigr - z_rep + z_init)/4.
# PRIMARY family fixed before looking: clean detectors on content-residualised ink (1,008 settings). Family-wise nulls: 24 page-swap surrogates (dips of page p laid on the text of page p+k by line/word coordinates; keeps dip spacing and line positions, breaks the pen-text link).
