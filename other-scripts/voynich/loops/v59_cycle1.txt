v59 cycle 1 - The book contains its own Rosetta stone: random A<->B transducers scored by meaning preservation
(tools/v59_lib.py, tools/v59_c1.py, tools/v59_c1b.py; checkpoints data/v59_ckpt/c1_*.json, c1b_*.json)

Score (new): translate side 1 by a transducer T, then ask whether each translated word is used in the same
line contexts as the same word on side 2 (PPMI line co-occurrence over the top 300 side-2 types; side-2-frequency
weighted mean reciprocal rank of the word's own side-2 context). Delta = gain over the identity map. Search on
train pages, verdict on held-out pages of both sides. Rule families: glyph-string substitutions, prefix and suffix
swaps (incl. deletion), insertions, deletions; 1-4 rules per set (c1), 1-3 rules from 1-5-gram pools with
min-of-two-folds screening (c1b); 3,000 random sets per pair, then greedy stacking of the best rules.
