# v14 cycle 1: "the words were rolled, not written" -- device fit of slot-filler frequencies + within-word slot independence
Tools: tools/v14_lib.py (devices, exact/local-search assignment of die outcomes to fillers, nulls, MI), tools/v14_cycle1.py, tools/v14_report.py.
Checkpoints: data/results/v14/c1_*.pkl. Slot grammar: v7 4-slot model (ZL3b cuts [0,10,16,19,31], 73.0% coverage); controls learn their own (same algorithm + tie-break: fewest distinct fillers).
Devices (atoms): astragalus (.1,.4,.4,.1), d6, sum of 2 astragali, coin+d6 (12 x 1/12), 2d6 sum (1..6..1 /36), 2d6 product (18 atoms /36), 3d6 sum (16 atoms /216), two dice + 36-cell lookup table (36 x 1/36). Any atoms may be merged onto one filler (merged faces / table cells). Fit = G at full resolution (top 9 fillers + OTHER; OTHER must also be rolled), best assignment (greedy exact for equal atoms; local search with restarts, checked against brute force for 2d6 sum: G 1.5428 both). Identification = least flexible device not rejected at 0.001. Null = the slot's own filler distribution log-jittered (sigma 0, 0.1, 0.3) and resampled to the same N, same full search (40 each) => search-size corrected.
Independence: MI (bits) between slot fillers (top 9 + OTHER), 200 permutations of one column (p floor 0.005).
