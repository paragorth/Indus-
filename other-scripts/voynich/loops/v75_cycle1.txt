v75 IF IT IS A CODE, WHAT KIND OF CODE IS IT? cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026): real designation systems of every kind, reduced to the same abstract form, against the v72 message stream.
Scripts: tools/v75_texts.py (corpus), v75_lib.py (streams, chunks, fingerprint), v75_c1.py (fingerprint table), v75_cls.py (random model types, LOSO), v75_run.py (driver), v75_report.py, v75_outlier.py, v75_spec.py. Checkpoints and downloaded texts: data/v75_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Two CPU workers. v73 (one word per line) and v74 (P1 on the vellum) not duplicated.
Prior art (web, 6 Oct 2026): no comparison of the Voynich with real designation systems (synonym glossaries, catalogues, nomenclators, sign lists) under one fingerprint with generator controls was found. v19 (no sorted glossary), v52 (field codes negative), v71 (genre classifier killed: generators land on a genre) are the nearest earlier loops; this loop adds the designation kinds, the v72 extraction and a generator class.
Corpus (36 systems, 11 kinds; data only, nobody's reading used): GLOSS Alphita (Mowat 1887 OCR), Sinonoma Bartholomei (Mowat 1882), Ruland's Lexicon alchemiae (Waite 1893), Forme of Cury glossary; INDEX Culpeper, Apicius, Pliny indexes; CATAL Christ Church Canterbury (Eastry catalogue, James 1903, 918 numbered entries), Syon (Bateson 1898, 1,425 shelfmarked entries); INGRED Antidotarium Nicolai and Apicius ingredient streams (v73); APOTH Culpeper 1649 catalogue of simples (drawer labels by ROOTS/BARKS/HERBS...), Pechey 1694 Latin preparation labels (Syrupus ..., Decoctum ...), Culpeper 1649 compound headings (EEBO-TCP A35390, A53916); ASTRO Almagest star designations in the medieval Latin version (Peters & Knobel 1915), zodiacal and southern halves (northern half too small); ALCH Gessmann 1899 symbol tables (Unicode alchemical block too small); NOMEN papal-curia nomenclator tables (Meister 1906), early and late halves; SIGNS Cluny (Udalric, PL 149) and Hirsau (William, PL 150) monastic sign lists; MODERN Unicode character names by block, IANA time-zone names, Debian package names + summaries; LANG 10 texts (Caesar, Manzoni, Kafka, Cervantes, Latin and Italian herbals, Hildegard, Macer, Circa instans French, Konrad). Apothecary jar labels proper (Ricettario fiorentino, Salmon 1691) were unusable (two-column OCR); St Augustine's and Dover catalogues likewise.
Pipeline: each system written into Voynich-like pages (entry = paragraph, lines <= 8 words, pages of ~160 tokens inside one section), spelled with a lossy 11-symbol merge code, dressed in the planted v72 surface machinery, pulled back with the frozen E1c (hash 2803cbeb0deb51bf checked by assertion). Voynich ZL3b and IT2a paragraphs through E1c directly. Generators WSHUF, MK2, SELFCIT, JUNC, SC10 fitted to every surface (reference and Voynich) and extracted the same way; global token shuffle of the Voynich as a further null. Fingerprint on 400-token chunks of random whole pages: 30 features (13 symbol-level: word length, h1, h2, slot rigidity, edge entropies...; 13 type-level: TTR, hapax, Zipf, reuse across entries, repeats inside entries, adjacent and lag-2 repeats, section MI excess, page recurrence excess, within-line direction, bigram MI excess, position MI excess, entry-head MI excess; 4 layout). Two independent replicates (A discovery, B held-out: new codes, surfaces, generator seeds, chunk draws); 10 chunks per system, 3 per generator, 20 per Voynich leaf-parity half.
Random model types: 3,000 per run (feature subset 3-16, layout allowed or not, nearest centroid / nearest system centroid / kNN k 1-15 / diagonal Gaussian / shrinkage LDA). Leave-one-system-out (the held-out system goes out with every generator fitted to it). Survivors: top 10% LOSO kind accuracy on A (open set also requires >= 80% of held-out reference generators classed GEN; fallback top 50), re-tested on B; null = the 20 best models re-run with kind labels permuted across systems (100 runs).
