First pass (v79_c3.py) had a leak: two key slots (first glyph of word 2, line-final glyph) are part of the body text the key was scored against, so every text, generators included, gave cross-page 'keys' at z 2.5-6.0. Rows below use the repaired pass only (margin slots only: the marks of the line and of lines -2..+2, and the line's second glyph).
Controls (opaque v72 surface, mark on 80% of lines): C_INDEX = Isidore with an index letter = the surfaced initial of the line's rarest word (an index column in the same script); C_TOPIC = Isidore with the line's chapter (CAPUT, mod 10) as a running item index; C_GENUS = Brumati with a genus key (species of one genus share vocabulary); C_CLASS, C_PART, C_DANTE, C_DOM, C_GOLD as in cycles 1-2; N_ISI = the v72 surface's own anti-repeat marker; generators SELFCIT, SC10, MK2, JUNC, STACK.
