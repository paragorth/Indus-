| V-18.5.V | Verdict, cycles 5-6 | - | The coherence survives edge-aware residualisation, both page halves and removal of repeats, and is at most half reproduced by content-only darkness; Latin shows a weaker same-sign effect. Size is small (~1 point of similarity). But the 'dips' are not a pen-load clock (V-18.3.1-2), so what the boundaries mark is unclear: a darkening may as well mark a change of pressure, speed or sitting. Grade C+: 'Voynich words written between two ink darkenings are slightly more alike than words the same distance apart across one'. Would support: the same on the herbal Currier-A pages, with IT2a, and growing with better dip detection (multispectral ink density); a stronger effect for last glyphs in Currier B than A. Would kill: z < 1 on new pages, or an equal effect in a second known-language manuscript with Voynich-like short repetitive words |
