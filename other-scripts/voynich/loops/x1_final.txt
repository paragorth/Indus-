X1 final (4 Oct 2026): is there an 'undeciphered signature'?
Question: do Indus, Linear A, Proto-Elamite and the Voynich manuscript share a statistical property that deciphered writing and designed codes lack (a reason they stay undeciphered)?
Design: 202 statistics (206 minus 4 size counts) in 18 families (tools/x1_battery.py); 4 undeciphered + 24 deciphered or designed corpora (17 sign-level, 7 word-level) + 4 test-only; two regimes (3,000-token matched windows x8; native size capped at 60k); exact label permutation over every 4-corpus labeling (20,475); LOCO logistic classifier with a label-permutation null; planted size control (deciphered corpora cut to Linear A / Indus / Proto-Elamite size); genre control. Positive control: real groups of four (e.g. Latin-script letter corpora, word-level corpora) score 25-31 near separators in the same scan.

| X-1.final | 202 statistics x 32 corpora, exact enumeration, LOCO + permutation, planted size control, 8 alternative definitions, 18 family scans, triad and genre tests (cycles 1-3) | The four: 0 perfect and 0 near separators in both regimes (null mean 0.2), cluster P 0.34 / 0.17, LOCO AUC 0.73 (P = 0.17) / 0.79 (P = 0.11); Voynich always lands among deciphered prose (P(U) 0.09 / 0.26). Corpus size cannot be the shared property (Voynich 155k glyphs is larger than 16 of 24 deciphered corpora) and subsampling deciphered corpora to undeciphered sizes does not give them the leading near-separators. Indus + Linear A + Proto-Elamite share a weak short-administrative-line profile (LOCO 0.986, P = 0.033 uncorrected, ~0.13 after the 4-triad correction), which strengthens when deciphered Linear B syllables, proto-cuneiform and Ur III names join (9 near separators, P = 0.0005; LOCO 0.95, P = 0.016) | NO UNDECIPHERED SIGNATURE, beyond corpus size or otherwise. Nothing in the sign statistics of these four systems is unlike deciphered writing as a class; three of them look like deciphered administrative writing of the same genre and Voynich looks like deciphered alphabetic prose. What keeps them undeciphered is outside the sign stream (no bilingual, no known language, few anchors), not a statistical anomaly. Not 'cracked'; nothing read. |

Caveats: Indus numbers come from data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (S-DARK-23: older inscriptions.csv build), mostly Mohenjo-daro + Harappa. 'Absence of a signature' is an upper bound for these 202 statistics and these 24 references; a statistic outside the battery could still separate. Wells and IM77 agreement is transcription-robust, not replication. Khipu (test-only) scores P(U) 0.07-0.34, on the deciphered side. Proto-cuneiform is counted as deciphered (values known via later cuneiform); dropping it does not change the four-corpus result (0 near separators; cluster P 0.31 matched, 0.16 native; x1_analysis_*_nopc.json).
