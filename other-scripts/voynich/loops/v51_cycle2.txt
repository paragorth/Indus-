v51 cycle 2: massive random mapping search with held-out re-test (tools/v51_search.py; rows data/v51_ckpt/c2d_*.jsonl discovery, c2h_*.jsonl held-out)
Discovery: 245 fresh random mappings x 16 corpora (V-ZL, Latin, German, Czech, Esperanto, Hebrew, self-citation and Markov-2 generators, each with its own unit shuffle), FIRST half of every text, one 6 s clip each (3,920 clips). Gain per mapping = speech score (signed sum of the six standardised listener scores) of the text minus that of its shuffle. Per corpus: top 15 mappings by discovery gain and 15 random others. Held-out: all 158 picked mappings re-rendered on the SECOND half of every text, 2 clips each (5,056 clips).
