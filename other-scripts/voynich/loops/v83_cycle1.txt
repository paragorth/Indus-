v83 cycle 1: TRANSCRIPTION-UNCERTAINTY AUDIT. An IVTFF parser that keeps what the transcribers were unsure of (6 Oct 2026).
Scripts: tools/v83_parse.py (parser, flags, three-way agreement, load(name, mode)), tools/v83_c1.py (validation and counts). Gold: data/derived/v83_gold_lines.json (hand-written flags for 30 lines). Checkpoint: data/v83_ckpt/{ZL3b,IT2a,GC2a}_v83.json, c1.json (git-ignored).
How the old parsers treated the markup (tools/parse_ivtff.py, used by vlib.load_voynich and every loop since v1): ',' (uncertain space) -> word break; [a:b] -> first reading, no flag; {..} and ' stripped, no flag; @nnn; -> '?', word flagged; '?' -> word flagged; inline comments deleted, so 'corr?', 'unclear', 'funny d', 'tear', 'hole' were lost; '#' damage notes ignored; '<~>' deleted (IVTFF 2.0 says it is a word space). Most loop loaders then drop '?' words, which joins their neighbours. GC2a (v101) went through tools/v74_gc.py, which deleted '!' and '%' as if they were IVTFF fillers; in v101 both are glyphs (Le, Lc: sh family).
Flags per word (first reading kept as before): alt [a:b]; ill '?'/'???'; rare @nnn; (EVA) or a v101 high-ascii glyph (GC2a); lig {..}; mark ' (ZL glyph-variant apostrophe); usp uncertain space on either side; cmt a comment questioning the reading; dmg a comment on physical damage (tear, hole, gap, intruding line); ldmg the page or block is marked damaged in '#' comments (f37r torn and stitched, f101v creases badly damaged, f72r3 C1 'could be missing', fRos block 'left margin unreadable'). Positional comments (clock times, N/S/E/W, label numbers, roman numerals) and the non-Voynich symbols @252-254 do not flag words. Agreement: a word 'agrees' when the same locus in the other two transcriptions has the identical word in an aligned position (difflib on word sequences; GC2a converted to EVA).
