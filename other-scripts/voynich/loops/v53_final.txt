# v53 EVOLVE THE MACHINE THAT WROTE IT: final (5 Oct 2026; tools/v53_lib.py, v53_run.py, v53_analyze.py, v53_readback.py, v53_check.py, v53_check2.py; loops/v53_cycle1-3.txt; data/v53_ckpt/, git-ignored)
Idea: do not pick a cipher family. Evolve small meaning-preserving writing procedures (segmentation, abbreviation, in-word reordering, word regrouping, single/positional/homophonic/line-rotating (table-and-grille)/two-piece/line-position tables, and ordered neighbour-dependent padding ops: prefixes, suffixes, filler words, line markers) over 9 real plaintexts (German herbal/natural history and religious; Italian medical, herbal, verse; Latin encyclopedic and verse; Old Czech chronicle; Hebrew legal). Tables are rank-matched to the target's own glyph pieces; every program has an exact decoder and its decodability is measured. Two fitnesses: FORWARD (output matches a 15-statistic Voynich panel incl. x4 glyph-pair irreversibility) and READ-BACK (the decoder turns the target into the corpus's words more than a position-class unit shuffle does). About 164,000 programs scored (forward 2 x 57,600; read-back 3 x 16,200). Controls: a German text written by a hidden program from the same space (language, genre, padding and message must come back), and a Rugg-style table-and-grille text (must give no decodable winner).
Prior art: GA glyph-to-phoneme maps on zodiac labels and GA phrase analysis (julianbunn.org), the hand-built Naibbe verbose cipher (Greshko 2025), our R2 program search and v26 evolved scribe (generators without plaintext). None evolves the encoding procedure over real plaintexts under a decodability constraint.

Surviving motifs (Grade B, layout only): the lowest-cost meaning-preserving machine for the Voynich has (1) no padding glyphs, (2) a whole-word table switch at line start instead of a marker glyph, (3) syllable-sized table units, (4) ~40-glyph lines. What no such machine produced: line-edge glyph effects, cross-word junction coupling and adjacent-word repetition. These are where a simple table code over a real plaintext breaks; any meaningful encoding that explains the Voynich needs an extra mechanism that ties neighbouring words and line edges together (e.g. a state carried across word boundaries) that the padding ops tried here (prefix/suffix by previous glyph, fillers, markers) did not supply at a statistical cost the panel accepted.
Next: give programs a carried state (the choice of code for a word depends on the previous word's code, Naibbe-like but evolved) and re-run the read-back with the trigram kill built into the fitness.
