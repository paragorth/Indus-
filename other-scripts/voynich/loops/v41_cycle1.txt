v41 cycle 1: calibrating the spelling-drift clock (tools/v41_lib.py, v41_cycle1.py, v41_cycle1b.py; checkpoints data/v41_ckpt/c1*.json)
Idea: if A became B through a gradual change of spelling habits, each page is a moment, and within a scribe the drift traits should co-move and order the pages.
Novelty check (web, 4 Oct 2026): the A-B spectrum and its gradual order across sections is known (Timm 2014; Davis 2020 hands; Layfield & Davis 2026 bifolio LSA); v8 here seriated whole vocabularies. Not found: a multi-trait spelling-habit clock inside each hand, calibrated on dated medieval manuscripts, with a topic floor and a bifolio check.
