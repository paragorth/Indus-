v68 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026): is the word-to-word coupling a one-dimensional pitch line?
Idea: a melody written one syllable per word makes the junction between words a function of the pitch STEP (entry note of the next
word minus exit note of this one), mostly small. Fit, blind, an entry class s(w) and exit class e(w) on a line of K = 9 positions for
every word type (count >= 4; rare words by first / last glyph), with three junction models: FREE (any 9 x 9 table), TOEPLITZ (depends
only on the step), SMOOTH (Gaussian step with drift: 2 parameters, i.e. 'mostly stepwise'). Exchange algorithm, random restarts,
correspondence-analysis and Fiedler-seriation starts, iterated local search; fitted on half the folios, scored on the other half
(bits per bigram over unigram). Controls: GregoBase chant in an opaque glyph code (entry/exit pitches known), Latin herbal and German
herbal words in an opaque code, within-line word shuffles of each. Tools: tools/v68_c2.py, tools/v68_c2b.py; data/v68_ckpt/c2*.
