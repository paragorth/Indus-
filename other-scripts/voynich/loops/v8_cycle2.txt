# v8 cycle 2: is Currier A -> B one drifting process? (tools/v8_cycle2.py, v8_cycle2b.py)

| ID | Method and control | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| V-2.1 | Graded-position test. Page B-score = mean token LLR (B vs A unigram, leave-one-page-out), from even lines and from odd lines separately; Spearman of the two inside each language after demeaning by section x hand. Null: tokens redealt within language x section x hand (10x). Controls: Isidore pages, same templates and group sizes, (G) one key drifting through the whole book, (D) two fixed keys with an abrupt switch | Voynich within A rho 0.52 (null -0.04, z 6.3); within B 0.51 (null 0.03, z 4.6). Control G (drift): A 0.39, B 0.54. Control D (discrete): A 0.21, B 0.51 | Not discriminating. Pages do hold a stable individual position on the A-B scale inside each language (z 6.3, 4.6 vs redeal), but the two-fixed-keys control (no drift at all) gives 0.21/0.51 from topic leakage alone, and the drifting-key control 0.39/0.54. Voynich A (0.52) exceeds both; B does not. A stable within-A position is real but cannot be credited to time rather than subject |
| V-2.2 | Gap test: share of pages whose whole-page B-score lies in the middle third between the A and B medians. Controls G and D as above | Voynich 0.036 (7 pages); G 0.122; D 0.000 | Mostly a jump. Only 7/196 pages (3.6%) sit in the middle third; a drifting key gives 12%, two fixed keys 0%. The 7 intermediates are not scattered: 4 A pharma pages (f88v, f89r1, f89r2, f102v2), hand-3 A page f58r, hand-5 B page f57r, one A herbal (f90v1). Group medians order as A herbal (-0.96) < A pharma (-0.50) < hand-3 A (+0.08) < hand-5 B herbal (+0.48) < hand-2/3 B herbal and stars (+0.72..0.82) < hand-2 bio (+1.53). So the A->B scale is a ladder of section x hand groups with few in-between pages, not a smooth page-by-page drift (the gradual-section ordering itself is Timm 2014; the calibration against keyed controls is new) |
| V-2.3 | Oddities as stages: inside A and inside B (demeaned by section x hand), Spearman of page B-score computed WITHOUT the feature-carrying words vs the feature. Calibration: same logic on controls with feature = share of tokens carrying a variant letter (letter excluded from score) | m/g line-end quota (B-A +0.074): A rho +0.15 p=0.11, B rho +0.02 p=0.88; q-initial share (B-A +0.060): A rho +0.02 p=0.85, B rho +0.19 p=0.084; ed share (B-A +0.197): A rho +0.11 p=0.26, B rho -0.37 p=0.00073; -ol/-or share (B-A -0.134): A rho -0.14 p=0.12, B rho -0.11 p=0.35; mean line length (B-A +2.010): A rho +0.16 p=0.097, B rho -0.04 p=0.7 || control G (drift) median rho A 0.30 / B 0.09; control D (discrete) median rho A nan / B -0.04 | Oddities are NOT stages along the A-B scale. Within A, none of m/g quota, q share, ed share, -ol/-or share or line length follows residual B-ness (all p > 0.09; drifting-key control gives median rho 0.30). Within B the strongest effect has the WRONG sign: pages richer in ed have less B-like other vocabulary (rho -0.37, p = 0.0007), transcription-robust in IT2a (-0.34, p = 0.002) and unchanged when all e-words are removed from the score (-0.39). B-ness is at least two-dimensional inside B (an ed-load axis trading off against other B-markers), so A/B cannot be one drifting scalar |
| V-2.4 | Transcription robustness of V-2.3 (tools/v8_cycle2b.py): IT2a; score excluding ed-words vs excluding every e-containing word | ZL within B -0.37 / -0.39; IT within B -0.34 / -0.34; within A +0.09..+0.13 (n.s.) in both | Transcription-robust (Wells/IM77-style caveat: ZL and IT read the same pages). The within-B trade-off is a property of the text, not of one transliteration |
