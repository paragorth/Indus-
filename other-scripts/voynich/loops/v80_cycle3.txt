v80 cycle 3: THE TABLE WRITTEN COLUMN BY COLUMN (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v80_lib.py (transposed_pages), v80_c3.py, v80_c3_report.py. Checkpoints: data/v80_ckpt/c3, transposed.pkl. Two CPU workers.
Idea (invert the problem): cycles 1-2 read the line as a row. Turn the page 90 degrees: each LINE is a COLUMN of the table (its stock of values = the 'line mode', the one thing v67 found a line agrees on), and the next row-block starts after C lines. Then line types recur down the page with period C. Statistic: mean cosine similarity of line vocabularies at lag g inside a page or paragraph; periodic excess PE_g = sim_g - (sim_{g-1} + sim_{g+1})/2 as a z against 40 within-group line-order permutations (which keep every line and the page vocabulary). 300 random hypotheses per corpus (token = full / first two symbols / last symbol; idf on/off; all word positions or a random subset of 2-7 positions; page or paragraph), lags 2-13; the 10 best (hypothesis, lag) pairs on the discovery leaf half re-scored on the held-out half; replication = share of the top 50 discovery picks with held-out z > 2.
Controls: the five real tables of cycle 1 written column by column (blocks of 8 rows; a column's 8 values fill one line), through the v72 surface: X_KAL (5-7 lines per block), X_ALF (13), X_HOR (9 / 6), X_CON (headword line + reference lines), X_DOS (4-6). Also the row-wise tables, prose and six generators. Prior art: v32 (no period in starred paragraph openings), v79 (no period in the line-initial glyph), v10 (period-2 alternation of line-initial glyphs). Recurrence of whole-line vocabularies at a fixed lag was not tested.
