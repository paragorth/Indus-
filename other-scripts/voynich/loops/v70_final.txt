v70 THE TRANSCRIPTION ALPHABET IS WRONG; LET THE INK DECIDE, final (6 Oct 2026). Cycles: loops/v70_cycle1-3.txt. Tools: tools/v70_lib.py, v70_synth.py, v70_build.py, v70_c1.py, v70_c1_report.py, v70_c1b.py, v70_c2.py, v70_c3.py, v70_c3b.py, v70_c3c.py. Checkpoints: data/v70_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Patches, embeddings and images stayed in the scratch cache.

Idea: EVA is a modern convenience alphabet; if the writer's units differ, every statistic is blurred. Cut units from the ink alone (25 Beinecke pages, 11,599 words), learn alphabets without supervision (PCA and self-supervised autoencoder codes, k-means at K 10-80, 3 cut thresholds, 0-12 ligature merges), and score 11,232 ink alphabets (+ 11,232 matched random-Voronoi nulls) by a calibrated language battery, by held-out page and section tracking, and by MDL. Controls: 28 real CREMMA Latin pages (diplomatic letters + abbreviation signs); the Voynich text, 3 generators and 6 languages rendered in a synthetic Voynich-like stroke hand with planted ligatures and a planted split letter.

Results
- Instrument on a known hand works: ink clusters recover the true units at NMI 0.65-0.74 vs 0.46-0.60 for random alphabets; planted ligatures partly (F1 0.34-0.49 vs 0.21-0.32); split letters re-joined by merges. Language vs generator texts separate at every alphabet size (held-out P 0.91-1.00 vs 0.00-0.20).
- Grade A (method, negative): 'choose the alphabet whose re-transcription is most language-like' is invalid. True units LOWER the language score in all 6 languages (k-means beats its random twin in only 14-23% of pairs), because right units make any text more predictable. MDL on ink pixels also fails (synthetic and Latin run to the K ceiling).
- Grade B (negative): in a constant hand the Voynich text reads as generator-like under every ink alphabet (P 0.000 vs 0.91-1.00 for languages).
- Grade B (negative): no ink alphabet tracks Voynich pages better than EVA (0/78; best 0.231 vs 0.267); ink-informed EVA (merging the pen's candidate ligatures) tracks sections and pages no better than random merges (|z| <= 0.9).
- Grade B (negative): the ink shows no pair EVA wrongly splits or merges; all join-rate outliers (i+r, a+i, a+r, i+i) are also the estimator's outliers on a synthetic hand with random touching.
- Grade A (confound): unit width alone identifies Voynich pages at 0.50 (EVA 0.27): physical page drift dominates image alphabets; any future claim must beat a width-only null.
- Limit: the real Latin control fails at 2000-px scans (units are 1-2 letter chunks; shape adds nothing over width), so the image side cannot overrule a transcription at this resolution. Voynich glyphs separate better (shape adds +0.05-0.07 NMI).

Best alphabet: none can be crowned; the cycle-1 'winner' (cut at thin joins, 12 classes + 8 merges) is a set of shape-size classes (minims, loops, tall strokes, benches), each EVA glyph spread over 4-8 classes. EVA stands. Not cracked.
Would revive: full-resolution Beinecke TIFFs with stroke tracing, where a real Latin manuscript gives back its letters and abbreviation signs; then a criterion other than language-likeness (e.g. cross-page consistency of a unit's context) that picks the true alphabet in the synthetic controls.
