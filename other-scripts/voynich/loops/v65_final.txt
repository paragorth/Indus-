v65 FINAL (5 Oct 2026): THE TEXT REMEMBERS WHICH PAGE CAME NEXT
Idea: a meaningful book carries a topic or a term from one page into the next; search every physically allowed page order for maximal page-to-page carry-over (tail of page a -> head of page b), held out by word class, and check the best order against physical codicology.
Prior art: Layfield & Davis 2026 (DHQ, LSA) resequenced bifolia by similarity across page transitions without a permutation null, a shuffled-known-text calibration or a physical check (voynich.ninja thread 5911 review). In-house v8 V-3.3 (conjugate leaves alike) and v58 (leaf orders vs source texts) are different questions.
Machinery (tools/v65_lib.py, v65_c1.py ... v65_c4.py, v65_c3b.py; checkpoints data/v65_ckpt/, git-ignored): quires, bifolios and leaves from the ZL3b IVTFF headers; every nesting order x inside-out refold of every quire enumerated (384 per herbal quire, 3,840 Q13, 322,560 Q20), lost leaves and lost centre bifolios as chain breaks; Layfield-Davis singulion model for Q13/Q20; about 130 million arrangement scores. Seam = idf overlap of last 3 (or 6) lines with first 3 (or 6) lines, double-centred within section x Currier language; whole words or payload skeletons; md5 word-class halves for hold-out. Controls: Isidore and Konrad von Megenberg (continuous) and Konrad / Macer (one entry per page), letters -> opaque codes with Voynich-like padding, laid into the exact Voynich page/line/word layout, in binding order or in an order that satisfies the physical claims. Nulls: per-page word-bigram Markov resynthesis; pages relabelled within section; self-citation generator.

Cycle 1 (carry-over strength): Voynich seam z 3.1 (ZL) / 2.3 (IT), resid after whole-page similarity 2.0 / 1.4, direction (arrow) z 0.9 / 0.4. Continuous opaque Isidore: seam z 14, arrow 4.4; Konrad 8.0 / 2.6. One-entry-per-page herbals: 0 (no power). Markov: 0. Self-citation generator: seam z 59, arrow 12.
Cycle 2 (search): no stable Voynich order (word-half agreement -0.02 to +0.02, null +-0.06); optimum shares adjacencies with the binding only in ZL skeleton (+0.17, z ~2.3), not IT. Calibration: the optimum recovers 29 / 18 extra true adjacency points for Isidore / Konrad; the held-out percentile statistic is miscalibrated (Markov z 2.8); the generator gives the strongest, most stable order (agree +0.63, bind +0.68).
Cycle 3 (physical): single claimed pairs (f78v/f81r, f33v/f40r, f10v/f15r, five contact-transfer openings) have no power even in calibrated prose. Leaf-to-leaf flow within quires in the binding is at the null level (pct 0.46-0.54 vs controls 0.52-0.69, Markov 0.45-0.52). Herbal quire joins (signature order) carry nothing. Quire centres: with whole words the text's preferred centre is the physically claimed one in 5 of 6 cases (Q13 78/81, Q5 33/40, Q2 10/15; top-5% share 0.37-0.47 vs prior 0.20-0.25, calibrated prose means 0.33-0.37, Markov 0.19-0.22); the skeleton keeps only Q13 (E2/E3 0.16/0.00), and line bootstraps give 0.19-0.39.

Reconstructed order (best, per the brief): Q13 nesting 75, 76, 79, 77, 78 (outer -> inner), 78/81 central but refolded (ZL skel, IT word, IT skel; ZL word 75, 78, 79, 76, 77); Q5 35, 36, 34, 33 (word, both transcriptions); Q20 unstable across all four runs. Agreement with physical evidence: quire centres yes (C); page-to-page openings no better than chance; quire order no.

GRADES
- A (method): exhaustive physically constrained order search with calibration is cheap and works for continuous prose in opaque padded code (true-adjacency excess +0.29 / +0.18), but a single page pair carries almost no evidence and word-class hold-out fails even on real prose at this size.
- A (control): the brief's premise is false for copy-based generators: a self-citation writer has 20x MORE page-to-page carry-over than meaningful prose and gives a stable, binding-matching order. Carry-over and order stability cannot separate meaning from generation.
- B (measurement, negative): Voynich pages do not flow into one another as continuous prose does: weak seam (1/5 of prose), no direction, and within quires the binding's leaf-to-leaf neighbours are no closer than any two pages. Either the text is entry-per-page (as one-plant-per-page herbals are, which have no power here) or the leaves are not in their written order; the test cannot tell which.
- C: the bifolios that physical evidence puts at quire centres (78/81, 33/40, 10/15) are the ones whose own halves share most vocabulary. Would support: same in a third transcription and for a newly documented centre; would kill: random bifolios showing the same preference under a conjugate-similarity null, or the effect vanishing with a different idf/line window. Even if it holds, it shows the two halves of a sheet were written together, which a drifting generator also predicts: it extracts no meaning.
Not cracked; nothing predicted outside the corpus beyond the grade-C centre lean.
