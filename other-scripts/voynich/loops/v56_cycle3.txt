v56 THE BOOK IS HYPERTEXT, cycle 3 (5 Oct 2026): learned pointer TABLES (no arithmetic)
Tools: tools/v56_c3.py, tools/v56_c3b.py. Checkpoints: data/v56_ckpt/c3_*.json, c3b.json
Method. Any word type (or payload skeleton) may point to one arbitrary page. Its target is learned on half the pages, as the page
that its contexts most resemble (residual affinity R; own page and +-2 excluded). On the other half, the same type's contexts must
again resemble that page. Three statistics, each averaged over 3 page splits: z_hit; z_spec (target beats the 5 pages most like it:
a pointer names ONE page, topical vocabulary names a neighbourhood); z_pair (learned targets shuffled among the types of the class,
200x; this removes class-level hub pages). Token classes: all, labels, paragraph-first, line-first, line-last, gallows words,
frame-only words. Controls: Culpeper with Roman-numeral pointers (CULP), Culpeper with the herb names left in place (real named
cross-references), Brumati's herbal (a real herbal with NO cross-references, topic only), planted positional pointers in the
Voynich. Nulls: relabelled pages, unit-Markov, self-citation.
