v58 FINAL (5 Oct 2026): FIND THE SOURCE BY ITS SHAPE, NOT ITS WORDS
Idea: if the Voynich encodes or translates an existing book, the sequence of its entry lengths (long and short chapters, recipes, plant entries) survives any encoding, word for word or not. Search it.
Machine: local Smith-Waterman alignment of log entry lengths with a free scale, merges (1-2 Voynich units vs 1-3 entries) and skips, in C (tools/v58_align.c). Nulls: Voynich units shuffled and BLOCK-shuffled (main null), source entries shuffled. Corpus: 25 works (Pliny NH Lat+Eng, Celsus Lat+Eng, Dioscorides Gr, Theophrastus Gr, Hildegard Physica (partial), Regimen Salernitanum, Konrad von Megenberg Buch der Natur, Hyginus De astronomia, Apicius Lat+Eng, Forme of Cury, Culpeper, Macer floridus, Psalms Heb+Eng, Circa instans Old French, Antidotarium Nicolai Middle Dutch/Latin, Leechdoms OE herbarium, De balneis Puteolanis synopsis, Gerard 1597 page OCR, Isidore XVI-XVII, an Italian herbal). Texts and caches in data/v58_ckpt/ (git-ignored).

Cycle 1 (controls, A): a translated, verbosely encoded, re-tokenised, merged/dropped copy is found from ~87 units (Celsus zb 9.6-10.0 at 87, 21-24 at 292; Psalms He->En 8.5-15). Different chapter division costs power (Pliny Lat->Eng found at 292 units, zb 8-9; not at 128). A different recension padded with commentary is NOT found (Apicius, zb 1-3.5). 56 wrong pairings: zb max 3.03. Bar: zb and zx >= 4, replicated in IT2a.
Cycle 2 (B, negative): 200 section x work tests in folio order: min(zx,zb) mean -0.28, sd 1.14, max 3.28, the null level. Every hit fails replication or the partner test: herbal paragraphs x Pliny Latin (ZL 2.8 -> IT2a 1.1; English Pliny 0.6 / -0.5), herbal pages x Apicius English (3.5 -> 1.8); the most stable 'hit' is the off-genre negative, English Psalms vs pharma paragraphs (3.2 / 2.9). Circa instans, Macer, Dioscorides, Hildegard, Konrad's plant books: all <= 2.3 on every herbal sequence.
Cycle 3 (B, negative): massive order guessing (300 random + 1,200 hill-climbing leaf orders within quires per test, same search on shuffled data as null). Planted misbound sources are recovered (z 5.8-13.4); real herbal pages and stars paragraphs x 10 candidate works: z -1.5 to 0.8. Misbinding within quires is not what hides a source here.
Cycle 4 (B, negative): best of 8 rulers (Voynich words, glyphs, lines, gallows+bench glyphs x source words, letters). Planted source with word breaks unrelated to source words found (zb 19-23). Real: 94 tests, max 2.5, none >= 3. The bio x Hyginus De astronomia lead (zx 3.8/4.5, zb 2.8/3.1 in ZL/IT2a) is a diffuse path over the whole work, vanishes on book 2 alone (0.9/1.7) and cannot be partner-tested: demoted to noise.

GRADES
- A (method): entry-length shape alignment finds a translated + opaquely encoded source at Voynich section size (>= 87 units), even misbound within quires or behind scrambled word breaks. It fails on a different recension with commentary.
- B (negative, bounded): none of the 25 works is the source of any Voynich section (herbal, stars/recipes, bio, pharma, text) at the strength a translated copy would show. In particular no Circa instans, Macer floridus, Dioscorides, Antidotarium Nicolai or Hyginus profile is present. Not covered: works outside this corpus (no Tacuinum, no Tractatus de herbis, no Arabic or Czech medical text with usable entry structure could be fetched), abridged/glossed recensions, re-ordering across quires, and paragraph markup that does not follow source entries.
- C- (demoted): bio paragraphs ~ Hyginus De astronomia. Would support: a full independent translation (or Aratus-derived parallel) that also aligns with f75-f84; would kill: z < 2 there.
Prediction outside the corpus: none survives (no folio -> chapter map is worth testing against the illustrations). Not cracked.
