v55 THE SKY IS THE ANSWER KEY, cycle 4: the CIRCULAR TEXTS of the zodiac pages as day runs (5 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/v55_cycle4.py (ring words from data/ZL3b-n.txt @Cc lines via v55_lib.load_ring_words), tools/v55_lib.py,
tools/v55_report.py. Checkpoints data/v55_ckpt/c4_*.
Data: 964 words of the circular texts of f70v2-f73v (10 signs, 66-160 words each, outer ring first as transcribed).
Word k of n in a sign = the day the Sun stands at degree 30k/n (+ rotation 0-29, both directions) of that sign in
year Y, every Y 1290-1611. 118 word classes (every type seen >= 6 times, 2/3-unit affixes seen >= 20 times,
substrings, 30 random merges of glyph features) x 24 sky features (Moon sign, phase8, Moon-Sun sign distance, and the
21 events of cycle 2) x 322 years x 60 alignments, ~55 million G-tests per condition; held-out halves A/B by sign.
Order-preserving null added in this cycle: sperm = each sign's ring text moved whole to another sign (derangement),
so every word sequence keeps its internal order and only the link to that sign's sky is broken.
