v58 cycle 1 (5 Oct 2026) - FIND THE SOURCE BY ITS SHAPE: calibrating the shape-alignment machine on known pairs
Machine (tools/v58_align.c, v58_lib.py): local Smith-Waterman alignment of log entry lengths with a free scale (grid of c around the median ratio, +-0.8), merges (1-2 Voynich units vs 1-3 source entries), skips. Score per matched pair = 1 - d^2/(2*0.3^2), d = log(V) - log(S) - c.
Nulls: zx = Voynich-side units shuffled; zb = Voynich side block-shuffled (blocks of 8, keeps short-range autocorrelation; the main null); zy = source entries shuffled (anticonservative when the source has runs of long/short chapters).
Encoding simulator: an opaque verbose cipher run from the source's letter counts (each letter -> 1-2 glyphs, glyph stream re-cut into tokens of mean 5 glyphs), 5% entries dropped, 10% merged with the next, 10% split, lognormal layout jitter sd 0.15; then a window cut to Voynich section size (44, 87, 128, 292).
Corpus (data/v58_ckpt/texts.json, 25 works): Pliny NH Latin + English (Perseus), Celsus Latin + English (Perseus), Dioscorides Greek, Theophrastus HP Greek (First1KGreek), Hildegard Physica (partial), Regimen Salernitanum, Konrad von Megenberg Buch der Natur (whole + plant books), Hyginus De astronomia (Augustana / Latin Library), Apicius Latin (Latin Library, abridged) + English (Gutenberg 29728 with commentary), Forme of Cury, Culpeper, Macer floridus (la.wikisource), Psalms Hebrew + English (Sefaria), Circa instans Old French (Dorveaux 1913 OCR), Antidotarium Nicolai Middle Dutch + Latin (OCR), Leechdoms vol. 1 (OE Herbarium + translation, OCR), De balneis Puteolanis synopsis (OCR), Gerard 1597 page OCR, Isidore XVI-XVII and an Italian herbal (repo v21). German and second French Circa instans OCR could not be segmented and were dropped; no Arabic or Czech text with usable entry structure was found in the time.
