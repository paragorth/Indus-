# v17 cycle 1 -- THE GHOST OF THE EXEMPLAR: candidate sites and calibration (4 Oct 2026). Script tools/v17_cycle1.py, lib tools/v17_lib.py, results data/results/v17/cycle1.json.
# Novelty check (web, 4 Oct 2026): copying errors and a separate copyist are discussed qualitatively on voynich.ninja (e.g. threads 61, 5718, 5830: dittography, skipped words, line-end fillers, 'line breaks belong to the copy'). No quantitative search for a lost exemplar line width (periodic grid of mid-line line-initial/line-final ghosts and repeats, with planted-exemplar controls) was found.
# Ghost channels per mid-line gap: INIT = LLR(first glyph of next word: line-initial vs mid-line), FIN = LLR(last glyph of previous word: line-final vs mid-line), REP = exact repeat or a-b-a-b near repeat. LLR tables cross-fitted (fit on the other half of folios). Each channel residualised by section x copy-position bin (word index from start x words left), z-scored; GHOST = sum/sqrt3. Copy line-break gaps weight 0. Position x = glyph units + 1 per word from paragraph start.
# Search: exemplar width L = 15..100 (step 0.5); A(L) = paragraph-anchored cosine score, R(L) = free-phase Rayleigh power; max over L vs 60 line-order shuffles (lines 2..n permuted in paragraph) and 60 within-line word shuffles.
