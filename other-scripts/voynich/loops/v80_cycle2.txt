v80 cycle 2: THE ROW AS A LEFT-TO-RIGHT COLUMN MACHINE (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v80_lib.py (RowHMM), v80_c2.py, v80_c2_report.py, v80_c2b.py. Checkpoints: data/v80_ckpt/c2, c2b.json. Two CPU workers.
Idea: cycle 1's column hypotheses assume cells sit at fixed word slots or x-positions. A table copied carelessly drops blank cells and lets a cell run over two words, so slot alignment breaks. Let the row itself pick the columns: a left-to-right HMM with K column states (a cell continues or jumps to any later column; skipped columns vanish), column-specific value laws smoothed to the unigram, fitted by EM on one leaf half and scored on the other (both directions). Random configurations: K 1-13, smoothing 0.4-0.9, three random starts per K (chosen in-sample), two token representations. Gain = held-out bits/token over the HMM with K=1; kill null = the same on interior-shuffled lines (3-4 seeds for the Voynich). Then (2b) the simplest form of the same question: held-out information of a token about its interior relative position (early/middle/late), real vs 5 interior shuffles.
