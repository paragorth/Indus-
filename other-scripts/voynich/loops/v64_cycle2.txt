v64 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026): spelled padding, fresh random search, hill-climbed survivors, held-out test, section topic profiles.
Scripts: tools/v64_cycle2.py, tools/v64_fix2.py (post-fix of truth fields after an indentation bug; recomputed, not edited), tools/v64_report2.py; report data/v64_ckpt/c2_report.txt (git-ignored).
Per corpus: 10,000 random alphabets (90,000 in all) ranked by selection-split Gfree; the 6 best plus the 20 commonest single glyphs are hill-climbed (20 rounds x 60 neighbours = 1,200 alphabets per start; ~75,600 in all); the best climbed alphabet is re-scored on the held split (train q2 / test q3, never seen in selection).
DELTA = held Gfree of the climbed alphabet (FREE Lullian model) minus held Gm1 of the plain glyph alphabet (concept Markov-1 on single glyphs): does a small concept alphabet combined freely beat a glyph sequence model on unseen pages?
