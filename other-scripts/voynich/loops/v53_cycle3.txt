Inversion of the fitness after cycle 1 showed that statistics cannot name the language. Same program space; each program's DECODER is applied to the target (3,000-token search slice of half A) and scored = coverage x (share of decoded multi-unit words that are words of the program's plaintext corpus, after the program's abbreviation) minus the same share after decoded units are shuffled among multi-unit words within position class (first / middle / last unit; word lengths kept). Lexicons exclude the search and held-out plaintext stretches and, for DE_rel, the planted stretch. 9 corpora x 300 programs x 6 generations per target (16,200 programs per target, 48,600 in all); top 5 per corpus re-scored on held-out half B with tables rebuilt from half B. Two earlier versions of the null were exploitable (single-unit function words; positional unit classes) and were replaced before the counted runs (logs rb_planted_v0/v1).
