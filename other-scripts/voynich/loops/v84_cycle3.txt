v84 CYCLE 3 (7 Oct 2026): POSITIVE CONTROLS, A DISCRIMINATING STATISTIC, THEN THE VOYNICH.
Tools: tools/v84_c3.py (fit, stats, voynich), tools/v84_c3lib.py. Checkpoints data/v84_ckpt/c3_*.jsonl, c3_ctrl_stats.json, c3_voy_stats.json. Two CPU workers.
Method: the cycle-2 generator family was fitted to each real control (Konrad and Culpeper herbals, Apicius and Forme of Cury recipe books, all through the merge code and v72 surface) to match the control's own PB, PB_far / PB and surface statistics (80 random settings + 6 climbing rounds x 20 per control). Eight candidate statistics were computed on each control, its page-shuffled twin and its best 3 fitted settings x 2 seeds. Pre-registered rule: a statistic discriminates if, in >= 3 of 4 controls, the real value lies outside the range of all 6 generator runs, on the same side each time. The Voynich counts as content-like on it if ZL3b and IT2a both lie beyond every Voynich-fitted generator run (best 6 settings of cycle 2 x 2 seeds), on the real side. T_conf (share of 2-4-token types confined to one page, over page-shuffled twins) was added after cycle 2's plug-in page-MI lead, but before any Voynich value of it was computed.
