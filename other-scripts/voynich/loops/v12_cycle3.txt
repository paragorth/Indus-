Cycle 3 verdict: the tandem swaps (l/r, k/t, l/o, ch/cth at lag 1) are neighbour assimilation inside one stream: they do not mark stream boundaries, line junctions show no pairing phase, there is no second-order alternation and no filler stream. One narrow exception with the weave shape: sh-word / q-word / ch-word echoes at a one-word gap (ch/sh enriched at lag 2 only, z 4.4-6.0 against a position-preserving null), mostly words 2-4 of a line.
