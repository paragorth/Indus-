# Search space: 8 section sets (HA SB BB HB PA TB other + pooled ALL) x 4 channels (GHOST, INIT, FIN, REP) x 2 units (glyph+space width 15-100 step 0.5; words 3-14 step 0.1) x 2 phase models (anchored at paragraph start; free common phase) = 120 combinations, ~ 16,800 width hypotheses per text. Nulls 150 each: line-order shuffle (keeps the copy layout), within-line word shuffle, Markov resynthesis (composed directly). Family-wise p = rank of the observed smallest p among each null replicate's smallest p (leave-one-out).
