v41 final: THE SPELLING DRIFT AS A CLOCK (4 cycles; tools/v41_lib.py, v41_cycle1.py, v41_cycle1b.py, v41_cycle2.py, v41_cycle3.py, v41_cycle4.py, v41_cycle4b.py; checkpoints data/v41_ckpt/, git-ignored)

Idea: if Currier A became B through a gradual change of spelling habits, each page is a moment. Inside each scribe the drift traits should co-move, order the pages, and agree with the physical writing units.
Novelty (web, 4 Oct 2026): the A-B spectrum across sections (Timm 2014), the hands (Davis 2020) and bifolio LSA similarity (Layfield & Davis 2026) are known, and v8 here seriated whole vocabularies. Not found anywhere: a multi-trait spelling-habit clock inside each hand, calibrated on dated medieval manuscripts, with a topic floor and a bifolio check.

Method: 11 word-level traits (bench_init, bench_med, the -edy share among A and B endings, -ol/-or, qo-, ee, ed, -aiin, y-, -dy, and the B-ending share among stems seen with both endings, i.e. spelling with the word held fixed), oriented by the whole-book A vs B difference. Page values are shrunken proportions on odd and even line halves. Co-movement S is the mean oriented cross-trait correlation across halves. Null: each trait permuted independently. Seriation: simulated annealing with 10-16 restarts.

Controls
- Real dated drift (97 ReF German manuscripts, 1360-1549). Traits chosen on half the texts order the held-out texts by date (rho 0.63-0.75), and traits co-move across texts (z 11-26). Within one manuscript (one scribe, one moment) S = 0 (z -1.5 to +0.8). PASS. Topic alone does not make spelling traits co-move.
- Planted drift on redealt Voynich pages (no native structure). Caught when it touches up to 20% of words (z 4-10, order rho 0.5-0.8); missed at 5-10%. The redeal null is clean.
- Shuffled traits: no co-movement. The held-out order statistic is biased (+0.13 from single-trait reliability), so it is read only against that null.
- A planted two-state jump is ordered as well as a drift, and the clock's shape cannot tell them apart. So 'drift vs jump' is NOT decided by this method.
- Bug caught: empirical logits with a fixed prior faked reliability through page length. Fixed before any reported number.

Results
1. Grade B. Every hand carries a coherent A->B axis of spelling habits from page to page: S z +4 to +11 in hand-1 A, hand-2 B, hand-3 B and all B, in both ZL3b and IT2a. Random trait orientations are as coherent in <= 0.7% of draws for 13 of 18 runs. S/R is 0.12-0.43, at or above real dated German. The carriers are the -edy/-dy endings, ed/ee and the stem-held ending choice. Word-initial benched gallows (v36) does NOT drift within hands: bench loss is a between-group jump, the endings drift.
2. Grade B. In B (hands 2 and 3) a bifolio is one moment of this clock. The two sides of a leaf and conjugate leaves sit closer than other same-quire pages (all B: leaf z -3.2/-3.4, conjugates -2.6/-2.4; hand-2 bio conjugates -3.0; hand-3 stars leaf -2.5). This fits the v8 bifolio writing unit, now for spelling habits and not only vocabulary.
3. Hand-1 A has no bifolio clustering (z -0.1 to +1.6). Its clock rises with foliation (rho +0.33/+0.31), but only between quires (9 quire means +0.73/+0.58, p 0.03-0.05), not within them (+0.10/+0.07, n.s.). Dropping the late quires O and Q weakens it to +0.19 (n.s.). Grade C: hand-1 herbal quires bound later are slightly more B-like in their endings. Would support: the same quire step (D, G, O up) in an independent feature set such as line-final glyphs; would kill: no step there, or the step tracks section boundaries (pharma-adjacent quires) rather than binding.
4. Which hands moved first: no shared order of trait changes across groups (concordance -0.13, below null z -1.9). Groups are not stages of one sequence. On the whole-book clock: hand-1 herbal -0.55 < hand-1 pharma -0.09 < hand-3 herbal +0.33 < hand-2 herbal +0.46 = hand-5 herbal +0.48 < hand-3 stars +0.71 < hand-2 bio +1.09. Section moves the clock more than hand does, which repeats the v8 ladder. Mixed pages come in quire blocks (A pharma and late A herbal f89-f102; hand-2 herbal f33-f40 in quire E; hand-4 cosmological pages), not as scattered single pages.
5. Grade C (few bifolios, pseudo-replicated). In hand 2, inner bifolios are less B-like than outer ones (depth rho -0.32/-0.34; bio -0.55/-0.61), and the second leaf of a pair is less B-like in 73% of 48 pairs. Would support: the same in hand 3 with more quires; would kill: the effect is one quire (quire 13).

Answer: a writing order exists only at the level of writing units. In B, bifolios carry a shared drift position. Hand-1 shows a weak quire-level trend. There is no page-by-page sequence inside quires, and drift cannot be told from a mix of states. Ink and colour were not tested (not in the data). Not cracked; nothing predicted outside the corpus.
