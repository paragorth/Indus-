v84 CYCLE 1 (7 Oct 2026): MEASURE THE PAGE VOCABULARY WITH ONE FROZEN STATISTIC.
Tools: tools/v84_lib.py (PB, frozen; definition hash 958834fb57667413), tools/v84_c1.py (build, run, extra). Checkpoints data/v84_ckpt/c1_*.pkl (git-ignored). VOY_MODE=glyph. Two CPU workers.
PB = held-out page information per word beyond onset, line and section: baseline p0(word | onset, section) trained on leaf half 0; on leaf half 1 each line-interior word is also predicted from the same-onset words on the OTHER lines of its page (own line excluded, so line mood and in-line repeats do not count); cache weight n/(n+kappa), kappa tuned on the swapped halves only. Null: test-half lines dealt at random to page slots of the same section (10 deals). PB = gain - null mean (bits/word). Variants: PB_far (cache only from lines >= 3 lines away), PB_word (no onset conditioning). Each corpus also run with the leaf halves swapped (second number).
