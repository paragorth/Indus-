Cycle-2 verdict: the Voynich succession space has none of the walk signatures that the planted grid shows strongly (lag-2
proximity, triangle closure, composition). Caveat on power: at 60% noise the planted grid is also near the language band on
V-2.1 and V-2.4 (z -1.3, 0.005 bits), so these rows exclude a moderately clean map, not a very noisy one; the cycle-1 map
advantage (0.57 vs 2.7 for grid-60) covers the noisy case.
