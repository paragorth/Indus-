v84 CYCLE 2 (7 Oct 2026): CAN A MEANINGLESS GENERATOR FAKE THE PAGE VOCABULARY?
Tools: tools/v84_lib.py (gen_page, sample_params), tools/v84_c2.py (sweep, climb, final), tools/v84_c2_report.py. Checkpoints data/v84_ckpt/c2_*.jsonl. Two CPU workers. The container restarted once during the sweep (520 of the planned settings done); the sweep was resumed from its checkpoint and capped at 1,400 random settings, and a few random settings may be repeated or skipped (the job order is unordered).
Generator family: the v82 line-mood + harmony generator (STACK / self-citation / junction base, fitted or random onset moods by line, word or passage, glyph-harmony agreement) extended with page mechanisms over word bodies: glyph page mood (random weights over body glyphs or glyph bigrams, AR(1) across pages up to section-level drift, drifting line by line), lexical page mood (random per-type weights, AR drift, or K recurring discrete moods), a per-page seed vocabulary with copy-and-vary restarted on each page, and self-citation within the page or across the book. Each setting scored with frozen PB and PB_far, the 9 v82 surface statistics (line edges, plug-in page vocabulary, doubling, neighbour ratio, junction, TTR, word length, hapax rate; v82 bands), and the frozen v81 onset link (3 definitions).
Pre-registered kill lines: SIZE = PB in [0.037, 0.102] + all 9 statistics in band + each onset gain in [0.5x, 2x] of ZL, in 3 of 3 fresh seeds. SHAPE = SIZE + PB_far / PB >= 0.80.

Cycle verdict: no meaningless generator in 3,470 settings matches the page vocabulary with all other statistics in band (kill lines not crossed in 0/90 fresh runs). The nearest is book-wide self-citation with constant variation, the Timm-Schinner mechanism, which v71 already found leaves a cross-page order trace that the Voynich lacks. Page vocabulary survives, narrowly.
