Cycle-5 verdict: the non-metric succession found in cycle 4 (two vectors per word) is mostly the glyph-junction rule: a word's
'predecessor vector' is its last glyph and its 'successor vector' its first glyph. There is no room left
for a hidden map: junction + topic account for almost everything a 2-D word model can learn.
