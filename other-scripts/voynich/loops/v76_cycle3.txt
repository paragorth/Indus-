Method: (a) the cycle-2b slot analysis restricted to the starred recipe section (S, both paragraphs of a pair starred) and to herbal first paragraphs (H, paragraph 0; in the controls, every page's first paragraph); (b) LINKED attributes: template groups with >= 3 cross-page members aligned to the medoid paragraph; for every pair of variable columns (varies in some members, not all), a perfect co-variation (|phi| = 1) counted when its exact permutation p < 0.05; rate = linked pairs / column pairs; (c) the IDENTIFIER STREAM: per page, the slot fillers that are page-own (in another paragraph of the page, absent from the partner page); 'item pages' = pages with >= 3 page-own fillers; concentration = share of the commonest filler on such pages; next-page sharing (Jaccard of identifier sets of consecutive pages, same section) vs 5 random same-section pages. All on real texts and the 7 fitted generators x 2 seeds (PARCOPYID = planted item slot).
