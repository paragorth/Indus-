words of 6+ units, total <= 3 edits), tools/v54_lib.py, tools/v54_c1.py, tools/v54_c1b.py. Units: Voynich glyph units
(ch, sh, benched gallows = one unit), words with '?' dropped, pages >= 20 tokens (ZL3b 207 pages / 34,857 tokens;
IT2a 206 / 34,411). Controls: Brumati's Italian herbal (40,851 tokens, 140 pages, 6 class sections) with planted

the same herbal without plants; 14th-15th c. Bavarian/Ripuarian German from ReF with its own real spelling variation
(88,393 tokens). Nulls (all keep the corpus's words or unit statistics): lineshuf (words shuffled across lines within
section), posshuf (within section and line slot first/middle/last), pageshuf (within page), markov (unit-trigram word
resynthesis per section), selfcit (copy-and-modify generator per section). Checkpoints data/v54_ckpt/c1.json, c1b.json.
