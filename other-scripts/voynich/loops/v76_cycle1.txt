v76 VOYNICH PARAGRAPHS AS DOSSIERS: cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v76_lib.py, v76_c1.py, v76_c1_report.py. Checkpoints data/v76_ckpt/ (git-ignored). Two CPU workers. v73 (one word per line) and v75 (designation-code kinds) running, not duplicated.
Idea (after pe63, Proto-Elamite template dossiers): if pages are independent records (v56), paragraphs may be written to a plan. Find groups of paragraphs that are near-copies of one plan by massive random template guessing, then (cycle 2) align them and type the slots (fixed = template, free swap = identifier, co-varying = linked attribute), and test whether identifier slots carry page-own items beyond a fitted copy-and-vary generator.
Prior art (web, 6 Oct 2026): Stolfi's notes 076/077 locate paragraph breaks in the starred section; voynich.ninja thread 918 on stars as bullets. No search for template-copied paragraph groups with slot typing against fitted generators was found. Nearest earlier loops: v22 (word-class positional program: only the head line), v32 (no calendar beat in starred openings), v54 (near-repeat collation of 3-word passages).
Corpus: every text taken through the v72 frozen extraction E1c (hash 2803cbeb0deb51bf asserted). Voynich ZL3b (740 paragraphs) and IT2a (772). Positive controls (data only): Culpeper's English herbal (page = herb entry, first 4 paragraphs: name+Descript., Place, Time, Government and virtues), Brumati's Italian herbal (page = species/genus entry), Apicius (page = Roman-numeral subsection, paragraph = numbered recipe with its title); each spelled with a lossy merge payload code, dressed in the planted v72 surface machinery, extracted with E1c. Generators fitted to every surface (2 seeds): WSHUF, MK2, SELFCIT, JUNC, SC10, and new PARCOPY (paragraph copy-and-vary: copy a random earlier same-section paragraph from any page token by token with per-token mutation m, with prob c; c and m grid-fitted so the extracted stream's mean top-1 cross-page paragraph similarity matches the target) and PARCOPYID (the fitted PARCOPY plus a planted page-own item word at token 1 of every paragraph, recurring once later: a positive plant of an identifier slot).
Template search: 20,000 random template definitions per corpus (75 corpora, 1.5 million template evaluations). A template = 1-3 of {opening tokens at chosen first-line positions, closing 1-2 tokens, line-initial tokens of lines 2-4, line-final tokens of lines 1-3, line-count or length bin, paragraph position on page}, each token reduced by a random glyph-class map (identity or 2-8 classes) and a random cut (full, first/last 1-3 symbols, length). Paragraphs with the same signature (group size 2-30) are a candidate dossier. Score = sum over cross-page same-section pairs in groups of the standardized idf-cosine similarity on the evaluation region, which is disjoint from every view (all line-initial/-final tokens, first-line positions 0-2 and the last 2 tokens excluded), divided by sqrt(pairs). Discovery = pairs within even leaves, holdout = odd leaves; the same template list for every corpus.
