Generators fitted to every surface (2 seeds): WSHUF, MK2, SELFCIT, JUNC, SC10, and new PARCOPY (paragraph copy-and-vary: copy a random earlier same-section paragraph from any page token by token with per-token mutation m, with prob c; c and m grid-fitted so the extracted stream's mean top-1 cross-page paragraph similarity matches the target) and PARCOPYID (the fitted PARCOPY plus a planted page-own item word at token 1 of every paragraph, recurring once later: a positive plant of an identifier slot).
Template search: 20,000 random template definitions per corpus (75 corpora, 1.5 million template evaluations). A template = 1-3 of {opening tokens at chosen first-line positions, closing 1-2 tokens, line-initial tokens of lines 2-4, line-final tokens of lines 1-3, line-count or length bin, paragraph position on page}, each token reduced by a random glyph-class map (identity or 2-8 classes) and a random cut (full, first/last 1-3 symbols, length). Paragraphs with the same signature (group size 2-30) are a candidate dossier. Score = sum over cross-page same-section pairs in groups of the standardized idf-cosine similarity on the evaluation region, which is disjoint from every view (all line-initial/-final tokens, first-line positions 0-2 and the last 2 tokens excluded), divided by sqrt(pairs). Discovery = pairs within even leaves, holdout = odd leaves; the same template list for every corpus.
