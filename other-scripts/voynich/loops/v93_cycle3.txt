# v93 cycle 3 (7 Oct 2026): IT IS A DIALOGUE? (candidate I3). VOY_MODE=glyph.
# Idea: medieval teaching books are often master-pupil dialogues. Two speakers alternate; turns of the same speaker share idiolect (I / you,
#   address forms, question words). Zig-zag statistic over a sequence of turns u_t: s_k = mean cosine(u_t, u_t+k), Z = s2 - (s1 + s3) / 2,
#   z = Z / SE over windows. An ordinary text, a copy generator or a shuffle decays monotonically; a dialogue zig-zags. Payoff: the words that
#   make lag 2 beat lags 1 and 3 are the speaker / person markers (meaning by function).
# Not done before: N4 (FINDINGS section 7) tested one line-level glyph score for ABAB alternation; v12 tested woven word streams. No
#   paragraph-level speaker alternation of vocabulary has been tested (web check 7 Oct 2026 found none).
# Planted: Petrarch's Secretum (1347-53; Augustine / Petrarch, 536 turns, 44,583 words; English tr. Draper 1911, Gutenberg 49450), each turn
#   a paragraph (<= 120 words), v72 code + surface. Kills: the same turns in shuffled order (PE_SHUF), Augustine's turns only (PE_MONO), Celsus
#   Latin and Psalms English (non-dialogue), Voynich line+paragraph shuffles, SELFCIT, MK2, JUNC.
