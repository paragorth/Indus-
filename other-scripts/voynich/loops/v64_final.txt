Verdict: Not cracked. The Lullian concept-combination reading is refuted at the glyph n-gram level (Grade B): the pipeline finds Lullian alphabets and rules when planted and rejects Latin, and the Voynich behaves like glyph-sequence text, not like a combinatoria.
