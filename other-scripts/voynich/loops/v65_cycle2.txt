v65 cycle 2 (5 Oct 2026): exhaustive search of every physically allowed arrangement of each quire for maximal carry-over, held out by word class
Tool: tools/v65_c2.py. Checkpoints: data/v65_ckpt/c2.json, c2.log (git-ignored).
Search: all nesting orders x inside-out refolds of every quire (384 per herbal quire, 3,840 Q13, 322,560 Q20; ~335,000 arrangements per corpus pass, x 3 word classes x (1 + 30 null relabellings) per Voynich run, about 130 million arrangement scores in all), plus the Layfield-Davis 'singulion' model for Q13 and Q20 (each bifolio its own gathering, sequence free). Score = sum of double-centred tail->head seams along the page sequence.
Statistics: held-out z = arrangement chosen on word-class 0 scored on class 1 (and vice versa), percentile, summed over 13 quires; agree = shared directed adjacencies of the class-0 and class-1 optima minus the random-pair rate; bind = shared adjacencies of the all-word optimum with the binding minus the random rate (for controls the binding IS the true order). Null: pages relabelled within section x language (30x).
