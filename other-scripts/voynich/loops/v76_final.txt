What this means for the meaning hunt: if each page is a record, its content is not laid out as a fixed-plan entry with a named item slot (as Culpeper's or Brumati's are), at the resolution of the v72 payload tokens. Either the record plan lives below the token level (inside words or in glyph choices), or the paragraphs are free prose-like records with no shared frame. The surviving C- item-page signal is the only lead and needs GC2a.
