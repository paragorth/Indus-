v90 FINAL (7 Oct 2026): WORDS AS WHEEL COORDINATES
Idea: read each Voynich word as coordinates on Lullian concept wheels (anchored glyph fields grouped into wheels, each with its own slot alphabet), search thousands of random decompositions, and ask whether one wheel carries the page's content while the others carry grammar or habit. Prior-work check: v64 (concept alphabets and wheel steps between neighbouring words) and the slot-grammar and paradigm loops (v7, v14, v33) never asked which part of the word carries page content; a web search found only a generation reading (voynich.ninja, 'Lullian Apophatic Machine'). Run as briefed, with the meaning test redesigned after cycle 1 failed its calibration.
Machinery: tools/v90_lib.py, v90_c1.py, v90_c1b.py, v90_c2.py, v90_c3.py, v90_freeze.py, v90_sum*.py; checkpoints data/v90_ckpt/ (git-ignored); frozen data/v90_frozen_predictions.json (sha256 8be032b74f8d4bb75d784d6cf4d5bfc034abd4e8801b5f75b2924e9cc63bdc6c). About 54,000 random hypotheses in total (1,299 decompositions x 11 corpora in cycle 1; 4,000 decomposition x slot-alphabet hypotheses x 10 corpora in cycle 2), plus an exhaustive 127-wheel x 3-level sweep x 10 corpora in cycle 3.

GRADES
- A (method): a within-unit field-shuffle conjunction test separates a planted Lullian concept wheel (z 7.6-26, held out, homophones included) from page-level spelling habit (<= 5.3) and from shuffles and Markov text (<= 2.1 for selected wheels). Compactness of the decomposition and page-gain (bits over a section/hand/line-position model) do not: they pick or reward habit wheels. A max-ent page-tilt null absorbs planted content and is unusable.
- B (negative): no part of the Voynich word (any of 119 field subsets, any of 4,000 random slot alphabets) carries page, paragraph or line content beyond slot habit in a way that replicates across ZL, IT2a and GC2a. The best line-level wheels (z 7.5-7.7) are different in each transcription and fall to 0.5-1.8 in the others.
- B (measurement): the whole word, or its frame (all glyphs except the middle), recurs on its own page beyond page-level slot habit in all three transcriptions (z 3.7-5.2; every null and habit plant <= 2.8). Frozen frame predictions for 114 held-out folios transfer to GC2a (lift 2.3).
- C- (guess): the word frame, not the full spelling, is the lexical unit (the middle is free variation). Would support: frame beats the whole word at page and paragraph level in all three transcriptions, and in a held-out page half. Would kill: GC2a paragraph level already has the frame below the whole word (3.5 vs 4.5), and the concept plant with an uninformative middle shows the same frame edge (5.0 vs 4.7), so the edge may be only the merging of rare types.
- Demoted: none carried in. Nothing predicted outside the corpus. Not cracked.
