v67 EACH LINE SAYS ONE THING, cycle 2 (5-6 Oct 2026): 1,000 random consensus rules, held out by folio parity
Scripts: tools/v67_c2.py (search), tools/v67_rep2.py (selection report), tools/v67_c2b.py (stream properties). Checkpoints: data/v67_ckpt/c2.jsonl, c2b.json.
Design: 1,000 random rules (reduction x unit x aggregation x tie, as cycle 1) x 9 corpora, each with a twin in which the words of every page are shuffled across its lines (line unity destroyed, page topic kept). Per rule: line AGREEMENT EXCESS (agx = mean share of the line's words carrying the consensus unit, real minus twin) and LANG (MI + ASYM + REC z, 20 within-page line shuffles), on odd and even folios separately. Two selections on odd folios, tested on even: (A) the rule on which lines agree most beyond their page (the line's "one thing", chosen without looking at the stream); (B) the rule with the highest LANG. Plants re-seeded (Caesar filler 0.25 / 0.6 / 0.85, Manzoni 0.6).
