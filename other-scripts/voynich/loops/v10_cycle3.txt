# v10 cycle 3 -- is the line-initial chain a plaintext ACROSTIC in a simple encoding? (tools/v10_cycle3.py, v10_lib.anneal_partition;
# data/results/v10/c3_runs.jsonl (checkpoint, one anneal per line), c3_summary.json)
# Chain per paragraph = first glyph of EVERY line (line 1 included); glyphs < 10 occurrences pooled as '?' (12 symbols in ZL).
# Search: simulated annealing (3,000 steps x 2 restarts) over onto maps letter -> chain glyph (lossy, several letters per glyph, the only
# form a ~20-letter text can take in a ~10-glyph margin), maximising the chain likelihood under the language's letter trigram counts pushed
# through the map (proper glyph-trigram probability, add-0.5; a first naive glyph->letter many-to-one score collapsed onto 'i/s' and was
# discarded). Models: Latin (Caesar + Descartes), Italian (Manzoni + Dante), German (Kafka), j/v/k/w folded.
# Nulls, each re-annealed: N1 = lines 2..n permuted in the paragraph (20x); N2 = first-order Markov surrogates of the corpus' own chain (20x).
