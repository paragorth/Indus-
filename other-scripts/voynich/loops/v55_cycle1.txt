v55 THE SKY IS THE ANSWER KEY, cycle 1: the zodiac nymph labels as day-by-day sequences on the real sky (5 Oct 2026)
Tools: tools/v55_sky.py (self-written ephemeris: Sun, Moon, Mercury-Saturn, tropical longitudes of date, after
Schlyter's published elements and perturbation terms), tools/v55_validate.py, tools/v55_lib.py, tools/v55_cycle1.py,
tools/v55_report.py. Checkpoints data/v55_ckpt/ (sky tables 1289-1613, c1_*.json/.npz).
Ephemeris check (V-55.1.0): against JPL DE421 at 400 random dates 1900-2050 the median error is 0.007-0.018 deg
(99th pct <= 0.07 deg), same zodiac sign 100 %; it recovers the recorded solar eclipses of 16 June 1406, 7 June 1415
and 17 June 1433 and gives Easter 1404 = 30 March (Julian). Sky taken at noon, central Europe / N Italy, Julian calendar.
Reading tested: nymph i of a sign (299 labels, 10 signs, order = page, ring, transcription order) = the day the Sun
stands at degree i (+ rotation 0-29, forward or reversed) of that sign in year Y, every Y 1290-1611 (1300 and 1550
skies included). 240 random word classes without sound values (first/second/last/penultimate glyph groupings, length,
counts of o and gallows, 20 substring pairs, random hash partitions of label types; 2-6 classes) x 8 daily sky
classes (Moon sign, Moon element, Moon modality, phase in 8 and 4, weekday, Moon-Sun sign distance, Moon north/south of
the ecliptic) x 322 years x 60 alignments = 37 million G-tests per condition (~1.4 billion over all conditions).
Held-out replication: alignment chosen on half A (Pisces, Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio) and scored on half B, and
vice versa. Statistics per condition: max score; replicated pairs (in-sample >= 2 and held-out >= 2; >= 3 and >= 3);
mean held-out score; z of the 1404-1438 years' maxima against all other years ('window z'), the same for replicated
counts; fraction of class-feature pairs whose best year falls in 1404-1438 (chance 0.109).
