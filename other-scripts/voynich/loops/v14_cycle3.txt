Cycle 3 verdict: no rescue works. The tail-dropping 'noisy dice' test has no power and is discarded; the exact test is brittle to contamination, so the case against dice rests on (i) chance-level lattice proximity, (ii) strong slot dependence everywhere (more than a half-rolled text), (iii) the junction rule, and (iv) no plateau in the number of dice tables.
