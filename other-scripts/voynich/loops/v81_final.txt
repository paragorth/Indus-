v81 FINAL: THE MESSAGE IS IN THE FIRST LETTERS OF WORDS? (6 Oct 2026). Cycles: loops/v81_cycle1.txt (3,000 random onset definitions), v81_cycle2.txt (re-roll: is the rest surface?; slot inversion), v81_cycle3.txt (junction-preserving and same-page nulls, lag-2 control, onset phrases). Tools: tools/v81_lib.py, v81_c1.py, v81_c1_report.py, v81_c2.py, v81_c2_report.py, v81_c3.py, v81_c3b.py. Checkpoints: data/v81_ckpt/ (git-ignored, ~104 MB). Two CPU workers.
Idea: if word beginnings carry the topic (v59) and the rest is surface, the message may be the stream of word onsets alone (an initials or abbreviation script). Onset streams were extracted under 3,000 random definitions (9 preprocessing variants incl. q-, line-marker, e, gallows and ch removal and the frozen v72 E1c; onset length 1-3; random 2-12 class merges) and scored as texts on unseen leaves and in both transcriptions.
Controls through the v72 surface machinery: Caesar and Manzoni reduced to word initials, v21 Latin reduced to two-letter suspensions, the Syon catalogue as an initials list, each padded with random Voynich tails; shuffled initials as a null; whole-word Isidore and Brumati; SELFCIT, SC10, MK2, JUNC and STACK generators fitted to ZL3b.

Grades:
- A (killed): the strong reading 'the message is in the first letters, the rest of the word is surface'. The rest of the Voynich word carries page information and neighbour coupling once the onset is fixed (z 43-90), and initials-only controls show none.
- B (measurement, new): the front of a Voynich word (glyphs 1-3) predicts the next word's onset beyond the last-glyph junction and beyond page topic (+0.03-0.10 bits on unseen leaves, ZL and IT2a, 2-3x every fitted generator). Word-to-word order sits at the front of the word, while page information is spread over the whole word. Related to v59's ordered adjacent alternations (o>l, ch>d), which it generalises beyond the junction.
- B (negative for language-likeness): two-glyph onsets attract their neighbours where all control languages repel, and the onset order has no skip-1 or trigram excess at its best definition.
- C: the front-of-word link is a left-edge agreement between neighbours plus line or passage mood, shaped like whole-word language (lag-2 at 30-65% of lag 1). Would support: the adjacent part survives a within-line re-roll that keeps line mood. Would kill: a generator with line-level onset mood and a front-glyph agreement rule fitted to ZL reaching +0.03 bits under the same junction-preserving null.
- C- (initials reading): demoted. Not cracked.
