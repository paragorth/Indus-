v73 cycle 3 (6 Oct 2026): THE LINE POINTS AT ITS OWN ITEM. Scripts: tools/v73_c3.py, v73_c23_report.py (arg 3), v73_pairs.py, v73_pairs_pos.py.
Pointer rules: a key read off the line (first glyph of its first word, final glyph of its last word, line length mod 4, first glyph of the previous line) picks the item's position (0,1,2,3, last, second-to-last) through a lookup table. 3,000 random tables (750 per key) plus a coordinate ascent of each table on discovery pages (identical on every null). This tests the forum idea that the line-initial letter marks the only real word. Controls: the Antidotarium list placed by a hidden random first-glyph or last-glyph table inside SELFCIT or MK2 filler, items spelled as ordinary Voynich words (VOY). False-positive targets SELFCIT and MK2. Same composite and gates.
Then the v67 kill test for single-word selectors: ordered adjacent item pairs recurring on >= 2 pages (RECz) and order consistency (ASYMz) against 200 within-page shuffles, for the 20 best cycle-1 rules and for fixed positional streams.
