v51 LET A SPEECH MODEL LISTEN, cycle 1 (tools/v51_lib.py, v51_run.py, v51_analyze.py; raw rows data/v51_ckpt/c1_*.jsonl, report c1_report.txt)
Set-up: text -> sound. Each random mapping assigns a phone to every unit by frequency rank (vowel share 0.2-0.5 at random; vowels with random F1/F2, nasals, liquids, fricatives, stops; random durations, f0, declination, stress rule first/penult/last/none, word gap, 8-word phrases with pauses); a numpy formant synthesiser renders a 6 s clip from a random position. All 28 corpora get the same mapping and the same voice. Listener: a small multilingual speech recogniser (39M parameters, trained only on speech in 99 languages), run on the clip itself. Six scores: no-speech probability, language-ID entropy, top language probability, mean greedy-token confidence over 5 transcription tokens (lex), frame-change peakiness (syllable-boundary confidence, bnd), 3-8 Hz rhythm autocorrelation (rhy). Composite = signed z across corpora within each mapping. Comparisons are paired by mapping.
Comparison texts: Latin (Isidore), German (Kafka), Czech (Dalimil), Italian (Manzoni), Hebrew (Genesis, consonantal), Esperanto (Wikipedia); 20,000 words each. Nulls: unit shuffle (~shuf) and order-1 Markov resynthesis (~mk1) of each text. Generators on Voynich units: self-citation, table grille, order-2 Markov, uniform gibberish.
