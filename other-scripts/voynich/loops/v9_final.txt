# v9 final: "the text was dialled out of a volvelle"

Files: tools/v9_lib.py, v9_cycle1.py, v9_cycle2.py, v9_cycle3.py, v9_cycle3b.py, v9_report.py;
loops/v9_cycle1.txt .. v9_cycle3.txt; checkpoints data/results/v9/*.pkl; slot grammars data/derived/v9_slotmodels.json.
Data: ZL3b (main), IT2a (transcription check; same pages, so agreement = transcription-robust, not replication).
Controls: planted 3-ring volvelle and planted one-disc volvelle (must be recovered); Isidore Latin and Manzoni Italian under
verbose substitution (must fail); within-line word shuffle (kills order, keeps line make-up).
Search: rings = cyclic lists of slot fillers (prefix / core / suffix, 12 + OTHER each, from a corpus-learned slot grammar),
40-48 cells, duplicates allowed; turning rule = learned step distribution (R1) or step distribution depending on the previous
word's suffix (R2); reset per line or carried over. Fitted by population Baum-Welch on the circulant HMM (soft cells), then
hardened to one filler per cell. Simulated annealing over hard rings was tried first and did NOT recover the planted volvelle
(stuck 2.5-5.8k nats short of the oracle on one ring); the overcomplete-ring Baum-Welch does, and is what all results use.
Prior work (quick search): Rugg 2004 table-and-grille (generator only); 'Syntaxis Volvella' Zenodo preprints 2026 (claimed
tri-rotor disc, no likelihood/planted test seen). This appears to be the first likelihood fit of volvelles to the text with
planted-recovery calibration.

Plain answer: a volvelle with a simple turning rule does NOT explain the Voynich line structure better than a Markov model.
It explains it worse than a model with no word order at all. Under a method that recovers planted volvelles almost to the
oracle, the Voynich gives near-uniform turning kernels and negative gains, like verbose-enciphered Latin and Italian.
The best fitted rings are therefore not worth publishing as a mechanism; for the record, the best Voynich 3-ring fit is
in data/results/v9/c1m_voynich_<ring>_n<40|48>.pkl (labels, kernel q, start distribution). What a mechanism would have to
supply instead: (1) coupling between the end of one word and the start of the next (a junction rule, not a rotation),
(2) strong agreement between a word's prefix and suffix (words are units, not free sector combinations), (3) a line-level
plan that knows where the line ends. Nothing here is a decipherment.
