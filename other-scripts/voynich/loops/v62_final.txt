v62 THE VOYNICH IS VERSE -- final (5 Oct 2026)
Idea (arrow in the dark): medieval medicine was memorised in verse (Regimen sanitatis, Macer floridus). Verse would explain the Voynich line-edge effects (line = verse, end = rhyme), neighbour coupling (metre constrains word choice) and adjacent repeats (refrains, litanies).
Prior check: web search found forum discussion of 'rhyming' adjacent words and a 'prosodic hypothesis', but no calibrated rhyme / metre / refrain test with verse and prose controls. Our own v5 (line-final glyph not paragraph-specific, Dante ratio 22 vs Voynich 1.0) touched rhyme only through the last glyph; v62 tests rhyme schemes, metre and refrains directly.
Controls throughout (opaque verbose code, letters -> 1-3 symbol groups): Regimen sanitatis (rhymed hexameters), Macer floridus (unrhymed hexameters), Dante (terza rima, hendecasyllables), Litany of the Saints (merged and versicle/response layouts), Hildegard Physica (prose herbal), Caesar; verse re-flowed; Markov resynthesis with planted line-initial / line-final distributions and page drift; within-page line shuffles; random re-flow of line breaks; planted metre, caesura and within-line likeness in Voynich vocabulary. Training on odd folios, held-out on even folios and on IT2a.

Overall verdict: THE VOYNICH IS NOT VERSE in any form these tests can see. Rhymed verse (Regimen, Dante), quantitative verse (Macer), and litany refrains are each recovered through the same opaque verbose code, on held-out pages, at a tenth of the Voynich's size; the Voynich shows none of the three, and on rhyme and metre it lies on the opposite side of chance. The line-edge effects, neighbour coupling and adjacent repeats that verse was meant to explain are not verse effects: line ends are anti-rhyming, lines are internally alike rather than metrically balanced, and repeats are runs, not periodic refrains.
What this adds about meaning: the line is a unit of LIKENESS (one line's words share weight and shape) rather than a unit of sound. A text whose writer chose a 'line mode' and filled the line with words in that mode, resetting at the line break (v-series junction reset), fits better than verse. Next arrow: treat each line as one item whose mode is the information (line-level code), e.g. test whether line modes predict the drawing part next to the line or recur across pages describing the same plant.
Not cracked.
