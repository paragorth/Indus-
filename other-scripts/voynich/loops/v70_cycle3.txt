Verdict cycle 3: Grade A (method, negative): MDL on ink pixels cannot choose an alphabet size (synthetic and Latin controls run to the ceiling). Grade B (negative): the ink gives no evidence of EVA mis-segmentation: no EVA bigram is joined beyond the artefact level of a hand with random touching, and ink-informed EVA tracks sections and pages no better than random merges (|z| <= 0.9). Grade A (confound): unit width alone tracks pages at 0.50 vs EVA 0.27, so image alphabets must be page-normalised and must beat a width-only null. The real Latin control fails at 2000-px resolution, so the Voynich negatives are bounded by the instrument.
