Per twin slot: load = content-residualised darkness (darkest 30% of ink, residual of glyph composition) of the 2 previous words in the line; next = 2 following words; own = the word itself; since = words since the last dip (top 12%). Base model (SCRIBE + CTX + FRAME) trained on the OTHER ZL pages. Offset gain, page-grouped 5-fold; null = 500 permutations of the feature among slots of the same line (keeps line-level ink). PLANTED: choice resampled from the base model, 'load above median -> B member' with prob s, 3 replicates x 100 permutations.
