Cycle 1 verdict: under fixed interleaving (odd/even, every 3rd, every 4th, by glyph class, continuous over line breaks) Voynich lines behave like ONE stream. All planted alternating weaves are recovered at 3-7x; the Voynich lag profile matches single prose/verse. A stochastic switch (LF-hmm) is not detected by fixed schemes (lag2 0.130 vs lag1 0.159): cycle 2 tests that class.
