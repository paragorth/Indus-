v59 cycle 2 - spelling-blind unsupervised word translation between Currier A and B
(tools/v59_c2.py, tools/v59_c2b.py; checkpoints data/v59_ckpt/c2_*.json, c2b_*.json)

Method (new for the Voynich): each of the 150 most frequent types on a side is described only by how it is
used (frequency, line-initial/final rate, relative line position, page burstiness, neighbour frequency,
in-line repetition, paragraph-first-line rate, length percentile, the shape of its PPMI co-occurrence row).
A self-learning matcher (Hungarian assignment, then anchor-pair context vectors, 8 iterations; 20-40 random
seeds with random feature weights, anchor fractions and noise) pairs side-1 types with side-2 types.
Spelling never enters, so an opaque verbose encoding of one side (each letter -> 1-3 private symbols) is
invisible to it. Cycle 2b: 8 replicates on disjoint random page halves; a pair 'recurs' if it is the
consensus partner in >= 4 of 8 replicates.
