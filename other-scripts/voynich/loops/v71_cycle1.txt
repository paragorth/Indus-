v71 cycle 1: shuffle the sentences to learn the genre (order-dependence fingerprints). Tools: tools/v71_texts.py, v71_lib.py, v71_c1.py, v71_report.py. Data: data/v71_ckpt/c1/*.json, rep_c1_all.txt.
Fingerprint = held-out damage (bits/token) to 360 random model types (17 word representations x 10 context atoms, 1-2 atoms, 3 smoothings; 5 cache windows; glyph n-grams), trained on 3/4 of the pages in written order, scored on the held-out quarter in written order and after shuffling glyphs in word (S0), words in line (S1), words in paragraph (S1p), lines in paragraph (S2), paragraphs in page (S3), pages in chunk (S4); 2 shuffle seeds; 108 features (scale x model family). Chunks of 2,000-3,000 words.
References: 29 texts, 107 chunks, each re-coded with a fresh opaque verbose code (letters -> 1-3 glyphs, random word-initial and word-final pad glyphs) and laid out with random line widths (7-11 words) and page sizes (60-350 words). Classes: procedure (Apicius la/en, Forme of Cury, Middle Dutch antidotary, Celsus la/en; 21 chunks), entry (11 herbals incl. Circa instans, Culpeper, Dioscorides, Macer, Hildegard, Konrad, Leechdoms; Pliny, Konrad encyclopedia, Theophrastus, bath synopsis, Hyginus; 53), list (Culpeper index, Apicius index, Cury glossary, Pliny book 1 contents; 14), narrative/argument (Caesar, Descartes, Manzoni, Kafka, Cervantes; 19). No alchemical or calendar text was available locally (gap).
