v78 WHY IS THE SAME WORD WRITTEN TWICE? cycle 1 (6 Oct 2026). Scripts: tools/v78_lib.py, v78_corpora.py, v78_c1.py, v78_c1b.py. Checkpoints and downloaded texts: data/v78_ckpt/ (git-ignored, ~45 MB). Two CPU workers. v76 (paragraph dossiers) and v77 (kill sweep) running, not duplicated.
Idea: v75 found the one Voynich property no real designation system has: the same payload token written twice in a row. Each candidate mechanism leaves a different trace: grammatical reduplication, list ditto, tally counting and copying slips all write the copy NEXT to its source (a lag-1 spike, a special set of doubled words); generator self-copying spreads copies over its window (no spike). Measure where the Voynich doubles come from before fitting mechanism mixtures (cycle 2).
Prior art (web, 6 Oct 2026): voynich.ninja threads on 'perfect reduplication' (daiin daiin) compare its rate with Latin and with Indonesian; Timm & Schinner explain repeats by self-citation. No lag-profile decomposition against nested shuffles, no re-spelling test of the copy and no mechanism assignment calibrated on reduplicating languages, real dittography and generators was found.
Tokens: frozen v72 extraction E1c (hash 2803cbeb0deb51bf asserted) of each word; a double = two adjacent words in one line with the same E1c token (their EVA forms may differ).
Controls through the v72 surface machinery (merge code, planted surface, E1c): REDUP = real reduplicating languages, full reduplication written with a hyphen split into two words: Tagalog medical book (Gutenberg 17479, Tagalog paragraphs only, 40k words), Tagalog Noli Me Tangere (20228), Malay 1001 Nights 1899 (archive.org, 40k), Hebrew Genesis (20k). DITTOG = Petrus Plaoul, five diplomatic Latin witnesses (SCTA), as first written, real manuscript lines, the 57 paragraphs holding the 59 struck dittographies. LIST = Syon catalogue and Sinonoma Bartholomei with a planted 'same again' convention (headword written twice, p 0.15). TALLY = Apicius + Antidotarium ingredient streams with planted quantities written as a unit token repeated 1-5 times. NONE = Isidore (Latin), Brumati (Italian). GEN = SELFCIT, SC10, MK2, JUNC, STACK (v77) fitted to ZL3b.

| V-78.1.V | Cycle verdict | | The doubled token is not a special doubling event. At the payload level the Voynich re-uses tokens inside a line at every short lag equally (no language-style avoidance of the neighbour, no doubling spike), and the second copy is re-spelled like any recurrence. The 'excess' over real systems (v75) is a concentrated vocabulary + line-level re-use + the absence of syntax's ban on neighbours. Implication if meaning is assumed: the unit written is not a word of running language (whose neighbours are constrained); it behaves like a value in a row (a cell, a syllable-like piece, a code item) drawn from a line's own small stock.
