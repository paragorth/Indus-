v75 cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026): SUBTRACT THE GENERATOR. A system's signature is not its statistics but what it has that its own fitted generators lack.
Scripts: tools/v75_c2.py (delta signatures), v75_run.py (V75_FEATS=delta), v75_chunkvote.py. Checkpoints data/v75_ckpt/delta_A.pkl, delta_B.pkl, run_c2*.pkl, chunkvote_*.json.
Why: cycle 1 failed its generator control because each kind's fingerprint is copied by generators fitted to it. Here generators (WSHUF, MK2, SELFCIT, JUNC, SC10) are fitted to the EXTRACTED stream of each system and subtracted: delta = mean of 8 real chunks - mean of 4 generator chunks, per generator, on the 26 non-layout features (130 numbers), 8 bootstrap deltas per system and replicate. GEN class = a reference's MK2 or SELFCIT stream treated as if real (its own generators fitted and subtracted). Voynich-side targets: ZL3b and IT2a leaf halves; controls: the Voynich's MK2, SELFCIT, JUNC and global-shuffle streams treated as if real. 3,000 random model types per run, LOSO, survivors re-tested on replicate B, permuted-label null.
