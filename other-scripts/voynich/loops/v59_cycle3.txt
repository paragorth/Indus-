v59 cycle 3 - the drawings and the subject matter as the Rosetta stone
(tools/v59_c3.py, v59_c3b.py, v59_c3c.py; checkpoints data/v59_ckpt/c3_*.json, c3b.json, c3c.json)

(a) Drawing pivot: each word placed at the centroid of the plant-drawing embeddings (v38 DINOv2 / EfficientNet-B0)
of the pages it occurs on; a word shared by A and B (or an A word and its B translation) should sit at the same
drawing centroid on both sides. Spelling-blind, so opaque encoding changes nothing. Null: drawings permuted
among side-2 pages (1,000x). Control: Gerard's Herball (OCR + woodcuts) split 91 vs 27 pages.
(b) Subject transfer: side-2 pages translated into side-1 words (identity, cycle-2 dictionary, shuffled
dictionary, v30 rewrite, glyph skeletons) and scored s = cos(A-herbal centroid) - cos(A-pharma centroid);
AUC of B herbal pages vs B biological/stars/cosmological pages. Null: A's H/P labels permuted (2,000x).
Control: Isidore odd vs even chapters, book XVI (stones) vs book IV (medicine), other = books XI-XIII.
(c) 1,500 random B->A transducers scored on that AUC in ZL3b, top 20 re-tested in IT2a, against the same
search run with permuted A labels.
