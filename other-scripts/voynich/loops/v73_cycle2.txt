v73 cycle 2 (6 Oct 2026): stranger markers and a learned marker. Scripts: tools/v73_c2.py, v73_c23_report.py (arg 2).
(a) 2,514 random rules that each use at least one model-based feature: left/right context surprisal (the word the filler cannot predict), glyph-trigram spelling surprisal, first mention on the page, first mention in the book, odd-one-out of the line (edit distance to nearest line mate), surprisal under the line's own glyph law; mixed with the 60 cycle-1 features.
(b) LEARNED marker: on discovery pages, iterated conditional modes choose one word per line that maximises the stream's recurrence on neighbouring pages relative to the word's global rate; a conditional-logit rule (softmax over a line's words) is fitted to those choices and applied unchanged to held-out pages; every null gets the identical procedure.
Targets: ZL3b and IT2a; five planted lists (Antidotarium FREE/SELFCIT/VOY, FREE/MK2/NOVEL, AFTER/SELFCIT/VOY; Apicius De re coquinaria ingredient lists POS2/SELFCIT/VOY and FREE/SELFCIT/NOVEL); false-positive targets SELFCIT and JUNC text. Same composite z(XP)+z(VS) and gates as cycle 1.
