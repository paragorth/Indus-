v71 cycle 2: the scrambling writer. Hypothesis: the Voynich is a known genre whose order was destroyed at some scales (words within line, lines within paragraph, paragraphs within page) by its writer. Tools: tools/v71_c2.py, v71_c2_report.py; data/v71_ckpt/c2/*.json, rep_c2.txt.
Six reference chunks (Forme of Cury, Middle Dutch antidotary, Culpeper, Circa instans, Manzoni, Apicius index) scrambled for real at the 8 corners of {0,1}^3 (S1, S2, S3 shuffle probability) plus 4 midpoints, fingerprinted with the cycle-1 model set (80 runs); a multilinear surrogate over the cube scores 9,048 writer hypotheses (6 texts x 1,500 random fractions + corners). Fit chosen on a random half of the 90 non-glyph features, distance reported on the other half (dB).
