v34 cycle 3 (4 Oct 2026): POWER BY PLANTING, Latin wrap and margin-gradient controls, within-line glyph drift. tools/v34_cycle3.py, v34_cycle3b.py; data/v34_ckpt/cycle3.log, cycle3.json, cycle3b.log, cycle3b.json.
Method: (a) the real 848 Voynich lines keep their measured slack; in the tightest 30% of each page, with probability p the last word is abbreviated (drop 1-2 final glyphs and write m, or just drop 2 glyphs); the cycle-1 statistic (within-page shuffle, 400 perms) is rerun, 20 replicates per p. (b) Latin ALTO lines: tightness vs letters of the next line's first token (greedy-wrap signature). (c) Latin image lines: abbreviation marks per letter of non-final, non-initial words by x-tercile of the line. (d) Voynich: per-glyph rates in non-final, non-initial words, right vs middle tercile (sign test over 25 pages); does the drift grow with tightness (within-page shuffle); penultimate word's glyph rates vs its x position (nwords covariate).
Web check: voynich.ninja thread 5309 and Stolfi's 1997 notes discuss line-end m and short line-final words as possible abbreviation/padding, without measured line room. Not done before as a measurement.
