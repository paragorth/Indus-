# Source-hunt brief (for another search agent)

TASK: Find published sources containing Indus-script inscriptions (or Indus seals found abroad) that are NOT already in the standard corpora, and report exactly where each can be obtained.

## Context
- The Indus script (c. 2600–1900 BCE) is undeciphered. We have a merged digital corpus of ~5,700 texts covering everything published up to about 2015: Mahadevan 1977 (ASI Memoir 77), Wells/ICIT, and CISI (Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions) vols 1, 2, 3.1, 3.2, 3.3.
- Already checked and fully covered, so don't report these: ASI Indian Archaeology – A Review 2001–14; Dholavira preliminary report 2015 (Bisht); Farmana 2007–08; Rakhigarhi 1997–2000 (Nath); Lothal (Rao, MASI 78); Mackay's Mohenjo-daro and Chanhu-daro volumes; Harappa 1986–90 (Meadow); Gadd 1932 (Ur seals); Laursen 2010 (Gulf seals); Frenez & Tosi 2005 (Lothal sealings); Frenez 2023 (Oxford Research Encyclopedia); SAHI Tell Jokha 2017 report; Shinde & Willis 2014 copper plates; Rakhigarhi 2023 Archaeopress vol. 1.

## What to find (priority order)
1. Indus-type seals, sealings or inscribed objects found OUTSIDE South Asia in a dated/stratified context, especially together with cuneiform tablets: Mesopotamia (Ur, Umma/Tell Jokha, Kish, Tello/Girsu, Nippur, Susa), the Gulf (Bahrain/Saar/Qala'at al-Bahrain, Failaka, UAE: Tell Abraq, Kalba, Umm al-Quwain; Oman: Ras al-Jinz, Salut), Iran, Central Asia (Gonur, Altyn Depe). Report any published after 2010.
2. Inscriptions from Indian/Pakistani excavations published AFTER 2015: Rakhigarhi (2013–2022 seasons), Binjor, Kotada Bhadli, Khirsara, Kanmer, Bhirrana, Kunal, Shikarpur, Bagasra/Gola Dhoro, Juni Kuran, Karanpura, Tigrana, Lothal revisitation, Harappa (HARP after 2010), Mohenjo-daro recent work; also CISI vol. 3.4 if published.
3. Museum catalogues of Indus-type seals held outside South Asia (Louvre, British Museum, Penn, Iraq Museum, Kuwait National Museum, Bahrain National Museum, BnF Cabinet des Médailles — especially an Indus-type chlorite seal with a cuneiform inscription in the BnF).
4. Any Mesopotamian or Gulf cuneiform document that names a person from Meluhha AND is sealed with, or found with, an Indus-type seal.

archive.org, Google Scholar, Crossref, JSTOR, Academia.edu, ResearchGate, Persée, OpenEdition, HAL, Shodhganga (Indian PhD theses), ASI publications site, Archaeopress open access, Proceedings of the Seminar for Arabian Studies, Arabian Archaeology and Epigraphy, South Asian Studies, Ancient Asia, Heritage (Journal of Multidisciplinary Studies in Archaeology), Puratattva, Man and Environment, Iraq, CDLI (cdli.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de).

## Rules
- Legal access only. Don't bypass paywalls, CAPTCHAs or logins, and don't use shadow libraries. If something needs a login, still report it with its URL so a human can fetch it.
- Don't include decipherment claims, readings or interpretations. We only want primary data: photos or drawings of inscriptions, find context, dates, museum numbers.

## Output (one entry per source)
- Full citation (authors, year, title, journal/book, pages, DOI if any)
- Direct URL, and access type: open / login needed / paywall / library only
- What it contains: number of inscribed objects; site; find context (stratified or surface; layer or period; any associated cuneiform); museum or excavation numbers
- Whether the objects look new (not in CISI / Mahadevan 1977) and why you think so
- Page or figure numbers of the inscription photos

Sort by priority (1–4 above). Say clearly where you are unsure.
