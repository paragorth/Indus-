## Why
One Indus text (twins + 24 strokes + 2 + three signs) is on moulded tablets at Kalibangan in India and also, per a drawing, on a Gulf-type seal from a Bahrain burial mound ("seal no. 10"). The "twins" sign is mostly found on seals from Bahrain, the UAE and Iran. Photos of those Gulf seals, and the find records, are the nearest thing to an outside anchor for the Indus script.
