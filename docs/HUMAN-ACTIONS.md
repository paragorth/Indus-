## C. A library visit

11. **CISI 3.1 (Parpola, Pande & Koskikallio 2010, *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions* vol. 3.1: New material, untraced objects, and collections outside India and Pakistan).** Look up "Cleveland" and "New York, Metropolitan Museum" in the concordance and copy the published readings and plate numbers for 1973.160, 1973.161, 1964.104, 49.40.1 to 49.40.4. Why: calibrates the single-reader transcriptions (measured 25% per-sign error) behind the two failed predictions P6 and P10, and shows whether ICIT skipped these pieces (S-DARK-36.3, 42.3, 47.3). Not open access; the user's earlier upload had the essay pages only, no plates (S157).
12. **Photo check of about 20 texts stored backwards** (dir = "-" texts read 30% better reversed vs 10% baseline, Fisher P = 2e-6; S-DARK-8.4).
