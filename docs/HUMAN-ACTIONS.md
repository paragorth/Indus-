## C. A library visit

11. **CISI 3.1 (Parpola, Pande & Koskikallio 2010, *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions* vol. 3.1: New material, untraced objects, and collections outside India and Pakistan).** Look up "Cleveland" and "New York, Metropolitan Museum" in the concordance and copy the published readings and plate numbers for 1973.160, 1973.161, 1964.104, 49.40.1 to 49.40.4. Why: calibrates the single-reader transcriptions (measured 25% per-sign error) behind the two failed predictions P6 and P10, and shows whether ICIT skipped these pieces (S-DARK-36.3, 42.3, 47.3). Not open access; the user's earlier upload had the essay pages only, no plates (S157).
12. **Photo check of about 20 texts stored backwards** (dir = "-" texts read 30% better reversed vs 10% baseline, Fisher P = 2e-6; S-DARK-8.4). Check in CISI vol. 1 (Pakistan sites) and vol. 2 (Indian sites) plates, in this order: C-88 and C-35 (Chanhu-daro tablets, 167-705-31), H-2085 (824-892-413), M-2062 (pot, 46-31-32-741), H-170 (827-336-630-511), then H-340, H-536, Ns-63, M-1336, H-1663, M-3, L-218, L-232. Why: positional tests treat these 92 texts as direction-uncertain; a confirmed flip list fixes the corpus. Full table: `data/derived/dark/loop8_cycle4.txt`.
13. **Photo check of the 60 seals recorded with non-standard direction** (L/R 49, BUS 9, T/B 2, SYM 1; list with CISI numbers in `data/derived/dark/loop30_cycle1.txt`). ## E. Lothal sealing archive sources (added after S-DARK-71)

## I. Proto-Elamite seal catalogues (added after pe35)
- [ ] **Amiet 1972, *Glyptique susienne* (MDP 43), Paris: Geuthner, 2 vols.** Not online (archive.org, HathiTrust, Gallica: no copy). The catalogue entries for the Suse III numbers 821–1308 (CDLI 'PESnnnn' with nnnn >= 340 is Amiet's number) would give a picture for every Louvre-held sealed tablet. Library record: https://zenon.dainst.org/Record/000017051 . A scan of the Suse III plates (pl. 24–30, 105–112 approx.) and their catalogue text is enough.
- [ ] **Paladre 2022, *Les sceaux-cylindres proto-élamites: productions d'un phénomène revisité* (thesis, Paris 1)**: https://theses.fr/2022PA01H045 . Marked not accessible online. It reportedly catalogues all proto-Elamite sealings with their tablets (Susa, Malyan, Yahya, Sialk); ask the author (C. Paladre) or the Paris 1 library for the catalogue volume.
- [ ] **Tehran, National Museum of Iran (NMI BK 03203–03686; MDP 26, 33 sealed tablets in CDLI)**: the CDLI photographs do not show the seal scenes legibly (checked P008694). Better photographs or the MDP 26 plates (Scheil 1935) would add about 33 tablets.
