The user is signed in to Cambridge University Library (iDiscover, e-resources, Raven) in this browser.

## What we are looking for, in priority order
The goal is an **outside anchor**: anything that ties an Indus-sign text to a known word, name, number or place from outside the script. Second goal: **new inscribed objects** not in the corpora above, and **photos** good enough to check disputed readings.

1. **Indus-sign seals or sealings found with cuneiform or Linear Elamite.** Mesopotamia, Susa, the Gulf (Bahrain, Failaka, UAE, Oman) or Iran. Especially any cuneiform tablet or bulla impressed with an Indus-sign or Gulf Type seal.
   Search terms: "Indus seal" cuneiform; "Harappan seal" Mesopotamia; "Meluhha" seal; "Gulf seal" tablet impression; "Dilmun seal" inscription; bulla Indus.
2. **Gulf Type / Dilmun seals with Indus signs, as photos or catalogues.**
   - Crawford 2001, *Early Dilmun Seals from Saar* (Haddon Library): list any seal with Indus-script signs, especially the "twins" sign (two standing stick-figures side by side). Scan & Deliver the relevant pages if allowed.
   - Also: Kjærum's Saar and Bahrain seal publications (not in the catalogue: note if found elsewhere); Al-Sindi 1999, *Dilmun Seals* (Bahrain National Museum); Eidem & Højlund; Denton; Ibrahim 1982 (Excavations of the Arab expedition at Sar el-Jisr).
3. **Meluhha in cuneiform: names of Meluhhan people, villages and goods.**
   - Parpola, Parpola & Brunswig 1977 "The Meluhha village" (JESHO 20).
   - Vermaak's papers on Meluhha / Guabba.
   - Laursen & Steinkeller 2017, *Babylonia, the Gulf Region and the Indus* (Eisenbrauns): get the index of Meluhhan and Dilmunite personal names.
   We want every personal name written with a Meluhhan or Dilmunite label.
4. **New excavation reports with inscribed seals, published after 2010.**
   - Sites: Rakhigarhi, Farmana, Kunal, Bhirrana, Khirsara, Kanmer, Kotada Bhadli, Shikarpur, Bagasra (Gola Dhoro), Dholavira (Bisht's final report), Lothal (new), Harappa (HARP post-2007), Mohenjo-daro (recent Pakistani work), Ganweriwala.
   - Journals: *Purātattva*, *Man and Environment*, *Ancient Asia*, *Pakistan Archaeology*, *South Asian Studies*, *Heritage: Journal of Multidisciplinary Studies in Archaeology*, *Indian Archaeology – A Review* (IAR) 2010–2020 volumes.
   - We want plates or photos of seals or tablets with visible signs, and the find context.
5. **CISI vol. 3.2 (2019) and 3.3 (2022).** Check whether any Cambridge library or an e-book platform has them. If not, note the inter-library loan option and cost, but do not place a paid request without asking the user.
6. **Sign-level photo sources for disputed readings:**
   - Marshall 1931 *Mohenjo-daro and the Indus Civilization*, vol. 3 plates;
   - Mackay 1938 *Further Excavations at Mohenjo-daro*, vol. 2 plates;
   - Vats 1940 *Excavations at Harappa*, vol. 2 plates.
   These are often digitised (HathiTrust, archive.org, or a Cambridge e-copy). Note links only.

## How to report back
Return a table with one row per find: citation; where it is (library shelfmark or e-link); what it contains that matters (the object, the signs visible, cuneiform yes/no); and access (downloaded / Scan & Deliver ref / not available).
Put downloaded e-journal PDFs in the Drive folder "Indus – Gulf seals" (the user drags them in). Summarise Scan & Deliver items in words only: the seal numbers and what the sign sequences look like, if the user reads them.
Stop after about 25 useful hits, or after 45 minutes of searching, whichever comes first.
