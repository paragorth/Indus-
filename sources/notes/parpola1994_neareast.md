# Parpola 1994, Near East Indus inscriptions: facts used (S353)

Source: A. Parpola 1994, "Harappan inscriptions: an analytical catalogue of the Indus inscriptions from the Near East", in F. Højlund & H. H. Andersen, *Qala'at al-Bahrain* 1, pp. 304–315. The scan came from Cambridge University Library Scan & Deliver and is for private study only. It is **not committed**. Only facts are recorded here, not Parpola's classifications.

Round Gulf-type seals carry mostly a bison + inscription (nos. 5, 7, 11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 19, 22, 34, 36, 39). Square seals carry unicorn, zebu or tiger (nos. 24–28, 37, 40).
