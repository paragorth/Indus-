# Diagnostic that motivated the change: in the real multi-unit texts of cycle 1 a later 'unit' re-uses a field of the first unit for only 4.3% of its tokens (same sign 1.9%), against 16% / 6.9% in cycle 1's synthetic corpora. The 'second units' are not second form instances: the whole text is one field subset written in a non-linear order.
