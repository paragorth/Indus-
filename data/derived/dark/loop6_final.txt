C4 whole-vocabulary JSD Mantel: all objects, 6 big sites rho +0.54 (exact p 0.017) -> seals only rho +0.10 (p 0.69): the

C5 IM77 check: same flat seal vocabulary with MD at the centre (coast rho +0.48, exact p 0.09; km p 0.22); links follow n_i x n_j.
   Transcription-robust (same objects read twice, S312), not an independent replication.

VERDICT: no sign, slot, closer, numeral value, sign family, text length or shared-text link has a geographic gradient that
survives correction for arrows fired, and none of the near-misses survives removing Mohenjo-daro and Harappa except two-site
noise. The script is geographically flat across ~1,000 km and three river systems; the only geographic structure in the corpus
is which object genres each site used (tablets at Harappa, bale sealings at Lothal), already known (S29, S66, S86, S216, S324).
Geography is not plaintext for any common sign. This also closes the 'seal names its home town' family one more notch (S11,
S105, S308): a town word would be the one thing that must vary with the map, and nothing does.
Caveats: 76% of texts are MD + HP; 15 of 20 sites have 5-36 texts; a gradient weaker than 2x across the region, or one living in
rare signs (< 30 tokens), is beyond this corpus. The site table's coordinates are approximate (+-0.05 deg) and the Harappan coast
is a reconstruction; neither affects the ranks used.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE VERDICT: new material from the small sites (Gujarat, Makran, the Ghaggar) raising them to >= 50 texts each,
then re-running loop6_c1_signs.py; a gradient of > 2x would then be detectable with power > 0.9.
