| S-DARK-40.1 | **Loop 40, cycle 1: are long texts concatenations of attested complete short texts? Decomposition census.** `tools/dark_loop40.py 1 LEVEL` (logs `loop40_cycle1_{seq_raw,seq_strong,seq_all}.txt`, cuts `loop40_c1_cuts_*.json`). 3,415 complete direction-recorded texts (merged-corpus-canonical, older build, S-DARK-23), 1,168 of >= 5 signs. Unit inventory = every complete text attested on another object (2,083-2,144 distinct). A long text 'decomposes' if it splits into 2-3 contiguous units all attested, each unit >= 2 signs (minlen 2) or >= 1 (minlen 1). Matching exact, S262 variants, and M-space via bridge_extended + S-DARK-27 proposals. Nulls: within-slot shuffle of the NAME signs (200x), within-text shuffle (200x), 10 synthetic corpora each from the S366 best model, the S366 M0 slot grammar and an order-2 chain (same inventory of REAL units). Random-concatenation control: 5,000 joins of two random attested 2-4-sign texts, drawn by object count; where does the join fall? All three merge levels | **3.7% (43/1,168 seq_raw), 5.3% (62, seq_strong), 5.4% (63, seq_all) of long texts decompose at minlen 2; 7.4% / 10.4% / 12.1% at minlen 1. S262 variants add 0, M-space adds 1 (64).** Above the slot-shuffle null (median 16 / 23 / 23, ratio 2.7-2.8, P = 0.005) and far above the text shuffle (median 1). But: (a) the share FALLS with length: 5 signs 7.1-9.6%, 6 signs 2.1-3.9%, 7 signs 0.4-1.8%, 8+ signs 1.1% (2/186), the reverse of a concatenation process, which has more cut points in longer texts; (b) the order-2 chain generates the same or more decompositions (MD+H minlen 2: chain 0.051 [0.039-0.061] seq_raw, 0.074 [0.065-0.087] seq_all vs real 0.039 / 0.055; the S366 slot models generate 0.011-0.017); (c) the random-concatenation control puts the join at a frame boundary (closer/suffix before, or opener after) in 70-71% of joins (68% of short texts end in a closer or suffix), whereas the real cuts fall at a frame boundary in only 8/43 = 19% (seq_raw) and 8/61-62 = 13% (strong/all); the joined text itself is attested 4/5,000 times; (d) 24 of 35 (raw), 41 of 52 (strong), 42 of 53 (all) non-boundary cuts are after W2, i.e. the 'first unit' is the opener phrase 817/861-2, which happens to stand alone as a complete 2-sign text on 7 objects, followed by one ordinary complete text (861-2 + 415-100-740, 861-2 + 590-390-740 ...). Units restricted to <= 4-sign texts: 40 / 55 / 58. By site: MD 22-34 of 671, Harappa 17-21 of 320 (Harappa holds 6 of the 8 closer-boundary cuts: two-faced tablets with a 33-700 / 34-700 count face, S-DARK-12.2); held-out sites with MD+H units 3/165 (1.8%, raw) to 8/165 (4.8%, strong/all). Seals 2.9-5.0%, tablets 5.8-6.5% | **Not concatenation. Long texts are not stacks of complete short texts: the 'decomposable' 4-5% are almost all opener-phrase + one text, the cut is at a closer boundary 4-5x less often than real joining would produce, the share drops with length, and an order-2 sign chain already generates as many. The excess over the slot shuffle is the phrase reuse of S-DARK-21.2 (P2: opener phrases, closer units), not a text-level operation. Transcription-robust across merge levels; not Mohenjo-daro alone (Harappa and held-out sites give the same shares, and Harappa carries the few closer-boundary cases) |
