# S-DARK-25 cycle 1: PHONETIC-COMPLEMENT SIGNATURE (shared small neighbour sets reused as free signs)
# Tool: tools/dark_loop25.py 1 <level> 200. Tables: loop25_cycle1_seq_raw/strong/all.txt, loop25_cycle1_smallsets_seq_raw.txt

Design. For every sign with >= 30 tokens, its right- and left-neighbour distribution over all 3,155 complete, direction-recorded
texts (>= 2 signs). A (sign, side) has a SMALL neighbour set when <= 4 signs cover 80% of >= 20 neighbour tokens. A HUB is a
neighbour sign that sits in the small sets of >= 3 different hosts on the same side (a shared inventory). For every hub: the
fraction of its tokens NOT adjacent to one of its hosts (free use), the cosine of its far-side neighbour profile attached vs free,
the JS divergence of its position attached vs free, the slot entropy of its hosts (S310 parser), and MOBILITY = entropy of the
host+hub pair's offset from the nearer edge divided by the entropy of a uniform position in the same texts (1 = anywhere, 0 = one
fixed place). Null: tokens shuffled across texts within site x object type x length bin (200x; keeps lengths and per-stratum
sign counts); Indus also against a frame-kept null (shuffle within S310 slot labels). The same code on Ur III seal legends
(6,675 syllable-split legends), Linear B tablet lines (2,643), Proto-Elamite lines (2,792), a planted logo-syllabic corpus with a
12-sign complement inventory + 3 determinatives (3,067 texts) and a planted frame-only corpus (opener / name / closer-specific
qualifier / closer / suffix, 3,144 texts).

Reading. The planted complement system is detected by this machine (hubs with free use 0.7-0.9 and mobility 0.74; the complement
signs reappear as free name syllables). Indus shows 3 hubs, all known frame signs (closer, connective, suffix, fixed-term numeral),
mobility 0.37-0.40, between the planted frame (0.16) and the languages (0.62-0.74) and nearer the frame; its small neighbour
sets are either host -> frame sign or lexicalised bigrams. No Indus sign behaves as a shared complement that is also a free
syllable. Caveat: the planted detector recovers only the frequent third of its complement inventory, so a complement system with
a flat inventory or a low complement rate (< 0.3 per word-sign) would be missed at this corpus size; 'none found' is an upper
bound, not proof. Transcription-robust (same hubs at all three merge levels; W2 vs W33/W400 swap at the 3-host threshold).
