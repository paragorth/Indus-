S-DARK-7.1  Random-statistic fingerprint across scripts: build + first pass
================================================================================
Tools: tools/dark7_fingerprint.py (builds 600 random statistics, bootstraps), tools/dark7_analyse.py (selection,
controls). Raw bootstrap output: data/derived/dark/loop7_boot.npz, loop7_meta.json; full tables: loop7_cycle1_full.txt.

Design
- Texts: Indus inscriptions (seq_raw, Wells numbers; numerals = W1-5, W16-18, W31-34, W55, W56), Proto-Elamite entry
  lines (CDLI, numeral signs kept as N-types), Linear A lines (syllabograms + logograms + NUM), Linear B lines
  (DAMOS; two granularities: sign-groups-as-words, and syllables), Ur III seal legends (data/codelib/ur3_legends.jsonl;
  words, and syllables), Voynich lines (ZL3b; words, and chars-within-word as a second unit).
- Length matching: only texts of 2-8 tokens; every bootstrap draw takes N=800 texts with the Indus length weights
  (stratified resampling with replacement), B=200 draws per corpus.
- 600 statistics = random compositions of (slice selector: all/first/last/first2/last2/middle/halves/second/penult/
  slices) x (20 per-token properties: log rank, is-numeral, top-10, hapax, rare, repeats previous, seen earlier/later
  in text, next/prev is numeral, unigram and bigram surprise, run length, distance to nearest numeral, PMI with
  previous, sign's text-initial rate, text-final rate, mean relative position, opener-type, document frequency) x
  (text aggregator mean/max/min/std/first-minus-last) x (corpus aggregator mean/median/frac>0/std); 120 ratios of two
  such; 60 slot entropies (bits, evenness, type/token).
- z = difference of bootstrap means / sqrt(sd1^2 + sd2^2); Bonferroni over 600 statistics: z_crit = 3.93.
- Fingerprint rule: Indus~PE~LA pairwise |z| < 2, AND each of the three differs from LB (both granularities) and
  Ur III (both granularities) with |z| > z_crit, same sign. Residue rule: Indus differs from all six others, same sign.

Controls
- Indus split-half (random halves): 2/600 statistics exceed z_crit (expected ~0.3%). Calibration passes.
- seq_raw vs seq_all (merge level): 0/600. Results do not depend on the sign merges.
- Mohenjo-daro+Harappa vs other sites: 87/600 exceed z_crit. The Indus corpus is NOT homogeneous; any Indus-vs-X
  claim must beat this within-Indus site difference (applied in cycle 2).
- Within-text shuffle for each corpus: tells whether a statistic depends on sign order or only on inventory/lengths.

Results
1. NULL for the 'accounting cluster'. Indus, PE and LA do not form a group against LB and Ur III. Strict fingerprint:
   1/600 statistics ([79], 'fraction of texts whose second half contains a sign that is ever text-initial':
   0.99 in Indus/PE/LA vs 0.75 LB, 0.90-0.95 Ur III). It is order-invariant under shuffling in Indus, PE, LA: it is an
   inventory-size effect (small inventories -> almost every sign is initial somewhere; LB words 7,464 types), not
   a notation feature. Loose fingerprint: 0/600.
   Median |z| over all 600 statistics (nearest to Indus first): LB_syll 1.34, LA 1.87, LB_words 2.31, PE 2.48,
   UR3_syll 2.89, UR3_words 2.93, Voynich_chars 3.13, Voynich_words 3.50. PE's nearest is LA (2.12) then LB_syll (2.33).
   So at the level of random text statistics Indus is about as close to Linear B (a language, in accounting use) as
   to Linear A and Proto-Elamite; there is no 'non-linguistic accounting notation' signature shared by the three.
2. Indus-specific residue (Indus differs from all six comparison corpora, same direction): 16/600.
   Numeral-based (11): all say the same thing in plain words -- Indus puts the numeral BEFORE the thing counted
   and at the START of the text; PE, LA and LB put it AFTER and at the END.
     numeral followed by a sign / preceded by a sign (texts of 2-8): Indus 2180 / 1566 (excluding W1, W2:
     1556 / 929); PE 78 / 5480; LA 14 / 1362; LB 2027 / 5426.
     text-initial / text-final numeral: Indus 927 / 313 of 4350 (excl. W1,W2: 846 / 219); PE 168 / 5570 of 5806;
     LA 8 / 1356 of 1903; LB 127 / 3526 of 9347. Ur III legends and Voynich have no numerals (trivial separation).
     Indus text-initial numerals are mostly the tall strokes W32/W33/W34 (196/186/117) = the Harappa tablet voucher
     count 'tall 2-4 + W700' (S197-S217), plus short W3 (138). Robust to dropping W1 (marker, S234) and W2 (connective).
   Non-numeral (5): (a) document frequency of signs in the second half / last slot is LOWER in Indus (mean 0.10,
   last-slot min 0.13) than PE (0.33/0.47), LA (0.22/0.34), LB (0.13/0.20), Ur III (0.32/0.19) -- Indus text ends
   are not dominated by a few very common signs the way PE/LA ends are (numeral signs) or Ur III ends are (arad/dumu);
   Voynich words are lower still (0.018). (b) Sign-distribution evenness (H / log2 types, all slots, matched sample):
   Indus 0.797 > LB_syll 0.774 > LA 0.766 > Ur III 0.76 > LB_words 0.754 > PE 0.725; Voynich 0.92/0.88. Indus uses
   its inventory more evenly than every other ancient corpus here (fits S309: large inventory, long tail);
   Voynich is flatter still. Both are order-invariant (inventory, not syntax).
3. Order-dependent statistics (change under within-text shuffle) in Indus: 109/600. On these, Indus's nearest
   neighbour is LB_syll (median |z| 5.6) then LA (6.2); PE is farthest (10.9). Sequence structure in Indus is not
   PE-like.
4. Voynich (unknown) placement: by all statistics, Voynich-as-words sits next to Ur III legends-as-words (median |z|
   1.12; large vocabulary, no numerals, line = short word sequence), and Voynich-as-chars next to Ur III syllables
   (1.95). On the single 'fingerprint' statistic it sides with LB (words) or with the small-inventory group (chars),
   i.e. the statistic measures inventory size, confirming point 1.

Verdict so far: the 'accounting-notation fingerprint' is a null; the real Indus-specific residue is numeral
placement (number first, text does not end in a number) and a flat sign-frequency profile. Cycle 2 applies the
site-heterogeneity filter and a unigram-resampling control.
