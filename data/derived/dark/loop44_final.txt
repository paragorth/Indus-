S-DARK-44 (loop 44, 3 Oct 2026): ARE THE HEADS (CLOSERS) MEASURE / COMMODITY CLASSES, AS IN PROTO-ELAMITE?
Four cycles, all three merge levels, MD + Harappa with replication on held-out Wells sites, IM77 (bridge) and the 210 IM77-only texts.
Script tools/dark_loop44.py; rows in loop44_cycle1.txt .. loop44_cycle4.txt; full logs loop44_c{1,2,3,4}_<level>.txt (+ .json for cycles 1-2).

ANSWER: NEITHER PROTO-ELAMITE CLASSES NOR PURE UR III TITLES - but much closer to Ur III.
1. The Proto-Elamite positive control passes through the same code (final sign x number system MI 0.249 vs within-tablet null 0.142, V 0.44 vs 0.33, P < 0.001; M297 capacity, M263 count, M376 fractions, M288 mixed). The planted independent-head control gives uniform P (share < 0.05 = 0.00-0.05).
2. Indus, all numerals: head x series MI 0.293 vs null 0.092, V 0.60 (P < 0.001) - as strong as PE on the surface.
3. But the whole link is the numeral written directly before the head (tall n + W700 339/343, tall 3 + arrow 39/39, short 3 + carrier W156 38/38, tall 2 + W226 7/7, tall 2 + jar 45/62): the frozen title terms of S197/S216/S303. Counts elsewhere in the same text: head x series MI 0.032 = null (P 0.36-0.52), head x value P 0.23-0.75, on seals alone, on non-jar heads alone, at held-out sites (P 0.78), in IM77 (P 0.49), on IM77-only texts (P 0.78), with fixed-term items removed (P 0.93-0.94). The jar, head of 78% of quantity seals, takes 117 short / 120 tall counts.
4. The counted good does not choose the head: head x item away from the head is significant only through the known closer-qualifier phrases (pincers W550 with box W527 and W617; W877 with W154) and one jar x bracket cell (P 0.01); fish/bracket/tree counts predict the head no better than 'jar' in or out of sample (0.72-0.80 vs modal 0.72-0.77).
5. Reverse prediction fails out of sample: series + item -> head 0.72 in 5-fold (modal 0.43) but 0.42-0.48 on held-out sites against their own modal 0.55; IM77-only 0.19 vs 0.41.
6. Out-of-sample prediction 'one-series heads keep their series' fails at 3/18 (Wells held-out) and 3/11 (IM77-only), including a short two-tier 3 before the arrow at Miri Qalat (13-520) against 39/39 tall at home, and W226 with short 3/4 three of three times abroad vs tall 2 seven of seven at home. The frozen pairs are strong tendencies, not writing rules (as S-DARK-21.1 found for the minimum-lot rule).
7. Quantity seals (190 at MD+H): [opener 27%] + count + good (fish 51, bracket 19, W585 12 ...) + [title] + jar 78%; short values mode 3 then 7, tall values mode 2 then 3. Pot numerals stand alone (181 of 200 numbered pots have no head).

Difference from Ur III: Ur III titles have no numeral at all; Indus heads carry a fixed numeral epithet (tall 3 + arrow, short 3 + carrier, tall 2 + W226, 7 + W585 before the jar), i.e. rank- or grade-like numbers inside the title, which is why the surface statistic looks PE-like. Difference from PE: the class sign there governs every quantity under it; the Indus head governs none but its own.

Grade for 'heads = measure/commodity classes': killed (would have needed FAR series MI above null at n = 307 and a held-out series purity; got null and 17-27% leakage).
Working-dictionary consequence: keep closers as title heads (A as a function); the numeral inside a head term (33-520, 3-156, 32-226) is part of the title, grade C 'rank/grade numeral', not a quantity or unit. Lead not followed: the one IM77-specific value effect (short 6 avoids the jar, P 0.004, cells of 3-6 tokens), absent in Wells.
