PLAIN WORDS (what the posterior describes, within what cycle 2 says is identifiable):
 A system in which the largest city holds of the order of 10^4-10^5 distinct credentials (H*k 40,000-140,000; the 1,341
 Mohenjo-daro seals are a few per cent of them, consistent with S309), issued by an undetermined number of authorities (A is
 not identifiable), with of the order of 100 office classes (O 60-200) under a Zipf law of slope ~1.2, a handful of commodity
 classes (C ~ 2-12) shown on about 30% of credentials, an opener on 15-25%, an office word on 60-75%, and a holder-specific
 middle of about 2-3 signs on 65-85% of credentials drawn from a vocabulary of ~500-900 signs (most never seen). Tablets are
 mostly (75-90%) re-issues of local credentials with a popularity slope ~1.1-1.2, plus a suffix on ~20%; about half the pot marks
 copy a local credential. Sealings travel: the travel probability is pushed high (0.6-0.95) but is weakly measured.
 What the data CANNOT tell: how many authorities; whether a holder had 1 or 5 credentials (only the stock H*k is seen); whether
 the middle is the holder's name or a per-credential qualification (p_share flat: this is S321-S322 again, from the generative
 side); whether tablet copies were deposited in batches (p_batch flat once holders have find areas).
