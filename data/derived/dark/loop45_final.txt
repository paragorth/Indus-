## Answer
The non-adjacent middle vocabulary is ONE SHARED PAN-INDUS POOL. It is not partitioned by head (office), not by site (city), and only
weakly by object type (the Harappa tablet voucher words).
1. Information. In texts with a closer the non-adjacent elements carry 31.9% of H(head), of which the permutation null already gives 23.2%
   (an element's head is mostly fixed by text length and type); the excess is +8.7% (8.7 / 8.8). Holding the ADJACENT sign (S366 chain null)
   leaves +1.6% (2.0 / 2.3); on held-out sites +0.3–0.5% (P = 0.08–0.22); in IM77 +2.2%. The adjacent qualifier alone carries +36% (S303).
   Site: +1.5% of H(site) (P = 0.007–0.027); Mohenjo-daro vs Harappa +2.1%. Type: +3.5% (P = 0.001), +5.8% once the unclosed tablets are in.
2. Loyalty (>= 80% of >= 5 tokens under one label). Head: 0.365 vs 0.296–0.355 under the nulls (P = 0.06–0.77); exactly 1 element in 93–96
   is loyal to a non-jar head (W468 -> 154, 4/5); 90% of well-attested elements occur under >= 2 heads. The 35 'jar-loyal' elements
   (W590, W255, W806, W840, W17, W503, W176, W435 ...) are members of frozen pre-jar titles standing one slot left (S229) and the jar is the
   67% default. Site: 0.156 vs 0.105 (P = 0.05–0.06), 97% of elements at >= 2 sites. Type: 0.60 vs 0.56 (P = 0.11), all to 'seal'.
3. Blocks. Spectral co-clustering of element x head: gain 2.1x null but element purity 0.551 vs 0.559 (P = 0.4–0.5); the blocks are the
   arrow phrase (fish words, 705/706, 350-798-415) vs the jar, the Harappa tablet words, and 3 elements with 617/154 — the frame's own phrases.
   Element x site: purity at chance; the one split is {Harappa, Dholavira} bar-seals/tablets vs the rest. Head-blocks and site-blocks of the
   same elements: adjusted MI 0.001–0.017. Ur III name syllables x profession through the same code: 3.7x null with purity 0.55 vs 0.37
   (Akkadian-type names under arad2/aga3-us2, Sumerian under dub-sar/gudu4: a real office-partitioned name stock); Proto-Elamite signs x
   numeral class 8.7x (commodity classes). The Indus sits at 2x with no purity gain, below both.
4. Prediction (frozen before the run: a head-loyal element should keep its head on the 324 IM77-only texts). FAILED, as the shared reading
   expects: Wells-fitted loyal elements keep their head in 33 of 65 tokens (0.51) vs base rate 0.54, permutation null 32.8 +- 3.8, P = 0.54
   (0.56 / 0.47); without the inflated C617 (= M245 = W615 + W617) heads 0.66 vs 0.61, P = 0.30; IM77-fitted set 48/81 = 0.59 vs 0.55,
   P = 0.16; site-loyal elements at their own city 50/77 = 0.65 vs 0.57, null 53.4, P = 0.88. The MD/H jar-loyal set does score 0.84 vs 0.64
   on held-out Wells sites (P = 0.005–0.014), but 17 of 52 hits are W590 of the frozen 590-390-jar title and all hits are the default closer.

## What it implies for the referent of the middle
- The middle is drawn from one inventory used under every closer at every city: whatever it denotes is independent of the office (closer) and
  of the place of issue. That is how personal names behave in Ur III legends (the same name stock under every title) — but Ur III names are
  not fully shared either: their ETHNIC type is partitioned by office (purity 0.55 vs 0.37), and the Indus shows no such partition at all.
  Combined with S321/S-DARK-18 (middles almost never repeat, unlike real names) the middle looks like an individual description built from a
  pan-Indus element inventory (S-DARK-26: a closed ~860-element set in free combination), not a stock of recurring names and not a register.
- The ~9% head information in the non-adjacent middle is real but almost all of it is the frozen title phrases next to the closer; once the
  adjacent sign is known the rest of the middle says nothing about the office (0.3–2.3%). This is the S366 chain model exactly: a text is
  a middle + a closer phrase chosen with reference to the last middle sign, and the middle itself is office-blind.
- City: the only partition is the Harappa/Dholavira bar-seal and tablet vocabulary (S157, S216), a genre effect, not a city register of identifiers.
- Consequence for decipherment: any reading that makes the middle a title, rank, office or place-name list must explain why its elements are
  shared across all closers and cities with no block structure; readings that make it an individual designation (name, patronymic string,
  descriptive epithet string) are consistent with this result.

## Caveats
- 76% of texts are Mohenjo-daro + Harappa; held-out sites have 22 well-attested elements and 203 closed texts, power only for large effects.
- The 324 new texts give 155 closed texts, power for a > 15-point loyalty effect; 77% of them are MD + Harappa; M245 inflates the C617 head.
- Cycle 2 permutation counts were cut to 40 / 20 (shared machine); P floors 0.024 / 0.048; the verdict rests on purity being AT null, not on a P value.
- IM77 and Wells are ~70% the same objects (S312): the IM77 agreement is transcription-robust, not a replication; the held-out sites and the
  new texts are the independent evidence.
- Heads are the parser's closer units; a text with no closer ('none', 38%) was treated as a head class in the all-texts runs and excluded in
  the closed-text runs; both agree.
