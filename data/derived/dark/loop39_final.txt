## Question

No as a generator (best form 25-28/50, Sigma|z| 195-200), yes for the six statistics the chain could not explain; and the fitted form, written out, is GRAMMAR.md's frame with an open middle, not a checklist.

## Verdict
The form hypothesis explains what the chain missed and the chain explains what the form misses; both controls behave as a form hypothesis predicts, so the field co-selection is real, but the elements inside the fields are not chosen independently: the texts have a chain-like local order inside a form-like global one. Written out, the form is the known frame (opener-connective, mutually exclusive closer menus, suffix) around an open middle with no compulsory field, no forbidden combination and ~1,400 field subsets for 90% coverage, so it is not a listable code of forms (S-DARK-33.4 again). Validity as a form instance generalises to held-out sites and to the IM77-only texts, but a Markov-2 clone is just as valid, so the test does not separate field-wise composition from a chain. Not 'cracked'; no reading proposed. Open items: (a) a hybrid generator (form-level subset + chain-level order within the middle) is the natural next model for S366's battery; (b) the 'two menu signs in one field' texts, 5-6% in MD+H and 11-15% at the small sites, are where the frame fails most and where a new site-level test should look; (c) the cross-site attraction pairs (26 at held-out sites vs 2 under either model) remain the one statistic no fitted model reproduces.

## Caveats
Fit set = MD + Harappa (76-86% of texts); S309 sampling applies. The pairwise-order sampler (sequential Babington-Smith) is an approximation and over-produces disorder; a strict order beat it. Cycle-2 runs of the cycle-1 'units' configurations were dropped for time (the machine was shared with other loops); their cycle-1 numbers stand. No files committed.
