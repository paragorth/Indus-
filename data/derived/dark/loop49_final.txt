S-DARK-49 FINAL (3 Oct 2026): IS THERE A CALENDAR OR CYCLIC SLOT IN THE INDUS TEXT?  Five cycles, three merge levels each, nothing committed.
Scripts: tools/dark_loop49_c1.py ... dark_loop49_c5.py (usage: python3 tools/dark_loop49_cN.py <seq_raw|seq_strong|seq_all> [nperm]).
Logs: data/derived/dark/loop49_c<N>_<level>.txt (+ .json for c1, c2); rows: loop49_cycle1.txt ... loop49_cycle5.txt (one 4-column STRATEGIES.md row each, insert before "## Summary").
New external control built: CDLI ATF dump (github cdli-gh/data, Git-LFS, 87 MB, 135,254 texts) in the scratchpad (not committed; URL in the c1 script) -> 52,866 Ur III-and-later month lines (iti X), 65,174 year-name lines (mu ...).

VERDICT IN ONE LINE: no slot of the Indus text behaves like a calendar (month / day), like a year-name, or like a serial counter; every field is a name/title-type Zipfian inventory or a small closed frame paradigm, and the one weak time signal (cross-city depth correlation of signs, rho ~0.3) is object-sub-type and findspot mix that vanishes under the right null and in IM77.

(1) Frequency profiles (49.1, 49.4). Calendar band fixed in advance: 8 <= K <= 40 elements, Simpson evenness D/K >= 0.6, Zipf slope > -0.5. Positive controls land in it (planted uniform 12 / 30: D/K 0.99; Linear B month names before me-no: K=8, D/K 0.89, slope -0.44; Ur III top-12 month names: D/K 0.82, slope -0.53, 19,235 tokens). Name/title controls do not (Ur III owner names D/K 0.15 slope -0.77; Ur III title word D/K 0.01; Linear B ideograms 0.16; Proto-Elamite numerals 0.12). Indus: NAME K=576 D/K 0.15 slope -1.30; COUNT 0.08; positions C-1..C-3 / P1..P3 0.14-0.24; TITLE K=49 0.44 slope -1.18; CLOSER K=11 0.25 slope -1.74; all 77 automaton states name-band. Flat fields exist only with K <= 5 (openers, fish, tall numerals, the 705/255/690 title set, suffix) and those are not mutually exclusive. Held-out sites (404 texts) and IM77 (2,801; small sites 369): same. Caveat: Ur III year names are themselves Zipfian (D 73-141 over K ~10^4), so evenness cannot exclude a year-name slot; that needs (2).
(2) Time-stamp (49.2, 49.4, 49.5). No sign is BH-significantly confined to a 4-ft band or a period at Mohenjo-daro (1,281 depth-dated objects, 745 Marshall-period) or Harappa (700 depth-dated, 384 HARP fine-period), nor in IM77's own levels (1,191 / 676 texts); raw P<0.05 counts equal the false-positive expectation except a small Harappa excess that is whole-class tablet-sign shifts (W700, jar, W176, W806) of a few percent. No sign is concentrated (P_area < 0.10) in both cities. Cross-city rho of sign depth percentile +0.27 to +0.32 (P 0.002-0.022, coarse-type null) drops to P 0.10-0.19 under full object type x area (null mean +0.16-0.18), is carried by W700/W156/W151 (deep tablets) vs tree/bracket signs (later seals), and is +0.09-0.10 (ns) in IM77. Every sign spans the whole sequence (mean displacement from the city median 0.05-0.08 of the range).
(3) Cycle structure (49.3, 49.4). Mutually exclusive sets = the frame paradigms only (closers O/E 0.21, openers 0.42-0.49, numerals 0.59-0.70); their members have no fixed mutual order (numerals 3 of 16 pairs fixed; 31 vs 32 15:23) and no i / i+1 pairing across the faces of the 766 multi-sided objects (numerals by value 0.12 vs null 0.20; tall 1-4 0.63 vs 0.71; IM77 sides tall numerals 0.47-0.58 vs 0.76-0.78). Sets that do co-occur (fish O/E 1.54, 255-435-690 O/E 2.7-3.3, 350-798-415 O/E 3.6) are fixed-order phrases. The only cross-face regularity is the known count-face + text-face voucher (W700 + W740, 245 of 349 pairs; M328 + M342 164 of 244), one fixed pair, not a ring.
(4) Replication. Held-out Wells sites and IM77 small sites agree on (1); IM77 levels and sides agree on (2) and (3); the Wells cross-city correlation fails in IM77. seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all give the same numbers throughout (differences only in K by 1 where a merge collapses W817/861 or W705/706). Zero counts are upper bounds: < 3/404 of held-out texts, < 3/112 and < 3/75 signs (IM77 BH), < 0.004 per object for marked-jar cross-face pairs.

What this changes: nothing in GRAMMAR.md needs rewording; add to the 'what the text is not' list: not dated (no month, day, year-name or counter slot), consistent with S356 (serials killed), S-DARK-5 and S-DARK-34 (numeric codes killed), S-DARK-9 (no feature moves with depth), S304/S357 (stability). Would reopen: a new multi-sided series with consecutive counts on successive faces, or a sign confined to one stratum at both cities in post-1977 stratified finds (Dholavira, Rakhigarhi), or any field with >= 8 near-equal elements in new texts.
Data cautions: canonical file from the older inscriptions.csv build (S-DARK-23); depth exists only for Mohenjo-daro and Harappa; multi-sided data 81% Harappa tablets; IM77 ~70% the same objects as Wells (S312).
