held-out), plus 5-fold random splits (the only split comparable with the controls, whose 'sites' are random). (iii) adjacent-pair MI vs
the stratified shuffle. Arrows fired: 5 populations x 3 statistics x 3 levels; permutation floor P = 0.005 (reported P = 0.016 at 60
calibration corpora); every Indus excess is > 10 null sd, so correction does not change any verdict.

READING. Independence is rejected: the middle is not a product code of freely combined attributes (Indus +0.41 and 15% vs the planted code
+0.15 and 0.8%). But it is far from a compound-name stock: Ur III names give +0.66, 35% and both compounds and exclusions at 0.27, three
times the Indus exclusion rate and twice its held-out gain. On the within-corpus statistics the Indus middle sits at or just above the planted
lexicon (a free concatenation of fixed words: +0.20, 20%), i.e. 'some fixed compounds inside otherwise free combination'; on the between-site
statistic it falls to the attribute-code level, because the compounds do not travel. About a third of middle tokens (28% after purging frame
leftovers) sit in a recurring compound; two thirds combine with no detectable partner preference.
