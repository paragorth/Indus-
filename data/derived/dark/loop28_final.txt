# LOOP 28 final: DIACRITIC ALGEBRA, does the Indus sign list have an operator layer? (2026-10-03)
Files: loop28_cycle1.txt (+ loop28_edges*.json, loop28_edges_sheet.png), loop28_cycle2.txt (+ per level), loop28_cycle3.txt (+ per level), loop28_cycle4_pe.txt. Scripts: tools/dark_loop28_c1.py, c1fix.py, c1b.py (dropped method), c2.py, c3.py, c4_pe.py.

Answer: NO operator layer, with one lead.
1. Graphic derivation is real and thick: 203 component edges (strokes 105, enclosure 27, doubling 27, roof 14, ligature 15) found blind at >= 92% planted recall (ligature 78%), 0.5% wrong-base rate; 55% of edges within 20 Wells numbers vs 4% random. Attached (touching) modifications defeated the subtractive template method on the thin-line font (dropped, documented); they enter only via a same-block + glyph_sim_fine >= 0.6 proxy.
2. The derived signs are mostly hapax: only ~35 component pairs (108 with the proxy) have 5+ tokens on both sides.
3. Added strokes = paradigm-mate, not operator: same slot, same label, same neighbours (JSD percentile 0.31-0.37, P < 0.05), zero positional shift, MORE numerals before the marked form (+0.12), minimal-pair substitution 9 vs 2 random. Transcription-robust (IM77), all three merge levels, confirmed out of sample on rare signs (P = 0.003-0.017).
4. Enclosure: 4 usable pairs, stranger-like; no grammar measurable.
5. Doubling (10 pairs): doubled closers/suffixes leave their slot and move to the front (shift -0.23 vs -0.09 strangers; W700 -> W697/698, W520 -> W519, W400 -> W401). Not 'plural in place'. Lead only.
6. Holes are not filled: 50-67 of 70 frequent bases have no marked form, and no rare Wells sign or unbridged IM77 sign takes a base's contexts.
7. Proto-Elamite behaves the same: variants keep slot (pct 0.20) and numeral class (81%), co-occur with the base at half expectation; compounds take the slot of their first element.
