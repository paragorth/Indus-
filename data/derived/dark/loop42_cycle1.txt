# loop 42 cycle 1: the grammar as an authenticity scorer, part 1: rules, calibration, power (tools/dark_loop42.py 1; per-level logs loop42_cycle1_seq_{raw,strong,all}.txt; rule tables loop42_rules_<level>.json)

| S-DARK-42.1 | **Grammar as an authenticity scorer, part 1: rule set and calibration.** Eight rules, every parameter fitted on the FIT set only = Mohenjo-daro + Harappa texts with an excavation register number (4,377 texts, 2,416 with >= 3 signs; data/raw/inscriptions.csv, 5,680 rows, texts read first-read-first): R1 non-adjacent repeat of a sign not among the 9 signs that repeat in >= 3 fit texts (S88, S-DARK-15); R2 both members of a mutual-exclusion pair (S360 cut: both >= 25 texts, E >= 4, O <= 0.1E; 113-124 pairs); R3 W2 twice, W60 twice, W2 text-initial, W2 with a marked jar (S-DARK-15.2); R4 adjacent bigram expected >= 8 times and never written (S-DARK-33.3; 31-36 bigrams); R5 sign pair written in the direction never used (>= 10 co-occurrences, 0 reversals; S-DARK-19; 209-226 pairs); R6 complete text ending in a never-final sign or starting with a never-initial sign (>= 20 tokens; S289 closer tendency); R7 frozen pair (S229 list + pairs adjacent in >= 90% of >= 10 co-occurring texts; 74-75) present but not adjacent in order; R8 fish qualifiers out of the S296 order. Score = rules broken. Calibration: 5-fold CV on the fit set; held-out excavated sites with register number (591 texts, 313 with >= 3 signs); power on within-text shuffles, on an order-2 KN chain fitted to the fit set (the S366 'skilled forger'), an order-1 chain and a unigram sign-copier, all length-matched. seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all | **False alarm (>= 1 rule, texts >= 3 signs): CV 0.129-0.131, held-out sites 0.214-0.217; by length CV 2: 0.03, 3: 0.07, 4: 0.09, 5: 0.13, 6+: 0.11; held-out 3: 0.20, 4: 0.18, 5: 0.19-0.23, 6+: 0.09. At >= 2 rules: CV 0.001-0.03 by length, held-out 0.004-0.04.** Held-out excess over CV is R3 (W2 placement, 19 of 67 flags; Lothal 22/124, Kalibangan 17/55, Chanhu-daro 13/57 flagged) and R7 frozen pairs, i.e. regional conventions, not misreadings. Power (>= 1 rule, >= 3 signs): within-text shuffles 0.71 (CV) / 0.68 (held-out); order-2 chain 0.37-0.40; order-1 chain 0.44; unigram copier 0.64-0.67. At >= 2 rules: shuffles 0.22-0.56 by length vs forger 0.01-0.12. Rules that catch the order-2 forger are R2 exclusion, R6 edge, R7 frozen and R1 repeat (a chain forgets what it has written); R4 forbidden bigrams almost never (5 of 2,955), because the chain learns them. Same on all three levels (rule counts move by < 10%) | **The scorer is calibrated but weak against a skilled forger.** A text that breaks one rule is 2.5-3x more likely to be a chain-generated fake than a genuine excavated text (0.39 vs 0.13), and 5x for shuffles; a text that breaks two rules is 4-10x more likely. But 60% of chain forgeries pass, so the scorer can rank and flag, not certify. The held-out false alarm of 0.21 means any object from outside MD+H must be judged against the 0.21 rate, not 0.13; the W2-placement rule in particular is a Mohenjo-daro/Harappa convention. Any single flag on a short text (<= 5 signs) is within the false-alarm band; only >= 2 rules or a repeat (R1 false alarm 0.016) carries weight |
