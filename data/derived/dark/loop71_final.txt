(1) Catalogue. 93 sealings (Frenez & Tosi 2005 Table 1 + text) joined with Rao 1985 vol. II plate entries
    (pp. 325-327, archive.org scan: 36 plates, find-spot / layer / depth / reverse / no. of impressions),
    Wells faces (72 sealings) and IM77 sides (15 unique links; 75 IM77 sealings analysed on their own).
    Rao's register numbers check the reconstruction: 22/23 printed warehouse (SRG 3 GX 10) find-spots have
    numbers 1830-2077 vs 0/12 elsewhere; Frenez & Tosi's seal-linked W1 group is 12/14 in that range vs 6/13 for
    the '?' group (P = 0.046). Six '?' sealings (L-143, 181, 182, 184, 187, 213) are probably warehouse;
    L-153 and L-188 have printed find-spots in SRG 2. Back types cannot be checked (Rao: 'reed mark' everywhere).
    Transcriptions are a lower bound: 25 of 93 sealings absent from Wells; FT count 130-133 impressions.
(2) Network. IM77: a fixed team that recurs in the same combinations (4 repeated pairs vs 1.2, P = 0.017;
    heaviest pair x5 vs 1.9, P = 0.002; fewer distinct pairs than chance, P = 0.001). Team: hub 705-500-741-1-55-
    220-740-90 (M336-209-343-98-121-59-342-1) + 880-2-32-590-390-(740) (M286-99-87-249-162-342) + M186-293-99-258-
    347-342 + 861-2-236 (M267-99-66); a separate fish-arrow pair 617-2-240-220-233-520 + M150-123-67-72-211.
    Wells too thin (0 repeated pairs). Team members share the jar head weakly (0.40 vs 0.27, P = 0.07), no names
    and not the opener+marker frame. Back type predicts seal/head ONLY through the elephant (10/10 boxes);
    without it P = 0.49 (die), 0.26 (head). The hub sits on 7 fastening types.
(3) Seal matching. 0 Lothal sealing dies match any Lothal seal text (0/29 Wells, 0/22 complete IM77; < 10%).
    Exact matches only with Mohenjo-daro seals (elephant text = M-895, 705-33-520 = M-122/M-1359, 861-2-235 =
    M-1778; 6 vs 0.8 under shuffling, P = 0.003); emblems differ where checkable (elephant vs unicorn), so same
    text on a different die. The whole warehouse team matches no seal anywhere.
(4) What the archive shows about heads and users.
    - Roles: no head marks a kind of container or good (apart from the single elephant/box seal); the hub is a
      general-duty seal used alone (7-8x) and with others (4-8x). Co-sealers are a standing team of 3-4
      jar-headed holders who appear together repeatedly, not a fixed consignor/inspector pair.
    - Users: different people/offices holding same-class (jar) titles with different names; the texts recur as
      offices between cities (same text, different emblem at Mohenjo-daro), but no Lothal seal stamped any
      surviving sealing, so the holders' seals are unrecovered (ghost issuers, S-DARK-16.2).
    - Same direction as S-DARK-65 (D, collegial co-sealing) and S325-S330 (text = office, emblem = house).
    Rests on Lothal alone; IM77 vs Wells = transcription-robust, not replicated. Not 'cracked'.
Seq levels: identical at seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all (except W817 -> 861 label; 705-33-520 gains Harappa
    H-53 and a Dholavira seal at strong/all).
