S-DARK-57 (loop 57, 3 Oct 2026): THE FOUNDER SET. Which seal signs descend from the pre-seal pottery marks, and are they the frame?
Arrow tested: the closers and markers of the seal code are the old pottery unit/count marks; the middle inventory is the new layer.

Rows: loop57_cycle1.txt (founder set vs seal roles, two nulls), loop57_cycle2.txt (per-sign chronology), loop57_cycle3.txt (held-out prediction + controls).
Scripts: tools/dark_loop57_build.py (transcription table), tools/dark_loop57.py <1|2|3>. Data: loop57_earlymarks.csv (217 rows, 985 marks), loop57_images/ + SOURCES.txt. Details: loop57_cycleN_detail.txt.

Legwork: 985 pre-Mature marks from open sources: Mehrgarh IV-VII (Quivron 1980 Tableau 4, 857 marks, Persee), Sarai Khola IA/II (Halim 1972, 60, archive.org), Harappa Ravi + Kot Diji (Kenoyer 2006 Figs 3-4, 66, harappa.com via non-headless Chromium), Kot Diji (Khan 1965 pl. XXIV, 2); plus the 41 corpus tokens of Harappa Period 1-2 pots. 630 marks got a Wells number (A/B/C per sign), 355 are non-Wells shapes (horizontal bars, plain T, arches, plus signs, suns). IAR 1961-64 Kalibangan sections read page by page: no graffiti figure (text mentions an arrow). Not open: Lal 2003 Kalibangan I, Rehman Dheri, Amri, Nausharo.

VERDICT: THE SEAL FRAME IS NOT THE OLD POT-MARK LAYER. THE COUNT LAYER IS.
  1. Founder set (new transcriptions A+B): 26 signs / 538 tokens = stroke numerals (9 types, 47% of tokens) + simple middles (X, V-cup W700, arcs W899/904/908, V+stroke, bowtie, chevron, tree, comb, arrow W515, Y, grid, quartered circle). Frame signs (opener, W60, jar, arrow W520, suffix, closer paradigm): 0 of 25 attested types (< 0.12) vs 0.18 under a frequency-matched draw and 0.22 under a complexity-matched draw (P-below 0.000 / 0.001); closers 0 vs 0.09 / 0.13 (P 0.007 / 0.019); numerals 0.36 vs 0.16 / 0.25 (P 0.001 / 0.063). Adding the corpus Period 1-2 pots: frame 0.06 (jar x2, W820 x1, Harappa/Kanmer only). Jar W740, W520, W60, W861, W817, W920, W90, W151, W527, W156, W617, W154, W226 have no pre-seal attestation at any site (each < 0.003 of 871 early tokens).
  2. No founder sign is recruited into the frame: 27 continuity, 6 class changes on <= 30 seal tokens (W904 double arc middle -> pre-jar title, a 14/14 split), 5 early-only signs. Founder numerals on Mature pots stand alone or first (marks/counts), not as connectives.
  3. Prediction outside the fitting data met: Mature pot marks at Lothal/Kalibangan/Dholavira draw 0.56 of tokens from the founder set vs 0.35 for the seals of the same sites (P < 0.001); complexity-matched random sign sets give a gap of +0.01; a founder set built only at Mehrgarh + Sarai Khola + Kot Diji (no Harappa data) still gives 0.33 vs 0.20 (P < 0.001). The pot-mark layer stays a pot-mark layer into the Mature period.
  So: the seals inherit the stroke numerals and about fifteen simple geometric signs that fill the open middle; the openers, connectives, jar, arrow, closer paradigm and suffix appear with the seals and have no pottery ancestor in the open record. The arrow's positive half (count/unit layer is older) holds and replicates S-DARK-43.4 at two sites outside Harappa; its strong half (closers and markers = old pot marks) is killed.

Dependence and reliability:
  - Harappa: 76 of 985 new marks + the 41 corpus tokens; the kill does NOT rest on Harappa (Mehrgarh alone 0/16 frame, P 0.004; Sarai Khola + Kot Diji agree). The held-out prediction rests on Kalibangan (146 of 180 tokens); Lothal is inconclusive, Dholavira 3 tokens.
  - Transcriptions: from published drawings / typology tables, single pass against the full Wells glyph set, no photographs, so no mirrored blind pass (all matched signs but the arrow and hooks are symmetric). A for X and arrow; B for strokes (tall vs short height is not recorded in the sources), V-cup, arcs, bowtie, chevron, Y; C for waves, lens/circle, hooks, hashes. Results are unchanged between A+B and A+B+C and at all three merge levels. Mehrgarh counts are Quivron's own category totals (table read twice, sum 857 vs the paper's 850).
  - Identity caveat: a stroke pair or an X on a 3000 BC Mehrgarh pot and W32 / W645 on a seal are the same SHAPE; nothing here shows they are the same sign in use. Every zero is an upper bound (< 3/n).
Would revive the strong arrow: five jar / W820 / W861 / W60 marks in a pre-2600 BC context (Kalibangan I, Kunal, Rehman Dheri). Would strengthen the kill: Lal 2003's Kalibangan I graffiti with the same zero.
