# S-DARK-25 cycle 2: DETERMINATIVE SIGNATURE (unpronounced class sign: never alone, fixed side, many hosts, hosts a coherent class)
# Tool: tools/dark_loop25.py 2 <level> 200. Tables: loop25_cycle2_seq_raw/strong/all.txt

Design. For every sign with >= 30 tokens and each side: candidates are never at the text edge on the host side (>= 0.90), have >= 8
hosts with >= 2 attachments and partner entropy >= 3 bits (attach to MANY different signs). For each candidate: tokens as a
one-sign text ('alone', from the full canonical file) and as the only non-numeral sign; two host-class tests:
  (i) COHERENCE: mean pairwise cosine of the hosts' far-side neighbour profiles vs 200 frequency-matched random host sets (z);
  (ii) LOYALTY: P(candidate on that side | host), token-weighted (loy) and the share of hosts that take it >= 50% of the time
       (loy50): an obligatory class marker should be taken by most of its hosts most of the time.
Calibration on the controls with REAL determinatives / classifiers: Ur III 'd' (divine), 'ki' (place); Linear B ideograms after
commodity words; planted D0-D2 (before their 10 logograms, p = 0.9). Negative calibration: the planted frame-only corpus (markers,
closers and their qualifier sets), where nothing is a determinative.

Calibration result
  (i) Coherence FAILS as a determinative test: Ur III d/R z +0.2, ki/L z -2.1, dumu/ARAD2/lu2 z -0.1 to -1.9; planted D0-D2
      z -1.9 to -3.4 (the hosts of a determinative are a semantic class, which does not make them share neighbours); while the
      planted FRAME-ONLY corpus produces 19 false 'determinatives' at z +6 to +9 (mk, c0-c8, q0-q22: closer-specific qualifier
      sets are coherent by construction). So a high host-coherence z is a FRAME signal (S303: closers select their qualifiers),
      not a class-sign signal. The 3-4 Indus signs that pass it (W1/R z +4.2, W233/R +4.2, W100/L +5.7, W350/R; all levels)
      are therefore read as frame/fixed-term effects: W100-jar (S301), W233 before W520 (S299), W1 before fixed numerals (S234).
  (ii) Loyalty (loy50) separates the systems: planted D0-D2 1.00 (loy 0.90-0.92); Ur III d/R 0.60, ki/L 0.52 (loy 0.25 / 0.07:
      token-weighted loyalty is low because the top hosts are also common syllables); Linear B ideograms AES 0.82, V 0.60,
      GRA 0.55, NUM 0.72; Ur III frame words dumu 0.00-0.04, ARAD2 0.04, lu2 0.28; planted frame-only marker mk 0.00, closers
      c0 0.12, qualifiers 0.00. Threshold loy50 >= 0.5 passes every real class sign and no frame sign in the controls.

Reading. Indus has no sign with the determinative signature: nothing that is never alone AND is taken obligatorily by a class of
hosts. The jar is the nearest (a third of its title hosts take it >= 50% of the time) but it is itself a free one-sign text, so
it is a word (the closer/title head of S289), not an unpronounced class sign. The openers' connective and the qualifiers are
optional attachments. The positive-looking coherence hits are the known frame rules re-found. Upper bound: a determinative used
with a class of RARE words (hosts under 2 tokens) is invisible to this test; the planted corpus shows the test needs ~10 hosts
with >= 2 tokens each. Transcription-robust (same ranking at all three merge levels; W350/R appears only in seq_raw).
