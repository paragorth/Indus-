THE MODEL (agent-based credential corpus laid over the real excavation skeleton)
 The skeleton is the real design: every complete object keeps its site, object class and whether its find area is recorded.
 Only the text on each object is generated. Parameters (21; priors in PRIOR):
   A   issuing authorities (1-8, each with its own opener sign + one of two connectives; Zipf popularity)
   O   office classes / closers (3-400 log-uniform), each = optional title sign + one of 12 final signs; Zipf slope zO (0.3-2)
   C   commodity classes (1-40), each an item sign with a count mode 2/3/4 and spread (-1..+2)
   H   holders at the largest city (50-60,000); other sites scale with their seal count; k credentials per holder (1-6)
   middle: p_mid (credential carries a holder-specific middle), lam_mid (length 1+Poisson), V_mid (30-3,000 signs), z_mid (Zipf),
           p_share (middle shared by all of a holder's credentials = a NAME; else fresh per credential = a QUALIFICATION)
   p_open, p_comm, p_off, p_ext: opener shown / commodity+count shown / office shown / extra qualifier sign (nesting route)
   p_reissue: a tablet copies a local credential (popularity Zipf z_copy) else is a voucher (tall 2-4 + unit sign) or office chit
   p_batch: a re-issued tablet lies in the holder's find area (else random area of the site)
   p_travel: a sealing was pressed by a seal from ANOTHER site (site chosen by seal count) else a local seal
   p_suf: tablet suffix sign; p_potcred: share of pot marks that copy a local credential (rest: numeral alone / office sign)
 Text order: [opener conn] [middle] [count item] [qualifier] [title final] (+ suffix on tablets). No sign has a meaning.
