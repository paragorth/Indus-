LOOP 36, CYCLE 2 (2026-10-03): transcriptions of the candidate objects (data/derived/dark/loop36_newfinds.csv).
Sign numbers are Wells numbers read against the lipi font glyph sheets (rendered from tools/indus_font.ttf, all 729 codepoints).
Confidence per sign: A = unmistakable, B = likely, C = guess; ? = unread/lost. Reading direction stated per row.
Reading-order convention: a seal FACE is read left to right, a modern impression right to left (= corpus seq_raw order).
Calibration (cycle 1): ~75% per-sign accuracy on good photographs; fish/oval family and the tall/short stroke
distinction are the main confusions.
