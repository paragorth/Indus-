VERDICT (one line): the edit-distance-1 neighbours of frequent Indus texts are not scribal errors; they are one-slot word swaps and prefixed qualifiers inside a fixed frame. Specific confusions do not recur above a frequency-weighted null (12-16 recurrent pairs vs 17-23 expected), so no hidden set of miscopies or unrecognised allographs exists; the swaps respect slot (90-93% vs 42-51%), recover the Wells merges blind (20 vs 4), and replicate at held-out sites (8 vs 2.1, P = 0.002). The apparent site/area clustering was the Harappa-tablet deposition confound. Optional signs are exactly the grammar's qualifiers (W400 suffix final, W240/W235/W803/W33 initial) and never the opener or jar (1 vs 6 expected).
