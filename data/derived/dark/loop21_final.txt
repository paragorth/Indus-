# LOOP 21 FINAL: true replication on material Wells never transcribed (3 cycles; engine data/derived/dark/loop21_engine.py)

## The held-out set
IM77 texts (one per text_no+side, lines joined in reading order): 3,445; 703 damaged (sign 0), 2,742 complete.
Matching to the Wells/ICIT merged corpus (audit_overlap.py extended: bridge W->M, site-aware, unbridged W signs as wildcards
capped at 2 with >= 3 concrete agreeing positions, NEAR = edit distance 1 or exact reverse). Shuffle control on the matcher:
0.3% false exact, 1.1% false near (unlimited wildcards would give 9.7% / 10.3%, which is why the rule was capped).
  exact 1,685 (61.5%) | near 157 (5.7%) | indeterminate (unbridged M sign, no match) 690 (25.2%) | NEW 210 (7.7%)
The task's '~30% IM77-only' expectation does not survive: with wildcards the audit-style overlap is 82% (not 69%), and the
certainly-new material is 210 texts: MD 91, Harappa 67, Lothal 27, Kalibangan 12, Chanhu-daro 7, other 3, West Asia 3;
117 seals, 51 sealings, 16 tablets, 15 pots, 11 rods/implements/misc; mean 3.6 signs (123 texts of 2-3 signs).
Spot-checked: these sequences are absent from the merged corpus at any site (e.g. M48-342-130 = W176-740-435).

Splits (cycle 3): frame, exclusivity, nesting and reuse hold for seals and non-seals and for MD / Harappa / small sites separately.

## Verdict
About half of the grammar is now confirmed on texts that Wells never transcribed: the opener-connective-middle-closer frame, the
never-initial connective, the mutually exclusive closer paradigm, and the credential structure (shared parts, nesting, reuse).
The fish order, the name calibration and the quantity-seal type are not refuted but cannot be tested at n = 210 (they stay
'transcription-robust'). The minimum-lot rule is the one claim that the new material contradicts, at a rate above its own kill line.

## Caveats
- 'New' means no exact or near counterpart in the bridged Wells corpus at the same site; Wells may have read the same object with
  two or more signs the bridge does not cover (690 texts are indeterminate for exactly that reason), so some of the 210 are re-readings.
- The two inventories differ: Mahadevan splits strokes by placement (M97 vs M98, M99 vs M100) and merges some Wells allographs;
  all tests here were run in M numbers with the GRAMMAR.md sign sets mapped through bridge_extended.json.
- The new set is short-text-heavy (mean 3.6 signs vs 4.1 in the overlap) and 44% non-seal, which lowers closer rates and makes
  'nesting' partly a re-measurement of the closer units.
- 158 of 210 are Mohenjo-daro + Harappa (75%), as always.
- Doubtful readings (41 of 210 texts) were kept; the engine has --nodoubt for a re-run.
