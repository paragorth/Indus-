  usually leaving out the count face or a one-sign face. So the two transcriptions do not even agree on how many sides carry text: the 'two faces' of the Harappa
  voucher are a Wells / CISI observation that IM77 mostly does not record. The IM77 >= 2-side set is therefore only 29% the size of the Wells one (482 vs 827 texts) and
  a different sample (Harappa sealings 119, incised miniature tablets 146, MD seals 20, copper tablets 16).
