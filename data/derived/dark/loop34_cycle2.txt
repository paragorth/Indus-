RECOVERED RULES (depth-3 tree, min leaf 15, trained on MD + Harappa seals; each leaf with train precision >= 0.85
tested on the hold-outs; bound < 3/n for zero exceptions)
last sign:   "if the sign before the end is tall-3 (W33) and the jar is absent, the last sign is the arrow W520":
             train 24/24; held-out sites 3 of 4, IM77 2 of 2 (< 3/2). Everything else falls to 'jar' (the modal class).
which closer:"if tall-3 (W33) stands before the closer -> W520" (train 23/23; sites 2/2, IM77 4/4, < 3/4 = 0.75);
             "if the plain fish W220 stands before the closer -> W520" (train 15/16; sites 2/2, IM77 3/3);
             "if W550 stands before the closer -> W527" (train 13/16). These are the S289 closer units and the
             S299 arrow phrase; the tree finds nothing else: 525 of 802 closer-ended texts fall in the jar leaf.
closer vs name-final: "a text with no jar elsewhere, with a fish and without W806 ends in a closer" train 301/339
             (0.89); held-out sites 54/62, IM77 47/59: a tendency (0.80-0.87), not a rule. "W4 (short 4) in a text
             without the jar -> name-final" train 22/25; sites 7/8, IM77 6/7.
emblem:      no leaf predicts anything but unicorn; the only 'rule' (count_W798 > 0 -> unicorn, 38/38 train; sites
             4/4, IM77 2/3) is the unicorn base rate (80%) on a small leaf, and the IM77 exception kills it.
No rule of the form "if closer X then emblem Y" or "if middle contains Z then no numeral" exists at precision
>= 0.85 with >= 15 training seals; the tree never splits a fact target on a sign count except W621/W798 for the
emblem, and those leaves predict the modal class.
