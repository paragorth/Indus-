S-DARK-8.1  Reading-order brute force: position-permutation templates (tools/dark8_permute.py; log loop8_cycle1_log.txt; numbers loop8_cycle1_permute.json)

Setup. Texts of length L = 3..7 without damage marks, canonical reading order. Every permutation template of the L positions (L! for L <= 5; 2,000 sampled for L = 6, 7, always including identity and full reverse) applied to all texts of that length. Score A: order-2 (interpolated trigram) model fit on Mohenjo-daro + Harappa texts of length L, mean nll/token on texts of length L from all other sites (held-out sites). Score B: H(first position) + H(last position) over all texts of length L. Controls: (1) 2,064 Ur III seal legends (CDLI, @seal lines, length 3-7), known order = line 1 -> 2 -> 3, 70/30 split; (2) Indus corpus with signs shuffled inside each text, the whole template search re-run (20 reps seq_raw, 5 reps others), best template's gain over identity recorded = max-corrected null. Run on seq_raw, seq_strong, seq_all.

Caveat stated up front: a forward Markov-2 chain is also a backward Markov-2 chain, so the full reverse ties with identity up to smoothing on both scores. The search tests adjacency structure (which positions are neighbours), not direction. Direction is tested per text in S-DARK-8.4.

Results.
Ur III positive control (Score A): identity rank 1 of 24 / 120 / 720 / 2,000 templates at L = 4, 5, 6, 7 (gain of best non-trivial template negative: -0.27, -0.17, -0.21, -0.25 nats/token). L = 3 (108 train legends): reverse beats identity by 0.045, identity rank 2/6. Score B (slot entropy) FAILS the positive control: identity rank 5/24, 25/120, 289/720, 1242/2000 - slot entropy rewards putting the most repetitive line (dumu / ARAD) at an edge, not the true order. Score B is therefore dropped as a criterion.
Indus, Score A (seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all):
  L=3 (740 train, 166 test): identity rank 1/6 in all three; best non-trivial gain -0.16.
  L=4 (632/104): identity rank 1/24 in all three; best non-trivial gain -0.003 / -0.07 / -0.06.
  L=5 (481/95): identity rank 5/120 (raw; best (3,4,2,1,0) gain +0.018 vs shuffled-null max 0.38), rank 1/120 (strong, all).
  L=6 (273/52): identity rank 75-91/720; best template (4,5,3,1,0,2) gain +0.31 / +0.37 / +0.40 vs shuffled-null best gain max 0.45 (20 reps), median 0.29. The full reverse also beats identity here by 0.29 (smoothing asymmetry at small n; see 8.4 for the per-text test, which is unambiguous).
  L=7 (207/36): identity rank 16-29/2000; best gain +0.10 / +0.09 / +0.16 vs null max 0.54.
Every gain over identity is inside the max-corrected shuffled null. No template depends on the merge level (the three seq variants agree in rank and sign).
