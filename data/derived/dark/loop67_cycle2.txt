S-DARK-67 cycle 2: reading the bound texts against the frame; the same texts and units elsewhere (tools/dark_loop67.py 2;
full log loop67_cycle2_log.txt)

Parse (S310/S331 frame parser, qualifier sets fitted on all complete Wells texts, seq_all). 62 distinct complete copper texts
on 176 faces: full name + frame 24 texts (87 faces), designation-only (title/count + closer) 9 (13 faces), 'name-only' 29
(76 faces). The parser has no W407 closer: 12 of the 29 'name-only' texts (45 faces, 40 with W845) end in the copper-specific
closing unit 845 (+61/63) + 407 of S306/S307 (hare 235-705-33-845-407-321-407, archer 740-900-850-407-61-845-806 reversed
in display, tiger/ox 798-240-845-61-407, knot 415-220-845-407, 231-17-585-95-520-32-407), the rest are 1-3 sign labels
(900-165, 5-706, 233-585-407, 341, 35). So the copper texts are ordinary seal-type texts: a frame closer (jar 740, W617,
W595) or the copper closer 845-407, with a 1-5 sign middle in front; none is frame-only.

The same texts elsewhere (Wells, non-copper complete faces, seq_all): exact matches 7/62 (503-615-740 on unicorn seal M-1803;
803-233-176-740 on seal K-62; 3-220-590-390-740 on unicorn seal M-94; 798-740 on two Dholavira unicorn seals and a bangle;
590-407-740 on sealing Hls-1; 900-165 and 35 on a misc. object and a pot). Control: Mohenjo-daro seal texts of the same
lengths recur exactly elsewhere 10.9 of 62 [6,16] -> copper texts recur at the ordinary rate. Closing units: the last-2 unit
of a copper text occurs on other objects for 43/58 texts and the last-3 unit for 19/51; these units sit under the unicorn
on seals: elephant text 705-33-923-740 -> 923-740 on 19 objects, 18 seals, 13 unicorn, 1 tiger, 0 elephant; rhino text
235-233-222-740 -> 222-740 on 4 unicorn seals; archer text ...850-900-740 -> 900-740 on 16 seals, 10 unicorn; goat text
3-421-176-100-740-790 -> 100-740 on 36 seals, 21 unicorn, 5 gaur; 435-690-740 (buffalo/gaur copper) on 15 seals, 11 unicorn,
1 elephant, 1 gaur. IM77 (M numbers): exact recurrence 5/37 distinct copper texts, e.g. rhino copper text 204-245-342 on a
unicorn seal (2294) and a buffalo seal (2663); goat copper text 249-169-342 on two unicorn seals and a Harappa tablet;
102-190 (buffalo/horned-tiger copper) on a unicorn seal. Over all copper texts, seals ending in the same last-2 unit carry
the copper animal 15 times vs 12.1 expected from base rates, and 10 of the 15 are the unicorn text 345-341-18-252-97-25-245-245
(unicorn is the base rate, 888/1143 seals).
