S-DARK-58 cycle 3: calibration with identical code. Script tools/dark_loop58_calib.py 1000; table loop58_c3_calib.txt (+ .json).

Position class of a token type = where it stands in the long texts (>= 4 tokens) of its own corpus: FIRST (>= 50% of its
long-text tokens first), LAST (>= 50% last), INTERIOR (otherwise, >= 3 long tokens), RARE (< 3 tokens in long texts, incl.
never). Then the class mix of 1-token and 2-token texts vs tokens drawn by overall and by long-text frequency (1,000x), and the
hapax share (type seen once in the whole corpus). Corpora: Ur III seal legends (CDLI, 6,381, words), Linear B tablet lines
(DAMOS, 10,091 after dropping editorial tokens), Latin EDH Italy (6,000, words), Indus Wells seq_raw/strong/all by object type
(roles and pools from the whole clean corpus, minimal set = the object type), IM77 in Mahadevan numbers.

Known composition of the calibration minimal texts: Ur III has no 1-word legends in the CDLI set; its 564 2-word legends are
name + title/patronym (second word INTERIOR class 412, RARE 101: "dumu", "dub-sar", "arad2" are interior in long legends
because a patronym follows). Linear B 1-token lines are NUM (183), ideograms (BIG, VIR, M) and word fragments. Latin 1-word
texts are names (august, felix, persi, probi, romanus...).
