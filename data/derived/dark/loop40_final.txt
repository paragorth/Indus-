LOOP 40 FINAL: THE CONCATENATION TEST (2026-10-03)
Question: are long texts (>= 5 signs) concatenations of attested complete short texts, joined at closer boundaries?
Script: tools/dark_loop40.py (cycles 1-4, argument LEVEL in seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all); logs loop40_cycleN_LEVEL.txt;
cut lists loop40_c1_cuts_LEVEL.json; fixed unit order loop40_c2_order_LEVEL.json; ready-to-paste STRATEGIES.md rows in
loop40_cycle1.txt .. loop40_cycle4.txt (S-DARK-40.1 - 40.4). Corpus: merged-corpus-canonical.json (older build, S-DARK-23),
3,415 complete direction-recorded texts, 1,168 of >= 5 signs; IM77-only set from loop27 (324 texts, 89 long).
All three merge levels run; every verdict is the same at all three (seq_strong and seq_all differ from seq_raw only in
merging 817/861, which raises the opener-phrase count from 24 to 41 but changes no conclusion).

VERDICTS
1. Decomposition (40.1). 3.7-5.4% of long texts split into 2-3 attested complete texts of >= 2 signs (7-12% if single
   signs count as units). This is 2.7-2.8x the within-slot shuffle, but: the share falls with length (9.6% at 5 signs,
   1.1% at 8+), the reverse of a joining process; an order-2 sign chain generates as many or more (0.05-0.07 vs real
   0.04-0.055); and the cut lies at a closer/opener boundary in only 13-19% of cases, against 70% when two real short texts
   are actually joined. Four fifths of the 'decompositions' are the opener phrase 817/861-2 (attested alone on 7 objects)
   followed by one ordinary text. Texts with two closer-bearing units: 4 of 1,168 (10 with single-sign units).
2. Order of units (40.2). The only fixed precedence is OPENER-unit before everything (21-58 : 0) and middle before closer
   unit; with the opener removed, PLAIN vs JAR is 4:4 / 5:4 (free). No cycle, longest chain 3 = the S289/S310 frame.
   Held-out sites: 0 violations. The reused units are the S-DARK-21.2 P2 inventory (opener phrases, closer units, 33/34-700).
3. Find spots (40.3). Parts of a decomposable text do not occur as separate objects at the same site, area or block more
   than chance (site P = 0.98-1.00; block 0/16-32; Mohenjo-daro 0/20-37 areas, 0/15-29 blocks, upper bound ~20%). The one
   positive (4 Harappa hosts in one area) is the known Harappa tablet-series clustering; its pair-level statistic is
   below chance.
4. Held-out material (40.4). IM77-only long texts: 1 of 89 decomposes into Wells-attested >= 2-sign units (upper bound
   3.4%); 7 of 89 with single-sign units, all opener phrase or stock jar text plus a loose sign. Same at all levels.

NOT CRACKED. Long Indus texts are not stacks of short complete texts. They are built once, in the slot frame, from reused
frame phrases plus a middle that is not itself a text. The concatenation hypothesis is closed at the text level; what
survives is the already-known phrase reuse (S-DARK-21.2) and the slot grammar (S289, S310, S366). Not Mohenjo-daro alone:
Harappa, held-out sites and the IM77-only set give the same shares and the same zero violations.
