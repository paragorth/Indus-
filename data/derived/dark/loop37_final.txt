S-DARK-37 final: multi-sided objects. Are the sides (A) one long text read across the sides, (B) paraphrases / alternates of one message, or (C) independent entries?
Cycles 1-4: tools/dark_loop37*.py, rows in data/derived/dark/loop37_cycle1-4.txt (37.1 and 37.2 already in STRATEGIES.md).
Data: inscriptions.csv (827 objects with >= 2 inscribed faces, 766 after collapsing identical moulded face-sets: Harappa tablets 621, Mohenjo-daro 104, seals 37, sealings 28,
12 MD objects with >= 3 faces); IM77 482 multi-sided texts (370 collapsed); 7 objects read as multi-sided by both.

Answer: C, with two riders.
A (one text) is dead three ways: openers fall on any face at chance and closers on the last recorded face at chance (37.1 A1); a concatenation in recorded order parses
like random faces glued together (0.58 frame violations vs 0.60 random vs 0.19 for a real long text, 37.1 A3); and the S-DARK-19 fixed-order pairs, which agree 0.94
inside a face, agree with the recorded face order 0.47 across the boundary (0.50 permuted; 0.94 for one text cut in two), no hidden face order recovers them
(orientable 0.70 vs 0.68 random vs 0.88 one text), and the faces share FEWER fixed pairs than random faces of the same type and length (1,549 vs 1,747, P = 0.01) (37.3).
B (paraphrase) is dead: 0-1 grade-swap pairs of 914 (0.5 expected), faces are on average less alike than random same-length faces (37.2).
C holds, but the sides are not entries drawn independently from a ledger: (i) the Harappa two-field voucher (one closed text + one count or short label; two closed faces
39 vs 107 expected, 37.1 A2), (ii) whole face-sets recur as stock units even when hand-incised (125 of 342 TAB:I vs 56; 27 of 104 MD objects vs 0.4, 37.2b), and
(iii) the same text is repeated on two sides (27 pairs vs 20; 26 vs 8 without count faces) and, on two-sided seals, a legend and its abbreviation (5 of 74 seal pairs vs 0.2).
So: fixed fields of one object type, written as separate texts, not one text and not restatements.

Controls: face labels permuted within object; faces reassigned within site x type; model C (random same site x type x length faces); model A (real single texts cut at the
boundary); within-face agreement as the positive reference (0.94). Merge levels: seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all agree within 0.02 (one object) on every statistic in cycles 1-3.
Not Mohenjo-daro-prism-dependent: the C result holds separately on 621 Harappa tablets, 37 seals, 28 sealings, 40 non-MD-Harappa objects and in IM77's own side recording
(recorded order 0.465, orientable at model C, 37.4); the 12 MD >= 3-face objects (6 TAB:B prisms, 4 four-sided seals, 2 tags) behave the same way (orientable 4/11 vs 0.69
for one text; 0.83 frame violations vs 0.81 random) but are too few to carry any result alone. Transcription: the direction is transcription-robust (IM77 alone gives the same
pattern, and identical-text + seal-abbreviation are the only excesses there too) but the overlap is 7 objects and IM77 omits count faces, so the voucher complementarity
(37.1 A2) rests on Wells / CISI. Pots and bangles: 4 two-text objects in Wells, 5 in IM77, 0 shared; each file's own cases (a repeat, a short label beside a closed text,
IM77 2930 with the opener on side 2 after a closed side 1) fit C. Sampling: 76% of the multi-sided objects are Harappa tablets, so 'C' is above all a statement about the
Harappa voucher; the seal and MD results are the same direction at n = 37 / 104.

Consequences for the reading: a multi-sided object must be entered as several texts, never joined (as merged-corpus-canonical.json already does per record); the recorded
face number carries no reading order; the two-sided seal abbreviation pairs (817-2-5-390 / 817-2-5; 861-741 / 861-741-240-630-740; 575-407-746 / 575-407; 575-31 /
575-31-2-3-390; 798-17-575-740-679 / 575-740-679) are a short lexical lead: the shorter face keeps the opener and drops the closer and part of the middle, which is what an
abbreviated owner formula would do. Leads to log: photo check of face numbering on MD two-faced objects (0.65 agreement, P = 0.02) and of obverse/reverse convention on
the 16 IM77 copper tablets (0.78, P = 0.003), the one class where recorded side order follows sign order.
