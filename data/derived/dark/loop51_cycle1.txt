| S-DARK-51.1 | **Origin tracing of the foreign-found texts (loop 51, cycle 1).** Catalogue: 34 objects with signs from 22 find-spots outside the Indus culture area (Mesopotamia: Ur 5, Kish 2, Girsu, Nippur, Tell Umma, Tello; Iran: Susa 2, Luristan, Tepe Yahya; Gulf/Oman: Karzakan 3, Ra's al-Junayz 3, Failaka 2, Salut 2, Dilmun, Janabiyah, Qala'at al-Bahrain, Saar, Hajar, Kalba; Central Asia: Altyn Depe, Gonur, Shortughai), all present in `merged-corpus-canonical.json` (older build, S-DARK-23); 31 distinct texts of >= 2 signs (17 round Gulf-type seals, 7 square seals, 1 cylinder, 4 pots, 1 sealing, 1 tablet). Miri Qalat and Shortughai flagged as borderland (Shortughai kept, Miri Qalat excluded). Home populations: Mohenjo-daro 1,883, Harappa 2,528, Lothal 202, Kalibangan 199, Dholavira 203, Chanhu-daro 72, other-Indus pooled 218 texts. Scorer (a): per-site boundary bigram model (START/END tokens, so opener and closer menus, frozen pairs and site-local stock texts enter together) with Dirichlet backoff to the pooled corpus (k = 10) -> log likelihood ratio per site, assignment = argmax; (b) nearest-neighbour normalised edit distance per site, ties broken at random; (c) exact copies at each site (S366 reuse). Calibration: leave-one-out assignment of all 4,396 home texts of >= 2 signs (object removed; and all copies of the text removed). Null for the foreign set: 1,000 draws of 31 length-matched home texts from (i) the corpus mix and (ii) a uniform mix of the six named sites, read off their LOO assignments. Levels seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all; sign space W and M (bridge_extended + loop27 + S-DARK-27 proposals); home population with and without stock-text copies collapsed | **Power: LOO accuracy 0.42 (object removed) vs 0.22 chance, 0.27 vs 0.18 with all copies removed (stock texts carry a third of the site signal); recall MD 0.33, Harappa 0.54, Lothal 0.37, Chanhu-daro 0.40, Kalibangan 0.23, Dholavira 0.16; precision is high only for MD (0.73) and Harappa (0.86), 0.04-0.13 for the small sites. NN accuracy 0.23-0.26 vs 0.14 (size-biased toward MD). Foreign assignments (seq_raw, W): Chanhu-daro 6, Harappa 6, Kalibangan 6, Dholavira 5, other-Indus 5, Mohenjo-daro 2, Lothal 1; top-site share 0.19 vs corpus-mix null 0.29 [0.19-0.42] (P(>=) = 0.998), top-2 0.39 vs 0.49 (P = 0.98): the foreign texts are MORE dispersed over home sites than home texts are. The one consistent per-site effect is a Mohenjo-daro deficit: 2/31 vs 5.8 expected [2-10], P_lo 0.044-0.052 at all three levels, 1/31 vs 4.9 (P_lo 0.025-0.032) with stock texts collapsed. Kalibangan excess 6 vs 3.0 (P_hi 0.07, W) rises to 8 vs 2.8 (P_hi 0.004) in M space but falls to 5 vs 4.9 (P 0.58) once stock-text copies are collapsed (W): it is the eight K-69..75/K-156 copies of the twins text that make START-W91 look Kalibangan-specific; in M space with dedup 8 vs 4.7 (P 0.08), driven by the proposed bridge entries. LR margin (best - second) foreign 1.02 vs length-matched home 1.45 [0.97-2.05], P_lo 0.05: foreign texts are assigned less confidently. LR and NN agree on 7-8 of 31. Exact copies at home: 0 of 31 in W space; 3 in M space, all 2-sign texts (Kish 240-643 = MD 240-636, Ur 90-405 = MD 90-390, Ra's al-Junayz 4-405 = Banawali 4-390), where 47% of home 2-sign texts also have a cross-site twin. By shape: round seals abroad (17) go Harappa 5, Kalibangan 3, Dholavira 3, Chanhu-daro 3, other 3, MD 0; square seals abroad (7) one each to seven labels | **The foreign-found texts do not trace to a home city. No site receives more of them than chance at every level and in both sign spaces; the only robust signal is negative (Mohenjo-daro, the largest producer, gets 1-2 of 31 against 5-6 expected), and the Kalibangan pull is the S149 twins text itself. A port concentration (Lothal/Dholavira) is absent: Lothal 1-2 vs 3.5-4.9 expected. The method has real but modest power (0.42 LOO accuracy, mostly from MD/Harappa stock texts), so a true single-city origin would have shown as a top-site share above 0.29; it is 0.19. Consistent with S276 (foreign texts misfit the home grammar, not a particular site) and S-DARK-6 (no geography in the signs). Caveat: 31 texts, 76% of the home population is MD+H, and M-space results depend on S-DARK-27 proposals. |
