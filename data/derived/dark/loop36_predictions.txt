Frozen: 2026-10-03, before any new text was transcribed or any statistic on the new set was computed.
Source of each prediction: GRAMMAR.md and the S-DARK rows named. Sign numbers are Wells (W) numbers; IM77 Mahadevan (M)
equivalents via data/derived/bridge_extended.json where given.

Scoring rules (fixed now):
- Test set = texts transcribed in loop36_newfinds.csv that pass the corpus-overlap check (no exact, reverse or edit-distance-1 match
  to any text at any site in inscriptions.csv or, via the bridge, in IM77), with >= 2 signs read at confidence >= B.
- Null 1 (as S-DARK-21.1): within-text shuffle of the new texts, 2,000 reps; P = share of reps with a statistic at least as extreme.
- Null 2 (as S-DARK-21.1 / S312): a bigram model fitted on the home corpus (seq_raw) generating length-matched synthetic texts, 2,000 reps,
  used for the rates that a within-text shuffle cannot test (unseen-sign share, nesting, reuse).
- Zero counts are reported as upper bounds by the rule of three: 0 of n means < 3/n (95%).
- A prediction PASSES when the observed statistic lies on the predicted side of the shuffle null at P < 0.05 (or, for 'rate' predictions,
  within the stated band); it is UNDERPOWERED when n is below the stated minimum; it FAILS when the observed value crosses the kill line.
