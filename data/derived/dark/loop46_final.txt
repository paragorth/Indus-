Transcription-robust distinctions (S-DARK-24 + loop24 alignments): 390|405/406 (M162|M169), 803|806 (M387|M389), 151|156 (M12|M15); the other Wells classes (705/706, 861/817, 154/158, 630/632/636, 717/711, 824/877) are drawn as one sign by Mahadevan.

ANSWERS
1. Do hands exist? Not detectably. Variant co-selection across sign pairs within texts is at its null at every merge level and under city-dialect, period and find-area nulls (z +0.2 to +0.9; 0-1 of 7 pairs beyond own null; deduplicated the same). Planted two-hand structure at a 70/30 vs 30/70 split is caught 25 of 30 times, so the corpus could have shown moderate hands and does not; weaker hands (60/40 vs 40/60) are below power. Only 229-268 texts carry two variable signs.
2. How many hands, how local? Not answerable from Wells numbers: the latent-class model fails its positive control (cannot recover the Harappa/Mohenjo-daro form split from 244 two-label texts), picks K = 1 at Mohenjo-daro and K = 2 only for Harappa seals (44 texts, P 0.02, 1 of 7 pools). Forced hands are not local in area, room, period or material. They align with the closer (last sign) because the form is set by the adjacent sign (below), not because scribes were office-bound. Office-bound vs commercial: no evidence either way from hands; the earlier result stands that the text layer is a city-wide standard (S-DARK-14).
3. Stock texts: Harappa incised tablet copies of one text agree on every form 0.934 vs 0.557 expected (moulded 0.989, one mould), so a stock text was cut in one batch by one hand or master (fits S-DARK-12.4 batch deposition). Seals carrying a stock text do not: same-city seal twins agree 0.625 vs 0.535 (P 0.12). Different-city copies follow the city dialect (0.62 vs 0.50), so the form is not part of the spelling across cities. Seal-tag twins: the form filter excludes 7 of 15 candidate seals (K-82 is not from the 817 seals; C-87 405-740 is not from M-1669; the ten Lothal sealings fit M-895) and contradicts no accepted match.
4. IM77: Mahadevan's distinctions give the same picture. Co-selection exists for one pair only, M387|389 x M162|169 (P 0.001, also at dedup), the same pair Wells shows at P 0.036: 389/806 opens texts that close with 249-169-342 (590-405-740), 387/803 opens texts ending in the free tree 162/390. It survives the context null because it is a formula collocation of two Mahadevan-separate signs, i.e. grammar, not a hand.
5. The real finding (cycle 5): the form is chosen by the neighbouring sign in 7 of 8 Wells classes and 7 of 8 Mahadevan sets (P <= 0.037): W405 after W501 29/29, W711 after W335 18/18 and before W33 15/15, W806 before W154 35/35, W817 never after W3 (0/29); M103 only before M403 (27/27 vs 0/116), M100 never after M267 (0/291), M98 after M99 15/15, M169 after M407 26/26, M12 after M2 11/11. These 'variants' are contextual allographs, bound forms or distinct signs; a carver's free choice would not look like this.

CONSEQUENCES
- Any future hand or workshop test on Wells variant numbers must use the neighbour-context null (cycle 5); S-DARK-14.2's 'variant follows site' and S-DARK-20's clock were run without it (their negatives stand; a positive would not have).
- Merge list: 717/711 and 803/806 are context-split and 803/806 collocates differently (S-DARK-24.2): keep both at probable, not strong; 405 after 501/590 is a candidate bound form of the tree; 151/156 stays separate (Mahadevan M12/M15 and M12 after M2 11/11).
- WORKING-DICTIONARY candidates (C): 806-154 (35/35) and 103-403 (27/27) as fixed compounds; 335-711-33 as one term.
- The question 'who cut the seals' needs letterform measurement from photographs (stroke order, proportions), not the transcribers' sign numbers, which encode spelling, not handwriting.

CAVEATS
Rests on Mohenjo-daro + Harappa (76% of texts); tablet-copy agreement may be inflated if Wells transcribed duplicate tablets from one reading (CSV 'ref:' rows); Wells and IM77 are ~70% the same objects, so the agreement between them is transcription-robust, not replicated; 'null' means below about 3/n at the stated n; results do not depend on a merge level except that the only hint (390x803) is P 0.036 at strong and P 0.18 once 838 joins 803.
