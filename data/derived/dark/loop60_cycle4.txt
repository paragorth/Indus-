S-DARK-60 cycle 4: pairwise functional distinctness of the heads (which heads are functionally indistinguishable), Wells at three merge levels + IM77
Script: tools/dark_loop60_pairs.py <seq_raw|seq_strong|seq_all|im77> 5000; detail in loop60_c4_seq_raw.txt / _seq_strong.txt / _seq_all.txt / _im77.txt; every pair in loop60_pairs.csv (630 rows: level, profile, pair, n, chi, p, balanced accuracy, verdict, drivers).
Data: Wells complete texts of >= 2 signs from data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (older inscriptions.csv build; S-DARK-23); IM77 texts without breaks, M numbers through the bridge (M15 lumps W154/156/158; M245 = W615/617 and M66 = W236 are proposed bridge entries, S-DARK-27). Heads with >= 10 texts: 13 in Wells (14 at seq_all, where W90/91 reaches 11), 12 in IM77; the marked jar (7 texts of >= 2 signs) is excluded.
Why a new cycle: the cycle-3 pairwise block ran 400 permutations per pair (smallest p 0.0025 > Bonferroni alpha 0.0009) and merged by single linkage, so '1 group of 11' was an artefact.

METHOD
- Two profiles per text: FULL (object type fine, site group + the grammar dimensions) and GRAMMAR-ONLY (opener, connective W2/W60, suffix, numeral epithet before the head, any count, fish word, quantity phrase, middle-length bin, second unit). Where 'none', W400-only or W90-only is in a pair, suffix and epithet are dropped (definitional).
- Statistic: summed chi-square across dimensions; null = labels shuffled between the two heads WITHIN LENGTH-BIN, 5,000x (min p 0.0002); Bonferroni over all pairs (alpha 0.00055-0.00076).
- Effect size: leave-one-out naive-Bayes balanced accuracy (BA) with its own length-preserving permutation null (300x); normalised excess = (BA - null)/(1 - null).
- Practical bar: the 95th percentile of normalised excess over 80 split-half pseudo-pairs (jar, arrow, W700, none split at random, n = 15-60 a side): 0.24-0.31.
- A pair is DISTINCT only if p < Bonferroni alpha AND excess >= bar. Number of functionally distinct heads = size of the largest set of MUTUALLY distinct heads (maximum clique; no chaining).
- Controls: split-half pseudo-pairs reach p < 0.05 in 4-7% (nominal 5%) and are never DISTINCT. Allograph pairs (seq_raw): W154 vs W158 (S262 seal/tablet forms of one sign) grammar p 0.0096, excess 0.21 -> INDISTINCT (full profile separates them only by medium: type chi 23, p 0.001, still not Bonferroni); W526 vs W527 grammar p 0.42, excess 0.05 -> INDISTINCT. The test does not split one sign into two.

RESULTS
1. Distinct pairs. Wells full profile 57/78 (raw), 56/78 (strong), 60/91 (all); grammar-only 49, 48, 49. IM77 full 50/66, grammar 45/66.
2. Largest set of mutually distinct heads:
   heads with >= 40 texts:  Wells full 6 / 5 / 6 (raw/strong/all), grammar 4 / 4 / 4; IM77 full 5, grammar 4.
   all heads >= 10 texts:   Wells full 7 / 6 / 7, grammar 5 / 5 / 5; IM77 full 7, grammar 7 (IM77 counts W615/617 apart only through the doubled M245, see 5).
   Every grammar maximum set holds one head from each of four groups: {jar, W151, box527 (or 'none')}, {arrow}, {W156, W154/158}, {W700}; IM77 grammar's 4-set is {arrow, W151, W156+154/158, W700}.
3. Pairs indistinguishable in grammar at all three Wells levels AND in IM77 (or lumped there):
   jar ~ W151 (Wells p 0.26, excess +0.06; IM77 p 0.08, +0.06); jar ~ box527 (Wells size-only, excess +0.35 at p 0.014; IM77 p 0.08) and W151 ~ box527 (IM77 p 0.11, excess -0.03); W156 ~ W154/158 (p 0.33, excess +0.13; IM77 lumps them as M15); jar ~ W400-only, W151 ~ W400-only, W154/158 ~ W400-only (W400-only texts are headed texts with the head left off).
   Statistically different but below the practical bar everywhere ('stat-only'): jar vs none (p 0.0002, excess +0.10-0.16), arrow vs box527 (+0.10-0.15), W151 vs none.
4. Separated from every other head in every run: W700 (excess >= 0.38 against every head; only W595, 10 texts, and in IM77's full profile W400-only, +0.27, fall short; drivers numeral epithet, middle length, type) and the arrow (vs jar excess +0.52 in both corpora; drivers T3 epithet, fish, no suffix), except arrow ~ box527 (stat-only).
5. W615/617: indistinct in grammar from jar, W151 and W400-only in Wells (all levels; full profile separates it from the jar only by copper tablets/bar seals); IM77 separates it from everything only by 'second unit' (M245 written twice = medial head token), the transcription artefact flagged in S-DARK-24. Read as a medium-specialised member of the jar group.
6. Small heads (n 10-20). W236 (14) is distinct in grammar from every head except W226 (Wells size-only, IM77 distinct): connective 50%, empty middles, W400 after it. W226 (20) is not separable from W156/W154-158 or W236 in Wells grammar (size-only, p 0.002-0.009). W595 (10) and W90/91 (11) cannot be separated from most heads: underpowered (a 10-text head needs excess >= 0.4 AND p < 0.0006), not shown to be the same.
7. Full vs grammar: adding object type and site raises the count by 1-2 (W156 vs W154/158 split by incised vs moulded/bar; jar vs W615/617 by copper; W151 vs box527 at seq_raw), i.e. some heads that share a grammar are kept apart only by the medium or city they are written in.
