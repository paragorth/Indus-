# S-DARK-45 cycle 2: block structure of element x head and element x site (tools/dark_loop45.py 2 LEVEL NPERM)
Logs: loop45_c2_seq_raw.txt (40 permutations; P floor 0.024) / _seq_strong.txt / _seq_all.txt (20 each; floor 0.048) + .json.
Permutation counts were cut from the planned 200 because the machine is shared by ten loops (load average 35 on 4 cores); the
statistics of interest (gain ratio, purity vs null) are stable across the three levels and the smoke run at 10, so this does not change a verdict.
Method: count matrix of non-adjacent middle elements (>= 5 tokens) x labels (>= 20 texts); spectral co-clustering (sklearn SpectralCoclustering,
k = 2..8, k chosen by the largest gain of 'diagonal concentration' = share of tokens inside blocks where element cluster = label cluster, over its
independence expectation); element purity = share of an element's tokens under the labels of its own block; null = labels permuted among texts within
length x type (same k, re-clustered). Controls through the same code: Ur III seal legends (one per distinct legend; owner-name SYLLABLES x profession
title, and whole NAME x profession; 20 titles with >= 20 legends) and Proto-Elamite entry lines (non-final signs x final numeral class, 11 classes).
