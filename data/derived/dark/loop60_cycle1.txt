S-DARK-60 cycle 1: object-type profile of every head (closer-slot sign), carriers, seal<->impression match, moulded/incised split
Script: tools/dark_loop60.py 1 <level> 1000; detail in loop60_c1_seq_raw.txt / _seq_strong.txt / _seq_all.txt
Data: data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (built from the older inscriptions.csv, 5,369 rows; see S-DARK-23) joined row by row to data/raw/inscriptions.csv for type sub-codes, size, area, period; complete texts (3,538) plus 87 end-intact sealing fragments (text begins with '+' on the reading-end side), 3,625 texts in all.
Heads: jar W740 (1,222), marked jar W741/742/745 (9 final), arrow W520 (230), W151 (65), W156 (87), W154/158 (65), box W527/526 (57), W226 (24), W615/617 (44), W236 (15), W700 (367), W595 (11), suffix-only endings W400 (84) and W90/91 (8), none (1,337).
Null: head labels permuted among texts within site x length-bin, 1,000x. Identical at all three merge levels (no merge touches a head except 158->154 and 526->527, which are grouped here anyway).

RESULTS (seq_raw; strong/all identical to the unit)
1. Object type (coarse). Three heads are medium-bound: W700 is 96% tablets (352/367; seals 9 vs 37.5 expected, pots 5 vs 22, p<0.001); W595 is 91% seals (10/11; p<0.001); W151 77% seals (50 vs 39.7; tablets 12 vs 19.4). Arrow 66% seals (151 vs 128, p<0.001; tablets depleted), box527 74% seals (42 vs 35; tablets 11 vs 19, p<0.001). The jar is slightly tablet-leaning (481 vs 452, p<0.001) and avoids bangles and 'misc' objects. W615/617 leans to tablets (21 vs 13) because of copper tablets. 'No head' is the pot/bangle/misc ending (pots 176 vs 145, bangles 37 vs 24, misc 53 vs 39; tablets 427 vs 474).
2. Object type (fine). Square vs bar seals split the heads: W154/158 is the bar-seal head (19 of 29 seals are bar seals vs 5.5 expected, p<0.001; square seals 10 vs 18.3) together with W615/617 (bar 9 vs 3.7) and W595 (bar 4 vs 0.9); W151 (0 bar vs 4.8), W156 (0 vs 4.0), arrow (11 vs 18.4) and the jar (90 vs 110) avoid bar seals. Copper tablets: W615/617 12 vs 3.9 (p<0.001); W151, box527, W400 and W154/158 have none (0 vs 2.2-3.7).
3. Moulded vs incised tablets (tablets only). W156 is incised (36 of 43, 84%, vs 20.6 expected, p<0.001; moulded 7 vs 22.4). W154/158 is moulded (25 of 31, 81%, vs 15.5, p<0.001). Arrow moulded 58 vs 49.6 (p<0.05), W400-only moulded 35 vs 27 (p<0.05), the jar slightly incised (148 vs 134.5). W700 splits 51/49 exactly at null: the voucher unit is written on both techniques.
4. Sealings and carriers. Heads on the 165 end-intact sealing texts: jar 69, arrow 11, W156 3, W700 3, W236 2, none 61; W151, W154/158, box527, W226, W615/617, W595 are never impressed on a sealing (0 of 165 each; upper bound < 2%). TAG sub-codes (TAG plain, L, P, B = bale (S330), W, O, R, C, PC) show no head preference: the jar is on bales (10), plain tags (31), L (16), P (8) at the expected rates; the only cell at p<0.05 is W156 on 3 'L' tags (expected 1.0). Lothal back fastenings (Frenez & Tosi 2005; 55 matched): the jar is on wooden boxes (14), structures (7), pottery (3), parallel canes (2), leather sacks, lockers, pegs, woven straw, all at expected rates; no head predicts what was sealed (no cell at p<0.05). Heads do not choose the carrier.
5. Seal <-> impression matching (1,520 distinct complete seal texts of >= 2 signs; a match = an identical complete sealing or an end-intact sealing fragment of >= 3 signs that is the seal text's end). Jar 45/622 = 7.2% (exp 5.8%, p<0.05); arrow 17/139 = 12.2% (exp 6.4%, p<0.01); none 26/526 = 4.9%; W151 0/46, W154/158 0/26, box527 1/40, W226 0/15, W615/617 1/19, W595 0/10, W400 0/29 (each < 7% as an upper bound; W151 0 vs 2.8 expected, p ~ 0.05). Of all head groups, only jar- and arrow-headed seal texts are found impressed above chance; the seal-only heads W151/box527/W154-158 are not found impressed at all. Ghost rate on sealings is the same for every head (jar 7/40, arrow 2/10, none 10/36 unmatched; null).
6. Tablet matching: jar-headed seal texts recur on tablets 23/622 (vs 13 expected, p<0.01); no-head seal texts almost never (8/526 vs 20, p<0.001). W156 4/27 (vs 1.9, ns), W615/617 2/19.
7. One-sign texts: only 'none' (19%) and the marked jar / W226 / W236 (1-3 cases) appear alone; the heads are not stand-alone marks (S-DARK-58 covers these).
