Plain words (base model, 20,000 simulations): the posterior describes an institution with a stock of a few 10^4 to 10^5 distinct
credentials at the largest city, ~10^2 office classes under a Zipf law, a few to a few tens of commodity classes shown on
30-40% of credentials, an opener on 10-25%, an office word on about half or more, and a middle of ~2.5 signs on ~70% of
credentials; tablets are mostly (> 55%, probably ~70-90%) re-issues of local credentials with a popularity slope of 1-1.5.
The number of authorities, credentials per holder, name-vs-qualification, batch deposit and seal travel are not recoverable
from this corpus with these statistics. Nothing here depends on the merge level.
