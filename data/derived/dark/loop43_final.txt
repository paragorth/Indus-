Verdict: BORN COMPLETE, as far as the dated record reaches (Mature Harappan 3B, c. 2600 BC onward), with the count/unit layer older than that.
  1. Every frame rule is at its all-period strength in the earliest measurable phase: MD Early seals opener 0.26 (all 0.24), closer 0.68 (0.67), jar 0.47 (0.39), W2 in slot 2 0.69 (0.65), repeat 0.04 (0.05), partial-order agreement 0.97 (0.94); Harappa Stratum VI-VII tablets and HARP 3B tablets within 0.05 of their series means. 214 trend arrows x 3 levels: 19-20 at P < 0.05 (10.7 expected), all text length at Harappa (S49 package), MD seal complexity (+0.10, opposite sign at Harappa), and two local effects (DK-G deep two-sided tablets ending in title signs; Trench 11 doubled-numeral stock texts). No frame rule strengthens in a way that another site reproduces. Direction votes for opener/closer/jar/W2/repeat/order are 6-17 of 22 either way, mean rho within +/-0.04.
  2. A code 70-80% formed at the earliest phase would have been detected (closer and order rules: 26-40 of 40 simulated runs in the seal series and Harappa tablets at ramp 0.2-0.3); only growth under about 10%, or growth confined to the opener on tablets, escapes this power.
  3. Inventory (distinct, Chao1, rarefied richness), closer-menu composition and the repeat ban do not move across phases: 0-2 of 90 arrows per level, none within one object class at seq_raw. MD Early seals draw 71 distinct signs from 148 tokens, the same rarefied richness as every later level, and already end in jar / arrow / tree.
  4. Pottery marks before the seals (Harappa Kot Diji) are tall numerals (41%), W700 and the jar (22%) and a few rare signs; openers 0 of 39 Harappa tokens (< 0.08), but Mature pots also lack them (2%), so the medium, not the date, explains the zero. W700 (6/41 vs 6/265, P 0.002) and tall-1 (P 0.001) are over-represented early, and one Period 2 pot reads 1 + W700, the later Harappa voucher form. The count/unit vocabulary is therefore older than the seal frame in the record; whether the frame itself is older cannot be tested from pots.

Dependence: 76% of dated texts are Harappa + Mohenjo-daro; the pre-Mature evidence is 39 of 41 tokens from Harappa alone; Dholavira / Kalibangan / Lothal phases are too small to test. All zeros are upper bounds (< 3/n). Results identical at seq_raw, seq_strong, seq_all. Would overturn 'born complete': Early Harappan (Kot Diji / Sothi) seals or tablets with the frame missing or at half strength, e.g. Kunal, Kalibangan I, Harappa Period 2 sealings.
