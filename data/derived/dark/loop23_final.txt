- (e) Coverage: 94 of 629 pots have any dimension; 27 are stamp-impression sizes (the seal, not the pot), 61 are sherd sizes; only 4 complete graffito vessels have a dimension, none with a lone numeral. Wall thickness exists for 52 sherds (48 Kalibangan). Shape is unrecorded for 612 pots.
- (a) Numeral value vs thickness: n = 1 (lone) / 4 (numeral-first), p = 0.84. The one nominal hit (vs sherd H, rho 0.41, n 9, p 0.035) is 1 of 18 arrows and sherd H is the break, not the vessel. Jar/tree/fish alone vs size: 0-2 pots each. Null by lack of data.
- (b) Harappa stamped pots are one Trench 43 / Period 3 lot as a class, so W440 is not special within it (p 0.59); W440 impressions are one size (sd 1.1 mm, p 0.006, 0.04 corrected), consistent with one die. New grade C: pre-firing potters' marks are opener signs (8/19 carry 820/861/920 vs 5% of graffiti, p 0.008, 0.056 corrected), at six small sites; not replicated at Harappa/Kalibangan (0/3).
- (c) Short pot texts are exactly as frameless as length-matched seal texts (0.03 vs 0.03 in every region). Multi-sign texts sit on complete museum vessels (p 0.001), one-sign numerals on Harappa Period 3 HARP sherds: a collection difference, not two pot forms.
- (d) Proto-Elamite in words: there the capacity-vs-counted split is carried by the numeral SYSTEM (N39 capacity codes vs N1 discrete) attached to specific commodity signs (M297, M002 measured; M263, M346 counted). The Indus analogue is the tall/short split (S204): tall before W700/W520/jar, short before trees/fish. Lone pot numerals are 70% tall at Harappa. That is consistent with 'tall = measured' but equally with 'tall = lot count'; without vessel volumes the direction cannot be fixed.
