S-DARK-58 cycle 2: the minimal text, part 2 (do 2-sign texts obey the frame; recurring pairs; impressions). Script
tools/dark_loop58.py 2 <level> 1000. Full tables: loop58_c2_seq_raw.txt / _seq_strong.txt / _seq_all.txt (+ .json).

Frame order = role rank of sign 1 <= role rank of sign 2 (opener < marker < numeral < middle < title < closer < suffix).
Attested = the pair occurs as an adjacent bigram in the complete texts of >= 4 signs. Nulls: random pairs drawn by overall
frequency and by long-text frequency (1,000x); control: the observed pairs read backwards.
Impressions: exact-text match of each clean seal text with sealing (TAG) texts and tablet texts at the same site and anywhere,
by seal text length; same-site rate restricted to the five sites with >= 5 distinct sealing texts (Lothal, Dholavira,
Kalibangan, Mohenjo-daro, Harappa). Wilson 95% intervals.
