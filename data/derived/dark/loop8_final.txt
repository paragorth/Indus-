Question. Does any non-standard reading order (position permutation, line 2 first, boustrophedon, column-wise, emblem-as-first-token, or a plain flip of some class of texts) beat the established right-to-left / first-read-first order after correction, and by how much?

Answer. No. The standard stored order wins or ties every comparison once the multiple-template null is applied, in seq_raw, seq_strong and seq_all alike. Quantitatively:
- Permutation templates (8.1): best non-standard template gains 0.0-0.4 nats/token over the standard order on held-out sites, below the shuffled-corpus max gain (0.18-0.54); standard order is rank 1 at L = 3-5. Ur III control recovers its known order at rank 1 for L = 4-7. Slot-entropy fails the positive control (dropped).
- Two-line (8.2): IM77 line 1 -> 2 beats 2 -> 1 by 0.79 nats/token (89 sides; 0.29 with CI -0.52..-0.06 on 52 sides with both lines >= 2); Wells '/' segments tie in either order (0.04, inside null) -> the Wells '/' does not record line order. Boustrophedon costs 0.4-0.9, column-wise 0.6-0.9 nats in both transcriptions. Ur III control: known order wins by 1.33.
- Emblem first (8.3): prepending the animal costs 0.11-0.12 nats, same as a shuffled label (P 0.47-0.56). Emblem x first sign MI = null; emblem x last sign MI 0.05 bits above the null 95th (weak closer-side lead, mostly unicorn-vs-rest).
- Per-text flip (8.4): R/L, L/R, T/B and BUS texts read better as stored in 89-100% of cases (mean 2.5-3.5 nats/token). The L = 6-7 'reverse wins' of 8.1 is a smoothing artefact (per text 0-6% reversed).

One correction worth applying. The 92 texts with no recorded direction (dir = '-') read better reversed 30% of the time vs 10% baseline (Fisher P = 2e-6), i.e. about a quarter (20-25 texts: pots, seals, a few tablets at MD, HP, Lothal, Chanhu-daro) are probably stored backwards. Treat dir = '-' as direction-uncertain in positional tests; strongest candidates for a photo check: C-88, C-35 (167-705-31), H-2085 (824-892-413), M-2062 (46-31-32-741), H-170 (827-336-630-511). Pottery graffiti are the least directional class even when a direction is recorded (21% reverse-better).

Caveats. Held-out = non-MD/HP sites (24% of texts), so 'held-out' here is a site split of one corpus, not new material. Wells and IM77 agreement is transcription-robust, not replicated. All nulls are shuffle/bootstrap; the Ur III control is the only known-answer check and it passed for every criterion kept.
