LOOP 6 - CYCLE 4: whole-vocabulary distance vs geographic distance (Mantel)
Script: data/derived/dark/loop6_c4_profile.py; full output loop6_c4_profile.txt
Site sign-frequency profiles over signs with >= 10 tokens (167 all objects; 120 seals). Distance between sites = Jensen-Shannon
divergence (bits). (a) the 6 sites with >= 50 texts, equal 50-text subsamples averaged over 200 draws, EXACT permutation of site
labels (6! = 720) of the geographic matrix (great-circle km, coast-distance difference, river change). (b) all 20 sites, JSD minus
each site's own bootstrap resampling JSD (sample-size correction), 20,000 label permutations. (c) seals only. (d) without MD + HP.
(e) nearest-neighbour test: is each site's vocabulary-nearest site its geographically nearest site?
12 Mantel arrows fired in total (3 variables x 2 object passes x 2 designs).

RESULT:
(a) all objects, 6 big sites: JSD ~ km rho = +0.54, exact p = 0.017; JSD ~ coast-distance difference rho = +0.53, p = 0.013;
    river change p = 0.15. Without MD+HP (4 sites, 24 perms): rho +0.83, p = 0.17 (floor 0.083).
    SEALS ONLY, same 6 sites: JSD ~ km rho = +0.10, p = 0.69; coast p = 0.56; river p = 0.13. The all-objects signal is gone.
    The JSD matrix shows why: with all objects Harappa (tablets) and Kalibangan are far from everyone; on seals the matrix is flat
    (0.23-0.32 bits everywhere, MD closest to all). The 'gradient' is the object-type mix of each site, not the script.
(b) all 20 sites, corrected JSD: km rho = -0.05 (p 0.56), coast -0.04, river -0.02; without MD+HP -0.08 (p 0.38).
    Seals, 13 sites: km +0.10 (p 0.32), coast +0.05, river change +0.22 (p = 0.012; BH q over 12 arrows = 0.07; without MD+HP km p 0.26).
(e) vocabulary-nearest site = geographically nearest for 0 of 20 sites (null mean 1.05); seals 1 of 13 (null 1.1).
    Vocabulary neighbours are random with respect to the map: Allahdino->Rupar, Bala-kot->Bhirrana, Gola Dhoro->Rakhigarhi,
    Kot-Diji->Nausharo; on seals Mohenjo-daro<->Harappa, Dholavira<->Lothal are the only sensible pairs and both are the big-n pairs.
Over the 12 arrows, BH min q = 0.067 (the three p < 0.02 are one all-objects 6-site design that vanishes on seals, and one seals
river-change arrow with no km or coast counterpart). Nothing passes at q < 0.05 and nothing replicates across object passes.
