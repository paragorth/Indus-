VERDICT. On seals the Indus system is as uniform across cities as one generator sampled twice: on the perfectly-central anchor,
below Ur III (one dynasty, one chancery formula, yet 1.5-2x divergence, 2-7x in the title menu, 20x in legend reuse) and Linear B.
The components other systems localise (title / commodity menu, whole-text stock) are the ones Indus holds constant or even shares
across cities. Who issued the texts: one menu, one inventory, one convention supplied the seal credentials in every city and at the
small sites; local scribes show only in the tablets (vouchers). Not 'cracked': nothing read; this bounds the administrative model.
Caveats: cross-system JSD ratios depend on inventory size (anchors are the exact calibration); IM77 = same objects read twice;
MD + H carry the n = 200 results; a <= 10% unigram excess at n = 25-90 is near the detection limit.
