PLAIN WORDS. With a mould mode the credential-institution model reproduces the tablet corpus as well as the seal corpus: 32 of
34 statistics, the two base-model failures (too few mass-copied tablet texts, tablet stock not local enough) repaired by
m_mould ~ 8 (3.5-15) tablets per mould with ~55-60% (42-75%) of tablets being re-issues of local credentials. The real Harappa
moulded series run to 89-120 copies of one face (cycle 3): a Poisson run of mean 8 does not produce such tails, so m_mould is a
mean over a heavy-tailed mixture, and the remaining miss on topcopy_share (the model's top text is now too common in relative
terms) is the price of fitting the bulk with one Poisson mean. Everything the base model said about the institution stands:
credential stock at the largest city 10^4.5-10^5 (adjusted 39,000-194,000; rejection 6,300-164,000), office classes of order
10^1.5-10^2 under a Zipf slope ~1-1.6, a few to a few tens of commodity classes on 30-45% of credentials, an opener on 10-25%,
an office word on ~half, a holder-specific middle of ~3 signs on ~70% of credentials from a vocabulary of several hundred signs.
Not identifiable at this corpus size (controls c, d, and cycles 2 and 4): the number of issuing authorities A (1-8 flat in every
fit and missed in half the planted worlds), the split of the credential stock into holders x credentials per holder (r = -0.52
ridge), whether the middle is a holder's NAME shared across his credentials or a per-credential qualification (p_share flat,
the 'names' world with p_share 0.95 recovers 0.72 [0.22, 0.98]), batch deposit of tablets, and (69 complete sealings) seal travel.
Caveats as in cycles 1-4: skeleton from the older inscriptions.csv build (S-DARK-23), 76% Harappa + Mohenjo-daro, tablets almost
all Harappa so m_mould and p_reissue describe Harappa; nothing here reads a sign.
