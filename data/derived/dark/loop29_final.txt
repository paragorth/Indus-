S-DARK-29 THE SAME-HOLDER TEST - final summary (loop 29, 3 Oct 2026)
Script: tools/dark_loop29.py <cycle 1-4> <seq_raw|seq_strong|seq_all> 1000. Per-cycle rows: loop29_cycle1.txt ... loop29_cycle4.txt (paste into STRATEGIES.md before "## Summary"). Raw tables: loop29_c{1,2,3,4}_{seq_raw,seq_strong,seq_all}.txt.
Data: data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (5,369 rows; complete texts >= 2 signs; 3,074 seals + tablets/sealings; area-section, block-house, room-grid, time) joined to data/raw/inscriptions.csv by CISI for depth (1,688 objects). Caution (CLAUDE.md): the canonical JSON predates the current CSV; counts here are from the JSON.

Question. Credential model (holder in the middle, grade in the closer) predicts that two seals with the same middle and different closers were one person's or firm's and should be found together. Name-text model predicts the same for identical middles. Stock-text/workshop model predicts kinship follows material and emblem, not deposition.

Results (all three merge levels agree):
1. Cycle 1 - kinship vs proximity, matched random pairs. Same-middle-different-closer seal pairs: Mohenjo-daro 18-24 (same area 2/17-23 vs 0.17-0.19 expected; same block 0/13-16, < 19%; same room 0/6-9; depth 6.6 vs 6.1 ft), Harappa 2 (0/1). Minimal pairs (193-258) and shared closers (3,000) equally unclustered (same room 0/117-159 and 0-1/1,450). Identical seal texts: 1.5x same area at Mohenjo-daro (P 0.02-0.03) but 0/40-50 same block, 0/32-40 same room. Tablets with identical texts cluster by square/block/depth/period at both cities.
2. Cycle 2 - co-deposited sets. Mohenjo-daro 218 same-room seal pairs: 0 share a middle, 0 identical, 0 minimal pairs, closer 1.1x, Jaccard 1.1x (P 0.13-0.26). Harappa 45 same-square seal pairs: same closer unit 6/45 (4.5-4.9x, P <= 0.008), identical 2/45, same middle 0/45 (< 6.7%). Harappa same-square tablets: identical 8x, same middle 37x, minimal pair 5x (all P 0.001); the one tablet 'holder' pair is noise (1/851). Seals and tablets in one room: 0/35 and 3/245 identical, 0 shared middles.
3. Cycle 3 - production control (null matched on material x emblem too). Holder classes stay null under both nulls. Identical seals share the emblem 0.84-0.86 vs 0.65 expected (series-cut stock), and their area-level excess survives (P 0.015-0.02) while block/room stay empty. Mohenjo-daro identical tablets lose all clustering when material + emblem are held (block P 0.001 -> 0.07; depth P 0.001 -> 0.33): workshop batches. Harappa identical tablets lose the square-level excess (P 0.001 -> 0.97-0.99) but keep area, period and depth (P <= 0.002): deposition of vouchers by area.
4. Cycle 4 - small sites and the direct test. Kalibangan, Chanhu-daro, Lothal, Dholavira: 0 same-middle-different-closer seal pairs (>= 2-sign middles) among 196 complete seals; same-room seals null except one Chanhu-daro duplicate (C-53 = C-58). Direct test over four sites with room data: 0 of 277 same-room seal pairs have the same middle and a different closer (< 1.1%; Mohenjo-daro 0/218, Harappa 0/45, Kalibangan 0/11, Chanhu-daro 0/3).

Verdict. NULL for the holder model. Textual kinship between seals does not predict where they were found; kinship that does cluster is copy-level (identical stock texts; one-sign tablet variants) and splits into a production part (same material + emblem; Mohenjo-daro copper tablets; Harappa square-level tablet batches) and a deposition part (Harappa tablet areas and periods; identical seal texts by area at Mohenjo-daro). The only slot that co-deposited seals share is the closer unit (Harappa, 4.5x), i.e. the office/title, not the middle. The 'one holder, several grades' prediction fails everywhere it can be read (0/277, < 1.1%). The middle still has no observable that behaves like an owner (with S321 and S-DARK-26); the closer behaves like a place-bound formula.
Caveats. Power: 18-24 holder pairs at Mohenjo-daro, 13-16 with a house block; room-level bounds alone exclude only > 33%. Rests on Mohenjo-daro + Harappa (the small sites have no holder pairs at all). Harappa 'room' is a trench grid square. Depth is a mix of ft and m converted. Zero counts are upper bounds (< 3/n); the corpus is a small sample of all seals ever made (S309), so absence of co-deposited holder pairs bounds the rate, it does not show that none existed.
Would revive: a newly excavated house yielding two seals with the same >= 2-sign middle and different closers (expected 0.03% of same-room pairs under the null; three such rooms would be decisive).
