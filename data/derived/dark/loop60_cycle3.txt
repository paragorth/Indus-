S-DARK-60 cycle 3: geography, Mohenjo-daro find areas, phases, emblem, material and seal size of every head
Script: tools/dark_loop60.py 3 <level> 1000; detail in loop60_c3_seq_raw.txt / _seq_strong.txt / _seq_all.txt; per-head shares in loop60_head_profiles.csv (all three levels).
Data: data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (older inscriptions.csv build, 5,369 rows; S-DARK-23) joined to data/raw/inscriptions.csv for area, period/phase, symbol, material and size; complete texts only (3,538).
Null: head labels permuted among texts within type x length-bin (site shares) or site x type x length-bin (areas, phases, emblem, material, size), 1,000x.
Levels: seq_raw and seq_strong identical to the unit; seq_all differs only in W400-only / W90-only / 'none' counts by 1-2 texts (W90/91 reaches n = 10-11 there) and changes no verdict.
NOTE: the pairwise-distinctness block at the end of loop60_c3_*.txt is INVALID and superseded by cycle 4: it ran 400 permutations per pair, so its smallest attainable p (0.0025) could never pass the Bonferroni alpha (0.0009), and every pair came out 'not distinct' by construction; it also used single-linkage merging, which chains any one weak pair into a single group.

RESULTS (seq_raw)
1. Sites (all objects). The two Harappa heads: W700 (360 vs 325 exp, p<0.001; the tablet unit), W154/158 (44 vs 32, p<0.001; on seals too: 14 of 29 seals at Harappa vs 4.9 exp). Two Mohenjo-daro heads: W615/617 (32 vs 18.8, Harappa 9 vs 18.9; p<0.001) and W226 (Harappa 4 vs 8.1, p<0.01). Box527 is over-represented at Lothal (7 vs 2.0, p<0.01; all seals). W151 at Chanhu-daro 4 vs 1.3. The jar is slightly Mohenjo-daro (575 vs 550) and under-represented abroad (4 vs 8.6); 'none' is the Kalibangan (70 vs 58), Chanhu-daro and foreign (16 vs 8.3) ending. Arrow, W156, W236 sit at their site expectations.
2. Mohenjo-daro find areas. W151 (18 vs 12.8) and W156 (17 vs 11.4) concentrate in DK-G South (p<0.05); W400-only in DK-G North (9 vs 3.3, p<0.01) and absent from HR (0 vs 4.1). Jar, arrow, box527, W615/617 at area expectation. No head is an area's private ending.
3. Phases. Jar Late/Str-V-VI slightly up (118 vs 108; 77 vs 65), W156 Mohenjo-daro Intermediate up (17 vs 11.4, p<0.01), arrow Harappa Str-III-IV up (18 vs 12.2), box527 Intermediate down (3 vs 6.8), W700 Str-V-VI up and III-IV down. Shifts are 1.2-1.6x; no head appears or disappears between phases.
4. Emblem on seals: head-blind (S-DARK-34 replicated): unicorn 75-100% under every head, all at expectation; only 'none' has more 'other' emblems (36 vs 29.9, p<0.05).
5. Material: W156 is steatite (66 vs 52.9, faience 5 vs 14.7; p<0.001), W154/158 clay (13 vs 8.7) and never copper, W226 steatite 15/16, W400-only faience (23 vs 16.7) and never copper (0 vs 3.8, p<0.001), 'none' copper (115 vs 106). Jar, arrow, W151, box527, W700, W615/617 at expectation.
6. Seal width: W156 seals are small (22.0 mm vs 24.5 null, p<0.01), W151 24.3 vs 25.7 and W400-only 24.0 vs 26.0 (ns), jar 27.9 vs 28.3 (p<0.05), 'none' 27.6 vs 26.9 (p<0.001); arrow, box527, W154/158, W226, W615/617 at expectation.
