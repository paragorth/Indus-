S-DARK-64 FINAL: do co-deposited seals share rare middle (name) elements? (4 cycles; rows in loop64_cycle1-4.txt, ready to paste before "## Summary"; 64.1 is already in STRATEGIES.md, 64.2-64.4 are not)
Scripts: tools/dark_loop64.py <1|2|3|4> <seq_raw|seq_strong|seq_all> [nperm]; tools/dark_loop64_site.py <level> (cycle 2b, Indus at Ur III city grain)
Logs: loop64_c{1,2,3,4}_{seq_raw,seq_strong,seq_all}.txt, loop64_c2b_site.txt
Data caution: Wells from merged-corpus-canonical.json (older CSV build, S-DARK-23); IM77 from data/im77/im77_corpus_lines.csv (transcription-robust, not independent, S312); Ur III from loop48_corpora/ur3_words.jsonl.

ANSWER
1. Co-deposited seals share middle elements slightly beyond chance at Mohenjo-daro (Wells rooms: 16-17/218 pairs vs 4.1-4.8%, 1.6-1.9x,
   P 0.01-0.02) and at IM77 Harappa / Chanhu-daro spots (1.4-1.7x), but ALWAYS in frequent or mid-frequency elements (W240 fish, W140,
   W368: the frame's title vocabulary). Rare-element sharing among seals is at chance everywhere: Wells Mohenjo-daro 0/218 (< 1.4%),
   IM77 Mohenjo-daro 3/9,853 (x0.86), Kalibangan, Dholavira, Chanhu-daro at or below null. Only tablets share rare elements (series).
2. No element 'surnames' (cycle 2): 18-19 local candidates vs 15-16 by chance, all but two in the largest dig area (DK-G(S)), none
   loyal to a non-dominant area, no positional fixity (edge share 0.67-0.69 vs 0.67). Positive control Ur III by city: +24.8 MI
   points and 88 place-loyal name words vs 2.7; Indus at the same city grain: +1.8-2.0 points and 0 loyal to a non-dominant site.
   Linear B: no find-spot data in DAMOS, not run.
3. Emblem (cycle 3): element-sharing same-room seals share the emblem LESS than chance (0.46-0.50 vs 0.61 permuted, P 0.84-0.91);
   rare-element pairs site-wide 0.57-0.61 vs 0.63. Consistent with S-DARK-34.2 (emblem not a function of text).
4. Replication (cycle 4): no third site reproduces the room excess (all underpowered, 2-289 pairs); IM77 Mohenjo-daro is flat at its
   coarser grain (dilution, not refutation). Merge levels agree within one pair, except two tablet results (Harappa tablet lineage
   candidates P 0.03 only at seq_raw; Mohenjo-daro elephant tablets P 0.001 only at seq_raw).

DEPENDENCE ON MOHENJO-DARO: the only positive seal result (64.1) is Mohenjo-daro; it holds 79% of Wells same-room seal pairs
(233/295) and 706 of 845 seal texts with an area. Everything else is upper bounds: Kalibangan room < 27%, Lothal < 75%.

WHAT IT DOES TO 'DESIGNATION = PERSONAL NAME'
- It removes the kin-structured version (patronymic, clan or household element): such names put shared material in RARE elements
  among co-resident seals, with place-loyal vocabulary (as Ur III shows at city grain). The Indus middles show neither.
- It does not refute single, unstructured personal names (one name per owner, no inherited part): that predicts no sharing at all,
  which is what the rare classes show. What co-resident seals DO share is the formula (title / fish phrase), as S-DARK-29 found for
  closers. So the designation reads as 'title formula + individual label', the label carrying no detectable family element.
- Sampling: the corpus is a small fraction of seals made (S309); a household line could exist outside the excavated rooms. Would
  revive the kin reading: >= 3 rooms of a non-dominant area at a new site sharing a rare element in a fixed slot.

VERDICT: rare-element co-deposit sharing NULL (bounds above); frequent-element sharing small and Mohenjo-daro-only (Wells), formula
not lineage. Personal-name reading survives only as unstructured individual names; kin-group / surname reading not supported.
Not 'cracked'; no new sign values proposed.
