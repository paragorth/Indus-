LOOP 36, CYCLE 1 (2026-10-03): where can a truly out-of-corpus test set come from, and what did the sweep find?
Scripts: tools/dark_loop36_match.py (corpus-overlap checker, Wells + IM77 via the bridge, S-DARK-21.1 wildcard cap).
Predictions frozen first: data/derived/dark/loop36_predictions.txt (written before any new object was looked at).

Source sweep (all open; every call logged here):
- Prior loops had already consumed: ASI Indian Archaeology - A Review 2001-02 to 2013-14 (S171: every published inscription is in
  ICIT/Wells), the Dholavira 2015 preliminary report (S172, S176: 225 seals + 60 sealings all already in the corpus), Nath's
  Rakhigarhi 1997-2000 report (S159, S162: graffiti drawings missing from the PDF; one lead ingot, unreadable), Farmana 2007-08
  (S247: in corpus), Laursen 2010 Gulf-type seals (S276/S278: 24 of 29 matched; nos. 6, 17, 20, 25 missing), Salut, Binjor,
  Tigrana, Kalba (S257/S258), Shinde & Willis 2014 copper plates (S170: unprovenanced), CDLI (S190).
- New this cycle:
  * Cleveland Museum of Art open-access API (openaccess-api.clevelandart.org): three inscribed Indus seals (1973.161, 1964.104,
    1973.160) with CC0 3400-px photographs. Dealer provenance (W. H. Wolff; MFA Boston exchange). Not among the corpus's 28
    'Unknown'-site texts.
  * Metropolitan Museum API (v1.1 search; v1 objects): 49.40.1-4 (Dodge Fund 1949), four inscribed seals, open-access photos
    (one at 2000/3783 px, three at ~350 px). Not in the corpus's Unknown set.
  * Victoria and Albert API: IS.8A-1951 script-only fragment (3 signs), IIIF image too small to read; O25161 is Mohenjo-daro (CISI).
  * Directorate General of Antiquities Sindh online exhibition (antiquities.sindhculture.gov.pk, 364 records; 9 seal records,
    3 with real photographs): Mohenjo-daro museum seals, most probably CISI objects.
  * archive.org: Gadd 1932 'Seals of ancient Indian style found at Ur' (in.gov.ignca.33779) downloaded; the OCR text gives
    museum numbers (no. 3 = BM 122946 = U.17342; no. 4 = BM 122188; no. 5 = Penn U.17341; no. 6 = BM 122947) but the plate
    pages of the scan are blank/black, so nos. 3 and 15 (known missing from the corpus) still cannot be read.
  * Wikimedia Commons (category pages fetched as HTML; the API and upload server returned HTTP 429 for every image download,
    even at 45-s spacing): identified but could not download BM 1932,0308.1 (bought in Baghdad by Gadd; Laursen's no. 23/24
    family), BM cylinder 1960,0718.1.1 (fragmentary, Indus signs), BM 1912,0629.1 ('north-west Pakistan'), a Harappa sherd.
  * Shinde & Willis 2014 (Ancient Asia 5, open access) PDF fetched from the journal's upload server; embedded figures at
    813-1201 px; plates 3 and 4 (bold two-row texts) partly readable, plate 1 (34 signs) not.
  * Chanhu-daro French mission (HAL): landing pages only; HAL and harappa.com images are behind anti-bot checks (Cloudflare
    'just a moment' / Anubis) in both curl and Chromium; harappa.com HTML pages load but image requests return 403.
  * Penn Museum Chanhu-daro seals (L-141-xx): pages load, no image URLs exposed; all are Mackay 1935-36 objects (CISI).
  * Web search for post-2015 excavated seals (Rakhigarhi 2013-16 and 2022-24, Dholavira, Kotada Bhadli, Lothal, Khirsara,
    Lakhan-jo-daro, Mohenjo-daro, Oman, Bahrain, Failaka al-Khidr, Konar Sandal): descriptions only (e.g. the Rakhigarhi
    cylinder seal 'five Harappan characters and an alligator'); no open photograph or drawing with legible signs.

Blind calibration of my own reading (needed before any 'NEW' verdict is trusted): Dholavira 2015 photo of acc. 2393 read
blind as 824-706-33-520 vs the corpus 233-706-33-520 (3 of 4 signs right; the fish/oval family is the confusion). Acc. 80 read as
400-465(+1 lost): no corpus match found, i.e. my 2-sign reading fails to find a text that IS in the corpus. So a single-reader
transcription from a photo has roughly 75% per-sign accuracy on good images, and a 'NEW' verdict for a 5-sign text has a
~25% chance of being a misreading even when the object is in the corpus.
