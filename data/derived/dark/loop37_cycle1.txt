Test A1 (frame position by recorded face; null = face labels permuted within object, 1,000x):
  opener-face is recorded face 1: 39/79 = 0.49 vs null 0.49 (P = 0.48); Harappa tablets 30/58 = 0.52 vs 0.50; seals 2/6; MD prisms 1/3.
  closer-face is recorded LAST face: 169/323 = 0.52 vs 0.49 (P = 0.17); complete faces only 110/196 = 0.56 vs 0.50 (P = 0.038); MD two-faced 25/36 = 0.69 vs 0.50 (P = 0.017, n = 36);
  Harappa tablets 136/260 = 0.52 (P = 0.24). So the recorded face order carries no opener information and at most a weak closer-last tendency at Mohenjo-daro.
Test A2 (one text => at most one closed face and one opener face; null = faces reassigned among objects of the same site x type within the subset, 1,000x; W700 count faces not counted as closed):
  all: objects with >= 2 closer-final faces 39 vs 107 expected (P < 0.001); >= 2 opener faces 6 vs 5.7 (P = 0.70).
  Harappa tablets 13 vs 79 (P < 0.001); Harappa tablets with no count face 11 vs 29 (P < 0.001); complete faces only 24 vs 79 (P < 0.001).
  Mohenjo-daro prisms 4 vs 4.1 (P = 0.68); MD two-faced 9 vs 9.7 (P = 0.45); seals 4 vs 4.4; sealings 11 vs 10.8; sites other than MD + Harappa 11 vs 11.2.
  The Harappa complementarity is the S93 voucher: of 582 two-faced Harappa tablets with exactly one closed face, the other face is a count face (tall N + W700) in 247,
  a single sign in 44, a broken face in 21 and a complete text in 73, and those 73 end in W700 14, W824 11, W690 10, W868 9, W861 6 (alternative endings, not frame closers).
Test A3 (concatenate faces in recorded order and parse: share of objects with a frame violation = an internal closer followed by a non-suffix sign, or a non-initial opener):
  observed 447/766 = 0.58; random same-site-same-type faces concatenated (model C) 0.60 (P_lo = 0.11); single-face complete texts of the same total length (model A) 0.19.
  MD prisms 0.83 vs C 0.81 vs A 0.37; Harappa tablets 0.60 vs 0.60 vs 0.17; seals 0.46 vs 0.73 vs 0.30 (P = 0.003, from the 1-closed-face structure, not from order); MD two-faced 0.45 vs 0.64 vs 0.25.
Test A4 (slot rank continues across the boundary: end slot of face i <= start slot of face i+1): 358/833 = 0.43 vs 0.41 with face order permuted (P = 0.07); MD prisms 20/30 = 0.67 vs 0.64 (P = 0.37).
All three merge levels agree within 1 object / 0.01 (seq_all: 39 vs 110; 0.59 vs 0.60 vs 0.19).

Verdict: arrow A is dead. A concatenated multi-sided object looks exactly like random faces glued together (0.58 vs 0.60), not like a single long text (0.19); openers sit on any face at chance.
The only inter-side structure is Harappa's 'one closed text + one unclosed field' (count or short label), i.e. the two-field voucher already in S93/S94, and it is absent at Mohenjo-daro, on seals and on sealings.
