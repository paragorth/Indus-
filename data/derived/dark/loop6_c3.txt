LOOP 6 - CYCLE 3: the shared-text network vs the map
Script: data/derived/dark/loop6_c3_network.py; full output loop6_c3_network.txt
Texts of >= 3 signs (site+text dedup; 2,347 on seq_raw, 2,323 on seq_strong). A link = two sites carrying the same exact text.
Sharing normalised by expected sharing n_i x n_j. Mantel test (Spearman over the 190 site pairs) against great-circle distance,
coast-distance difference and river change; null = site labels permuted (5,000x; the unit is the site). Also the linked-vs-unlinked
mean distance, the same-river share, the S324 seal -> sealing flow subset, and power by simulation.

RESULT (seq_raw): 52 texts shared by >= 2 sites, 74 links over 17 of the 190 site pairs. Harappa-MD 32, Lothal-MD 8, Chanhu-daro-MD 4
(130 km), Dholavira-Harappa 4 (794 km), Harappa-Kalibangan 4 (177 km), Kalibangan-MD 4, Harappa-Lothal 4 (904 km), ...
Mantel: normalised sharing ~ km rho = -0.03 (p 0.73); ~ coast difference -0.01 (p 0.92); ~ river change -0.07 (p 0.29); raw counts
~ km -0.03 (p 0.71). Restricted to the 9 sites with >= 1 link: km rho = -0.17 (p 0.37). Without MD + HP only 3 links remain
(p 0.67). Linked pairs mean 548 km vs unlinked 567 km, site-perm P = 0.40. Same-river share 0.35 vs 0.24 (n.s.).
seq_strong: 60 shared texts, 94 links, same numbers (km p 0.62; linked 539 vs 568 km, P 0.34).
S324 flow (sealing site <- seal site): Lothal <- MD 4, Kalibangan <- MD 1, Lothal <- Harappa 1, Rupar <- MD 1: 5 of 7 sealings sit
nearer the sea than their seal, but 2 of 4 site pairs, and all four flows start at the two big cities, so this is MD's size, not
a coastal direction.
POWER: with 17 links placed with probability ~ n_i n_j exp(-d / 300 km) the Mantel test detects the decay 50% of the time; with
links confined to pairs < 400 km apart, 100%. So a strongly local network would have shown; a gently decaying one could hide.
