S-DARK-67 cycle 3: the copper image set against the seal emblem set; copper texts against emblem-specific seal statistics
(tools/dark_loop67.py 3; full log loop67_cycle3_log.txt)

Image sets. IM77 copper (110 labelled tablets): ox-antelope 23, goat-antelope 17, hare 10, two-headed fabulous animal 9,
elephant 7, rhino 6, horned archer 6, endless knot 4, horned tiger 3, unicorn 3, short-horned bull 3, tiger 1, buffalo 1,
composite 1, double-axe 1, plus 14 image-as-sign faces. IM77 seals (1,375 with a field symbol): unicorn 1,043, short-horned
bull 83, uncertain bovine 53, zebu 51, elephant 36, composite 19, rhino 16, tiger 13, scenes 13, goat-antelope 12, buffalo 12,
gharial 4, personage 4, horned tiger 2, ox-antelope 2. Shared classes 12 (72/110 copper tablets); copper-only: two-headed
animal, hare, endless knot, double-axe (none on any seal); seal-only: zebu, scenes. The frequency order is inverted: the
unicorn is 3/110 on copper vs 1,043/1,375 on seals; the ox-antelope 23/110 vs 2/1,375. Wells agrees (copper: hare 12,
'Anth' 11, 'Elep' 11, 'Comp' 8, 'Gaur' 8, 'Bull1:W' = unicorn 4; seals: Bull1 = unicorn 1,248 of ~1,700).

Copper text vs emblem-specific seal statistics (1,143 IM77 seals with complete text and emblem; for each copper text and its
image class: share of that emblem's seals with the same head sign, the same last-2 unit, mean sign-set Jaccard; emblem
labels shuffled among seals 1,000x). Among the 18 copper texts whose animal has >= 5 seals, 2 of 54 statistics reach
P < 0.05 (expected 2.7): goat text 102-190-48-8-342-373 has a non-jar head, like 8/11 goat seals (P = 0.019); bull text
102-59-8-342 ends in 8-342, as do 5/72 short-horned bull seals (P = 0.022; 8-342 = the three-headed person + jar, 26 seals,
18 of them unicorn). Elephant: the copper texts 336-89-296-342(-98) have head jar / other at the elephant-seal rate
(0.32 vs null 0.39; 0.42 vs 0.36), their unit 296-342 is on 0/31 elephant seals and 13 unicorn seals, sign-set Jaccard
0.08 vs 0.085. Rhino: 4 copper texts, all at or below the null on 13 rhino seals. Unicorn: the copper unicorn text's unit
245-245 is on 10/888 unicorn seals = 0.011 = the overall rate (P = 0.49). Pooled, the copper last-2 unit is on 15/1,249
same-emblem seals (0.012) vs 100/19,325 other-emblem comparisons (0.005), the difference carried by the unicorn text and
the 8-342 unit. Wells check (shared signs per seal between the copper text and seals of the same emblem vs others, 1,000x):
0/19 at P < 0.05; elephant seals share fewer signs with the elephant copper text than other seals do (0.45 vs 0.63,
P = 0.96), goat seals 0.29 vs 0.62 (P = 0.99), tiger 0.23 vs 0.17 (P = 0.43).
