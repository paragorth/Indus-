LOOP 12 (stock texts as denominations), cycle 1: does the number of copies per stock text follow a structured series?
Data: data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json joined to data/raw/inscriptions.csv by cisi + sign sequence (all 5,369 records matched; faces of one object counted once per text). Stock text = complete text on >=3 objects.
seq_raw: K=163 stock texts, 1,288 copies (75 texts with >=5 copies). seq_strong 170/1,331; seq_all 171/1,334.
(a1) copies in {4,8,16,32,64,128}: 36/163 (22%); null 1 (copies reassigned multinomially among the K texts) 33.2, P=0.32; null 2 (log-normal fitted to log copies) 32.9, P=0.30. Weight-series set {1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,160,...}: identical, P=0.32. seq_strong P=0.60, seq_all P=0.63. Harappa alone 10 vs 15.4 expected (P=0.96, i.e. FEWER than chance); Mohenjo-daro 9 vs 10.7 (P=0.79).
(a2) count x copies conservation, stock texts carrying exactly one numeral (n=52): Spearman(count, copies) rho=-0.12, p=0.40; CV(count*copies)=1.97 vs permuted-count null 1.90, P=0.62 (no conservation). The three Harappa voucher faces: tall 2 x 89 copies = 178, tall 3 x 120 = 360, tall 4 x 106 = 424 - rising, not constant and not inverse.
Copy-count histogram is a smooth heavy tail (3:62, 4:26, 5:19, 6:13, 7:10, 8:9, ... 38, 89, 106, 120), no peaks at series values.
Arrows fired this cycle: 2 statistics x 2 nulls x 3 levels; nothing below P=0.30 before correction.
Verdict: NULL. Copy numbers are a Zipf-like tail, not a denomination series.
