IM77 names only 5 sites (MD 1,338 texts, Harappa 725, Lothal 159, Kalibangan 94, Chanhu-daro 66; 2,382 distinct texts), so the
20-site arrows of cycles 1-2 cannot be re-fired there. What can be checked: (a) the whole-vocabulary Mantel of cycle 4 on the
5 sites (60-text equal subsamples x200, exact permutation, 120), all objects and seals; (b) the shared-text links of cycle 3.

RESULT: (a) all objects: JSD ~ km rho +0.46 (exact p 0.17), JSD ~ coast difference rho +0.56 (p 0.050); seals only: km +0.43
(p 0.22), coast +0.48 (p 0.09). Same shape as Wells/CISI: Mohenjo-daro is the vocabulary-nearest site of every other site
(0.21-0.23 bits on seals), Lothal-Kalibangan the farthest pair (0.28). The positive rho is the same object-mix pattern seen in
cycle 4 (Lothal sealings, Harappa tablets) and does not reach the exact threshold even on all objects; on seals it is weaker,
as in Wells. Transcription-robust in the sense that both transcriptions give a flat seal vocabulary with MD at the centre.
(b) shared texts (>= 3 signs): 74 links (58 on seals): MD-Harappa 41, MD-Lothal 11, Harappa-Lothal 6 (904 km), Chanhu-daro-MD 4
(130 km), Harappa-Kalibangan 3 (177 km). Links scale with n_i x n_j, not with distance: the two nearest pairs (Chanhu-daro-MD,
Harappa-Kalibangan) have 4 and 3 links, the 900-km pair Harappa-Lothal has 6.
