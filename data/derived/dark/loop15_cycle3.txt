grammatical markers; 1 and 2 are written with tall strokes only and only in fixed terms (II-fish, II-jar, II-W700, 2-tree

tree, W900 or any short-counted good; a tall 2 before a tree may appear, a tall 1 before a tree should not (if W31 is a marker).
