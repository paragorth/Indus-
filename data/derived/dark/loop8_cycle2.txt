S-DARK-8.2  Two-line, boustrophedon and column-wise reading (tools/dark8_lines.py, dark8_lines_match.py; logs loop8_cycle2_log.txt, loop8_cycle2b_log.txt, loop8_cycle2c_ur3_log.txt; numbers loop8_cycle2_lines.json)

Line-break sources. (A) Wells raw 'text' field in data/raw/inscriptions.csv: 118 texts carry '/', 74 usable (no damage, two segments). The canonical build reverses the whole string, so it reads the segment after '/' first (= 'line 1' below), then the segment before '/'. (B) IM77 corpus lines: 89 sides with lines 1 and 2 (122 before dropping damaged / 3-line sides); directions recorded per line: 45 R-L/R-L, 27 R-L/single, 5 R-L/L-R (explicit boustrophedon record), 2 L-R/L-R, ...
12 orders tested per text: line order {12, 21} x flip line 1 {no, yes} x flip line 2 {no, yes} (8), plus column-wise interleave zip(l1,l2), zip(l2,l1), each with l2 flipped or not (4). Score: mean nll/token under a trigram model trained ONLY on one-line texts of the same transcription (3,239 Wells / 2,513 IM77). Paired bootstrap for the best order vs stored 12; null = signs shuffled inside each line, 200 reps, best-order gain over 12 recorded (search-corrected). Held-out split MD+HP vs other sites. Positive control: 400 two-line Ur III legends (model on 13,556 flattened three-line legends).

Results (nll/token; lower = better).
Ur III control: 12 = 3.60, 21 = 4.93, best boustrophedon 6.48, best column-wise 7.47; known order wins by 1.33 nats, shuffled-null 95th 0.21.
IM77 all 89: 12 = 5.18, 12-r (line 2 flipped) 5.72, 21 = 5.97, col12 6.02, others 6.2-7.8. Stored order best in every subset: MD+HP (64) 5.19 vs 21 5.92; other sites (25) 5.15 vs 6.10; both lines >= 2 signs (52) 5.05 vs 5.34; the 5 recorded R-L/L-R sides 5.24 vs 5.36; 45 R-L/R-L sides 5.00 vs 5.29. Margin 12 vs 21, both lines >= 2 (n = 52): -0.285 nats/token (95% CI -0.52..-0.06) but order 12 wins in only 30/52 texts (binomial P = 0.33): the mean is carried by a minority of texts with a strong frame.
Wells all 74: 21 = 5.62 vs canonical 12 = 5.67 (gain +0.044, CI -0.30..+0.22, shuffled-null median 0.07, P = 0.61) - a tie. MD+HP 55: tie (5.71 vs 5.72). Other sites 19: 21 better by 0.16 (CI -0.72..+0.46). Both segments >= 2 (56): 21 better by 0.074 (CI -0.22..+0.36), canonical wins 25/56. Boustrophedon variants (12-r, 21r-) cost 0.4-0.5 nats, column-wise 0.6-0.9, double flips 1.5+.
Wells vs IM77 line labelling: of 74 Wells '/' texts only 9 could be matched to an IM77 two-line side through the bridge; 8 have canonical order = IM77 line 1 -> line 2, 1 (Mohenjo-daro, canonical lines 2-140-269-740 | 692-60) the reverse.
Interpretation. IM77's line 1 -> line 2 is clearly the better reading of IM77's own two-line sides (its lines are read in reading order and numbered by the editor, so this partly tests Mahadevan's numbering). The Wells '/' marker carries no usable line-order information: its two segments are read equally well in either order, i.e. the '/' is not a consistent 'line 1 | line 2' record (or the two registers are independent units, consistent with S255: jar never begins a line). Boustrophedon as a general rule is rejected in both transcriptions (flipping either line costs 0.4-0.9 nats/token); the 5 sides Mahadevan recorded as R-L/L-R are already stored in reading order and need no further flip. Column-wise reading is rejected (0.6-0.9 nats worse, never better than null).
