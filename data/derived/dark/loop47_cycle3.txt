The 1949 purchase predates the main forgery market and the text copies nothing published; the modern impression is the museum's. (5) Cleveland 1973.160: GENUINE EXCEPTION or PROBABLE MODERN PIECE, not separable by the script alone: the identical double grid is real (not damage, not a confusion pair), unattested in Wells (0 of 3,463) but attested once in IM77 (Mohenjo-daro 1004, M245 twice), and the rest of the text (615 with 400, person W90, bracketed leaf W793, comb final) is well-formed; the copy test rules out a copy of any published seal (NN d = 3, P = 0.99), which is the usual mode of 1970s forgeries; the seal was published in 1974 and 1998 by the museum and never challenged.
