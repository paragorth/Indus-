LOOP 33 CYCLE 4: size of the code (tools/dark_loop33.py 4 <level>; logs loop33_cycle4_seq_<level>.txt)

Method. Seal automata fitted on all 1,259 MD+H seals (ALERGIA alpha 0.05; Markov-1 and Markov-2 as PFAs for comparison), RAW (120 signs + OTHER)
and CLASS alphabets. Best-first enumeration of every string with probability >= 1e-3, 1e-4, 1e-5 (max length 12); how many of them occur in the fit
set; the probability mass of the enumerated set; P(next text is one already seen) = sum of model probabilities of the 1,107-1,116 distinct seen
seal texts; Good-Turing P(new) = share of singleton texts.

Results (seq_raw; seq_strong and seq_all within 0.01):
  RAW, ALERGIA 15 states: p >= 1e-3: 49 strings (mass 0.125), 25 seen; p >= 1e-4: 606 strings (mass 0.273), 108 seen (18%); p >= 1e-5: 5,879 strings (mass 0.41), 265 seen (4.5%).
       P(next seal text already seen) = 0.10 -> P(new) = 0.90; Good-Turing P(new) = 0.82-0.83 (1,030-1,041 singletons of 1,259 seals).
  RAW, Markov-1: 745 strings at 1e-4 (mass 0.38), 192 seen; P(new) 0.79.  Markov-2: 1,066 strings (mass 0.45), 500 seen; P(new) 0.67 (overfits the seen texts).
  CLASS, ALERGIA 14-15 states: 723-775 strings at 1e-4 (mass 0.40-0.42), 188-206 seen (24-28%); P(new) 0.73-0.77; Good-Turing 0.70.

Reading. At probability >= 1e-4 the automaton generates about 600 (RAW) to 770 (CLASS) distinct seal texts, of which only a fifth to a quarter have
been seen, and this set carries only 27-42% of the automaton's probability mass: 58-73% of the mass lies in strings each rarer than 1e-4, i.e. in an
effectively unbounded tail. A compact code with a bounded vocabulary of messages would put most of its mass on a listable set. The automaton's own
estimate of the share of new texts among future seals (0.90 raw signs, 0.75 classes) agrees with Good-Turing on the same texts (0.82 / 0.70) and
with S309 (95% new for texts of 3+ signs over the whole corpus): the seen/possible ratio is low because the generator is open, not because the
sample is small relative to a fixed message set. Under the automaton, the ~240 unseen signs of S309 would only widen the tail.
