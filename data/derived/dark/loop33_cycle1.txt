LOOP 33 CYCLE 1: learning the code as a probabilistic finite automaton (tools/dark_loop33.py 1 <level>; logs loop33_cycle1_seq_<level>.txt, tables loop33_c1_<level>.json)

Data. Fit set = Mohenjo-daro + Harappa texts, complete, direction recorded (2,946 texts: 1,259 seals, 1,472 tablets, 215 other), from
data/derived/merged-corpus-canonical.json (CLAUDE.md data caution: older CSV; counts not final). 80/20 train/dev split. Alphabets: CLASS (52 symbols:
OPEN, CONN, marked jar, SUF, each of the 11 paradigm closers, fish marks, plain fish, trees, leaf, short/tall numerals, TITLE, bracket, tablet-title
class, 40 frequent lexicon signs, OTHER) and RAW (120 commonest signs + OTHER). Learners: ALERGIA (Hoeffding merging, alpha 0.5-0.001, t0 3, root
never a merge target, target chosen by distribution similarity), an MDL-greedy merger (MDI-like), MAP-EM HMM with termination (K 5/10/20/40,
Dirichlet 0.1), and unigram / Markov-1 / Markov-2 as PFAs. MDL = model bits (per transition log2 S + log2 |A| + 0.5 log2 N; per HMM parameter
0.5 log2 N) + data bits. Controls with the same learners: within-text shuffle, within-slot shuffle, a Markov-2 synthetic clone of the training set,
and (seq_raw) Ur III seal legends, Linear B tablet lines, Proto-Elamite lines (3,000 texts each, loaders as in tools/dark_loop25.py).

Results, dev nats per sign (gain = unigram minus model), ranges over seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all:
  Indus seals, CLASS: unigram 3.02-3.03; Markov-1 (51-53 states) 2.46-2.47; ALERGIA best 8-16 states, 272-426 transitions, 2.56-2.59 (gain 0.45);
     MDL-merge 7-9 states 2.60-2.63; HMM K=40 2.33-2.35 (gain 0.68; K=10 2.42, K=20 2.34). Lowest MDL: HMM-5/10 (22.1-23.4 kbit) < MDL-merge 23.7-23.9 < ALERGIA 23.8-24.7 < Markov-1 28.7.
     within-text shuffle: ALERGIA collapses to 2-4 states, dev 3.00-3.02 = unigram (gain 0.00-0.03); best MDL = unigram.
     within-slot shuffle: 5 states, 2.70-2.73 (gain 0.30; HMM 0.41) - about 2/3 of the Indus gain comes from the frame positions alone.
     Markov-2 clone: 7-16 states, 2.43-2.52 (gain 0.47-0.54; HMM 0.66): the clone is learned as well as or better than the real texts.
  Indus all types + type symbol, CLASS: unigram 3.12-3.13; Markov-1 1.98-2.00; ALERGIA best alpha 0.5 with 105-106 states (1.98-2.01), MDL-merge 19-20 states
     2.11-2.13, HMM-40 1.98-2.02 (gain 1.1, mostly the tablet formula 'tall numeral + W700' and the type-specific closers). Shuffle gain 0.5-0.6 (type + length), slot-shuffle 0.8.
  Indus seals, RAW: unigram 3.72-3.74; Markov-1 3.29-3.34; ALERGIA 8-15 states 3.30-3.34 (gain 0.42); HMM-40 2.96-2.98 (gain 0.76). Shuffle control: 2 states, gain -0.01 to -0.04.
  Other scripts (RAW-style alphabet, 3,000 texts): Ur III legends ALERGIA 111 states, gain 1.21 (Markov-1 1.29); Linear B lines 113 states, gain 0.63 (HMM 0.67);
     Proto-Elamite lines 4 states, gain 0.55 (HMM 0.77). Indus seals RAW sit with Proto-Elamite and Linear B (0.42 / 0.76), far below Ur III legends.

Reading. A small automaton is learnable and real: on shuffled texts the learner finds nothing (2-4 states, no gain), on Indus it finds 8-16 states worth
0.45 nats/sign on seals, and the MDL optimum is a 5-10-state HMM. But (i) two thirds of that gain is in the frame positions (the slot-shuffle keeps 0.30);
(ii) a Markov-2 clone of the corpus is learned equally well, so nothing in the Indus texts is more 'machine-like' than a second-order chain of themselves;
(iii) the automaton that best predicts held-out texts is not compact: when all object types are pooled the best ALERGIA automaton has 105 states and still
only matches Markov-1; (iv) the predictive gain per sign (0.4-0.8 of 3.0-3.7 nats) is in the Linear B / Proto-Elamite range, half of what Ur III seal legends
give (1.2-1.3), i.e. the texts are much less predictable than a formulaic legend system, let alone a designed code. Same on all three merge levels.
