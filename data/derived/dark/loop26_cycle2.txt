dependency in a handful of pre-jar compounds (255+435, 220+415, 415+798) with NO exclusions. The tablets (24%, 55%) carry most of the
corpus-wide signal, and that is whole-text repetition (the Harappa moulded series), not middle-internal structure, which cycle 4 removes by
deduplication. Quantity seals show the one attribute-like signature in the corpus: the numerals exclude one another (exclusions 0.10 vs
compounds 0.03), i.e. one count per middle.
