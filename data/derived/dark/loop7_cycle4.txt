S-DARK-7.4  Whole-space geometry: clustering, principal axes, Voynich placement
================================================================================
Tools: tools/dark7_cycle4.py; tables in loop7_cycle4_tables.txt. 13 corpora (Indus all/seals/non-seals, PE, LA,
LB words/syllables, Ur III legends words/syllables, Ur III names, Linear Elamite, Voynich words/chars), 600
statistics, distance = median |z| over statistics.

1. Average-linkage clustering, all statistics:
   {Indus all, seals, non-seals} (0.5-0.7) + {LB words, LB syll} at 1.88, then + {PE, LA} at 2.33.
   Other branch: {Ur III names + Linear Elamite} 0.90, {Ur III words + Voynich words} 1.12, {Ur III syll + Voynich
   chars} 1.95, joined at 1.7-2.1. The two branches meet at ~3.4.
   Plain words: the 600 random statistics split the corpora into 'short administrative lines with numerals'
   (Indus, Linear B, Linear A, Proto-Elamite) and 'name / royal / unknown text without numerals' (Ur III, Linear
   Elamite, Voynich). Indus is in the first group, but its nearest member is Linear B (a language), not PE or LA.
2. Non-numeral statistics only (477): same picture; Indus -> LB_syll 1.27-1.33; PE's nearest is Indus seals (1.81);
   Ur III names' nearest is Indus (1.54). Removing the numerals does not move Indus toward PE/LA.
3. Order-dependent, non-numeral statistics (99; the sequence-syntax part): {Indus, LB} at 5.6, then + {Ur III words,
   names} at 7.5; {PE, LA} join each other only at 8.5 and everything else later; {Linear Elamite, Voynich words} 5.4.
   Sequence-wise, Indus is with Linear B lines and Ur III legends (name + title + patronym strings), not with the
   accounting entries.
4. PCA of z-standardised profiles: PC1 (28%) = 'bigram surprise / rarity / inventory richness' (loadings: bigram
   surprise in middle and penult, rare signs early, slot entropy). Voynich words +21, Ur III words +13, LB words +11
   (large open vocabularies) vs Ur III syll -10, LA -8, Voynich chars -21 (closed inventories); Indus -2.6, seals -0.2,
   non-seals -4.2 -- Indus sits at the inventory-richness of LB syllables (+2.2) and Linear Elamite (+0.8), i.e. like a
   syllabary-sized sign list in use, not like a word list and not like a 26-letter alphabet. PC2 (23%) = numeral
   placement (next-is-numeral early vs distance to numeral late): PE -17, LA -10, Indus -7/-4.6/-9.5, LB -5/-1.5 vs
   the numeral-free corpora +2.5 to +12. On PC2 Indus is on the accounting side (it has numerals) but less extreme
   than PE/LA because its numerals are not line-final. PC3 (12%) separates Indus (+6.9, +5.5, +6.6) with Voynich
   words (+7.8) and names (+4.7) from Ur III words (-15.9), UR3 syll (-7.6), PE (-6.1): the 'number first / few
   repeats' residue axis.
5. Voynich (unknown) placement: words -> Linear Elamite 1.08, Ur III legend words 1.12, Ur III names 2.14; far from
   Indus (3.50) and PE (3.58). Chars -> Ur III names 1.85, Ur III syll 1.95, Linear Elamite 1.93; Indus 3.13. On the
   non-numeral Indus-residue axes Voynich words are the extreme opposite of the accounting corpora (document
   frequency of late words z -30, evenness z +33): an open vocabulary with almost no repeated line-final words.
   Plain words: the Voynich lines behave like running text in a language with a large word stock (Linear Elamite
   dedications, Ur III legends), not like Indus seals and not like accounting entries. It does not share the Indus
   numeral residue (it has no numerals) and it is not Indus-like on any order-dependent axis (median |z| 9.2).

Verdict (what the random statistics say the Indus texts are): short texts drawn from a syllabary-sized inventory
used flatly, with the sequence behaviour of Linear B lines and Ur III seal legends (name/title strings), carrying
numerals that precede their item and open rather than close the text. They are not Proto-Elamite-like entries
(item + closing number, rigid template, most beyond-unigram structure) and not Linear A-like. No 'accounting-notation'
fingerprint uniting Indus with PE and LA exists at this level of description.
