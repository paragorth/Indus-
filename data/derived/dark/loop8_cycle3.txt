S-DARK-8.3  'Emblem first': does the field symbol as a leading token tighten the grammar? (tools/dark8_emblem.py; log loop8_cycle3_log.txt; numbers loop8_cycle3_emblem.json)

Setup. 1,546 seals with a recorded symbol (canonical 'symbol' field, coarse class before ':'; 29 classes, unicorn 1,180, gaur 102, zebu 50, elephant 39, ...), texts of >= 2 signs without damage. Trigram model fit on MD+HP (1,247), scored on other sites (299): nll/token of the SIGN tokens with (a) no emblem, (b) emblem class prepended as the first token (so sign 1 is predicted from the emblem). Also (c) emblem predicted from the last two signs, and the mutual information MI(emblem; first sign) vs MI(emblem; last sign) over all 1,256 seals of >= 3 signs. Control: emblem labels shuffled across seals, 200 reps (re-fit each time). seq_raw / seq_strong / seq_all.

Results.
(a) vs (b): prepending the emblem makes held-out signs HARDER to predict: 4.731 -> 4.854 (raw), 4.665 -> 4.777 (strong), 4.646 -> 4.757 (all); the shuffled-emblem null gives the same loss (median -0.12, 95th -0.07), i.e. the loss is the cost of fragmenting the <s> context by 29 labels and the real emblem buys back nothing beyond a random label. P = 0.47-0.56.
(c) Emblem predicted from the last two signs: 3.46-3.49 nats vs shuffled null 2.78 - WORSE than random labels. This metric is confounded: the held-out sites have a different emblem mix from MD+HP, which shuffling removes. Not interpretable as grammar; reported for completeness.
MI, whole corpus (bits): emblem x first sign 0.63-0.64 (null median 0.60-0.62, 95th 0.63-0.66) - not above null; emblem x last sign 0.43-0.44 (null median 0.38-0.39, 95th 0.41-0.42) - nominally above the 95th in all three merge levels, excess about 0.05 bits; emblem x middle sign 0.57-0.59 (not tested, between the two).
Interpretation. The emblem does not act as an opener: it adds no predictive context for the first sign, out of sample, beyond what a random label adds. The only signal is a small excess association between emblem and the LAST sign (closer), about 0.05 bits over null, consistent with earlier closer-animal links (S223/S253 zebu-W930 type findings) rather than with an 'emblem first' reading. Caveat: 76% unicorn; most of the MI is a unicorn-vs-rest split.
