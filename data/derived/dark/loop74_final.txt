ANSWER. (C) partly: across sites the recurrence is fully a bigram effect, and that is the part new material reproduces. The rest
(~20% of the statistic) is LOCAL: within one site, under the same head, with no positional preference, no shared emblem and no age
order. That fits (A) in a weak form: a fuller and a shorter writing of the same designation within one local repertoire (it is
equally open to local formula reuse). It does not fit (B): no prefix stem, no emblem or room sharing, no corpus-wide stems. It does
not fit name-list hypocoristics either, which are one-sided (prefix or suffix).
