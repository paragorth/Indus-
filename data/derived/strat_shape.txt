## Verdict: does form encode function?
(a) Shape family does carry slot information beyond chance grouping of signs (sign-level MI P = 0.0005; one vote per sign P = 0.0035),
    but no family is slot-pure: purity 0.45-0.77 vs 0.58-0.62 for random sign sets, no family significant (best fish 0.75, P = 0.09).
    The family signal is carried by a few frequent signs that happen to be frame words: the jar (closer) in the 700s, the
    diamond/leaf openers in the 800s, stroke numerals in 1-89. Remove those three and the families are ordinary mixed vocabularies.
(b) FAILS. The added mark does not do one job across families: offset-cosine 0.035 (auto, 41 pairs) and 0.109 (curated, 31 pairs),
    P = 0.32-0.42 against random within-family partners, on all three merge levels. The one sub-result, mark above the base
    (fish hat/whiskers, U + tall stroke, 455->456, 702->703; 6 pairs, P = 0.022), is uncorrected and is the known fish qualifier effect (S296).
    The weak trend of S147/S323 (marked sign -> NAME element, 77% of curated pairs) repeats but is not stronger than drift to the baseline.
(c) MOSTLY FAILS. 3 of 13 ligatures read as their component sequence (880 = 861+820 is an opener variant; 705/706 = U + tall stroke,
    P = 0.05); 10 of 13, including leaf+tree 803/806, person+pitchfork 130, U+fork 772, share no context with either component (new atoms).
    226 = fish + 2 strokes resembles the plain fish (cos 0.77, P = 0.014) but not the sequence 220-32 (S3 found 220-520 instead).
(d) Script-like, not code-like: frequent signs are simpler (rho = -0.14, P = 0.003, as S164), rank-frequency is a straight log-log line
    (slope -1.48, R^2 0.96). Complexity by slot is weak (P = 0.22 raw; P = 0.03 only after removing frequency, driven by 14 COUNT signs).
Overall: form encodes function only where shape and frame word coincide (jar, diamond openers, numerals). The designed-code predictions
(slot-pure families, one modifier = one job, ligature = component sequence) fail their controls. The inventory looks like an evolved
pictographic script: shape families are semantic/iconic groups, not grammatical slots. Caveat: lipi font drawings are modern normalisations,
and 76% of tokens are Mohenjo-daro + Harappa.
