================ VERDICT ================
Coverage: 1,996 of 2,250 seals have parsed slots; 1,527 have a non-empty middle (NAME slot). Dated seals: Mohenjo-daro 609 (HARP-style 'time') / 612 (Early-Intermediate-Late); Harappa 255 ('time') / 205 (Vats strata). Same-closer dated seals with a middle: Mohenjo-daro 307, Harappa 95-117. Results are the same on seq_raw, seq_strong and seq_all (no merge dependence).

Prediction 1 (closers/openers = few-valued high digits, middles = many-valued low digits): the SHAPE holds trivially (15 closers vs ~520 middle signs) but the closer digit is not used as a digit: one value (jar) takes 70% of closers (H/Hmax 0.43 vs 0.997 for uniform digits). A counter does not spend 70% of its issues on one high-order value.
Prediction 2 (shared middle prefix = issued close in time): FAILS. Mohenjo-daro, 43 closer groups, 66-75 prefix-sharing pairs vs ~4,200 others: diff in |delta time| -0.10 to +0.03 levels, P = 0.42-0.70 on both dating schemes, first-sign and two-sign prefixes alike. Harappa 'time': P = 0.35-0.43. Harappa Vats strata: P = 0.04 on 8-9 sharing pairs (1 of 8 tests at 0.05, not robust to the other Harappa scheme; not evidence).
Prediction 3 (near-uniform digits): FAILS. Middle signs H/Hmax 0.80 (uniform digits: 0.99); Zipf slope of the middle-sign ranks -0.74, identical to Ur III name syllables (-0.75) and far from flat digits (-0.09 to -0.12). 41% of middle signs are singletons; uniform digits would have none.
Prediction 4 (open class, near-linear growth): PARTLY. Indus middles grow at 0.75 new per added seal, between Ur III names (0.27-0.29) and planted single-counter serials (0.93); a per-office counter model gives 0.57. Unique fraction 0.73 vs names 0.25 vs serials 0.91. But 82 middles recur under DIFFERENT closers and 33 full texts repeat on 70 seals (4.6%); serials forbid both. Middles avoid repeated signs (25-27 vs 89-105 in the sign-shuffled control, ratio 0.27), as words and names do and as counters cannot.
Test d (issue order): FAILS. Within-closer Spearman of mixed-radix middle value against stratigraphic rank: Mohenjo-daro rho -0.05 to -0.01 (P = 0.5-0.9, 16 groups, 201-205 seals); Harappa rho -0.01 to +0.09 (P = 0.5-0.97). Middle length vs time: Mohenjo-daro -0.01 to -0.03.

Serial-number hypothesis: KILLED. Middles are an open, non-repeating class (the one serial-like trait, already known from S85/S312 and equally expected of names), but their digits are Zipfian with the exact slope of Mesopotamian name syllables, they avoid sign repetition, they recur under different closers, and nothing about them tracks stratigraphic time at either city.
Strongest single number: Zipf slope of Indus middle signs -0.74 vs Ur III name syllables -0.75 vs uniform digits -0.1 (Mohenjo-daro issue-order rho = -0.02, P = 0.78).
Caveats: dated material is 76% Mohenjo-daro + Harappa by construction; stratigraphic ranks are coarse (7-8 levels) and Marshall/Mackay periods are known to be unreliable; a time signal finer than one level could not be seen. Wells and IM77 transcriptions were not both run (slot labels exist only for the Wells-based canonical corpus).
